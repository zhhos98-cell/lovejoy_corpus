## Harvard / Paris field architecture

Pre-Lovejoy Harvard is controlled as a distributed history-of-religions ecology rather than a single transmitted method. Jastrow 1893/1899 distinguishes specialist source jurisdictions, direct recourse to sources, and comparative synthesis. Everett, Toy, and Lanman represent different evidentiary resolutions; Lovejoy later traverses all three.

Jastrow also demonstrates that source specialization can coexist with progressive/civilizational hierarchy. Therefore source discipline cannot be used as evidence of anti-totalization or political innocence.

Paris/Marillier is an overlap zone, not a clean Harvard→Paris handoff. Thomas 1898 provides a same-workshop control in which local form/function/origin distinctions coexist with racial/civilizational reaggregation.

Safe rule:

> **decomposition does not imply anti-totalization.**

No direct Marillier→Lovejoy total-method transmission should be claimed.

## 1898 → 1906 Buddhist source-practice bridge

The strongest continuity is actor-level source recurrence.

In the 1898 JAOS article Lovejoy directs inquiry into the original signification of Buddhist technical terms first to the **Sutta Nipāta**. In his April 1906 review of Paul Elmer More he again invokes **Sutta Nipāta 1073–1076** against an over-positive reconstruction of Nirvāṇa.

The 1906 review also explicitly values `induction of evidence`, inquiry into `historical conditions and antecedents`, `wide-ranging comparisons`, and correlation across heterogeneous literatures.

This establishes:

> **1898 source-controlled Buddhist semantics → 1906 reuse of the same canonical source to police a Nirvāṇa interpretation.**

It does not establish continuous annual Buddhist research, the same physical edition, or Buddhist causation of the later history-of-ideas programme.

## 1907–1910 actor-explicit controls

1907 `The Entangling Alliance of Religion and History` is direct-primary recovered. Lovejoy distinguishes universal/cosmic propositions from contingent historical occurrence, gives historical propositions probabilistic status, imposes stronger burden on extraordinary claims, and explicitly calls politics practical/relative/temporal.

1907 Toy work separates specialist chronological authority from Lovejoy's own logical/developmental reconstruction. 1908–09 pragmatism and Smith controversies make proposition decomposition and cumulative proof burden explicit. The 1909→1910 Kant revision supplies actor-acknowledged self-correction from umbrella classification to domain/proposition distinctions.

These are later controls; do not retroject their explicit vocabulary as Lovejoy's consciously formulated 1898 method.
