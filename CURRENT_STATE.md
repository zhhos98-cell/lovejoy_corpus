## New logical-analysis branch — current evidence state

### Direct Lovejoy actor-level controls

The strongest evidence is now a sequence rather than a retrospective resemblance.

- **1907 — `Kant's Classification of the Forms of Judgment`:** Lovejoy explicitly subjects Kant's classificatory table to formal-logical scrutiny, attacking ambiguity, equivocation, and failures of classificatory precision.
- **1908 — `The Thirteen Pragmatisms`:** the thirteen contentions are treated as logically independent; acceptance/refutation of one does not automatically transfer to the others under a shared historical label.
- **1909 — `Pragmatism and Realism`:** currently the strongest bridge. Lovejoy requires an historically real intellectual tendency to be subjected to **logical analysis and historical comparison**; compatible motives may then be recombined as logic permits, with historical novelty possibly residing in a `new compound in intellectual chemistry`.
- **1910 — `The Treatment of 'Opposition' in Formal Logic`:** direct intervention in formal logic; Lovejoy criticizes the traditional treatment for lack of clearness, precision, and systematic completeness and proposes a Venn-diagram treatment.
- **1911 — `Existence and Formal Logic`:** Lovejoy explicitly limits the jurisdiction of formal logic. Meaning/intelligibility, formal implication, verification/evidence, and historical truth are not interchangeable.
- **1912 — review of Bosanquet/Coffey/Schiller:** Lovejoy is operating inside a plural and contested field of logics rather than one settled logical school.
- **1913 — New Realism disputes:** a shared movement-name does not guarantee logical identity among constituent doctrines or shared solutions to a specific problem.
- **1916–17 — APA programme:** defined problems, methodological self-consciousness, cautious belief, cooperation, hypothetical treatment of ideas, logical austerity, and communicable/verifiable propositions become an explicit programme for philosophical inquiry; contemporaries contest the isolability of philosophical problems themselves.

Current actor-authored sequence:

`historical complex`
→ `logical disaggregation`
→ `relation / compatibility test`
→ `historical comparison`
→ `warranted recombination`
→ `historical distinctiveness at element or configuration level`.

This is not yet a demonstrated genealogy of the mature `unit-idea`, but it is now too strong to treat analytical decomposition as merely our retrospective vocabulary.

Current candidate for Lovejoy-specific portability is not `analysis` simpliciter but **jurisdiction partition**: after decomposition, he repeatedly controls which relation-type or evidence can warrant which proposition.

### Berkeley / Howison formation has moved from generic biography to logic ecology

`research_notes/Howison_Berkeley_formal_logic_training_ecology_control_2026-08-28.md` adds a direct contemporaneous curricular control before Harvard.

A University of California curriculum reported in 1890–91 gives `Propædeutic to Philosophy` as empirical psychology **including formal logic, deductive and inductive**, and states that the first three philosophy courses are permanent and repeated from year to year in substantially the same form. Lovejoy entered Berkeley in 1891 and remained through 1895. Howison's own professional biography also includes earlier appointments explicitly in logic/philosophy of science and logic/speculative philosophy.

Secure conclusion:

> **Formal logic belonged to the documented philosophical training ecology immediately before and during Lovejoy's Berkeley years.**

Open edge:

> **Lovejoy's exact enrollment in that course has not yet been demonstrated.**

Do not turn curricular availability + teacher relation into direct method transmission.

## Revised unit-idea problem

Do not replace the old caricature with a rehabilitation myth.

The live question is now:

> **To what extent was a `unit-idea` first an analytically isolated component of a historical complex, and what warrant then allowed Lovejoy to treat that component as identifiable across different contexts?**

Working distinction:

`identity sufficient for analytical/historical tracing`
≠ automatically
`identity independent of history`.

This preserves the genuine Lovejoy/Skinner dispute over trans-contextual identity while requiring the production of the analytical `unit` itself to be historicalized.

The branch therefore distinguishes four levels:

1. **operation:** decomposition, implication, opposition, logical independence, compatibility, classification;
2. **jurisdiction:** what logic can establish versus semantics, chronology, psychology, genealogy, or historical evidence;
3. **configuration:** how analytically distinguishable elements may recombine and modify one another;
4. **historical identity:** what licenses a component to remain identifiable across contexts.

The mature unit-idea problem sits mainly at level 4; levels 1–3 have their own history.
