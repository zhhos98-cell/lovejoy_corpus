## 5. Final logical-analysis calibration

`formal logic → one migrated method → history of ideas`.

Controlled model:

> **Lovejoy mobilized different analytical operations in different contexts; `logical` did not have one stable local meaning. The strongest recurrent candidate is typed relations + inferential jurisdiction rather than one transported formal-logical technique.**

Evidence levels must remain separate:

1. logic as object;
2. actor-explicit logical method outside formal logic;

Mandatory adversarial rule:

## 6. Final Carnap control

The Carnap line is frozen as field/adversarial control.

Controlled developmental sequence:

`May 1934 conceptual liberalization`
→ `spring 1935 Testability and Meaning draft`
→ `June 1935 plural verification conditions + still-strong translation/equipollence`
→ `September 1935 explicit non-eliminative reduction`
→ `31 December 1935 Baltimore jurisdiction clash`

Baltimore is therefore a public dispute over what an already liberalized and technically differentiated empiricism was still entitled to exclude as metaphysics. It is not the origin of Carnap's liberalization, reduction machinery, or verification-route plurality.
