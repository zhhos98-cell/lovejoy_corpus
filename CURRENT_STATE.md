### Marillier / EPHE research apparatus — now actor-near controlled

N. W. Thomas's own 1896 description of the EPHE materially upgrades the workshop claim. Thomas describes Marillier's course through the formula **`work by the pupils, directed by the lecturer`** and specifies a concrete procedure: pupils prepared papers on particular questions, read them in class, received Marillier's comments, and the other pupils discussed them. He also describes the formal diploma as a thesis on a **`critical or historical question`** after supervised study.

Official EPHE 1894–96 reports independently show Marillier's `Religions des peuples non civilisés` course working through comparative myth and ritual problems, including classification, missionary/Biblical contamination, and criticism of inherited anthropological theories.

The defensible institutional formulation is therefore:

> **directed student research + collective criticism + revisable classifications**, not transmission of one fixed Marillier doctrine.

Thomas's same-teacher relation and diploma-product status are secure. MD-007 now asks only whether his 1898 published diploma article itself already states the later form/meaning/origin or survivance-control operator.
