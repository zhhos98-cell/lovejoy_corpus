### 005 — shared discriminanda, particular redivision and dense case reallocation

Direct-primary attribution is now substantially closed by the complete user-returned Marillier `Survivance` text together with direct Söderblom 1901 same-workshop control.

Marillier himself already provides:

- the determinant field: rank, manner of death, ritual fidelity, journey skill/strength, courage/cowardice, divine arbitrariness, chance and moral conduct;
- an explicit moral/non-moral criterion: differentiated destiny is not moral unless allocation tracks the good/bad quality or moral value of earthly conduct;
- mixed cases in which `good/bad` classification is partly moral but principally ritual;
- contact/borrowing and superposition controls;
- older non-moral structures transformed but not erased by later moral reward/punishment;
- a transitional condition in which moral reward/punishment is recognizable but not yet disengaged from contrary conceptions or sharply defined;

Söderblom, another participant in Marillier's 1898–99 seminar environment, independently shows that student-level **serial redivision** was workshop-shared. He explicitly proposes `d’autres divisions`, lays out successive selector classes, uses `principes moraux ou quasi-moraux`, corrects observer `good/bad` categories by the underlying selector, and blocks a single `pour ainsi dire mécanique` mapping between conceptual stages and global civilizational degree.

- `desert` = Lovejoy-local compact working term, while the inferential criterion itself has a direct Marillier equivalent;

- **serial selector ordering as such = same-workshop secure, not Lovejoy-specific**;

- non-mechanical, relation-specific developmental staging = available in the same Marillier environment.

The stronger Lovejoy notebook delta is narrower and documentary:

### Marillier / EPHE research apparatus — now actor-near controlled

N. W. Thomas's own 1896 description of the EPHE materially upgrades the workshop claim. Thomas describes Marillier's course through the formula **`work by the pupils, directed by the lecturer`** and specifies a concrete procedure: pupils prepared papers on particular questions, read them in class, received Marillier's comments, and the other pupils discussed them. He also describes the formal diploma as a thesis on a **`critical or historical question`** after supervised study.

Official EPHE 1894–96 reports independently show Marillier's `Religions des peuples non civilisés` course working through comparative myth and ritual problems, including classification, missionary/Biblical contamination, and criticism of inherited anthropological theories.

The defensible institutional formulation is therefore:

> **directed student research + collective criticism + revisable classifications**, not transmission of one fixed Marillier doctrine.

Söderblom's `d’autres divisions` gives an actor-near example of the research product this pedagogy could generate: inherited problem/source apparatus followed by student-specific redivision rather than doctrinal reproduction.

Thomas's same-teacher relation and diploma-product status are secure. MD-007 now asks only whether his 1898 published diploma article itself already states the later form/meaning/origin or survivance-control operator.
