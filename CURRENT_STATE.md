## 5. Final logical-analysis calibration

Superseded model:

`formal logic → one migrated method → history of ideas`.

Controlled model:

> **Lovejoy mobilized different analytical operations in different contexts; `logical` did not have one stable local meaning. The strongest recurrent candidate is typed relations + inferential jurisdiction rather than one transported formal-logical technique.**

Evidence levels must remain separate:

1. logic as object;
2. actor-explicit logical method outside formal logic;
3. analyst-recognized relation-sensitive/logical resemblance.

Positive metarule:

> identify the relation at stake, identify what evidence can warrant it, and require a new proof step before upgrading to a stronger relation.

Mandatory adversarial rule:

> **A ≠ B does not establish that A is historically or functionally independent of B.**

Lovejoy's distinctions therefore require bridge reconstruction: a legitimate non-identity can sometimes become overpartition, scope widening, or an overstrong target reconstruction.

This line is a calibration device for the Blog, not a standalone section.
