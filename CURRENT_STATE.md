Harvard→Paris is an overlap zone, not a clean handoff. 1904 controls show portability of proposition-sensitive inference outside the Buddhist setting without proving Buddhist causation of the later method.

Jastrow 1893 now supplies the strongest pre-Lovejoy institutional-method control for Harvard. Before Lovejoy's 1895 arrival, Jastrow already described Harvard's history-of-religions provision as Everett's general Comparative History of Religions supplemented by religion-specific work including Toy on Hebrew religion and Lanman on the Sacred Books of Buddhism — the same three pedagogical jurisdictions Lovejoy later directly traversed in 1896–97. The 1893 conclusion explicitly says general/comparative work acquires value through careful prolonged study of a particular religion, which requires source knowledge and lies within the specialist's province. The Cambridge History of Religions Club (formed winter 1891–92) and Toy-chaired American lecture committee are direct pre-Lovejoy institutional context, not Lovejoy participation evidence. Jastrow 1899 should now be read as a later sharpening into explicit `axiom` language, not as the first appearance of this architecture.

Toy 1899 is now a full-primary American teacher/problem-field control, not title-only adjacency. `Taboo and Morality` directly separates antecedent presence from active causal role, transmission from moral effect, and religious reinforcement from origin of a right; `Relation between Magic and Religion` directly restricts category scope by mechanism, rejects hard early binaries, redivides composite functions and blocks a local transmission mechanism from becoming a universal genealogy. Everett 1899 is now a second full-primary Harvard teacher control for the 004 side. His `Psychology of the Vedanta and Sankhya Philosophies` directly engages Garbe/Dahlmann, makes a strong Vedānta→Sāṃkhya developmental claim, marks a different origin hypothesis as merely conjectural, warns that imported terms such as `Soul`, `Nature` and `matter` distort the system, and actor-declares that author/commentator source strata are being compressed because his object is synthetic school psychology. **Chronology firewall:** the paper postdates Lovejoy 1898 and cannot be its source. Jastrow 1899 makes the same multi-jurisdiction structure more formal: he calls it almost axiomatic that the historical study of religions cannot be done by one individual, assigns Indian, Semitic/Egyptian/Judean/Greek/Roman and `primitive` religious fields to source-competent specialists, and separately makes direct recourse to sources an axiom for study of a particular religion; general/comparative work belongs to a different synthetic level. He names Everett/Toy as Harvard's distributed religion-study provision and Paris `Sciences Religieuses` as an institutional model. **Period-evolutionism firewall:** both 1893 and 1899 combine source-specialist discipline with progressive/developmental totalization. In 1893 Jastrow explicitly anticipates the `steadily upward tendency of religious thought`; in 1899 he ranks `advanced` religions against `naive`/`primitive` religions and assigns the latter special access to fundamental religious problems. This directly shows that fine source specialization and civilizational staging could coexist in the field; do not transpose that hierarchy into Lovejoy's political semantics or infer direct Jastrow→Lovejoy uptake.
