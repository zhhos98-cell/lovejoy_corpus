## 9. Provenance and cleanup policy

- `archive_index/` = locators, custody, archive coverage, reproduction/rights status, search provenance.
- `archive_transcriptions/` = canonical page records, deltas, material audits, integrated reading surface.
- `research_notes/` = terminal dossiers plus historical batch/process provenance.
- root payloads = legacy OCR/source payloads retained at stable paths and inventoried in `root_payload_index.md`.
- Git history = recovery layer for genuinely removed supersession-only files.

The repository is intentionally **not physically reorganized by moving heavily referenced legacy paths**. Authority is organized through living routing files instead.

A historical note may contain unique evidence even when its `ACTIVE`, `HOLD`, `final`, `complete`, or `next action` language is obsolete. Follow the living authority chain.

## 10. Reopening rule

Reopen a frozen research line only for:

- new/direct primary evidence bearing on a live proposition;
- a contradiction in current evidence;
- publication-level exact verification;
- a materially necessary actor-level reconstruction of a category already in use.

Argument development and diplomatic transcription are separate tracks. Do not force one to wait on the other unless the proposition actually depends on the unresolved manuscript reading.
