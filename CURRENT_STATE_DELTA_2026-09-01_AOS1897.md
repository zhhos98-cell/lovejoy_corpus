## Publication-safe core

> **In April 1897, while enrolled in Lanman's advanced Pāli course at Harvard, Lovejoy announced an AOS communication on the Milinda-pañha. The final Baltimore Proceedings show that he did not attend the meeting: his No.30 was instead read by title, under a revised description as a paper on the meaning of Buddhist technical terms. At the same meeting Lovejoy was elected a corporate member of the AOS, which also formally established its Section for the Historical Study of Religions. In 1898 JAOS published Lovejoy's `The Buddhistic technical terms upādāna and upādisesa`. The late-April 1897 title therefore provides a direct publication-path precursor to the 1898 article, although manuscript-level identity and the exact relation to the earlier Milinda title remain unresolved.**
