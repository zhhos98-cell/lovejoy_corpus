# What Was Arthur Lovejoy Doing with Buddhism?

## 中文逐段对照：洛夫乔伊究竟为何研究佛教？

The bibliography appended to Arthur O. Lovejoy’s [*Essays in the History of Ideas*](https://www.press.jhu.edu/books/title/5183/essays-history-ideas) gives him an unlikely beginning. Its first entry is an [1898 article on two Buddhist technical terms](https://dlbs.liberal.ntu.edu.tw/FULLTEXT/JR-ENG/love.htm), a piece of specialist Buddhist scholarship published decades before *The Great Chain of Being*.[^1] Lovejoy had in fact already published an essay on Lord Monboddo in 1895. Yet the bibliographical sequence poses a useful question: what was the future historian of the great chain of being trying to settle in a dispute over Buddhist terminology?

**中文：** 阿瑟·O·洛夫乔伊的《观念史论文集》所附书目，为他的学术生涯安排了一个颇不寻常的开端。书目第一项是一篇发表于1898年的佛教专门论文，讨论两个佛教技术术语；它问世时，距离《存在巨链》尚有数十年。[^1] 事实上，洛夫乔伊早在1895年已经发表过一篇关于蒙博多勋爵的文章。不过，这一书目次序仍提出了一个值得追问的问题：后来那位书写“存在巨链”历史的学者，当时究竟试图在一场佛教术语争论中解决什么？

Nor was this Buddhist episode simply forgotten. Daniel J. Wilson documented Lovejoy’s comparative-religion training, his Sanskrit and Buddhist studies, and his later work in Paris. Simon Schaffer could still [note in 2010](https://doi.org/10.1177/007327531004800309) that Lovejoy studied Pāli and Sanskrit there (484). The biographical fact survived; what survived less clearly was the work inside it. Among Lovejoy’s papers at Johns Hopkins is a Harvard Cooperative Society notebook labeled in his hand “Sankhya + Buddhism.” Its pages bring us into the disputes behind the published article: whether two Buddhist terms meant the same thing, how the links in a formula fitted together, and what borrowing could explain about the resulting doctrine.[^4]

**中文：** 这段佛教经历并非后来彻底被遗忘。Daniel J. Wilson 已经记录了洛夫乔伊在比较宗教、梵文和佛教方面的训练，以及他后来在巴黎的工作；到2010年，Simon Schaffer 仍可指出洛夫乔伊在那里研习过巴利文与梵文（484）。传记事实保存了下来；较少被保存的，是这些事实内部实际发生过的学术工作。约翰斯·霍普金斯大学所藏洛夫乔伊文稿中，有一本哈佛合作社笔记本，他亲笔题作“Sankhya + Buddhism”。这些页面把我们带回那篇已发表论文背后的争论现场：两个佛教术语是否同义，一个公式中的不同环节如何彼此衔接，以及“借用”究竟能解释后来形成的教义到什么程度。[^4]

Lovejoy himself named the larger difficulty from Auxerre on 20 October 1898. He proposed “to elucidate the theoretical presuppositions and doctrines of Buddhism from a philosophical point of view.” Philologists, he complained, had expounded Buddhist literature while caring little for philosophy. Philosophers had reconstructed Buddhist conceptions “more or less out of their own heads” while neglecting “the technicalities of the texts.” He therefore chose Sylvain Lévi as a teacher on the Buddhist texts. By December, when no Pāli Buddhism course was being offered, he expected instead to read Buddhist texts with Lévi privately.[^2]

**中文：** 1898年10月20日，洛夫乔伊在欧塞尔写信时亲自点明了这个更大的困难。他说自己的目标是“从哲学的观点阐明佛教的理论前提与教义”。在他看来，语文学家解释佛教文献，却很少关心哲学；哲学家则忽略“文本的技术细节”，多少“凭自己的脑袋”重构佛教观念。因此，他选择 Sylvain Lévi 作为研读佛教文本的老师。到12月，由于没有开设巴利佛教课程，他预计改为与 Lévi 私下阅读佛教文本。[^2]

One of the clearest notebook disputes begins with [Henry Clarke Warren](https://buddhadust.net/resources/pdf/warren/warren-on-p-s.pdf). Warren had warned that dependent origination should not be treated as a simple “chain of causation”: its links expressed different kinds of dependence, and each had to be examined separately (xxvii–xxx). His own treatment of *taṇhā* (desire) and *upādāna* (attachment) was more nuanced than a flat identity claim. He described attachment as the “tenacity of desire” and also reported a stage distinction before and after possession. Lovejoy nevertheless treated that close connection as an identity problem and interrupted it: “But not so if we are to follow the present text” (“Sankhya + Buddhism,” MS p. 75). He then set four kinds of attachment beside six forms of desire and asked what different work the two terms were doing. Following Warren’s own instruction to examine each link separately thus became a reason to resist Warren’s account of this one.[^7]

**中文：** 笔记中最清楚的一场争论之一始于 Henry Clarke Warren。Warren 曾警告，缘起不应被理解成一条简单的“因果链”：链条中的各环节表达的是不同类型的依存关系，因此必须逐项分别考察（xxvii–xxx）。他自己对 *taṇhā*（欲、渴爱）与 *upādāna*（取、执取）的处理，也比简单断言二者同一更为细致；他把执取说成欲望的“顽固持续”，同时还报告了占有之前与之后的阶段差别。洛夫乔伊仍然把这种紧密关系压缩成一个“是否同一”的问题，并在笔记中直接打断 Warren：“But not so if we are to follow the present text”（“Sankhya + Buddhism,” MS p. 75）。接着，他把四种执取与六种欲望并列起来，追问两个术语究竟分别在做什么。于是，Warren 自己要求逐项检验各环节的原则，反过来成了洛夫乔伊拒绝 Warren 在这一处具体解释的理由。[^7]

A second dispute moved from the relation between links to the history of the formula itself. In [“À propos de la théorie bouddhique des douze nidânas”](https://play.google.com/store/books/details/M%C3%A9langes_Charles_de_Harlez?id=g849OKp_GrsC), Émile Senart argued that the twelve *nidānas* contained heterogeneous categories assembled from different sources. Repetition and mixture could therefore be evidence of a secondary construction (284–88). Lovejoy conceded much of the premise. The terms need not all be originally Buddhist, the series is not a homogeneous temporal sequence, and materials may have been borrowed. But he refused the further inference that a composite origin made the formula accidental or unintelligible. Hermann Oldenberg had already challenged important parts of Senart’s semantic case in the third edition of *Buddha* (272–73, 443–55), so Lovejoy’s significance is not priority. What the proposed borrowings left open was what Buddhist authors had made of them: what structure had they given the parts, and what did that structure explain?[^7]

**中文：** 第二场争论把问题从各环节之间的关系推向了公式本身的历史。在《À propos de la théorie bouddhique des douze nidânas》中，Émile Senart 认为十二 *nidāna* 由来自不同来源的异质范畴拼合而成，因此重复与混杂可以成为后期构造的证据（284–88）。洛夫乔伊接受了其中相当一部分前提：这些术语未必都原本属于佛教，这一序列也并不是同质的时间顺序，其中的材料完全可能经过借用。但他拒绝进一步推断说，只要来源是复合的，整个公式就因此变得偶然或不可理解。Hermann Oldenberg 已经在《Buddha》第三版中反驳过 Senart 语义论证的重要部分（272–73, 443–55），所以洛夫乔伊的意义并不在于优先权。所谓“借用”真正没有回答的是：佛教作者后来怎样处理这些材料？他们赋予各部分什么结构，而这个结构又解释了什么？[^7]

The same limit appears in Lovejoy’s 1898 article. There he names Senart, tests the textual witnesses Senart had cited, and says that the terms of the formula, “perhaps even the ideas,” may be largely borrowed while their “arrangement and application” remain original and characteristic (127, 132). But reading article and notebook together also shows how much was left out. The notebook ranges across Sāṃkhya-Buddhist chronology, psychological classifications, textual criticism, etymology, causation, and competing genealogies. The article is much narrower. Publication did not simply polish the notebook; it reduced the size of some of its claims. The surviving AOS record sharpens that point without giving us a simple manuscript genealogy: *Milindapañha*, *upādānakkhandha*, and *upādisesa* change documentary roles across the 1897–98 title carriers, while the mechanism of revision remains unrecovered.[^5]

**中文：** 同样的限度也进入了洛夫乔伊1898年的论文。在那里，他点名 Senart，逐一检验 Senart 所引的文本见证，并承认公式中的术语，甚至“perhaps even the ideas”，都可能大体来自借用；但它们的“arrangement and application”仍可以是佛教自身独特而有特征性的工作（127, 132）。然而，把论文与笔记放在一起读，也会看到有多少东西没有进入发表文本。笔记横跨数论派—佛教年代问题、心理分类、文本批判、词源、因果以及彼此竞争的谱系解释；正式论文的范围要窄得多。出版并不只是把笔记打磨得更干净，它同时缩小了若干主张的尺度。现存美国东方学会记录进一步强化了这一点，却并没有给我们一条简单的“手稿演化谱系”：*Milindapañha*、*upādānakkhandha* 与 *upādisesa* 在1897—98年的不同标题载体中承担了不同文献角色，而具体修订机制仍未恢复。[^5]

Across the manuscript, the same materials recur under different tests. Lovejoy asks whether two terms are identical in use, what causal work a term performs, what comes before what in time, what belongs inside what as a classification, where a doctrine or term came from, and what a borrowed element does once placed in a larger structure. No result on one axis automatically settles another. On manuscript p. 123, for example, he places *viññāṇa* within the *nāma* side of a classification while writing beside it that “Viññāṇa is temporally (?) an antecedent of nāmarūpa, and logically a subdivision of it.” Forced onto one axis, the statements can look contradictory. On the page they belong to different operations: classification and sequence. The same term can occupy one position in the first and another in the second. The question mark beside “temporally” matters. Logical inclusion is stated; temporal order is still being tested. The page is less a declaration of method than a record of unfinished sorting.[^4]

**中文：** 在整本手稿里，同一批材料不断接受不同检验。洛夫乔伊会问：两个术语在实际用法中是否同一？一个术语承担什么因果作用？时间上谁先谁后？分类上什么包含在什么之中？某个教义或术语来自哪里？借入的成分一旦进入更大的结构，又在那里发挥什么作用？任何一个轴线上的答案，都不会自动解决另一个轴线的问题。例如在手稿第123页，他一方面把 *viññāṇa* 放在 *nāma* 的分类内部，另一方面又在旁边写道：“Viññāṇa is temporally (?) an antecedent of nāmarūpa, and logically a subdivision of it.” 如果把这些陈述强行压在同一条轴线上，它们看起来会互相矛盾；但在页面上，它们属于两种不同操作：分类与序列。同一个术语可以在前者中占一个位置，在后者中占另一个位置。“temporally”旁边那个问号尤其重要。逻辑上的包含关系已经被陈述出来；时间顺序却仍在检验之中。这一页与其说是在宣布一种“方法”，不如说是在记录一次尚未完成的整理。[^4]

A different technical term shows what happened when this kind of unfinished sorting moved into print. In the notebook Lovejoy moves among competing etymologies of *upādisesa*, textual passages, and stronger possibilities about the term’s relation to death and Nirvāṇa. Oldenberg had already assembled much of the case for an ethical-residue reading, while nineteenth-century lexical literature preserved competing derivations. Lovejoy did not choose one word-history and let it decide the meaning. In the published article he makes the restriction explicit: *upādi* must be settled “rather by an examination of its use than by etymological arguments” (134). Contrary passages remain. Some evidence is “relevant but not necessarily conclusive.” The matter, he concludes, “cannot be regarded as finally settled” (136). Publication here narrows the claim partly by making uncertainty part of it.[^8]

**中文：** 另一个技术术语则显示，这种尚未完成的整理一旦进入出版会发生什么。在笔记中，洛夫乔伊不断穿梭于 *upādisesa* 的不同词源解释、不同文本段落，以及关于它与死亡和涅槃关系的更强推断之间。Oldenberg 已经汇集了支持“伦理残余”解释的大量材料，而十九世纪的词汇学文献仍保留着相互竞争的派生说。洛夫乔伊没有挑选一个词源史，再让它单独决定意义。到了正式论文中，他明确规定了这一限制：*upādi* 的意义必须“rather by an examination of its use than by etymological arguments”来确定（134）。相反材料仍被保留；有些证据只是“relevant but not necessarily conclusive”；最后，他明确说这一问题“cannot be regarded as finally settled”（136）。这里，出版之所以缩小了主张，部分恰恰是因为它把不确定性本身纳入了论证。[^8]

That combination of close textual discrimination and broader comparison was already present in Lovejoy’s Harvard coursework. His individual graduate record shows that in 1896–97 he took `Sanskrit 5` and `Semitic 13`, receiving an A in both. The contemporaneous announcements identify `Sanskrit 5` as Charles Rockwell Lanman’s advanced Pāli course, “The Sacred Books of Buddhism,” reading the *Dhammapada*, *Sutta Nipāta*, and *Majjhima Nikāya*. `Semitic 13` was Crawford H. Toy’s “History of the Hebrew Religion, with comparison of other Semitic religions.” Charles Carroll Everett’s comparative study of religions, including Buddhism and Hindu philosophies, occupied the same graduate environment.[^3] The point is not that Harvard supplied one unified “method.” It placed close textual study, work across related traditions, philosophy, and comparative religion within the same institutional field.

**中文：** 这种细密的文本辨析与更宽范围的比较，在洛夫乔伊的哈佛课程训练中已经并置出现。他的个人研究生记录显示，1896—97年他同时修读 `Sanskrit 5` 与 `Semitic 13`，两门课都获得 A。当年的课程公告表明，`Sanskrit 5` 是 Charles Rockwell Lanman 的高级巴利文课程“The Sacred Books of Buddhism”，阅读《法句经》、*Sutta Nipāta* 与 *Majjhima Nikāya*；`Semitic 13` 则是 Crawford H. Toy 的“History of the Hebrew Religion, with comparison of other Semitic religions”。Charles Carroll Everett 关于宗教比较的课程，同样把佛教和印度哲学放进这一研究生环境。[^3] 这里的意思并不是哈佛传授给洛夫乔伊一套统一“方法”。更准确地说，细读文本、跨相关传统比较、哲学与比较宗教在同一个制度空间里同时存在。

In Paris the material widened without changing the burden of inference. Notebook 005 has a strong Paris 1898–99 core but was reused later. Léon Marillier’s official EPHE report names Lovejoy among the *auditeurs ayant pris une part active aux travaux* of his conferences on *Religions des peuples non civilisés*.[^6] Here the objects are no longer primarily Pāli terms but death, ritual, afterlife, sacrifice, and social practice. A differentiated fate after death cannot simply be called moral reward or punishment because it looks like one. The notebook tests manner of death, ritual provision, social status, kinship or group relation, taboo, and moral conduct as rival explanations. Much of the explanatory vocabulary came from the sources Lovejoy was reading. His own work on the page is narrower: ranking sources, reclassifying cases, rejecting proposed genealogies when chronology fails, and leaving some mechanisms unresolved. The material changed; the question was still what an observed difference allowed him to infer about its cause.

**中文：** 到了巴黎，材料的范围扩大了，但推断所承担的证明负担并没有改变。005号笔记本有一个很强的1898—99年巴黎核心，不过后来又被继续使用。Léon Marillier 的 EPHE 官方报告把洛夫乔伊列入其“Religions des peuples non civilisés”课程中那些 *auditeurs ayant pris une part active aux travaux*。[^6] 此时对象已不再主要是巴利语术语，而变成死亡、仪式、来世、祭祀与社会实践。一个人在死后遭遇不同命运，并不能仅仅因为看起来像奖惩，就直接被解释成道德报偿。笔记会分别检验死亡方式、仪式供给、社会地位、亲属或群体关系、禁忌以及道德行为等相互竞争的解释。大量解释性词汇来自洛夫乔伊正在阅读的材料；他在页面上真正做的工作要窄得多：排列来源的可信等级，重新分类案例，在年代不成立时拒绝某条谱系解释，并把若干机制保留为未决状态。材料已经改变；问题仍然是，一个可观察到的差异，究竟允许他对其原因推断到什么程度。

A similar restraint reappeared in print at a larger scale. In [“Religion and the Time-Process”](https://www.jstor.org/stable/3153919) (1902), Lovejoy sent readers back to his earlier Buddhist article while discussing wider comparisons. The affinity between Aristotle’s theology and Vedānta, he wrote, was “obvious.” The theory that Aristotle therefore came under eastern influence was nevertheless “entirely gratuitous,” because the internal development of the Platonic dialectic supplied an account of the Greek position (446–47). Comparison could hold while genealogy failed. A resemblance still required a separate argument before it could become a claim of historical transmission.[^9]

**中文：** 类似的克制后来以更大的尺度重新进入发表文本。1902年的“Religion and the Time-Process”在讨论更广泛的比较时，还把读者重新指向洛夫乔伊早先的佛教论文。他说，亚里士多德神学与吠檀多之间的相似性“obvious”；但若因此就主张亚里士多德受到东方影响，这一理论仍然“entirely gratuitous”，因为柏拉图辩证法自身的内部发展已经能够解释希腊一侧的位置（446–47）。比较可以成立，而谱系推断仍然失败。相似性若要变成历史传播的主张，还需要另一套独立论证。[^9]

By 1906, deciding which differences mattered carried explicit political stakes. In “Democracy in the Twentieth Century,” Lovejoy described social-evolutionary stages and racial or national difference as having split Enlightenment “generic Man” “in two directions—longitudinally and cross-wise” (94). Institutions fitted to one stage of culture were, he said, certainly unfitted to an earlier one. Racial or national difference might make institutions inappropriate elsewhere, but he refused to say that they “must,” and he warned that race had become an overworked category (94–95). These lines of division then entered his discussion of colonial policy and political franchise. On p. 102 he regretted the previous generation’s “hasty” enfranchisement of Black Americans and sympathized with attempts to disfranchise illiterate Black voters, while withholding a final judgment on those cases (101–2). Finer classification here became part of an argument about who was fitted for what institutions and under what conditions.[^10]

**中文：** 到1906年，究竟哪些差异值得区分，已经在洛夫乔伊的文字中带有明确的政治后果。在“Democracy in the Twentieth Century”里，他说社会进化阶段与种族或民族差异共同把启蒙时代的“generic Man”沿着“in two directions—longitudinally and cross-wise”切分开来（94）。在他看来，适合某一文化阶段的制度，当然可能不适合更早阶段；种族或民族差异也可能使某些制度在别处不合适，但他明确拒绝说这种不合适“必然”如此，并警告“race”已经成为一个被过度使用的范畴（94–95）。随后，这些划分直接进入他的殖民政策与政治选举权讨论。在第102页，他把上一代对黑人美国人的“仓促”赋权视为遗憾，并对剥夺不识字黑人选民选举权的尝试表示同情，同时又不愿对具体案例作最终判断（101–2）。在这里，更细的分类不再只是描述偏好，而成为判断谁适合什么制度、以及在何种条件下适合这些制度的政治论证的一部分。[^10]

The same year is less tidy in another respect. In [“Kant’s Antithesis of Dogmatism and Criticism”](https://doi.org/10.1093/mind/XV.58.191), Lovejoy tested a broad inherited contrast against local propositions and rejected the classification when it failed. Reviewing Paul Elmer More’s *Shelburne Essays*, he returned to the *Sutta Nipāta* to resist an over-positive account of Nirvāṇa. Yet in [“The Fundamental Concept of the Primitive Philosophy”](https://doi.org/10.5840/monist190616324) he moved in the opposite direction, gathering heterogeneous local reports into a broad comparative claim about “primitive philosophy.” Decomposition and generalization were contemporaneous possibilities, not successive stages.[^9]

**中文：** 但同一个1906年，在另一个层面上却并不整齐。在“Kant’s Antithesis of Dogmatism and Criticism”中，洛夫乔伊用局部命题去检验一个继承而来的宏大对立，一旦对立失效，他就放弃这个分类。评论 Paul Elmer More 的 *Shelburne Essays* 时，他又回到早在1898年佛教论文中就很重要的 *Sutta Nipāta*，反对把涅槃解释得过于正面化。然而在“The Fundamental Concept of the Primitive Philosophy”里，他却朝相反方向移动，把大量异质的地方性报告汇聚成关于“primitive philosophy”的宽泛比较命题。拆解与概括在这里是同时存在的可能性，而不是思想成熟过程中前后相继的两个阶段。[^9]

By the time Lovejoy published [*The Great Chain of Being*](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL7692618M/The_Great_Chain_of_Being), Asian materials remained but had changed historical function. The preface defines the book’s principal object through “Occidental philosophy” and persistent presuppositions of “Western thought.” Buddhism and Vedānta still appear in its account of otherworldliness (30), and “primitive Buddhism” recurs as a comparative form (97). On p. 35 Plato becomes the principal source of an “indigenous” Occidental strain distinguished from “imported Oriental varieties.” In the notebooks of the 1890s, Buddhist and Indian materials had themselves been subjects of close textual and historical inquiry. In *Great Chain*, they more often mark comparisons and boundaries within a predominantly Occidental genealogy. What changed was not simply their presence but the work they were asked to do.[^11]

**中文：** 到洛夫乔伊出版《存在巨链》时，亚洲材料并没有消失，但它们的历史功能已经改变。序言把全书的主要对象明确规定在“Occidental philosophy”以及“Western thought”中持续存在的若干预设上。佛教与吠檀多仍出现在关于出世主义的讨论中（30），“primitive Buddhism”也仍被作为一种比较形式使用（97）。到了第35页，柏拉图被指定为一种“indigenous”的西方出世主义传统的主要来源，并与“imported Oriental varieties”区别开来。十九世纪九十年代的笔记里，佛教与印度材料本身还是需要被细密处理的文本与历史问题；到了《存在巨链》中，它们更多成为一条以西方为主要中心的谱系之中的比较点与边界标记。变化的不只是这些材料是否还在，更是它们被要求承担什么工作。[^11]

Returning from the mature book to the notebooks therefore does not reveal a forgotten Buddhist origin of the history of ideas. Wilson had already preserved the early training, the 1898 article, and Paris. Schaffer later retained the Pāli and Sanskrit fact while organizing Lovejoy around evolution, hierarchy, seriality, and *Great Chain*. The formation was known. The notebooks make it possible to reconstruct what Lovejoy actually did within that biographical episode: where he disagreed, what he left open, and how far his published claims went.

**中文：** 因此，从成熟时期的著作重新返回这些笔记，并不会揭示一个被遗忘的“观念史之佛教起源”。Wilson 早已保存了早期训练、1898年论文与巴黎经历；Schaffer 后来也保留了洛夫乔伊学习巴利文和梵文这一事实，同时却围绕进化、等级、序列性与《存在巨链》重新组织了关于他的叙述。早期形成过程并非无人知晓。笔记真正改变的是我们能够知道什么：它们让我们重新构造这段传记经历内部洛夫乔伊实际做过的学术工作——他在哪里发生分歧，哪些问题被保留为未决，以及最终发表的主张究竟走了多远。

The odd first item in the *Essays* bibliography thus looks different at the end. A scholarly career takes shape more than once. Notes turn materials into working problems. Publication narrows some into defensible claims. Mature books assign earlier materials to new narrative centers. Later bibliographies and disciplines determine which of those forms remain easiest to see. The first bibliographical entry now points back not to a hidden origin but to unfinished disputes: Warren’s account of desire and attachment, Senart’s reconstruction of the formula, and the passages that kept *upādisesa* open. For the young Lovejoy, Buddhism was a field in which historical judgments still had to be worked out.

**中文：** 到这里，《论文集》书目中那个奇怪的第一项已经呈现出不同意义。一个学术生涯会不止一次被重新塑形。笔记把材料转化成工作中的问题；出版把其中一些问题压缩成作者愿意捍卫的主张；成熟著作又把较早材料重新分配到新的叙事中心；后来的书目与学科史则决定，其中哪些形态最容易继续被看见。因此，这个书目第一项最终指向的不是一个隐藏的起源，而是一组尚未解决的具体争论：Warren 对欲望与执取的解释，Senart 对公式构成史的重建，以及那些始终没有让 *upādisesa* 意义彻底封闭的文本段落。对年轻的洛夫乔伊来说，佛教首先是一个必须实际作出这些历史判断的工作场域。
