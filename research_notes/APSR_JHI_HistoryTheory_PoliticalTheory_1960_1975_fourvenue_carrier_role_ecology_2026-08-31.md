# APSR / JHI / *History and Theory* / *Political Theory*, 1960–1975: four-venue carrier-role ecology

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: **QUALITATIVE FOUR-VENUE CONTROL / DIRECT ISSUE-ARCHITECTURE EVIDENCE / NO COMMON EXHAUSTIVE SHARE DENOMINATOR**

## Core result

Adding *History and Theory* changes the cross-venue model materially.

The late-1960s/early-1970s methodological transformation is not well represented as:

`LOVEJOY / JHI`
-> `SKINNER 1969`
-> `POLITICAL THEORY / CONTEXTUALISM`.

A better infrastructure model is a **differentiated carrier ecology** in which distinct journals allocate historical, methodological, theoretical and polemical work to different publication roles while arguments and actors move among them.

- **APSR**: broad disciplinary flagship; political theory remains permeable but unevenly represented in research-article output, with additional formal-theory and review-field channels.
- **JHI**: history-of-ideas/intellectual-history venue; methodology, transmission, historiography and regional intellectual history can appear as ordinary full-length research or review objects without a dedicated method section.
- ***History and Theory***: dedicated metahistorical venue; from the early 1960s explicitly differentiates articles, review essays, communications, symposia and thematic Beihefte, so Skinner 1969 enters a pre-existing multi-form method infrastructure.
- ***Political Theory***: specialist political-philosophy venue; from 1973 publicly designs a school-nonexclusive multi-role architecture and explicitly aggregates controversy via comments, symposia, rejoinders and editorial metatheory classification.

The strongest proposition is:

> **Methodological change was carried by role reallocation, aggregation and migration across an already differentiated publication ecology. A rupture argument can be historically important without creating the carrier form or method jurisdiction in which it appears.**

Core controls:

`ARGUMENTATIVE RUPTURE != CARRIER-FORM RUPTURE`;

`VENUE IDENTITY != ONE METHOD`;

`ARGUMENT CONTINUITY != CARRIER-FORM CONTINUITY`;

`SPECIALIZATION != DISCIPLINARY EXIT`.

## I. APSR — permeable flagship, uneven article capacity

Direct APSR controls already in repo show:

- continued political-theory/method articles in the Ranney/Polsby years;
- a standing review/book-note jurisdiction named `Political Theory, History of Political Thought, and Methodology`;
- the 1972 `Present and Absent` census of 1971: 46 articles, only 3 classed political philosophy, 13 operationally formal theory, 35 containing at least one table/figure;
- editors' own warning that visible publication mix may feed back into future submissions;
- Walter Odajnyk's theory/history-of-thought article accepted by APSR before the launch of *Political Theory*;
- Melvin Richter serving on the Ranney-era 1970 APSR Editorial Board.

Therefore APSR's role is not `theory absent` but:

`BROAD FLAGSHIP`
+
`THEORY PERMEABILITY`
+
`UNEVEN ARTICLE VISIBILITY`
+
`REVIEW / FIELD-SURVEILLANCE CHANNEL`.

## II. JHI — ordinary research form can carry method controversy

JHI does not require a separate methodological carrier for method to become a central research object.

Direct example:

Bhikhu Parekh and R. N. Berki, `The History of Political Ideas: A Critique of Q. Skinner's Methodology`, JHI 34.2 (1973), 163–184, appears as a full-length lead article.

The same field also carries, through ordinary articles/reviews:

- internal conceptual histories;
- transmission/influence problems;
- proof-burden disputes;
- regional intellectual histories;
- reception/transformation;
- explicit historiographical and field-method questions.

Thus JHI's architecture can be described qualitatively as:

`LOW EXPLICIT SECTIONING`
+
`HIGH OBJECT / RELATION HETEROGENEITY INSIDE ORDINARY SCHOLARLY FORMS`.

The present JHI regional/role census is an expanded seed, not an exhaustive denominator; no cross-journal article-share statistic is licensed.

## III. *History and Theory* — dedicated metahistory with differentiated carrier forms before Skinner

Direct official/JSTOR controls establish a method infrastructure already operating before 1969.

### 1960–63

The journal distinguishes:

- ordinary articles;
- substantive review essays;
- communications.

### 1963

Volume 3 no.1 is explicitly organized as:

`Symposium: Uses of Theory in the Study of History`.

It bundles an introduction, methodological argument and historically executed cases, followed by review essays.

### 1964–65

Beiheft 5, `The Historiography of the History of Philosophy`, includes:

- Passmore;
- Mandelbaum on history of ideas / intellectual history / history of philosophy;
- Walsh;
- Kamenka.

This directly shows history-of-ideas field definition and methodological differentiation already carried in a thematic metahistorical supplement.

### 1969

Skinner's `Meaning and Understanding in the History of Ideas` appears as the long lead article in ordinary issue 8.1.

The same annual volume also has Beiheft 9:

`Studies in Quantitative History and the Logic of the Social Sciences`.

Thus:

`SKINNER METHOD CRITIQUE`
+
`QUANTITATIVE / SOCIAL-SCIENCE METHOD WORK`
coexist within one venue/year through different carrier forms.

The correct venue-level interpretation is:

`SKINNER 1969 ENTERS PRE-EXISTING METHOD INFRASTRUCTURE`.

This does not diminish his argument's later canonical importance.

## IV. *Political Theory* — explicit multi-role design and controversy aggregation

The direct-full February 1973 inaugural `Editorial Statement` declares:

- broad/international scope;
- avoidance of exclusive identification with any school/orientation;
- parallel publication of history of political thought, modern theory, conceptual analysis and polemic argumentation;
- additional special-issue, review-discussion and reassessment forms.

Execution rapidly adds:

- Skinner 1973 on empirical democratic theory/value neutrality;
- Pateman 1974 comment on Skinner;
- August 1974 Skinner symposium;
- Haddock 1974 history-of-ideas method aggregation;
- May 1975 editorial census classifying live metatheory and admitting category overlap;
- later communication/rejoinder.

Thus *Political Theory* makes controversy itself a recurrent editorial object.

`DECLARED PLURAL ROLE DESIGN`
-> `VISIBLE CONTROVERSY AGGREGATION`.

But:

`DECLARED PLURALISM != EQUAL ACCEPTANCE OR NO EDITORIAL PREFERENCE`.

## V. Same methodological dispute, changing carrier role

The Parekh/Berki/Skinner route demonstrates cross-venue transformation:

`JHI 1973 FULL-LENGTH CRITIQUE ARTICLE`
->
`POLITICAL THEORY 1974 SYMPOSIUM / EXTENDED RESPONSE`
->
`POLITICAL THEORY 1976 COMMUNICATION / REJOINDER`.

The argument remains historically traceable while its carrier form changes.

Likewise the broader Skinner route includes:

`H&T 1969 ORDINARY LEAD ARTICLE`
-> later
`PT 1974 EXPLICIT METHOD SYMPOSIUM OBJECT`
-> later
`ANTHOLOGY / CANON EXTRACTION`.

Therefore:

`TEXT IDENTITY / ARGUMENT FAMILY`
!= `INSTITUTIONAL ROLE IDENTITY`.

## VI. Reframing the Skinner rupture

The strongest current formulation is two-level rather than celebratory or deflationary.

### Argument level

Skinner 1969 can legitimately be treated as a major polemical and methodological rupture in later intellectual-history canonization.

### Infrastructure level

It did not create metahistorical method jurisdiction, methodological symposia, thematic supplements, review-essay intervention, history-of-ideas self-critique, or cross-disciplinary carrier infrastructure from scratch.

Those were already distributed across H&T, JHI and broader political-science/intellectual-history venues.

Therefore:

> **Skinner's rupture is strongest when treated as an argument inside an existing ecology, not as the origin of the ecology itself.**

This permits influence without origin mythology.

## VII. Reframing Lovejoy / JHI memory

This four-venue model also changes how later histories can make Lovejoy visible.

The issue is not only whether later scholars accept or reject `unit ideas`.

Publication infrastructure repeatedly performs additional operations:

- classifies kinds of method;
- allocates problems to ordinary articles, reviews, symposia or thematic issues;
- bundles critics and respondents;
- creates specialist capacity;
- makes some texts portable as foundational readings;
- leaves other executed practices less portable.

Thus:

`METHOD MEMORY`
is partly produced by
`CARRIER SELECTION + ROLE ALLOCATION + LATER CANONIZATION`.

This matters for the Lovejoy Buddhist notebooks because their historical disappearance from the center of Lovejoy's later image need not be explained as simple forgetting. The materials can remain bibliographically known while occupying a low-portability role in later disciplinary narratives.

That proposition is compatible with the JHI Blog's existing formulation that the `formation was known but differently classified`.

## IX. Implication for the JHI Blog calibration

The four-venue evidence strengthens three existing cautions in the blog:

1. **No origin claim** — the notebooks should not become a Buddhist origin for mature history-of-ideas method.
2. **No linear maturation** — decomposition and generalization coexist; later method change is likewise not one clean replacement sequence.
3. **Known but differently classified** — Lovejoy's early Buddhist training can remain known while carrier/canon structures make other versions of his work analytically central.

It suggests only a narrow stylistic calibration:

- prefer language of `made easier to see`, `classified`, `selected`, or `made portable` over language implying that a discipline consciously `decides` a single canonical arrangement;
- where useful, distinguish `publication` from `later disciplinary memory` as separate sorting operations;
- do not add a Skinner excursus to the blog merely because the larger repo now reconstructs the later method ecology.

---

## Evidence ceiling

Direct carrier architecture is secure for the named issues/forms.

Do not claim:

- a common quantitative article-share comparison across the four journals;
- that H&T caused Skinner's method;
- that JHI corporately opposed Skinner;
- that PT's programme guaranteed equal pluralism;
- that APSR deliberately contained theory in review sections;
- that carrier architecture alone explains Lovejoy canonization.
