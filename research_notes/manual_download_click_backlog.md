## Operating rule

From now on, whenever automated retrieval is slower than a simple human click/download, add a new `MD-###` item here **in the same research turn** with:

1. exact source/title;
2. direct landing URL(s);
3. exact pages/part/file to download;
4. what proposition it resolves;
5. minimum acceptable return (full PDF, selected pages, screenshot, or folder metadata);
6. status and priority.

Do not rely on chat history as the only record of manual retrieval requests.
