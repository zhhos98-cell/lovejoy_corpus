## 2. The Great Chain of Being: page-level findings /《存在巨链》逐页发现

### Printed p. 27 / PDF p. 45

India appears twice in the discussion of otherworldliness. Lovejoy compares medieval Europe and India, including contemporary India, as societies in which professed otherworldliness shapes values and political power. This is civilizational comparison, not a citation of an Indological authority.

第 27 页两次提到印度，用于比较中世纪欧洲与印度社会中出世主义、价值秩序和现实权力的关系。这里是文明类型比较，不是具体印度学家的引文。

### Printed p. 30 / PDF p. 48

The major Buddhist/Indian passage. Lovejoy clusters the Upanishads, Vedānta, the Buddhist and Vedāntist elements in Schopenhauer, and primitive Buddhism. He describes early Buddhism as pragmatic otherworldliness, emphasizes its negativity, and contrasts insistence upon the insubstantiality or worthlessness of this world with the absence of an unequivocal positive affirmation of an alternative reality. A second paragraph criticizes Vedānta's treatment of empirical existence as illusion.

佛教与印度哲学最集中的段落。Lovejoy 将《奥义书》、吠檀多、叔本华的佛教与吠檀多倾向以及早期佛教并列，并把后者描述为实践性的出世主义。他强调佛教判断的否定性，指出其否认经验世界的实体性和价值，却没有同样明确地肯定另一种积极实在。下一段又以吠檀多为例，批评把经验存在理解为幻象的做法。

**Comparison with notebook 004 / 与 004 笔记对读:** PDF p. 56 / manuscript p. 151 calls Nirvāṇa a purely negative conception. PDF p. 62 / manuscript p. 165 states that Buddhism's final goal is the absolute cessation of existence. These pages support continuity of negative soteriological interpretation; they do not establish unchanged argument or direct draft transmission.

004 笔记 PDF 第 56 页、手稿第 151 页，已经将涅槃称为纯粹否定性的概念；PDF 第 62 页、手稿第 165 页，又把佛教最终目标理解为存在的彻底终止。两者支持解释倾向的延续，但不能证明论证完全不变或存在逐字底稿传递。

### Printed p. 35 / PDF p. 53

Plato is described as the source of an indigenous Occidental otherworldliness, distinguished from imported Oriental varieties. This is an explicit boundary-making sentence. It shows that Indian/Asian alternatives are present as comparanda while the central historical narrative is organized as the genealogy of a specifically Western tradition.

Lovejoy 将柏拉图描述为西方内部出世主义传统的历史来源，并与输入的东方类型区分开来。这里直接呈现边界划定：印度或亚洲传统作为比较对象出现，而主体叙述仍围绕西方思想谱系展开。

### Printed p. 42 / PDF p. 60

An Upanishadic sage exemplifies negative predication: true reality is described by denying that it resembles particular features of ordinary experience. The Indian source functions as a comparative philosophical analogue for Platonic and later theological accounts of ultimate reality.

Lovejoy 借《奥义书》中贤者的否定式表述，说明最高实在如何通过否认日常经验中的各种属性来被描述。印度材料在此充当柏拉图主义及后期神学的哲学比较项。

### Printed p. 93 / PDF p. 111

Lovejoy states that Neoplatonic and Catholic otherworldliness need not be less thorough than Vedānta or other Indian systems. Plotinus, Augustine, the Pseudo-Areopagite, John the Scot and Aquinas are compared with the mystical Upanishads and Shankara. The distinction he draws is structural: Occidental doctrine is dual, affirming both radical otherness and the reality or value of the created world.

Lovejoy 认为，新柏拉图主义和天主教哲学的出世倾向并不必然弱于吠檀多或其他印度体系。他将普罗提诺、奥古斯丁、伪狄奥尼修斯、爱留根纳和阿奎那，与《奥义书》及商羯罗比较。真正差别在于西方教义的双重结构：它同时肯定最高实在的绝对他异性，以及被创造世界的真实存在或价值。

### Printed p. 97 / PDF p. 115; note 43, printed p. 344 / PDF p. 362

Lovejoy reduces primitive Buddhism to a practical refusal to discuss the world's origin, metaphysical status, or the positive character of the desired end, while stressing escape from temporal and sentient existence. Endnote 43 cites the Buddhist formula of the ten points not discussed.

Lovejoy 将早期佛教概括为对世界起源、形而上地位及终极目标积极性质等问题的实践性拒答，同时强调摆脱时间性和感性存在。第 43 条尾注明确提及佛教的「十个不予讨论的问题」。

**Crucial notebook comparison / 最关键的手稿对应:** Notebook 004 PDF p. 18 / manuscript p. 65 initially says that the material appears to represent the agnosticism of a purely practical teacher, but then questions that interpretation. Lovejoy argues that refusal may respond to the *form of the questions*, not merely their speculative or impractical character. The mature book therefore preserves the topic and some vocabulary while simplifying an interpretive distinction visible in the early notebook.

004 笔记 PDF 第 18 页、手稿第 65 页，先承认材料乍看上去像纯粹实践性教师的不可知论，却立即质疑这种解释，认为拒绝回答可能针对问题提出的方式，而非只是因为这些问题过于思辨或无关实践。成熟著作保留了论题及部分判断，却压缩了早期手稿中更细密的解释区分。

### Printed p. 142 / PDF p. 160

Indian religious philosophy is linked to a racialized generalization about the supposed numerical or temporal imagination of an entire race. This is evidence against idealizing Lovejoy as a proto-decolonial or uncomplicated global historian. Geographical breadth and Orientalist hierarchy coexist.

Lovejoy 将印度宗教哲学的出世主义与关于整个族群数字或时间想象力的种族化判断联系起来。此处说明，恢复其印度材料并不意味着可以将其塑造成没有等级偏见的全球思想史先驱；知识地理的广度与东方主义式判断可以同时存在。

### Printed p. 218 / PDF p. 236

Lovejoy observes that an eighteenth-century argument for optimism closely resembles formulas through which primitive Buddhism expressed pessimism. The comparison turns upon similar argumentative structures yielding opposed evaluative outcomes. This is an analytically suggestive parallel to the early distinction between received elements and their arrangement/application, but not proof of causal methodological continuity.

Lovejoy 指出，一套为十八世纪乐观主义辩护的论证，在结构上与早期佛教表达悲观主义的某些公式相似。相近的成分或关系结构可以导向相反的价值结论。这与早期对于既有材料和「排列与应用」之间差别的处理形成可供比较的呼应，但仍不能直接证明方法论上的因果延续。

### Printed p. 305 / PDF p. 323

A quoted European writer asks why an Indian should be condemned for speaking his own language. This is a European argument about historical and cultural judgment, not evidence of a specific Buddhist authority.

这里引述欧洲作者关于不能因为印度人使用自己的语言就加以谴责的论点，属于文化判断和历史相对性的例证，并非佛教学术引文。

## 3. Essays in the History of Ideas: paratext and disciplinary boundaries /《观念史论文集》的副文本与学科边界

### Foreword, printed p. vii / PDF p. 11

The History of Ideas Club's purpose is defined through the development of philosophical, ethical, and aesthetic ideas **in occidental literature**, together with their relation to philosophy, science, and political/social movements. This is direct institutional evidence for a Western delimitation of the field, despite the collection's later inclusion of a chapter on Chinese sources of European Romanticism.

前言把观念史俱乐部的任务界定为追踪「西方文学」中的哲学观念、伦理思想与审美风尚，并考察其与哲学、科学及政治社会变动的关系。这是学科制度自我界定的直接证据：即使论文集包含中国材料，讨论重点仍被组织为西方思想与文化的发展。

### Author's preface, printed p. xi / PDF p. 15

Lovejoy states that his general methodological conceptions grew out of investigations of particular topics rather than preceding them. This is an actor-level warrant for reconstructing historical practice before a mature methodological programme. It does **not** identify the Buddhist notebooks specifically as the origin of the method.

作者自序说明，他的一般方法论观念生长于具体专题研究，而不是先有确定的方法再强行套入材料。这为「先考察研究实践，再讨论成熟方法宣言」提供了 Lovejoy 本人的表述依据，但仍不足以证明佛教笔记就是该方法的单一起源。

### Author's preface, printed p. xv / PDF p. 19

Lovejoy says that the volume excludes some historical studies dealing with technical philosophical issues. This helps explain why the bibliography preserves the early Buddhist/Indian work while the reprinted essays largely do not. It is evidence of selective construction of a collected disciplinary persona, not evidence that the earlier work disappeared.

作者说明，论文集排除了部分处理技术性哲学问题的历史研究。这解释了为什么早期佛教和印度哲学工作留在书目中，却没有成为正文重印的中心。此处反映的是经过选择的学科形象建构，而非那些早期研究完全消失。

### Printed pp. 99–135 / PDF pp. 121–157: “The Chinese Origin of a Romanticism”

A complete chapter argues that European admiration for Chinese gardening and irregular beauty contributed to the emergence of an anti-neoclassical aesthetic. Lovejoy traces missionary writing, travel accounts, Chinese political reputation, William Temple, William Chambers, garden design, sharawadgi, and the movement of aesthetic principles across genres. China is not merely a decorative background: the chapter makes a historical-transmission argument about the formation of European taste.

整篇文章追踪欧洲对于中国园林、不规则之美与中国文明的理解，论证这些材料如何参与反新古典主义审美观念的形成。其证据包括传教士文本、旅行记、中国政治制度的欧洲声誉、William Temple、William Chambers、园林设计及 sharawadgi 一词。中国在这里不是简单背景，而是进入关于欧洲审美形成与观念传递的历史论证。

**Important qualification / 重要限定:** The framework still asks how a non-European object contributed to a European intellectual transformation; it is not an independent account of Chinese aesthetics on its own terms. / 论证仍以非欧洲材料如何参与欧洲思想转变为中心，并非按中国自身问题独立重建其美学史。

### Printed p. 102 / PDF p. 124: updated China bibliography

The chapter's first substantive footnote cites Reichwein, G. Atkinson, V. Pinot, A. H. Rowbotham, and Lewis S. Maverick. Lovejoy expressly notes scholarship published after the essay was first written. These references document later revision and an active scholarly network around Jesuit transmission and Europe–China relations.

该章的重要脚注列出 Reichwein、G. Atkinson、V. Pinot、A. H. Rowbotham 和 Lewis S. Maverick，并明确说明部分研究发表于原文写成之后。这说明文章在收入论文集时经过更新，同时显示 Lovejoy 接触了围绕耶稣会传递与中欧关系展开的现代研究。

### Printed p. 111 / PDF p. 133: Y. Z. Chang

The footnote records that **Mr. Y. Z. Chang considered the sharawadgi problem at Lovejoy's request**, proposes a possible Chinese reconstruction, and refers to Chang's 1930 *Modern Language Notes* article, pp. 221–224. This is direct evidence of named linguistic consultation; it establishes a request and response, not their full institutional relationship or the correctness of the proposed etymology.

脚注明确说明 **Y. Z. Chang 应 Lovejoy 的请求研究了 sharawadgi 一词的问题**，提出可能的汉语语音重建，并指向 Chang 发表于 1930 年《Modern Language Notes》第 221—224 页的文章。这是可以具体核实的语言咨询关系，但目前不足以证明双方完整的制度联系，也不能据此判定词源解释必然正确。

### Printed p. 340 / PDF p. 362: bibliography

The first two listed publications are the 1898 Buddhist technical-terms article and the 1901 four-lecture syllabus on Buddhist philosophy. These are visible in the disciplinary collection's bibliography even though Buddhist philology is not reproduced as an essay.

论文集末尾书目的前两项依次为 1898 年佛教技术术语论文与 1901 年四讲佛教哲学教学提纲。佛教语文学没有进入重印正文，却仍以作者学术生涯起点的形式保留在书目的显眼位置。

### Printed p. 341 / PDF p. 363: 1906 and 1907

The bibliography also includes the 1906 “The Fundamental Concept of the Primitive Philosophy” and the 1907 review of P. Deussen's *Outline of the Vēdanta System of Philosophy According to Shankara*. This confirms continuing comparative-religion and Indian-philosophy publication without establishing an uninterrupted Buddhist programme.

书目同时列出 1906 年《原始哲学的基本概念》及 1907 年对 P. Deussen 关于商羯罗吠檀多体系著作的书评。这证明比较宗教学与印度哲学持续出现在其发表目录中，但不能由此推出从 1898 年开始从未中断的单一佛教研究计划。

## 5. Strongest interpretive result / 最有力的解释结论

The mature books retain Indian and Buddhist materials but relocate them. The technical reading community of Lanman, Warren, Senart, Oldenberg, and others becomes largely invisible in print; Buddhism and Vedānta survive mainly as comparative philosophical types. *Essays* institutionally defines the field through occidental literature while retaining Indian/Buddhist publications in its bibliography and publishing a substantial argument about Chinese contributions to European aesthetics. Thus the relevant transformation is not simple absence but a change in evidential visibility, citation ecology, and disciplinary framing.

成熟著作保留印度与佛教材料，却改变了它们出现的方式。Lanman、Warren、Senart、Oldenberg 等人组成的技术性阅读共同体在这些出版物中基本不可见；佛教与吠檀多主要作为哲学比较类型存续。《论文集》以「西方文学」界定观念史俱乐部的制度范围，同时在书目中保留印度与佛教学研究，并收录讨论中国因素进入欧洲美学的长篇论文。因此，真正发生的不是材料单纯消失，而是证据可见度、引文生态与学科框架的变化。

A second key result is **continuity plus compression**: the notebook's negative account of Nirvāṇa survives in the mature book, while its qualification that Buddhist non-answering may concern the form of the questions is compressed into a broad account of pragmatic refusal of speculation.

第二个关键结果是「延续与压缩并存」：早期笔记对于涅槃否定性的判断在成熟著作中延续，但关于佛教拒答可能源于问题形式本身的细致限定，被压缩为更宽泛的实践性拒绝思辨。

A third result concerns James: Lovejoy's own preface directly documents hearing William James early in his philosophical training. This materially strengthens the James connection while leaving the narrower James→Buddhism or James→Marillier transmission claim unproved.

第三个结果涉及 James：Lovejoy 在自序中直接回忆早年听 William James 授课，这显著加强了师承关系的证据基础，但仍不能据此证明 James 直接传递佛教知识或介绍 Marillier。
