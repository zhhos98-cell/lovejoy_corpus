# APSR 1971–1972: comment/rejoinder architecture and the Miller historicism controversy

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: **DIRECT ISSUE-ARCHITECTURE CONTROL / DIRECT MILLER ABSTRACT + RESPONSE RECORDS / PRINTED METHOD-CONTROVERSY CLUSTER LOCKED / EDITORIAL COMMISSIONING CAUSE HOLD**

## Core result

The Shklar–Polsby December 1972 reply-invitation edge occurred inside an APSR that already possessed a visible **comment/rejoinder controversy carrier**, including for methodological and epistemological disputes.

The strongest direct case is *American Political Science Review* 66.3 (September 1972).

Its printed sequence is:

1. Eugene F. Miller, `Positivism, Historicism, and Political Inquiry`, 796–817;
2. David Braybrooke and Alexander Rosenberg, `Comment: Getting the War News Straight: The Actual Situation in the Philosophy of Science`, 818–826;
3. Richard S. Rudner, `Comment: On Evolving Standard Views in Philosophy of Science`, 827–845;
4. Martin Landau, `Comment: On Objectivity`, 846–856;
5. Eugene F. Miller, `Rejoinder to “Comments” ...`, 857–873.

JSTOR and Cambridge both directly preserve this issue architecture.

Thus APSR could make a methodological argument into a **multi-text controversy object** inside an ordinary issue.

Core relation:

`METHOD ARTICLE`
-> `MULTIPLE NAMED COMMENTS`
-> `AUTHOR REJOINDER`.

This predates the 1974 *Political Theory* Skinner symposium and is contemporaneous with PT's 1971–72 formation/recruitment phase.

It does not prove causal influence on PT's later controversy architecture.

`PRIOR CARRIER FORM != SOURCE OF LATER CARRIER FORM`.

## I. Miller's article explicitly makes epistemology a political-science problem

Cambridge's direct abstract states that Miller interprets the contemporary behavioral/postbehavioral dispute as part of a deeper conflict between theories of knowledge.

His article relates:

- behavioral political inquiry to positivism / British empiricism;
- anti-behavioral protest to historicism;
- philosophy-of-science developments to political inquiry;
- historicist premises to the problem of relativism.

This makes the article a direct methodological/metatheoretical intervention rather than an empirical political-science article with incidental philosophical references.

Role:

`APSR ORDINARY RESEARCH ARTICLE`
carrying
`PHILOSOPHY OF SCIENCE / HISTORICISM / POLITICAL-INQUIRY METHOD DISPUTE`.

Therefore:

`APSR BROAD FLAGSHIP`
!= `METHOD QUESTIONS CONFINED TO REVIEW SECTION`.

---

## II. The three comments are substantively differentiated

The response architecture is not one generic letter-to-editor response.

### Braybrooke / Rosenberg

Title directly positions their intervention as a correction to Miller's account of the contemporary philosophy of science:

`Getting the War News Straight: The Actual Situation in the Philosophy of Science`.

### Rudner

Direct Cambridge text explicitly reads Miller as constructing a relation between developments in philosophy of science and `postbehavioralist historicist` movements in political science, then challenges Miller's account of those developments.

Rudner notes that Miller ultimately rejects both positivism and historicism as adequate foundations for political inquiry and reads Miller as moving toward traditional political philosophy.

Thus the comment itself differentiates:

- positivism;
- historicism;
- postbehavioralism;
- traditional political philosophy;
- philosophy-of-science developments.

### Landau

`Comment: On Objectivity` explicitly enters the cluster through the objectivity problem; Cambridge's direct reference record also identifies Landau's 1972 book *Political Theory and Political Science: Studies in the Methodology of Political Inquiry*.

Do not collapse the three responses into one anti-Miller position without full-text comparison.

`SAME RESPONSE CLUSTER != SAME CRITIQUE`.

## III. Miller's rejoinder makes the controversy a printed unit

APSR prints a dedicated 17-page rejoinder immediately after the three comments.

This gives a bounded carrier object:

`MILLER ARTICLE`
+
`3 COMMENTS`
+
`MILLER REJOINDER`.

This is stronger evidence for controversy capacity than merely finding later citations or independent critiques elsewhere.

The journal itself places the dispute serially in one issue.

However:

`PRINTED SERIAL ARRANGEMENT`
!= `DIRECT EVIDENCE OF WHO SOLICITED EACH COMMENT`.

Without editorial correspondence, hold:

- whether comments were commissioned;
- whether Miller saw/revised against comments prepublication;
- whether particular respondents were selected by Polsby personally;
- causal editorial reasons for assembling the cluster.

## IV. Comment/rejoinder form already recurs before September 1972

The same carrier form is visible in APSR before the Miller cluster.

### December 1971, vol.65 no.4

Direct JSTOR/Cambridge contents include:

- Wolfinger article / Frey `Comment` / Wolfinger `Rejoinder`;
- VanDeVeer on Oppenheim's noncognitivism / Oppenheim `Comment` / VanDeVeer `Rejoinder`;
- additional comment/rejoinder material in the issue.

### June 1972, vol.66 no.2

Direct contents include:

- Gerald Pomper article;
- two comments by Brody/Page and Kessel;
- rejoinders by Pomper and Boyd.

### September 1972, vol.66 no.3

Two controversy sequences occur in the same issue:

- George `Multiple Advocacy` -> Destler comment -> George rejoinder;
- Miller method article -> three comments -> Miller rejoinder.

Therefore by late 1972 the comment/rejoinder format is not a one-off anomaly.

Safe claim:

> **APSR had a recurrent printed capacity to turn selected articles into bounded comment/rejoinder exchanges.**

Hold stronger claim:

`RECURRENT PRINT FORM != FORMAL STANDING EDITORIAL POLICY`
unless the managing-editor reports or editorial comments explicitly state such policy.

## VI. Relation to PT / Skinner chronology

This finding weakens a possible carrier-modernization chronology:

`APSR = NONDIALOGIC EMPIRICAL FLAGSHIP`
-> `PT = NEW CONTROVERSY CARRIER`.

That is not sustainable.

Before PT's February 1973 launch, APSR already printed:

- comments;
- multiple comments on one article;
- rejoinders;
- a long, explicit philosophy-of-science / historicism / political-inquiry controversy.

PT's later innovation should therefore be located more carefully in:

- specialist political-theory jurisdiction;
- declared school-nonexclusive role design;
- repeated aggregation of political-theory controversies;
- dedicated symposium and communication ecology;

not in inventing comment/rejoinder form itself.

Control:

`SPECIALIST CONTROVERSY AGGREGATION != INVENTION OF RESPONSE FORM`.

This parallels the H&T finding:

`ARGUMENTATIVE RUPTURE != CARRIER-FORM RUPTURE`.

`FLAGSHIP OUTPUT SKEW`
