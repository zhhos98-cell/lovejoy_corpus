## Core correction

The review is **printed pp.113–115**, not pp.113–118.

The complete 1896 RHR 34 contents sequence is:

- `R. Steinmetz, Endokannibalismus — M. L. Marillier` — begins **p.113**;
- `Une fausse lettre de saint Basile contre Eunomius — Diekamp` — begins **p.116**;
- `J. B. Bérenger-Féraud, Superstitions et survivances — M. L. Marillier` — begins **p.119**.

Therefore the intervening Diekamp item fixes the Steinmetz review boundary at the end of p.115.

This is the same correction already recorded in Batch 38 and must control all future retrieval instructions. Later notes that inferred pp.113–118 solely because the next *Marillier* review begins p.119 were too loose: they missed the independent p.116 item.
