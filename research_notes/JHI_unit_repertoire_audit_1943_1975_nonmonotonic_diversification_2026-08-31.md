# JHI historical-unit repertoire audit, 1943–1975

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: **40-CASE SEEDED UNIT TYPING COMPLETE / NON-MONOTONIC DIVERSIFICATION RESULT / NOT AN EXHAUSTIVE FREQUENCY CENSUS**

## Core correction

The first recoded UNIT × RELATION seed suggested a tempting historical hypothesis:

`EARLY ANALYTIC TYPES / PRACTICES`
-> `LATER AUTHORS / CHAINS / TRADITIONS / FIELDS / CORPORA`.

The independent 40-case JHI regional execution seed does **not** support a simple monotonic expansion model.

After assigning object-level unit types to all 40 cases, the safer result is:

> **JHI's midcentury internationalization involved diversification and coexistence of admissible historical units, not a linear replacement of author-centered intellectual history by larger or more distributed units.**

The same structural correction already found for region-role history now applies to unit history:

`UNIT DIVERSIFICATION != UNIT REPLACEMENT`.

This is analytically stronger than a narrative in which the field simply learns to move from `great thinkers` to `traditions`, `networks`, or `contexts`.

## III. The important historical fact is not first appearance but durable coexistence

### Author/agent never disappears

Examples:

- 1945 Leibniz;
- 1951 Fu I / Ch'en Tu-Hsiu / Galileo–Avempace;
- 1956 J. H. G. Justi;
- 1959 Aristotle/Confucius;
- 1963/65 al-Fārābī;
- 1967 Emerson;
- 1968 Paul Carus.

Thus:

`LARGER HISTORICAL UNITS BECOME AVAILABLE`

while

`AUTHORIAL UNIT REMAINS OPERATIVE`.

### Tradition is early, not a late global turn

The 1949 Mexican revolt, 1951 China/West comparison, 1951 Confucian syncretism, 1952 Indian thought, 1954 Spanish America and 1955 Hindu philosophy already make `U6 tradition/formation` a normal unit before the International Society's 1960 Cambridge meeting.

Therefore do not equate `tradition-level unit` with later globalization.

### Networks/chains become explicit but remain problem-specific

- Rowbotham 1956: Jesuit Figurist mediation chain;
- Gates 1967: Ibn Khaldun spread/influence route;
- Ching 1974: internal Confucian succession as secondary `U8`.

The chain becomes the unit where transmission is itself the explanatory object.

It does not replace authors or traditions elsewhere.

### Field/institution becomes an object when scholarship itself is historicized

- Wright 1960: study of Chinese civilization / Sinology;
- Schirokauer 1965: China scholarship as review field;
- Klaiber 1971: collective regional Left;
- Gracia 1975: Latin-American history of ideas as historiographical field.

This looks less like a universal methodological shift than a **new reflexive capacity**: intellectual history can now take its own regional scholarly infrastructures and collective formations as objects.

## IV. Unit diversification and role diversification are homologous

The earlier regional-role audit established:

`ROLE DIVERSIFICATION != LINEAR DECENTERING`.

The unit audit now establishes:

`UNIT DIVERSIFICATION != UNIT REPLACEMENT`.

These are related but not identical.

A region can change role while its unit stays the same.

Example:

`U3 named thinker`
can appear in:

- West-facing source genealogy;
- internally addressed Islamic philosophy;
- active reception/rejection history;
- East-West mediation.

Conversely, the same role can be carried by different units.

`TRANSMISSION`
can be reconstructed through:

- `U3` author/source pair;
- `U8` mediated chain;
- `U6` tradition/internal succession;
- potentially `U9` diffuse climate/field.

Therefore a full JHI morphology needs both axes:

`UNIT × RELATION/ROLE`.

## VI. What the seed does and does not show

### Supported as a hypothesis

By the late 1950s and 1960s, JHI visibly accommodates a wider repertoire of historical units than a narrow great-thinker/unit-idea caricature would predict.

### Not supported

`JHI moved linearly from authors to traditions/networks/fields`.

The evidence directly contradicts this.

### Not yet supported quantitatively

`unit diversity increased statistically over time in the whole journal`.

The current 40 cases were selected because they are non-Western/cross-regional high-signal cases, not drawn as a complete or random sample.

### Better current model

`MULTIPLE UNIT TYPES ALREADY PRESENT`
-> `MIDCENTURY REPERTOIRE BROADENING`
-> `DURABLE COEXISTENCE / PROBLEM-SPECIFIC SELECTION`.

This should be tested against the complete JHI issue run before any prevalence or trend claim.

## VII. Revised article-level proposition

A strong formulation is now:

> **JHI's postwar internationalization altered not only the geography of its objects but the repertoire of units through which intellectual history could be written. Yet this was an additive rather than substitutive transformation. Named thinkers continued to organize histories of influence even as traditions, transmission chains, intercultural encounters, source infrastructures, scholarly fields and collective movements became legitimate intellectual-historical objects in their own right.**

This connects the global-history branch directly to the UNIT × RELATION framework without requiring a stage theory.
