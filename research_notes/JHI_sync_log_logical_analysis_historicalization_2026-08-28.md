## 1. Why the freeze was reopened

The 2026-08-27 repository freeze stated that no generic Lovejoy-method sweep should be reopened. The present reopening satisfies the exception because a live proposition changed: `logical` and `analytical` had been used throughout the repository partly as our own descriptive vocabulary, while direct Lovejoy evidence shows that logical analysis was itself an actor-level philosophical practice requiring historical reconstruction.

The reopening is therefore narrow:

> **historical genealogy of Lovejoy's logical analysis, not a general Lovejoy-method sweep.**

## 3. Direct actor-level bridge now especially important

Lovejoy's 1909 `Pragmatism and Realism` is currently the clearest explicit bridge.

He insists that a movement-name retain historical reference to a real stream of tendency, but that the tendency then be subjected to **logical analysis and historical comparison**. He simultaneously warns against arbitrary essentialization and against excessive atomism: compatible elements should be combined as logic permits, allowing conjunction to modify their significance; novelty may belong to a `new compound in intellectual chemistry`.

This produces an actor-authored sequence:

`historical complex`
→ `logical analysis`
→ `historical comparison`
→ `compatibility / incompatibility test`
→ `warranted recombination`
→ `historical distinctiveness at element or configuration level`.

This is substantially closer to later history-of-ideas procedure than a generic resemblance between the Buddhist notebooks and *Great Chain*.

## 4. Revised unit-idea problem

Do not replace the old caricature with a rehabilitation myth.

The revised question is not:

`unit-ideas are actually contextual, therefore Skinner was wrong`.

It is:

> **To what extent was a `unit-idea` first an analytically isolated component of a historical complex, and what warrant then allowed Lovejoy to treat that component as identifiable across different contexts?**

This preserves the genuine later dispute over trans-contextual identity while historicalizing the production of the unit itself.

Current working distinction:

`identity sufficient for analytical/historical tracing`
≠ automatically
`identity independent of history`.

The exact warrant for trans-contextual identity remains open.

## 7. Evidence ceilings retained

### Secure

- Lovejoy directly published on formal-logical problems in 1907, 1910 and 1911;
- 1908 `Thirteen Pragmatisms` makes logical independence an anti-transfer rule;
- 1909 explicitly joins logical analysis to historical comparison;
- analysis was also a field-level methodological currency among Lovejoy's opponents/interlocutors;
- Berkeley's philosophical curriculum immediately before Lovejoy's entry explicitly included formal logic, deductive and inductive;
- Howison had professional logic credentials;
- Harvard/Royce had an active logic environment while Lovejoy was present.

### Open

- exact Berkeley logic-course enrollment by Lovejoy;
- exact Harvard logic-course/seminar enrollment;
- attendance at Peirce's 1898 Cambridge lectures;
- direct source genealogy for Lovejoy's 1910 Venn-diagram treatment;
- exact path from early logical practice to the mature `unit-idea` terminology;
- relative contribution of philology, comparative religion and philosophy to notebook relation-partition.

### Do not claim

- Lovejoy was straightforwardly an `analytic philosopher` in the later disciplinary sense;
- Frege/Russell/Moore supplied his method absent evidence;
- Royce, Peirce or Howison caused the history-of-ideas method;
- formal logic caused notebook 004/005;
- analysis was uniquely Lovejoyian;
- unit-ideas were simply formal-logical atoms.
