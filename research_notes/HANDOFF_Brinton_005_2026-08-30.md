## Strongest current content-level identifications

### Sacrifice
Daniel G. Brinton, *Religions of Primitive Peoples* (1897), Lecture V is presently the strongest match for the unresolved p.125 item at the level of content and case architecture, not yet at paleographic/title level.

Its sequence overlaps strikingly with 005:
`first-fruits -> ancestor offerings -> piacular/propitiatory sacrifice -> self-mutilation/human sacrifice -> magical efficacy -> blood + grain/agricultural rites -> corn-spirit/harvest victim -> sacramental communion`.

Lovejoy then decomposes that cluster into distinct mechanisms and eventually treats sacramental union as comparatively exceptional. This is best described as **shared problem-field + different aggregation rule**, not straightforward doctrinal inheritance.

1876 *The Religious Sentiment* remains a weaker comparator: it explains some sacrifice/self-denial material but does not reproduce the full 1897 agricultural/communion cluster.
