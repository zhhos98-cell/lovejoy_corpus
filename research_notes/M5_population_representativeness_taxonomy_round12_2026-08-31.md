## Why M5 needed recoding

Earlier project shorthand treated `M5` too narrowly as one desired negative case:

> Lovejoy promotes a population-level / representative / fundamental type, encounters counter-distribution, and explicitly demotes his own previously promoted type.

Call that strict form **M5-SELF-REP-DEMOTE**.

A bounded search has not recovered a clean direct example satisfying all of those clauses.

The wider corpus, especially notebook 005, shows that Lovejoy does perform several nearby population/representativeness operations. They should be separated rather than forcing all of them into the strict gate.

Governing distinction:

`I = identity / type membership`

`P = presence / recurrence`

`V = validity or domain of a relation`

`R = representativeness / promotion from cases to a population, stage, fundamental concept or common type`.

M5 belongs primarily to **R**, but R interacts with I/P/V in several distinct ways.

# M5-0 — POSITIVE REPRESENTATIVE-TYPE PROMOTION

Question:

> When does Lovejoy move from recurring local cases to a claim about a population, stage, worldview, or `fundamental` comparative type?

### Strongest case: 1906 `Primitive Energetics`

Lovejoy's title itself promotes a type to `The Fundamental Concept of the Primitive Philosophy`.

The article argues against an overwhelmingly anthropomorphic account and promotes a recurrent impersonal-power relation across American Indian, Melanesian/Polynesian, Bantu and other materials into a broad description of `primitive philosophy` / `primitive energetics`.

This is a clear positive M5 operation:

`local lexical / ethnographic cases`
-> `cross-case identity predicate`
-> `recurrence`
-> **population / stage-level promotion**.

Important Round-12 control:

The 1914 Leuba review shows that the identity core must be thinner than the rich 1906 formulation. Lovejoy still calls Leuba's `dynamism` essentially/precisely the same conception despite Leuba rejecting strict `regular/mechanical` characterization.

Therefore:

`THIN I CORE`
= impersonal power/potency

can coexist with

`THICK R CLAIM`
= fundamental concept of primitive philosophy.

The thinner the I criterion, the easier recurrence becomes and the more independent proof is needed for R.

Code this explicitly:

`EASY TYPE RECURRENCE != POPULATION FUNDAMENTALITY`.

Status: **DIRECT POSITIVE M5 SECURE**.

# M5-1 — STRICT SELF-REPRESENTATIVENESS DEMOTION

Definition:

1. Lovejoy himself first states a population-level / representative / fundamental generalization;
2. contrary distribution or counterexamples are then introduced;
3. Lovejoy explicitly retracts, demotes, or narrows the population-level status because the distribution no longer supports it.

This remains the original `REP-DEMOTE` target.

Current state: **OPEN / NO CLEAN DIRECT CASE**.

Do not close this gate with:

- a critic's theory Lovejoy demotes;
- a causal predicate Lovejoy reassigns;
- a `may`/`must` scope restriction without prior self-promotion;
- a chronological claim separated from a type claim;
- a case where a universal sequence is merely stress-tested without an explicit prior Lovejoy commitment.

The absence of a clean case may itself be historiographically meaningful: Lovejoy often appears to hedge or locally qualify a generalization before the corpus supplies a dramatic self-retraction.

# What M5 now means

Do not use `M5` as a synonym for one dramatic retraction.

The broader family concerns **the jurisprudence of moving from tokens to populations/stages/fundamental types**:

1. what makes a token eligible;
2. how thin the identity predicate is;
3. how frequent the token is;
4. whether the distribution is representative;
5. whether the relation holds across the claimed population;
6. whether the population relation licenses chronology, causation, or institutional/political inference;
7. what happens when counter-distribution appears.

Compact formula:

`TOKEN ELIGIBILITY`
-> `TYPE IDENTITY`
-> `RECURRENCE / FREQUENCY`
-> `REPRESENTATIVENESS`
-> `POPULATION / STAGE PROMOTION`
-> `RELATION-SPECIFIC CONSEQUENCES`.

At every arrow, a new warrant is required.

This is the correct Round-12 expansion of M5.
