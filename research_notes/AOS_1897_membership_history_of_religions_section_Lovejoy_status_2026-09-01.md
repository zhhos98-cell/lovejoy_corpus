# AOS 1897 membership and History of Religions section: Lovejoy status

Date: 2026-09-01

## New evidence target

The American Oriental Society material now separates three questions that must not be collapsed:

1. Lovejoy's announced Milinda-pañha communication in 1897.
2. Lovejoy's election as an AOS member in 1897.
3. Lovejoy's relationship to the newly organized Historical Study of Religions section.

## Current state

The 1898 JAOS membership list records:

`ARTHUR ONCKEN LOVEJOY (Harvard University) ... 1897`

The list explains that the year following a member's address indicates year of election.

Therefore:

- Lovejoy's AOS membership election year: DIRECT PRIMARY CONFIRMED = 1897.
- Lovejoy's membership before publication of the 1898 Buddhist technical-terms article: CONFIRMED.

## Important negative evidence

The 1898 AOS volume separately lists members of the Section for the Historical Study of Religions.

Lovejoy appears in the general membership list but not in the separately enumerated Religion Section membership list.

Therefore:

- AOS membership: CLOSED.
- Religion Section membership: NOT CONFIRMED / current evidence indicates absent from the 1898 listed section members.

Do not write:

`Lovejoy was a member of the History of Religions section.`

unless nomination, minutes, or later section records prove it.

## Institutional proximity

The section's organization is closely related to figures already present in Lovejoy's Harvard training environment:

- Crawford H. Toy.
- Charles Rockwell Lanman.
- Morris Jastrow.

This is institutional proximity, not yet a personal transmission chain.
