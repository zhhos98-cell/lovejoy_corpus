## Core result

The strongest cross-object sequence is a change in **what a discriminating relation is used to identify**, followed by a change in scale.

> **004: discriminate a philosophical system/witness by a system-defining relation**
> → **005: discriminate a ritual case by its operative causal bearer/mechanism**
> → **1906: abstract one recurrent mechanism-family into a broad comparative category**.

The sequence is not evidence that Lovejoy invented the underlying diagnostic or mechanism-sensitive rules. Direct Oldenberg/Jacobi and Marillier controls show those proof disciplines were already available in his contemporary research environments. The documentary continuity lies in **selection, local application, cross-domain transport, redivision, and later reaggregation**.

The 1906 move adds an important tension. The notebook is pluralist about local mechanisms and historical origins, while the published article promotes impersonal, diffusive, transferable, quasi-mechanical efficacy as a very broad category described as more fundamental and pervasive than personality. The best formulation is:

> **historical pluralism + mechanism universalism + explicit evidentiary self-qualification**.

A recurring mechanism can be compared across historically unrelated practices without implying one common institutional origin. But once Lovejoy moves from recurrence to claims such as `most nearly ubiquitous`, `more fundamental`, or `dominant preoccupation`, the politics and burden of scale become a separate historical problem.

Project terms such as `diagnosticity`, `classification target`, `causal bearer`, `mechanism universalism`, `genealogical monism`, and `reaggregation` are analytical vocabulary unless explicitly anchored in Lovejoy's own wording.

## I. 004: diagnostic relation used for philosophical-system identification

The clearest manuscript hinge follows Rhys Davids:

`To R.D.'s remark it should be added...`

The candidate historical problem is whether a Buddhist passage can count as evidence for early Sāṁkhya.

Lovejoy's local move is to change the discriminating variable.

Low-discrimination feature:

- eternity of Matter and Self.

Higher-discrimination relation:

- separateness of Self and World;
- `thorough-going dualism` tied to the Sāṁkhya logic of release.

Target:

- identify a genuinely Sāṁkhyan doctrine or witness.

The operation is:

`shared proposition`
→ `ask whether it actually distinguishes the target system`
→ `prefer a more system-defining relation`
→ `seek a better textual witness`.

This manuscript intervention is real. Its underlying operator is not a Lovejoy priority claim: Oldenberg and Jacobi independently supply pre-existing field-level diagnostic controls.

## II. 005: outward ritual morphology repeatedly loses classificatory force

The 005 notebook moves the diagnostic problem from systems to practices.

Visible forms repeatedly fail to settle explanation:

- blood;
- killing;
- eating;
- ceremonial repetition;
- shared food;
- human versus animal victim;
- sacred/magical object;
- foundation deposit;
- body part;
- agricultural/fertility rite.

The notebook asks instead what relation or bearer makes the observed effect intelligible.

Candidate mechanisms include:

- personal divine will;
- dangerous or ancestral spirit;
- provision or communication for the dead;
- propitiation / transaction;
- incorporation or social status;
- indwelling spirit;
- intrinsic/direct/transferable efficacy;
- sympathetic relation;
- sacramental union.

Thus 005 repeatedly changes the variable on which classification is based:

> **visible morphology → causal architecture**.

Form remains evidence, but it no longer has the right to define the genus by itself.

## III. Key local 005 controls

### Sacrifice / syncretism

Several motives can coexist within one ritual complex; historically distinct sacrifices can later be combined. A single visible ceremony can therefore contain multiple causal/historical components.

### Human sacrifice

Killing can serve funerary provision, message-sending, protection/magic, propitiation or later sacramental interpretation. Victim type does not itself determine the mechanism.

### Foundation blood and bodily material

Blood/flesh/bones in a foundation do not prove `union through blood`; efficacy can be protective, direct, spirit-mediated, magical, propitiatory or otherwise relationally distinct.

### Fetish versus charm/amulet

This is the cleanest local causal pair:

- fetish: efficacy depends on an inhabiting/present spirit;
- charm/amulet: efficacy may be intrinsic to the object without a spiritualistic hypothesis.

The visible class `magical object` therefore contains different causal bearers.

### Agrarian rite

A plant-soul sacrifice can be `quasi-mechanical and magical rather than sacramental` when the act is not addressed to a personal plant deity.

### Cannibalism

Ceremoniality, kin relation and ingestion do not by themselves prove communion. Selected body parts can work through transfer of qualities rather than sacramental union.

Across these blocks, the recurring question is:

> **what relation has enough causal specificity to distinguish this case from outwardly similar cases?**

## IV. Mechanism is inserted between form and genealogy

A typical 005 proof sequence is:

`reported / outward practice`
→ `reject morphology as sufficient classifier`
→ `identify operative mechanism / causal bearer`
→ `only then ask origin, survival, borrowing, substitution, syncretism or later reinterpretation`.

This creates two separations:

1. **same historical derivation does not guarantee the same current function**;
2. **same causal mechanism does not guarantee the same historical origin**.

The second point is crucial for the later 1906 abstraction. A mechanism can recur across independently derived practices and therefore become a comparative variable without becoming a common-origin claim.

---

## V. Direct Marillier control: mechanism-sensitive decomposition is upstream

The Paris environment already contained a strong anti-morphological proof rule.

Marillier's 1896 review of Steinmetz states directly that practices grouped by external resemblance can arise from disparate reasons and are not susceptible to one interpretation. His later blood/foundation work distinguishes direct magical efficacy, guardian-spirit mechanisms, propitiation and other relations. His `Survivance` work likewise separates visible differentiated outcomes from the selectors producing them.

Lovejoy is independently documented as taking an active part in Marillier's 1898–99 EPHE work.

Therefore the historical formulation must be:

> **Paris did not introduce diagnostic comparison ex nihilo; it placed Lovejoy inside a comparative-religion environment where causal discrimination among outwardly similar rites was already an explicit research practice.**

The 004 control blocks an equally simple Marillier-origin story: Lovejoy already used discriminating relations in pre-Paris textual/doctrinal work.

The strongest history is **cross-disciplinary convergence and redeployment**, not one-directional invention.

## VI. 1906: a local causal bearer becomes a fundamental comparative category

`The Fundamental Concept of the Primitive Philosophy` attacks theories that make personality, anthropomorphism or spirits the master explanatory scheme of the whole field.

Lovejoy does not deny personal agency. He ranks another causal conception above it.

His own actor-near language includes:

- a category `more fundamental than personality`;
- causation `distinctly impersonal and quasi-mechanical`;
- diffused / communicable energy or power;
- unequal localization in persons and things;
- transfer through contact;
- direct efficacy without volition;
- practical regulation, control and insulation of power.

The publication therefore performs:

`many local mechanism distinctions`
→ `recognize a recurrent causal family`
→ `abstract that family into a comparative category`.

The fetish/charm distinction in 005 is a miniature of the later move:

`spirit-mediated efficacy`
versus
`intrinsic/object efficacy`

becomes, at broader scale,

`personal agency`
versus
`impersonal quasi-mechanical power`.

## VII. Historical pluralism is compatible with mechanism recurrence

005 is pluralist both about mechanisms and about origins.

The same visible form can hide different mechanisms; the same mechanism can recur across differently derived forms.

Examples of recurrent direct/intrinsic/transferable efficacy include:

- blood cures and rejuvenation;
- foundation protection;
- selected body-part transfer;
- charms/amulets;
- fecundative/agricultural rites;
- some anthropophagy;
- sympathetic relations among persons, objects and images.

The logical structure is:

`A and B instantiate mechanism M`

**does not entail**

`A and B descend from one ancestor H`.

This permits a non-genealogical comparative output:

`independent cases instantiate M`
→ `M may define a significant comparative causal type`.

This is **mechanism universalism without genealogical monism**.

---

## VIII. 1906 nevertheless increases theoretical scope sharply

The published article does much more than neutrally abstract a repeated local mechanism.

Lovejoy calls the impersonal conception:

- `more fundamental` than personality;
- `most nearly ubiquitous`;
- `most all-pervasive`;
- among the most influential features of the target cultural field;
- probably the `dominant preoccupation` of most savages.

This is a real scalar jump.

The transformation should therefore be described as:

> **abstraction + ranking + scope expansion**.

That matters politically and epistemically. A mechanism distilled from heterogeneous local evidence is reattributed upward to `primitive philosophy / savage thought` at a much larger scale.

Project formulation:

> **decomposition can generate a new totalization.**

---

## IX. The article records its own evidentiary brakes

The strong scope claims are primary-secure, but so are Lovejoy's qualifications.

He says he will state the thesis `somewhat dogmatically, and somewhat too categorically` before adding evidence and qualifications.

He calls the linguistic comparison only the `first stage` of the inquiry.

He explicitly says the testimonies brought together are not complete and partly invites further evidence from anthropologists, philologists and field workers.

Thus the published architecture is:

`broad category announced`
→ `partial lexical/ethnographic test`
→ `future extension / qualification expected`.

The historically precise statement is:

> **1906 makes a strong universalizing extrapolation from convergent but explicitly incomplete evidence while marking the extrapolation as provisional and programmatic.**

## X. p.376 and the non-genealogical comparative inference

A crucial 1906 synthesis says that near-verbal identity in descriptions supplied by independent observers of three distinct racial/cultural fields is itself significant.

Lovejoy extracts a common profile across North American, Oceanic and African materials:

- religio-magical vocabulary denotes something more impersonal than personal;
- power can be a communicable property, mobile substance or transferable energy;
- it is widely diffused among natural objects and persons;
- unusual efficacy is explained through exceptional possession or concentration;
- practical rites seek to manipulate or regulate it.

The independence of the fields does not defeat the comparison. It blocks a simple historical-transmission inference while helping support a typological/causal abstraction.

Thus:

`historically independent fields`
→ `convergent lexical + causal profile`
→ `general comparative category`.

---

## XI. The real tension is proposition type and evidentiary scope

The archive and 1906 article show different evidentiary burdens for different claims.

### Historical-origin claim

Lovejoy often demands local source/history control and permits plurality.

### Local mechanism claim

He permits classification by recurrent causal relation even across morphologically different cases.

### Comparative-category claim

He allows independent recurrence to support a cross-cutting causal type.

### Prevalence / priority claim

Once the category is said to be `more fundamental`, `perhaps earlier`, `most nearly ubiquitous`, or dominant among `most savages`, the burden rises sharply again.

This is why 1906 is simultaneously a developmental continuation of the notebook and a stress test of its proof discipline.

---

## XII. Final developmental formulation

The strongest bounded sequence is:

> **004: identify system-defining relations rather than generic similarities**
> → **005: identify mechanism-defining causal relations rather than visible ritual forms**
> → **1906: abstract one recurrent causal relation into a cross-cutting comparative category**
> → **then expand that category upward into a broad claim about primitive philosophy, with explicit but incomplete evidentiary qualification**.

This is not a story of the same algorithm moving unchanged across fields. The **classification target changes**, the **ontological level of the discriminant changes**, and finally the **scale of attribution changes**.

That combination is more historically precise than `philology became anthropology` or `Lovejoy invented mechanism analysis`.

---

## XIII. Attribution firewall

Do not claim Lovejoy-specific invention of:

- diagnostic specificity;
- function-sensitive comparison;
- anti-morphological ritual classification;
- recognition of multiple mechanisms/origins;
- distinction between direct magical efficacy and spirit-mediated action.

Field-level controls exist in Oldenberg/Jacobi and Marillier.

The stronger Lovejoy documentary delta is:

- use of these proof disciplines in one working archive across domains;
- dense reclassification of cases;
- separation of mechanism from genealogy;
- cross-domain portability;
- later reaggregation of one recurrent causal family at publication scale.

The last move is where the politics of scale become especially important.
