## Supersession statement

Batch 206 does **not** invalidate Batch 19’s source concordances or the 005 paper architecture. It supersedes only the stronger attribution that Lovejoy’s distinctive contribution consisted in inventing the determinant taxonomy between Marillier’s continuation/retribution poles.

Current publication rule:

> **Do not assign originality to Lovejoy for the individual determinant classes or for the basic demand that moral retribution be tied to genuine deservingness. Attribute his manuscript delta more narrowly to redivision, local ordering, lexical compression where demonstrable, repeated case application, and cross-domain operational portability.**

## Chronological cautions

- Söderblom 1901 is a same-workshop sibling control, not proof that Lovejoy read the printed book before writing early 005 leaves.
- Marillier 1901 is a later primary control within the user’s 1910 boundary; it cannot automatically be treated as an upstream source for undated 005 material.
- Marillier 1893/94 and Mauss 1896 are the stronger chronological antecedents for the early survivance block.
- Later-than-1910 material is excluded from ordinary forward genealogy under the current scope rule.
