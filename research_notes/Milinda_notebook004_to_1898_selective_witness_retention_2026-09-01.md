### F. 1898 print — Milinda is reattached to the stabilized causal partition

The 1898 article states the corresponding distinction:

- karma = cause of the **particular condition** in which one is reborn;
- upādāna = cause of the **fact of rebirth itself**.

At this exact published locus, its footnotes cite:

- Samyutta/Warren + **Milinda Pañha p.65** for karmic differentiation;
- earlier passages + **MP p.32** for upādāna / renewed existence.

The correct printed reference is p.32, not p.33.

Direct collation identifies both with Vilhelm Trenckner's 1880 Roman-script Pāli edition. Trenckner p.32 contains the `saupādāno ... paṭisandahissāmi / anupādāno ... na paṭisandahissāmi` condition; p.65 treats differences produced by karma.

Therefore the relation is:

`MS97 causal partition + Hardy`

`-> publication stabilization + Milinda primary-text warrants attached proposition by proposition`.

This is **witness reassignment / evidentiary reattachment**, not simple unchanged witness carry-over.
