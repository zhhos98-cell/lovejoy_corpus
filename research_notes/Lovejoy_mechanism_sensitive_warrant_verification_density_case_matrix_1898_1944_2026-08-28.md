# Lovejoy — mechanism-sensitive warrant: verification-density case matrix, 1898–1944

Date: 2026-08-28  
Status: **CANONICAL CROSS-CASE MATRIX / GOVERNING HYPOTHESIS TEST**

## Purpose

Test the current hypothesis that Lovejoy's strongest recurring evidentiary discipline is **mechanism-sensitive rather than ontology-sensitive**.

The hypothesis predicts that Lovejoy should be relatively willing to assert historical relations of very different kinds—semantic, intellectual, affective, practical, political—when he can:

1. individuate the target;
2. specify a mediation path;
3. connect that path to relation-appropriate evidence;
4. control obvious alternatives / scale upgrades;
5. calibrate causal or epistemic weight.

It also predicts agnosticism or weaker language where the candidate relation rests mainly on resemblance, coexistence, or an under-specified mediation.

This matrix tests that pattern across the best-controlled cases currently in the repository.

# II. Preliminary pattern

The cases support mechanism-sensitive warrant better than any ontology hierarchy.

Lovejoy positively uses or admits:

- semantic mechanisms;
- formal/logical relations;
- textual transmission;
- ambient tradition;
- psychological association;
- affective predisposition;
- practical/design embodiment;
- ideological rationalization;
- political mobilization;
- social/institutional causation in programme.

What separates stronger and weaker case-level conclusions is often not the causal category but the mediation/evidence architecture.

---

# III. Strong positive cases usually contain at least one intermediate operator

Recurring intermediate operators include:

### `USAGE`

term → observed textual use → semantic role.

### `FUNCTION`

component → differentiated causal/systemic job.

### `CONTACT`

source → documented reading/access → recipient.

### `UPTAKE`

carrier → quotation/reuse/acknowledgment → downstream change.

### `JUSTIFICATION`

prior disposition → new theory supplies rational/conceptual legitimation.

### `ASSOCIATION`

ambiguous label / psychological affinity → historical bundling without logical necessity.

### `APPLICATION`

concept/norm → practical/design instantiation → cross-domain migration.

### `RATIONALIZATION`

ambition/passions/social condition → doctrine/ideology that articulates or legitimates them.

### `MOBILIZATION`

ideology → converts latent motive into coordinated action.

These are not one technical method. They are **typed mediation mechanisms**.

---

# IV. Weak or rejected upgrades often lack an intermediate mechanism

### Generic resemblance

`A resembles B`

is repeatedly insufficient for:

`A caused B`.

### Structural coexistence

`social hierarchy exists`
+
`philosophical hierarchy exists`

is insufficient without a mediation model.

### Shared term

`same word`

is insufficient for:

`same idea`.

### Shared historical name

`same -ism label`

is insufficient for:

`logical unity / same argumentative fate`.

### Direct contact

Even:

`B read A`

is insufficient for:

`B depends causally on A to degree D`

without recipient-state and alternative-route controls.

Thus Lovejoy's method repeatedly distinguishes **edge evidence** from **effect evidence**.

---

# V. Verification density is multidimensional

A simple count of sources will not capture Lovejoy's evidentiary practice.

Verification density should be coded along at least five dimensions:

1. **target density** — how well is the phenomenon itself established?;
2. **mediation density** — how many intermediate steps are evidenced?;
3. **alternative control** — are plausible rival routes/mechanisms addressed?;
4. **counterfactual calibration** — does Lovejoy distinguish cause from necessary/sufficient cause or degree of dependence?;
5. **scale calibration** — is local evidence prevented from automatically becoming corpus/system/general history claim?

A relation can have many citations and still be weak on mechanism; another can have fewer sources but a diagnostically strong mediation trace.

# VI. Case-level score heuristic for future audits

Do **not** turn this into pseudo-quantification for publication. As an internal research aid only, future cases can be marked:

- `T0–T2` target-state clarity;
- `M0–M3` mediation specificity;
- `A0–A2` alternative-route control;
- `W0–W2` causal-weight / necessity-sufficiency calibration;
- `S0–S2` scale-control;
- `E` evidence substrate type(s).

Example:

### Trinkhaus 1937

- T: 2 (Plato hierarchy clear)
- M: 0/1 (social-to-philosophical mechanism under-specified)
- A: 1
- W: 2 (agnostic restraint)
- S: 1
- result: no causal closure.

### 1941 Shakespeare

- T: 2
- M: 2/3 (prior taste → receptivity → justificatory philosophy)
- A: 1
- W: 2 (`one factor`, direction question left open generally)
- S: 2
- result: bounded positive attribution.

The score is a research prompt, not a historical claim.

The current corpus has:

### Programmatic admissibility

1938 explicitly allows:

`institutions / laws / mores / social conditions`
→ `ideas / climates of opinion / rationalizations`.

### Actual positive non-intellectual causation

1941 supplies affective and political mechanisms.

### Non-textual evidentiary carriers

1924/1932 landscape practice.

### Still under-represented in controlled Lovejoy case law

`specific institution / professional structure / economic condition`
→ through an identified mediation mechanism
→ `specific intellectual proposition/configuration`,

with evidence and confidence calibration comparable to the best textual/psychological cases.

This is now a **defined research gap**, not a conclusion that Lovejoy lacks such cases.

---

# VIII. Consequence for the `logicalian` question

The original correction distinguished:

- doing logic;
- using explicit logical analysis;
- using logic-shaped relation-sensitive practice.

The present matrix suggests the cross-context stable feature, if any, is even more abstract than `typed relations`:

> **relation-specific proof requires a relation-specific mediation path.**

Formal logic is one case where the mediation is inferential/formal.

Historical genealogy requires contact/uptake/alternatives.

Psychological history requires disposition/association and evidence of operation.

Political history requires rationalization/mobilization/conditioning.

Semantic history requires usage/disambiguation.

Thus a `logician-like` style may be visible at the meta-level—explicitly specifying what connects A to B—without implying that all these mechanisms are logical in the actor's sense.

---

# IX. Strongest current formulation

> **Lovejoy's recurring epistemic discipline is not one logical method transported across genres. It is a demand that historical relations be typed and mediated: the historian must identify what A and B are, specify the relation connecting them, show the relevant mechanism at work, and control the promotion from local evidence to stronger historical claims. Different contexts supply different mechanisms and evidence.**

This remains a historical reconstruction, not a demonstrated single self-conscious invariant principle from 1898 onward.
