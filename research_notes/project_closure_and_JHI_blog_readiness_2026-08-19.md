## 2. Fresh full-repository JHI Blog audit

### Decision

**GO. The repository comfortably supports the proposed JHI Blog essay.**

The evidentiary problem is no longer sufficiency. It is selection and compression.

The strongest first-form essay remains a **005-centered documentary microhistory**, not a compressed survey of the entire Harvard → Paris → 1906 trajectory.

### Recommended center

Working title:

**A Notebook in Paris: Arthur Lovejoy Before the History of Ideas**

Core claim:

> A surviving Paris notebook and the records of Léon Marillier's EPHE seminar show the young Arthur Lovejoy actively reclassifying comparative-religion evidence rather than simply receiving finished theories. Exact citations to Mary Kingsley make that classificatory labor visible at page level, while Lovejoy's 1906 “Primitive Energetics” paper shows how the working distinctions were later abstracted into a more general causal category.

This claim should remain about **documentary and inferential practice**, not about discovering a hidden origin of the later `unit-idea` method.

### Why 005 is the best blog object

The narrative has a compact archival spine:

1. physical Paris notebook;
2. Lovejoy officially named as an active participant in Marillier's EPHE conferences;
3. direct labels `Marillier — Survivance — 12 June` and `Marillier — Hum. Sac. — June 20`;
4. notebook's two dominant blocks correspond to the two Marillier course sequences;
5. exact source transformation can be demonstrated rather than merely inferred;
6. the 1906 publication provides a controlled endpoint without requiring the notebook to be called a draft.

The new `Hum. Sac. — June 20` reading strengthens the opening substantially because there are now direct manuscript labels for **both** principal course blocks.

### Best evidentiary microhistory: Mary Kingsley

The Kingsley sequence remains the strongest 2,000-word centerpiece:

- notebook cites *Travels in West Africa* p.511 exactly;
- Kingsley describes a `suhman` whose efficacy depends on an indwelling spirit;
- Lovejoy immediately turns that case into a boundary condition and distinguishes an amulet in which there need be `no spiritualistic idea`;
- the next manuscript page cites Kingsley p.525 and reopens the causal status of blood;
- Hubert–Mauss use the same Kingsley book at a different page and for a different causal architecture.

Compact formulation:

> **same book → exact pages → different causal bearer.**

Or, more specifically:

> **citation becomes contrast.**

This is the most legible demonstration that a notebook does not merely preserve what Lovejoy read; it preserves the act of **reclassification**.

### 004 as chronological control, not co-equal narrative

Notebook 004 should appear only briefly in the blog. Its purpose is to block the overclaim that Paris or Marillier created Lovejoy's analytical method from scratch.

004 already shows a layered proof architecture:

`textual witness → semantic relation → doctrinal/causal function → historical genealogy`.

005 shifts the object:

`ethnographic witness → formal comparative relation → mechanism/function → historical genealogy`.

The safe cross-notebook claim is therefore:

> **004 and 005 change evidence objects, not inference syntax.**

Or, in less technical prose for the blog: Lovejoy arrived in Paris with an existing habit of separating what a source proves from what a stronger historical claim would require; Paris supplied a new documentary field in which that habit was applied to rites, reports and comparative religion.

Do **not** turn this into `Buddhism caused the history of ideas` or `Marillier taught Lovejoy decomposition`.

### 1906 as coda

The 1906 *Monist* article should be used as the endpoint, not the organizing text.

The repository-level concordance supports a strong but qualified relation:

> **1906 article = theoretical/linguistic prolegomenon; notebook 005 = substantial evidentiary and analytical apparatus for the projected comparative-customary continuation.**

The article explicitly describes itself as a first stage/chapter and announces later analysis of customs, sacrifice, agriculture, initiation, taboo and primitive eschatology — exactly the domains extensively worked through in 005.

This is stronger than thematic continuity but still different from 004 → 1898, where notebook argument can be followed more directly into a narrowly delimited article.

## 3. Historiographical limit / novelty statement

The repository now contains enough secondary control to define novelty narrowly.

Do not claim:

- that historians failed to notice Lovejoy's early religious work;
- that Buddhism or comparative religion directly generated the mature `unit-idea` method;
- that Lovejoy invented decomposition, source criticism or mechanism-sensitive comparison;
- that 005 is a transcript of Marillier's lectures;
- that Mauss directly influenced Lovejoy;
- that `Primitive Energetics` is an unknown text.

The publishable novelty is more specific:

> **the archive makes it possible to reconstruct the documentary and inferential labor by which Lovejoy transformed named sources before later categories stabilized in print.**

The strongest historiographical payoff is therefore not a teleological prehistory of *The Great Chain of Being*. It is an archival history of **how distinctions were made before they became arguments**.

A concise closing formulation for the blog remains:

> **the history of an idea can sometimes be recovered as the history of what a notebook allowed a reader to separate.**
