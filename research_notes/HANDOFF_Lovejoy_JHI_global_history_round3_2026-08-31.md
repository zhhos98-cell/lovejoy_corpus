# I. Core round-3 conceptual upgrade: UNIT × RELATION

## Governing question

The project previously focused on relation-specific proof burdens:

`IDENTITY`
!= `CLASSIFICATION`
!= `CHRONOLOGY`
!= `CAUSATION`
!= `TRANSMISSION`
!= `REPRESENTATIVENESS`.

Round 3 added a logically prior question:

> **What is the historical unit being related?**

Same relation-word can require different evidence when the unit changes:

- author/source pair;
- proposition/term;
- ritual/practice;
- source/witness;
- tradition/canon;
- institution/debate arena;
- traceable network/chain;
- diffuse field/corpus;
- composite archival object;
- analyst-created type/schema.

Compact formula:

`WHAT IS RELATED?`
-> `WHAT RELATION HOLDS?`
-> `WHAT EVIDENCE LICENSES IT?`.

## Codebook correction

Initial seed matrix used `U7` too broadly for institution, debate, analytic type, selector schema and comparative generalization.

That was recognized as precisely the aggregation error the project studies.

New v2 ontology splits:

- `U6 TRADITION / CANON`;
- `U7 INSTITUTIONAL / COLLECTIVE ARENA`;
- `U8 TRACEABLE NETWORK / CHAIN`;
- `U9 DISTRIBUTED FIELD / LARGE CORPUS`;
- `U11 ANALYTIC CONSTRUCT / CLASSIFICATORY APPARATUS`.

Important rule:

> **Unit type belongs to the proposition/claim, not once-for-all to the physical named object.**

Example: the same Lovejoy notebook page can be:

- `U2` when treated as a dated/material textual segment;
- `U11` when treated as a classificatory schema.

`UNIT DIVERSIFICATION != UNIT REPLACEMENT`.

- intercultural event/contact;

- scholarly field/institution;

## Core distinction

Not all later critiques `expand context` in the same way.

### Same or similar unit, expanded context/relation

Computational/sociolinguistic work can retain utterance/author while enlarging evidence and contextual scale.

### Unit supplementation/replacement

- A. P. Martinich: shifts from what one philosopher is doing toward multiple conversations and networks;
- Humeira Iqtidar: long learned interpretive chains + institutions of Islamic learning;
- Rochana Bajpai: explicitly asks what happens when constitutional/parliamentary **debates are treated as entities**, instead of writings of individual thinkers;
- Sor-hoon Tan (2023 independent route): Chinese classics/traditions as meanings co-produced through production, reproduction, consumption and practice, making identifiable author-intention an insufficient primary unit in some cases.

New three-way control:

`CANONICAL PRIORITY`
!= `HISTORICAL PRIORITY`
!= `DOMAIN ADEQUACY`.

Iqtidar can show historically prior analogous context/intention-sensitive interpretive practice without proving a genealogy into Skinner.

Tan can show a domain-limit on author-centered contextualism without implying a universal Chinese alternative method.

# VI. Pre-canonical Cambridge ecology: Dunn ↔ Skinner, not one-way diffusion

John Dunn's `The Identity of the History of Ideas` appeared April 1968.

Dunn says the essay arose from several years of discussion with Peter Laslett and especially Quentin Skinner.

Dunn already uses Austin's *How to Do Things with Words*; later Skinner credited Dunn with recognizing Austinian speech-act theory's relevance to historical interpretation.

Dunn's written footnote control points to Skinner 1966 `The Limits of Historical Explanations`, so **do not use Dunn 1968 to prove circulation of the unpublished PSA conference version**.

Safer network:

`DUNN ↔ SKINNER MULTI-YEAR DISCUSSION`
+
`SKINNER 1966 PUBLISHED PRIOR METHOD`
-> `DUNN 1968`
-> Skinner later acknowledges a particular debt to Dunn in 1969.

Margaret Leslie 1970 says her `In Defence of Anachronism` arose from reflections on Skinner's 1968 PSA paper and thanks Skinner for comments on her draft.

Thus critique/reply ecology begins before the 1969 article has stabilized canonically.
