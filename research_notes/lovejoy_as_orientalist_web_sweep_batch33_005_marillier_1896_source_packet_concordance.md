## Research move

Batches 31–32 established a close conceptual and controversy-level relation among Marillier’s work on taboo, Mauss’s 1896 Steinmetz review, Marillier’s 1898–99 EPHE conference, and Lovejoy 005 pp.16–19. This batch tests the relation at the level of actual books and reference packets.

The result now has two layers:

1. **shared source ecology:** Brough Smyth and R. H. Codrington are securely present both in the Marillier/Mauss taboo environment and in Lovejoy 005;
2. **one exact-title bridge:** a direct visual recheck of 005 PDF p.62 / notebook p.125 resolves Lovejoy’s handwritten Steinmetz item as **`Endokannibalismus — Steinmetz.`** The same 1896 work was reviewed by **Léon Marillier** in *Revue de l’histoire des religions* 34, p.113 and is repeatedly used by **Marcel Mauss** in his 1896 Steinmetz critique.

This materially strengthens the reconstruction. It still does **not** prove that Marillier personally handed this work to Lovejoy or assigned it in the seminar.

## 1. Marillier 1896 `Tabou mélanésien`

Léon Marillier, **“Sur le caractère religieux du tabou mélanésien”**, appeared in *Études de critique et d’histoire*, 2e série, Bibliothèque de l’École des hautes études, Sciences religieuses, 1896, 7,2, pp.35–74.

A complete direct facsimile collation remains desirable. For the internal source packet, the most useful contemporary witness is Mauss’s 1896 article.

## 2. Mauss identifies Marillier pp.53–56 as an assembled source cluster

In Marcel Mauss, **“La religion et les origines du droit pénal d’après un livre récent”**, *Revue de l’histoire des religions* 34 (1896), 269–295; 35 (1896), 31–60, the taboo-sanction discussion cites:

> `Voir L. Marillier, « Tabou mélanésien », pp. 53-56 ...`

and states that in those pages **most of the texts relating to Melanesia proper were gathered**.

The surrounding source cluster includes:

- H. Hastings Romilly, *The Western Pacific*, pp.17, 61;
- R. H. Codrington, *The Melanesians*, p.69;
- A. C. Haddon, Torres Straits material, JAI XIX;
- Brough Smyth, *The Aborigines of Victoria*, I, p.62;
- Curr, I, p.569;
- Taplin, p.13;
- Ridley, p.186.

Elsewhere in the same discussion Mauss uses Mariner, Penny, Gaussin and Ellis. For Gaussin and Ellis he explicitly says that he owed the references to Marillier and **borrowed them from Marillier’s course on flood myths**. This is direct contemporary evidence that Marillier’s teaching circulated bibliographic references as research material.

## 6. The exact same 1896 work was being handled by Marillier and Mauss

The work is Rudolf S. Steinmetz, **`Endokannibalismus`**, Vienna, 1896, issued from the *Mittheilungen der Anthropologischen Gesellschaft in Wien* (vol. XXVI; contemporary reviews describe a 60-page offprint).

Two independent contemporary bibliographic notices identify the work as a Vienna 1896 offprint.

More importantly, the table of contents of **RHR volume 34 (1896)** gives:

> `R Steinmetz Endokannibalismus M L Marillier — 113`

Thus Marillier himself reviewed Steinmetz’s *Endokannibalismus* at p.113 of the same journal volume in which Mauss’s first Steinmetz article appeared at p.269.

Mauss’s 1896 essay repeatedly cites the work, calling it Steinmetz’s recent opuscule, and uses it to discuss method, revenge, mortuary cult, ritual cannibalism, solidarity of the clan, and the need to distinguish broad classificatory rubrics from precise mechanisms.

This creates a much stronger documentary chain than we had in batch 32:

> **Steinmetz 1896, `Endokannibalismus`**
> → **Marillier reviews the exact work in RHR 34 (1896), p.113**
> → **Mauss uses the exact work throughout his 1896 Steinmetz critique**
> → **Lovejoy 005 p.62 lists the exact title in his own `Sacrifice / Bibliography`**
> → **005 then decomposes cannibalism, human sacrifice, funerary killing, communion, magical transfer and propitiation into distinct mechanisms.**

The route by which the physical title reached Lovejoy remains open. The exact bibliographic identity, however, is no longer conjectural.

## 7. Why the exact-title match matters more than Brough Smyth / Codrington

Brough Smyth and Codrington establish a common ethnographic library. `Endokannibalismus` does more.

It is:

- a recent 1896 specialist intervention;
- explicitly part of the Steinmetz controversy that Marillier’s later seminar resumed;
- reviewed by Marillier himself;
- used by Mauss in the same controversy;
- copied by exact title into Lovejoy’s sacrifice bibliography.

Therefore it provides a concrete **bibliographic object** connecting the Paris research environment to 005. It does not by itself prove a teacher-to-student handoff, but it substantially narrows the historical distance between “background literature” and Lovejoy’s actual working materials.

## 12. Revised evidentiary ladder after the p.62 correction

### Level A — institutional fact
Lovejoy actively attended Marillier’s 1898–99 EPHE conferences.

### Level B — controversy identity
The EPHE report says the survivance conference resumed/refuted Steinmetz; 005 pp.16–19 works through the same moral / ritual / social / source-critical problem.

### Level C — material transmission mechanism
Mauss 1896 says he borrowed references from Marillier’s course; Söderblom 1901 says Marillier lent him personal notes/dossiers.

### Level D1 — shared source ecology
Brough Smyth and Codrington are demonstrably shared between Marillier/Mauss’s source environment and 005.

### Level D2 — exact bibliographic-object identity
**Steinmetz, `Endokannibalismus` (1896)** is reviewed by Marillier, repeatedly used by Mauss, and entered by exact title in Lovejoy 005 p.62.

### Level E — conceptual transformation
005 repeatedly decomposes outwardly similar practices by mechanism: funerary provision, vengeance, clan solidarity, taboo, magical transfer, direct natural efficacy, propitiation, expiation, communion, fecundation, social incorporation. Lovejoy 1906 later isolates impersonal/transferable causal efficacy as a comparative abstraction.

### Still not established

- identical Marillier dossier sheet in Lovejoy’s hands;
- direct copying from Mauss 1896;
- exact page identity between Lovejoy’s Brough Smyth/Codrington notes and Marillier pp.53–56;
- primary collation of Marillier’s `Tabou` encyclopedia entry and 1901 `Notes...`.

## 13. Paper-level payoff

The strongest defensible formulation is now more concrete:

> **Lovejoy’s 005 notebook emerged within a Paris research environment in which comparative-religion problems were worked through circulating references, extracted cases, reviews and thematic dossiers. At least one recent specialist work at the center of the Steinmetz controversy — `Endokannibalismus` (1896), reviewed by Marillier and used by Mauss — appears by exact title in Lovejoy’s own sacrifice bibliography. Lovejoy’s distinctive operation was to reorganize such materials by causal and normative mechanism rather than preserve them as a single evolutionary type.**

This gives the paper a recoverable archival object and a concrete scholarly operation without projecting Lovejoy’s later `unit-ideas` vocabulary backward.
