# H&T / JHI / CSPT / *Political Theory* actor traffic, 1963–1973: Pocock, Shklar, Mandelbaum by role rather than generic network membership

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: **DIRECT VENUE/ROLE CONTROLS + ARCHIVAL/PUBLISHED NETWORK EDGES / THREE-ACTOR HIGH-CONFIDENCE TRAFFIC MODEL / NO GENERIC NETWORK FLATTENING**

## Core result

The emerging method ecology should not be represented merely as a network graph of people who `knew each other` or appeared in adjacent institutions.

Three actors already supply enough evidence for a stronger role-specific model:

- J. G. A. Pocock;
- Judith Shklar;
- Maurice Mandelbaum.

Across 1963–1973, each occupies several **different institutional/publication roles** across H&T, JHI/CSPT, private circulation and *Political Theory*.

The historical point is:

> **Method formation and field differentiation were carried by actors changing roles across carriers: reviewer, author, founder, correspondent, draft reader, programme interlocutor, recruit, and methodological source.**

Thus:

`SAME ACTOR ACROSS VENUES`
!= `SAME ROLE ACROSS VENUES`.

And:

`NETWORK MEMBERSHIP`
!= `ONE METHOD POSITION`.

# I. Pocock: reviewer -> society founder -> script circulator/programme interlocutor -> specialist-journal author

## 1. H&T 1963 — review essay

Official H&T Volume 3 contents directly list:

J. G. A. Pocock reviewing J. H. Hexter's *Reappraisals in History*, H&T 3.1 (1963), 121–135.

Role:

`REVIEW ESSAY / HISTORIOGRAPHICAL INTERVENTION`.

This places Pocock inside H&T's method/historiography carrier years before CSPT formation and Skinner 1969.

Do not infer that his later contextual programme was already fully formed in this review without article-level reading.

## 2. CSPT 1967 — founder / institutional strategist

Direct recent archival reconstruction from UMSL Box 2 makes Pocock one of the founding correspondence actors with Richter and Wood.

Role:

`SOCIETY FOUNDER / INSTITUTIONAL-STRATEGY INTERLOCUTOR`.

The correspondence shows Pocock resisting a simple disciplinary-secession strategy and emphasizing `relatedness` to behavioralist work.

This is not the same role as author/reviewer.

## 3. 1968 — reciprocal prepublication/programme network

High-grade published control of Pocock -> Skinner correspondence, 8 Oct. 1968, establishes:

- recognition of methodological overlap;
- explicit distinction between Pocock's more sociological and Skinner's more philosophical orientation;
- proposal for some kind of `joint manifesto`;
- circulation of Pocock's unpublished scripts to Dunn/Skinner.

Skinner 1969 independently acknowledges Pocock as a reader/commentator on earlier drafts.

Role:

`PREPUBLICATION SCRIPT CIRCULATOR + DRAFT READER + PROGRAMME INTERLOCUTOR`.

Firewall:

`DRAFT / SCRIPT CIRCULATION`
!= `FORMAL REFEREEING`
!= `SPECIFIC REVISION CAUSE`.

## 4. *Political Theory* 1973 — author in inaugural issue

Pocock publishes `Verbalizing a Political Act` in the first volume of *Political Theory*.

Role:

`AUTHOR / METHOD-PROGRAMME PERFORMANCE INSIDE NEW SPECIALIST CARRIER`.

This does not by itself establish formal editorial office.

### Pocock role sequence

`H&T REVIEWER`
-> `CSPT FOUNDER`
-> `PRIVATE SCRIPT/DRAFT PROGRAMME INTERLOCUTOR`
-> `PT AUTHOR`.

The sequence is strong evidence for actor mobility across carrier forms, not a causal chain in which one role necessarily produces the next.

# II. Shklar: H&T reviewer -> CSPT coalition actor -> PT recruitment/self-classification correspondence

## 1. H&T 1963 — review essay

Official H&T Volume 2 contents directly list:

Judith N. Shklar reviewing Hannah Arendt's *Between Past and Future*, H&T 2.3 (1963), 286–292.

Role:

`REVIEW ESSAY / THEORETICAL-HISTORIOGRAPHICAL INTERVENTION`.

This locates Shklar in the H&T metahistorical review ecology before CSPT and PT.

## 2. CSPT 1967 — coalition/recruitment actor

Recent archival reconstruction of CSPT Box 2 identifies Shklar correspondence with Richter during founding-era coalition formation and shows her as important in broadening recruitment beyond a narrow methodological/political grouping.

Role:

`COALITION ACTOR / INSTITUTIONAL RECRUITMENT INTERLOCUTOR`.

Again, this is not an editorial-journal role.

## 3. PT 1971–72 — recruitment correspondence / actor self-classification

Harvard directly controls the Shklar papers collection HUGFP 118.

Gatta's archival reconstruction reports Shklar correspondence with Walter Odajnyk and Robert Lamb during the journal's formation and says Shklar described herself as stronger in `history of political thought` than `conceptual analysis`.

Those exact category labels are striking because the Feb. 1973 PT inaugural statement later includes history of political thought and conceptual analysis among its published role architecture.

Current role:

`PROSPECTIVE PARTICIPANT / RECRUITMENT CORRESPONDENT / ACTOR SELF-CLASSIFIER`.

Evidence ceiling:

original letters not yet direct-read in repo.

Therefore:

`SHKLAR CATEGORY LANGUAGE = SECONDARY ARCHIVAL CONTROL`

not direct quotation.

Also preserve:

`SHKLAR USED CATEGORY LANGUAGE`
!= `SHKLAR CAUSED PT'S FEB. 1973 CATEGORY ARCHITECTURE`.

### Shklar role sequence

`H&T REVIEWER`
-> `CSPT COALITION ACTOR`
-> `PT RECRUITMENT / SELF-CLASSIFICATION CORRESPONDENT`.

This is a particularly clean example of method categories moving through **actor positioning and institutional recruitment**, not only published manifestos.

# III. Mandelbaum: JHI method interlocutor -> H&T thematic-method author -> Skinner draft reader/source

## 1. JHI internal method ecology

Mandelbaum appears within the JHI/history-of-ideas methodological ecology as an actor explicitly differentiating recurrent similarity from historically continuous transmitted ideas.

Role:

`METHOD CRITIC / HISTORICAL-RELATION DIFFERENTIATOR INSIDE HISTORY-OF-IDEAS FIELD`.

This predates Skinner's canonical 1969 critique.

## 2. H&T Beiheft 5, 1964–65 — thematic-method author

Official H&T / JSTOR issue contents directly place Maurice Mandelbaum's:

`The History of Ideas, Intellectual History, and the History of Philosophy`

inside Beiheft 5, `The Historiography of the History of Philosophy`.

Role:

`THEMATIC METAHISTORICAL AUTHOR / FIELD-CLASSIFICATION INTERVENTION`.

This is not just a citation source; H&T gives the field-definition problem its own thematic carrier.

## 3. Skinner pre-1969 — draft reader / acknowledged source

Skinner 1969:

- cites Mandelbaum 1965 for field-definition control;
- directly thanks M. Mandelbaum for critical comments on earlier drafts.

Role pair:

`PUBLISHED METHODOLOGICAL SOURCE`
+
`PREPUBLICATION DRAFT COMMENTATOR`.

This dual role is unusually important.

It proves a direct edge between an older history-of-ideas/metahistory actor and Skinner's prepublication method text.

But:

`CITATION + DRAFT COMMENTS`
!= `FORMAL H&T REFEREE`
!= `EDITORIAL CAUSE`
!= `SPECIFIC CLAUSE REVISION`.

### Mandelbaum role sequence

`JHI/HISTORY-OF-IDEAS METHOD INTERLOCUTOR`
-> `H&T THEMATIC METHOD AUTHOR`
-> `SKINNER PUBLISHED SOURCE + DRAFT COMMENTATOR`.

This sequence is one of the strongest direct controls against a clean external-rupture story.

# IV. Why these three actors are enough to change the network model

A generic network graph might simply draw edges among:

Pocock — Shklar — Mandelbaum — Skinner — CSPT — H&T — JHI — PT.

That loses the historical content.

The meaningful structure is role-typed:

| Actor | H&T | JHI/history-of-ideas | CSPT | Private/prepublication | Political Theory |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pocock | review essay | wider intellectual-history/method field | founder/strategist | scripts + correspondence + draft reading | inaugural-volume author |
| Shklar | review essay | adjacent intellectual-history/political-thought field | coalition/recruitment actor | PT recruitment correspondence | prospective/founding participant; exact formal role pending |
| Mandelbaum | thematic-method author | method interlocutor | — | Skinner draft commentator | indirectly present through Haddock/Skinner debate architecture |

The same actor can change role without changing identity.

Therefore:

`ACTOR CONTINUITY`
!= `ROLE CONTINUITY`.

And:

`ROLE CHANGE`
may be part of
`FIELD DIFFERENTIATION`.

# V. Carrier implications

The three actors reveal different pathways by which method becomes recurrent:

### Review -> institution -> article

Pocock / Shklar show scholars moving from review/intellectual intervention into society-building and specialist-carrier formation.

### Field critique -> thematic carrier -> draft circulation

Mandelbaum shows a history-of-ideas methodological problem moving through JHI ecology, H&T thematic packaging, and direct prepublication interaction with Skinner.

Thus method circulation occurs through:

`PUBLISHED TEXT`
+
`REVIEW`
+
`THEMATIC ISSUE`
+
`SOCIETY CORRESPONDENCE`
+
`PRIVATE DRAFT COMMENT`
+
`NEW JOURNAL PARTICIPATION`.

This is stronger than a citation-network model.

# VI. Relation to Skinner canonization

Skinner 1969 becomes highly portable later because it packages a polemical method argument in one long text.

The actor-traffic evidence shows that the prepublication ecology was less portable and more distributed:

- Mandelbaum supplies published prior method classification + direct draft commentary;
- Pocock supplies scripts, draft commentary, programme comparison and proposed manifesto;
- Shklar participates in adjacent method/institutional recruitment ecology;
- CSPT and H&T provide different infrastructures before PT consolidates another carrier.

Therefore:

`PORTABLE CANONICAL TEXT`
!= `FULL UNIT OF METHOD FORMATION`.

A better unit for formation is:

`ROLE-TYPED ACTOR + TEXT + INSTITUTION NETWORK`.
