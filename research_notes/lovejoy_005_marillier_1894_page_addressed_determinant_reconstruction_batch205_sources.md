## Claim allocation after Batch 205

### Do not claim as Lovejoy invention

- social/status determinant versus moral merit;
- ritual determinant versus moral desert;
- manner-of-death determinant versus moral desert;
- existence of genuinely moralized future-life cases;
- provenance/contact criticism as a methodological operation;
- historical superposition of moral sorting on older eschatological machinery.

### Still plausible Lovejoy-specific systematization

- explicit serial adjacency/order of determinant classes;
- `half-moral` as local compression label;
- strict `desert` threshold as explicit diagnostic wording;
- repeated reclassification of ambiguous cases by operative selector;
- portability of the same diagnostic operation from eschatology to sacrifice / efficacy and later conceptual abstraction.

## Evidence hierarchy

`DIRECT MARILLIER` > `PAGE-ADDRESSED CONTEMPORARY MAUSS RELAY` > `STRUCTURAL INFERENCE`.

No direct wording should be attributed to Marillier pp.37/42/44 until page images or direct OCR are recovered. The relay securely establishes that Mauss attributed the relevant propositions/evidence to those pages.
