# MS38_005 paper architecture — Batch 52 causal-language correction

Date: 2026-08-18
Purpose: preserve the one-notebook 005 article while correcting an over-strong claim about where Lovejoy's analytical method originated.

## Correction to the current one-sentence argument

Current architecture says that, by reconstructing Marillier's workshop, `we can see Lovejoy learning to decompose apparently similar beliefs and rites by the mechanism that produced them`.

004 now supplies a chronological control against that wording. Before the Paris year, Lovejoy's Buddhist notebook and Harvard-affiliated article already separate:

- formal recurrence from semantic identity;
- etymological/filiational relation from contextual meaning;
- composite provenance from present systematic intelligibility;
- shared intellectual atmosphere from discriminating evidence of direct influence.

Recommended replacement:

> **Lovejoy's Paris notebook 005 records not the passive reception of a comparative-religion doctrine but participation in a paper-based research workshop in which an analytical discipline already visible in his Harvard philological work was extended to a new evidentiary domain: ethnographic reports and outwardly similar rites were criticized, circulated and reclassified by source provenance, motive, social relation, causal mechanism and historical layer; the resulting distinctions were later abstracted in his 1906 account of `primitive` causal efficacy.**

## Section-level adjustment

### Before current section 2 (`What the seminar was doing`)

Insert a short control paragraph, not a new full section:

> Lovejoy did not arrive in Paris methodologically blank. His immediately preceding Buddhist work already required separate proof for provenance and function: a term's derivation did not settle its contextual meaning, and a formula's composite history did not prove its present unintelligibility. The question for Paris is therefore not where decomposition began, but what happened when this evidentiary discipline was transferred from philological terms to ethnographic practices.

This should take no more than 250–350 words in the eventual article.

### Current section 5 (`A controversy teaches source criticism`)

Change the causal verb from `teaches` to something like:

**`A controversy expands the jurisdiction of source criticism`**

The section's conclusion should become:

> The Marillier environment did not originate Lovejoy's distinction between provenance and function. It expanded the unit of evidence: missionary testimony, diffusion, social status, manner of death and ritual practice now had to be separated with the same discipline that 004 had applied to etymology, lexical recurrence and textual configuration.

### Current section 6 (`From future-life determinants to sacrificial mechanisms`)

Add the cross-notebook isomorphism explicitly:

> In 004, same word/form did not guarantee same semantic job. In 005, same visible rite did not guarantee same causal/religious job. The change of topic is therefore also a change of evidentiary object, not a change of basic inferential discipline.

### Current section 7 (`1906 as abstraction, not origin`)

Strengthen the non-teleological bridge:

> The 1906 paper can be read as a cross-domain abstraction from distinctions made in multiple evidentiary settings — philological in 004, ethnographic/ritual in 005 — rather than as the maturation of a doctrine first supplied by Marillier.

## Why this improves the article

1. It prevents a reviewer from using 004/1898 to falsify the claim that Paris `taught` Lovejoy decomposition.
2. It preserves Marillier's importance at the level where the evidence is strongest: workshop form, dossier circulation, source criticism, comparative scale, and the movement from outward form to mechanism.
3. It makes the transatlantic argument non-zero-sum: Harvard and Paris contribute different evidentiary ecologies.
4. It fits the Mauss control better. Marillier can be a shared research node without becoming the sole inventor of either student's analytic method.
5. It gives a cleaner developmental bridge to 1906: **operator migration → domain expansion → causal abstraction**.

## Preferred vocabulary

Use:

- `retooling`
- `extension`
- `domain shift`
- `operator migration`
- `expansion of evidentiary jurisdiction`
- `from term-in-text to rite-in-report`
- `from semantic identity to causal identity`

Avoid unless explicitly qualified:

- `Marillier taught Lovejoy decomposition`
- `Lovejoy learned source criticism in Paris`
- `Paris was the origin of the method`
- `005 contains the birth of unit-ideas`

## Compact paper formula

> **004: provenance does not determine semantic function.**
>
> **005: outward form does not determine causal function.**
>
> **1906: causal function becomes an abstract comparative category.**

This formula can carry the Harvard → Paris → 1906 bridge while leaving the main paper documentary center on notebook 005.
