## 6. Important editorial correction: summary grammar is not authorial evidence

Across pp.92–99, the current `corrected_text` fields are explicitly heterogeneous: headings may be diplomatic while much surrounding prose is conservative argument summary.

Therefore phrases such as:

- `Lovejoy examines...`;
- `Lovejoy asks...`;
- `Lovejoy contrasts...`;
- `Lovejoy tests...`;
- `Lovejoy again resists...`

must be treated as **editorial shorthand for what the note packet is doing**, not manuscript evidence that the proposition or relation is authored by Lovejoy.

For source-critical writing, replace the authorial subject with forms such as:

- `the inserted sheet records...`;
- `the Marillier-mediated packet juxtaposes...`;
- `the notes distinguish...`;
- `the insert poses the problem whether...`.

This correction does not require rewriting canonical `corrected_text` from source inference alone. It governs citation and argument use until the original image permits clause-level separation.

- chronological priority for a later Lovejoy argument.

`MARILLIER-MEDIATED PROPOSITION FIELD -> LOVEJOY NOTE-TAKING / SELECTION`.

A stronger operator such as

`MARILLIER CLAIM -> LOVEJOY OBJECTION / RECLASSIFICATION`

requires a local marked seam that has not yet been recovered here.
