# Lovejoy as Orientalist / comparative religion — web sweep batch 14

Date: 2026-08-18  
Focus: narrowing the newly identified Southern Illinois University Open Court archive target for Lovejoy’s 1906/1908 *Monist* publications.

## 1. Collection-level Lovejoy indexing is confirmed, but web discovery does not expose a dedicated Lovejoy folder

Southern Illinois University’s **MSS 027 Open Court Publishing Company Records** explicitly indexes **Arthur O. Lovejoy (1873–1962)** among persons represented in the collection. The same collection contains editorial/business correspondence and article/book manuscripts for *The Monist* and *The Open Court* during Paul Carus’s editorship.

A targeted web sweep for:

- `Correspondence - Publishing: Lovejoy`
- `Arthur O. Lovejoy` + `1906`
- the exact titles `The Fundamental Concept of the Primitive Philosophy` and `The Theory of a Pre-Christian Cult of Jesus`

has **not** surfaced a dedicated Lovejoy folder or manuscript record.

This is a useful negative result: collection-level indexing alone should not be converted into a claim that a specific 1906 submission letter/manuscript survives.

## 2. The relevant correspondence sub-series is now much better localized

The SIU finding aid states that **Series 1, Sub-Series 6 Publishing** contains editorial, sales, production and business correspondence, predominantly 1885–1920, and is arranged **alphabetically by correspondent**.

The web-indexed Box 44 listing is especially informative. It contains:

- a generic `Correspondence - Publishing: L`, dated 1888–1933;
- `La`;
- Lane;
- Langley;
- **Lanman, Charles R.**, 1910–1913;
- Laufer, Berthold, 1911–1932;
- `Le`;
- Leuba, James H., 1912;
- `Li`;
- `Ll`;
- Lloyd, Alfred H., 1910–1914.

The finding aid’s web representation stops at Lloyd in this Box 44 result. Lovejoy alphabetically falls immediately after `Lloyd`/within the following `Lo...` range, but the current indexed search does not expose the next box/folder listing or a named Lovejoy record.

### Evidentiary caution

It is **not yet safe** to state that Lovejoy is in Box 44 `L` or in an inferred Box 45 `Lo` folder. Alphabetical arrangement narrows the likely location, but the actual folder index must be checked.

## 3. Best concrete archive request

The archive request can now be much more specific:

> Please identify all **MSS 027 Open Court Publishing Company Records** folders/items indexed to **Arthur O. Lovejoy (1873–1962)**, especially in **Series 1 Correspondence, Sub-Series 6 Publishing**, and **Series 2 Manuscripts**, for approximately **1905–1909**. We are particularly interested in records connected with Lovejoy’s *Monist* articles “The Fundamental Concept of the Primitive Philosophy” (1906) and “The Theory of a Pre-Christian Cult of Jesus” (1908), including submission letters, editorial correspondence, typescripts, proofs, offprint/payment records, or other production material.

Also ask whether the repository has an internal/partial correspondent index for the `Lo...` portion of Sub-Series 6 that is not exposed in the public web interface.

The collection description itself says that a partial correspondent index for some correspondence sub-series is available upon request; even where this does not directly cover Sub-Series 6, staff may be able to search the database by the person authority entry for Lovejoy.

## 4. Why this archive is now a high-priority rather than speculative target

We now have three independent facts:

1. Lovejoy published in *The Monist* in **1906** and **1908** under Paul Carus/Open Court.
2. MSS 027 preserves *Monist* editorial correspondence and manuscript/production material from precisely this period.
3. Lovejoy is explicitly present in the collection’s person indexing.

Taken together these facts justify an archival query even though no folder-level Lovejoy record has yet surfaced online.

## 5. Additional network context that should remain separated from direct evidence

The same archive indexes or documents numerous people relevant to the project’s Orientalist/comparative-religion ecology, including:

- Charles R. Lanman (a named publishing-correspondence folder in Box 44, dated 1910–1913);
- Berthold Laufer;
- Anagarika Dharmapala;
- Shaku Sōen;
- D. T. Suzuki;
- Swami Vivekananda;
- Lucien Lévy-Bruhl;
- Max Müller;
- Josiah Royce.

This makes MSS 027 potentially valuable for mapping the editorial ecology around Lovejoy, but no direct relationship between Lovejoy and these particular figures should be asserted solely because their papers sit in the same archive.
