## 4. Revised analytical map

The branch now distinguishes four levels:

1. **operation:** decomposition, implication, opposition, logical independence, compatibility, classification;
2. **jurisdiction:** what logic can establish versus semantics, chronology, psychology, genealogy or historical evidence;
3. **configuration:** how analytically distinguishable elements may recombine and modify one another;
4. **historical identity:** what licenses a component to remain identifiable across contexts.

The mature unit-idea problem sits mainly at level 4, but levels 1–3 have their own prehistory and cannot be collapsed into a single ontology of ideas.
