## Revised 004 canonical thesis

The strongest documentary line is now:

> **Harvard/Lanman/Warren formation + live 1896–98 Indological controversy + Paris 1898–99 comparative-religion / Buddhism notebook environment → notebook 004 as a source-transformation workshop → 1898 technical philology → 1901 Buddhism teaching → 1902 comparative history of categories → 1907 explicit separation of logical from chronological sequence.**

The continuity is not proto-`unit-ideas`. It is a discipline of **dividing evidentiary burdens** among:

- historical provenance / borrowing / transmission;
- semantic identity;
- causal role;
- logical or systematic configuration;
- chronology versus logical sequence.

Notebook 004 matters because named source propositions can be followed into Lovejoy’s own reweighting, testing and recomposition before publication.

## Senart 1896 is a methodological control, not a straw-man foil

Direct primary pp.288, 293 and 295 establish that Senart already:

- decomposes dependent origination into a historical nucleus plus extensions/borrowings;
- uses semantic/formal comparison to reconstruct `upadhi/upādi` genealogy;
- explicitly advocates **analysis and filiation of terms**;
- identifies **successive stratifications**;
- warns against mistaking inherited/scholastic nomenclature for deliberate original philosophical system.

Therefore Lovejoy cannot be credited with inventing decomposition, lexical filiation, stratification or the broad notion that borrowed components can be recombined.

The sharper contrast is:

> **Senart uses term-filiation to reconstruct layers and to distrust apparent systematic depth; Lovejoy grants much of the layering while requiring independent semantic/causal evidence before concluding that repeated or borrowed elements are functionally identical or that the later arrangement is unintelligible.**

Principal manuscript hinge: 004 p.49.

## Hardy → 004 → 1898 remains the strongest microgenesis

Hardy 1853 pp.394–396 directly supplies the two-factor architecture of renewed existence:

- `upādāna` produces renewed existence;
- karma controls its manner/state/grade.

004 p.33 explicitly converts this into a `fact` versus `manner` question. Lovejoy 1898 stabilizes it as:

- karma = cause of the **particular condition** of rebirth;
- `upādāna` = cause of the **fact of rebirth as such**.

Lovejoy then adds a historical explanation: karma is inherited moral-causal vocabulary presupposing rebirth, while Buddhism’s possibility of ending rebirth creates a separate causal problem.

This is **historical-functional recomposition**, not invention of the bifurcation.

## `upādisesa` half: Oldenberg priority + Lovejoy evidentiary recalibration

Oldenberg’s 1882 English translation, exact version cited by Lovejoy (`p.433`), already contains most of the ethical-residue argument and canonical apparatus:

- Itivuttaka counterevidence;
- Anāgāmin/Arahat and deceased-monk evidence;
- poison-remnant analogy;
- `upādāna ≈ saṁyojana`;
- strong `upādi = upadhi` orthographic hypothesis.

The true 1897 third German edition, recovered in Batch159, strengthens the field control further. It already contains explicit warnings that:

- inherited keywords/categories can be recombined under new fundamental ideas;
- generic resemblance is weaker than genuinely characteristic doctrine;
- formally similar sequences may answer different questions/functions;
- negative coincidence and absence have little genealogical weight;
- source chronology and literary genre constrain historical inference;
- canonical usage can defeat speculative etymology;
- `upādāna` is not identical with the five `upādānakkhandha`.

Lovejoy’s 1898 delta is therefore controlled recomposition:

1. he stages competing derivational lines (Childers/Rhys Davids vs Müller/Dahlmann);
2. he explicitly says both derivations may be etymologically possible;
3. therefore **meaning must be settled without reference to etymology**;
4. he decides by usage/context and retains contradictory witnesses;
5. he ends more provisionally than Oldenberg’s orthographic resolution.

Published rule:

> **derivation/filiation evidence is not sufficient semantic evidence.**

## Jacobi 1897: diagnostic specificity was already live controversy practice

Batch159 recovered Hermann Jacobi’s 1897 review of Dahlmann. Jacobi differentiates Sāṃkhya and Yoga through nontrivial doctrinal markers rather than generic affinities and explicitly criticizes failure to see their differences.

This directly controls the 004 move in which Lovejoy supplements Rhys Davids by treating generic traits as insufficient and seeks a more characteristically Sāṃkhyan diagnostic relation.

Therefore `diagnostic specificity` remains a useful project description of Lovejoy’s operation, but not a priority claim. The 004 value is application/reweighting inside Lovejoy’s own research process.

## 1902 and 1907: Lovejoy’s own scale expansion

### 1902 — `Religion and the Time-Process`

Primary article explicitly self-cites the 1898 Buddhist technical paper and makes Buddhism/Vedānta the first major comparative case. It separates:

- structural affinity;
- demonstrated historical transmission.

Aristotle–Vedānta resemblance does not establish eastern borrowing if an internal Greek genealogy accounts for the conception.

Operational continuity:

- 1898: derivation does not settle semantic function;
- 1902: resemblance does not settle historical transmission.

### 1907 — `The Origins of Ethical Inwardness in Jewish Thought`

Lovejoy explicitly distinguishes **logical sequence** from chronological sequence, noting that texts of the same period can embody different stages of reflection and older intellectual forms can survive beside newer ones.

This is the strongest early Lovejoy published control against conflating logical reconstruction with calendar chronology.

## La Vallée Poussin 1902–1913: anti-teleological field control

Poussin’s later Buddhist work shows that the methodological tension was a wider field problem, not uniquely Lovejoyan.

### 1905/06 `Deux notes sur le Pratītyasamutpāda`

Recovered opening OCR says:

- nidāna formula is made from **arbitrarily associated formulas**;
- it suggests **several successive stratifications**, explicitly after Senart;
- nearly every term is hazardous to interpret;
- Senart, Garbe, Jacobi and Oldenberg illuminated these difficulties;
- Buddhist exegetes tried to **put order into the formula’s initial incoherence** and introduce logic into the inherited arrangement.

His second note addresses the orthodox distribution of links among **past, present and future**.

Critical control:

> a scholar can affirm strong historical incoherence/stratification while still reconstructing systematic and temporal models.

Thus Lovejoy did not monopolize the combination of historical heterogeneity with system reconstruction. 004’s value is **microgenesis of his own version**.

### 1913 `Théorie des douze causes`

Poussin states programmatically that analytical reconstruction is indispensable to intelligibility, because ideas must be reconstructed according to a **logical or at least intelligible development**, but that such analysis **does not necessarily have historical value** and may yield only a plausible account of real historical development.

This is the clearest external field control for our phrase **division of evidentiary burdens**.

It should never be used as an upstream source for Lovejoy. Its function is anti-teleological: similar methodological distinctions arise within Buddhist studies independently of Lovejoy’s later history-of-ideas programme.
