## 1. RAI MS99: distinguish custodial archive from Wiley delivery layer

The Royal Anthropological Institute itself remains the custodian of Léon Marillier MS 99. The RAI catalogue resolves the series as eleven files, including:

- MS 99/1 `Origine de la mort – Séjour des morts – Conditions des âmes dans l’autre vie`
- MS 99/2 `Folk-lore – Survivance – Völkerpsychologie E. Torday psychologie religieuse`
- MS 99/4 `Anthropologie rituale – Sacrifices humains (Afrique, Inde)`
- MS 99/11 `Notes bibliographiques et divers`

The RAI archive access page treats Wiley Digital Archives as an in-house digital resource available to visitors, not as the identity or sole access route of the archive. RAI policy also provides a direct scholarly enquiry / copying route through the archivist, subject to permission and possible charges. No independent remote facsimile of MS 99 was located in this sweep.

Implication: Senate House's Wiley access being onsite-only is inconvenient but not blocking. WDA can be used on the next London visit as a direct digital control; remote reproduction from RAI remains a legitimate route. For the current Lovejoy comparison, the most relevant files are /4 and /11, with /1–2 also potentially high-yield because MS38_005 contains extensive survival/eschatology notes and an inserted note headed `Marillier — Survivance — 12 June`.

## 4. Uppsala: major source-control breakthrough in Söderblom catalogue

The uploaded 335-page Runestam catalogue contains a substantially better target than the previously noted general diary/notebook series.

At the end of series E, `Handlingar i särskilda ämnen`, under `Udda objekt`, item 1 is described as:

> `Sju kapslar innehållande: 1) kollegieblock från studietiden 1883-1892, 2) kollegieböcker från Paristiden 1894-1901, 3) Anteckningar från föreläsningar 1886-1892.`

Important parsing point: `Sju kapslar` means seven capsules total across these three categories. The `2)` before `kollegieböcker från Paristiden 1894-1901` is a category number, not a statement that there are two Paris capsules.

This yields a precise requestable archival target:

`Nathan Söderbloms efterlämnade papper (N. Söderblom), E. Handlingar i särskilda ämnen, Udda objekt, nr 1, kategori 2: kollegieböcker från Paristiden 1894–1901.`

This is potentially the best available second-student control for Lovejoy's Paris formation because it may preserve contemporaneous course/study notebooks rather than retrospective publications alone.

The same catalogue also identifies a lower-priority memorabilia route in D. Minnen: `Minnen 1894-1901. Parisåren`, including `Föreningar och institut`, `Religion och kyrka`, invitations/programmes, and miscellaneous Paris material. Series E also contains `Från Paris andliga horisont`, mainly clippings from French press during 1894–1901. These are useful contextual controls, but the Paris `kollegieböcker` should be prioritized.

The manuscript catalogue independently preserves a dense Paris-era research genealogy, including:

- 1899 `Frestelseberättelserna om Gotama Buddha, Zarathustra och Jesus Kristus`
- 1899 `Les Fravashis` thesis materials and annotated printed copy
- Swedish manuscript `Det kommande lifvet`, begun Uppsala 1893–94 and worked on at Calais in summer 1899
- 1900 `La vie future. Etude comparative des formes eschatologiques faite en partant du mazdéisme`
- 1901 `La vie future` thesis manuscripts, excerpts and proofs
- 1901 inaugural lecture on general history of religions and theology, with an early concept in diary B 6:6 (1897–1901), pp.232–239, continuing in B 6:7.

No direct Marillier-name hit was recovered from the catalogue search. This does not imply absence from the notebooks themselves.
