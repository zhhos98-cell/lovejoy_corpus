## 2. p.112 — the rejected `circumcision = substitute/partial human sacrifice` genealogy is a historically concrete rival theory family

Round 27 treated p.112 as a strong source-adjudication seam because the notebook rejects a partial-human-sacrifice explanation of circumcision and instead prefers initiation / blood-covenant / incorporation. This pass makes the rejected theory historically more concrete.

### A. Frederick Starr, 1891

Frederick Starr's `Dress and Adornment IV`, *Popular Science Monthly* 40 (December 1891), explicitly interprets Jewish circumcision as a blood covenant and also as a substitute for human sacrifice.

### B. Kuenen and related nineteenth-century genealogy

J. M. Wheeler's *Bible Studies* (1892) states directly that Kuenen holds circumcision to be in the nature of a substitute for human sacrifice. Later reference literature still lists Movers/Ghillany, Letourneau, Élie Reclus and others in the same sacrificial-theory family.

Thus the rival was a live nineteenth-century explanatory family, not a retrospective foil.

Notebook 005 elsewhere directly extracts Trumbull. *The Blood Covenant* supplies circumcision as blood-covenant, fraternity, incorporation, adoption, and a rights-and-obligations relation.
