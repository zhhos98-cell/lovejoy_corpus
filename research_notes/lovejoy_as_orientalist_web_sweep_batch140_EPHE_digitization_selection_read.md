# Batch 140 — EPHE digitization selection criteria: revised Paris retrieval priorities

Date: 2026-08-19  
Status: synced  
Source basis: user-supplied transcription of the Archives nationales / EPHE project section `Critères de sélection des documents numérisés`.

## Core correction

The selection criteria materially sharpen the Paris digital queue. They **do not, in the text supplied here, explicitly list student registration registers or lesson-by-lesson attendance lists among the digitized EPHE institutional series**. Earlier Paris notes treated such registers as a likely digitized target because a broader project description had suggested registration/attendance documentary types. That must now be downgraded:

> `lesson-level register existed institutionally` does not equal `lesson-level register is confirmed digitized in this project`.

Until an actual component in the finding aid exposes a digitized registration/attendance object, the archive queue should prioritize the record classes that the selection criteria explicitly confirm.

## Highest-value confirmed digitized targets for Lovejoy's 1898–99 Paris year

### 1. Ve Section handwritten conference reports and synthetic reports

The selection criteria explicitly say the digitized Ve Section corpus contains:

- `rapports manuscrits sur les conférences envoyés aux présidents de la section`;
- `rapports synthétiques ayant servi pour élaborer les annuaires de la section`.

This is currently the single highest-value EPHE target for notebook 005.

For 1898–99, retrieve all surviving reports for:

- Léon Marillier;
- Maurice Vernes;
- Alfred Foucher / `Religions de l'Inde`;
- any neighboring comparative-religion conference that overlaps afterlife, sacrifice, anthropology, Buddhism, India, or method.

Research payoff:

- test what was omitted or compressed in the printed Annuaire;
- recover exact wording, examples, source titles, timetable changes, or participant references;
- compare manuscript report strata directly against notebook 005;
- potentially resolve the programme/report weekday discrepancy already identified for Marillier.

### 2. Ve Section assembly minutes, 1886–1980

The selection criteria explicitly confirm digitization of the Ve Section `procès-verbaux des assemblées de section de 1886 à 1980`.

For 1898–99 search meetings around:

- rentrée / opening of academic year;
- course programme approval or alteration;
- timetable changes;
- chair vacancies/candidatures;
- substitutions and leaves;
- Marillier, Vernes, Foucher, Sylvain Lévi, Finot.

This is particularly important because Batch129 found a live discrepancy between Marillier's advance programme (Wednesday/Friday) and retrospective report (Monday/Tuesday). A section minute or schedule-change record could explain the difference.

### 3. The 250 digitized course/conference posters

The selection criteria confirm a `remarquable collection de 250 affiches de cours et conférences` digitized across the three sections.

For Paris 1898–99, retrieve every poster touching:

- Ve Section: Marillier, Vernes, Foucher, `Religions de l'Inde`;
- IVe Section: Sylvain Lévi, Louis Finot, Sanskrit, Indian philology;
- terms: `pâli`, `bouddhisme`, `sanscrit`, `religions de l'Inde`, `survivance de l'âme`, `sacrifices humains`, `anthropophagie`.

These posters are the best electronic control for advertised course titles and last-minute corrections, analogous to the corrected Collège de France poster already recovered manually.

### 4. IVe Section typed assembly minutes, 1877–1912

The selection criteria explicitly confirm digitization of `procès-verbaux dactylographiés des assemblées de section de 1877 à 1912`.

The 1897–99 slice should be searched for:

- Lévi's mission absence and return;
- Finot/Foucher substitution arrangements;
- opening dates and course allocation;
- Sanskrit/Pāli/Buddhist teaching modalities;
- any administrative reason why regular teaching in 1898–99 differed from what Lovejoy reportedly expected.

This is now a stronger digital target than a generic search for student registers.

### 5. Sylvain Lévi fonds: 6000 digitized views

The project explicitly says 6000 views were selected from the Sylvain Lévi fonds, but also describes the three scholar-fonds selection as **mainly theses and conference materials**.

Therefore the first-pass query should not assume correspondence. Prioritize:

- conference manuscripts / notes;
- teaching texts;
- thesis-related material only if it intersects Buddhism/Pāli/Indian philosophy;
- 1897–99 mission-return lecture material.

Keywords:

`Pâli`, `bouddhisme`, `sanscrit`, `Bhagavad-Gîtâ`, `Mahâyâna`, `Asaṅga`, `Sûtrâlaṅkâra`, `Népal`, `Japon`, `mission`, `conférence`, `cours`.

A Lovejoy name hit would be exceptional, but the more realistic payoff is a manuscript-level reconstruction of what Lévi was teaching or preparing immediately after the mission.

### 6. Ministry F/17 digitized selection: 1157 views

The digitized ministry corpus contains creation/organization files, section and laboratory organization, annual synthetic budgets, and personnel states.

For the Lovejoy problem its value is administrative rather than participant-level. Search:

- `Sylvain Lévi`;
- `Foucher`;
- `Finot`;
- `mission`;
- `congé`;
- `suppléance` / `remplacement`;
- `rentrée 1898`;
- IVe / Ve Section organization.

This layer can close leave/substitution/return and institutional responsibility but is unlikely by itself to identify Lovejoy.

## Lower-priority but potentially useful confirmed digitized material

### Ve Section chair-candidature dossiers

Useful only if a 1890s appointment/reorganization bears directly on Marillier/Foucher/Lévi or course structure.

### Ve Section book-purchase lists and invoices

Potentially valuable later for a **material bibliography** test. If 1897–99 lists survive, compare purchased titles against books explicitly cited or excerpted in notebooks 004/005. This could reconstruct what reference works were physically entering the Section, but it is lower priority than reports/minutes/posters.

### Thesis-defense reports

Low priority for Lovejoy unless tied to a person/text already demonstrably in his seminar network.

## Material explicitly not worth prioritizing for this question

- IIIe Section 1956–97 annuals;
- 1960s–80s audiovisual laboratory files;
- later institutional presidency records;
- generic budgets unless they contain a named mission/substitution entry.

## Revised manual-click order

1. Ve Section **1898–99 manuscript conference reports**, especially Marillier.
2. Ve Section **1898–99 assembly minutes**.
3. EPHE **1898–99 course posters**, Ve and IVe Sections.
4. IVe Section **1897–99 typed assembly minutes**.
5. Sylvain Lévi digitized fonds: conference/teaching manuscripts.
6. F/17 ministry files for mission/leave/substitution.
7. Only after those: book-purchase lists/invoices and any registration/attendance object that the finding aid actually proves digitized.

## Evidence discipline

The supplied selection criteria support the existence of digitized conference reports, assembly minutes, course posters, selected scholar papers, ministry organization records, and several other record classes. They do **not** by themselves support the claim that Lovejoy's enrollment or lesson-signature register has been digitized. Any such claim must come from a component-level finding-aid object, not from extrapolation.

No new GLA/GAL/COV identifier is created in this batch.
