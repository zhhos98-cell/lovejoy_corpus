Consolidated: 2026-08-26

## Core result

The strongest evidence connecting Lovejoy's early 005 eschatology block to Marillier's Paris research environment is not merely thematic similarity. A same-workshop control, Nathan Söderblom's 1901 *La vie future d’après le mazdéisme*, overlaps with Lovejoy 005 at the level of **named sources, regional clusters, specific ethnographic problems and rare case combinations**, while explicitly acknowledging Marillier's conferences/study and declaring a different redivision of the shared material.

The safe model is:

> **shared Marillier problem/source apparatus → different student redivisions and uses.**

This is stronger than generic `influence`, but it still does not prove Lovejoy physically used the same Marillier sheets as Söderblom or copied any particular passage.

## 1. Material anchor inside Lovejoy 005

The inserted slip around PDF p.29 / manuscript p.55 is headed:

> `Marillier — Survivance — 12 June`

The surrounding notebook block then turns to Madagascar/Hova material, the abode of the dead, continued earthly conditions and a second death/annihilation after limited post-mortem survival.

In the context of Lovejoy's documented active participation in Marillier's 1898–99 EPHE survivance work, this is a strong internal material bridge between notebook 005 and the Marillier problem field.

It does not by itself identify whether a given datum came from oral teaching, Marillier's published memoir, Marillier's working notes, or a common printed source.

## 2. Murray / Pawnee cluster

### Söderblom

Söderblom cites C. A. Murray, *Travels in North America*, I, p.365, for Pawnees hunting peacefully and smoking in the future life.

### Lovejoy 005

Lovejoy also cites Murray, *Travels in North America*, vol. I, in connection with Pawnees / happy hunting ground material. He immediately turns the object into a provenance problem: an early-nineteenth-century statement must be checked for missionary/contact influence before being treated as secure indigenous evidence.

### Evidentiary value

The shared named source is a source-apparatus control. The difference of use is equally important:

- Söderblom: typological example;
- Lovejoy: source-critical test.

Do not infer direct student-to-student borrowing.

## 3. Greenland / Egede-type two-destination problem

### Söderblom

In his discussion of two places of the dead, Söderblom cites Greenland/Egede material: women dying in childbirth and people drowned during whaling can reach a favorable realm, yet the structure does not constitute moral reward/punishment.

### Lovejoy 005

Lovejoy records essentially the same diagnostic complex: two abodes; favorable destination associated with drowning and childbirth; explicit refusal to treat the difference as moral; additional Eskimo material then extends the case.

### Evidentiary value

This is a shared **case problem**, not merely a shared region. It shows the same Marillier survivance question moving through two student products, while Lovejoy gives the case a more explicit selector-test role.

## 4. Madagascar / Abinal–Vaissière / Hova / second-death cluster

This is the strongest shared-source constellation currently recovered.

### Söderblom

Söderblom cites the Révérends Pères Abinal et Vaissière, *Vingt ans à Madagascar*, and discusses:

- Hova beliefs;
- Ambondrombé / mountain or region of the dead;
- Vazimba prestige / secret or sorcerous power;
- continued post-mortem status;
- a limited future life followed by **second death**;
- contradictory reports and the risk that European observers impose excessive systematic consistency.

### Lovejoy 005

Immediately around the inserted `Marillier — Survivance — 12 June` slip, Lovejoy turns to Madagascar, population/layer questions, Vaissière / *Vingt ans à Madagascar* material, Hova beliefs, the abode of the dead, continued occupations/status and **second death / annihilation after a limited future existence**.

### Evidentiary value

The convergence includes:

- same Marillier survivance label;
- same region;
- same named source/book;
- same Hova cluster;
- same second-death problem.

The safest inference is a **shared Marillier source apparatus / seminar dossier field**.

Because Söderblom directly testifies to benefiting from Marillier's conferences and notes, dossier-mediated convergence is materially plausible. For Lovejoy, identical physical note-sheet transmission remains unproved.

## 5. Cheyenne / status-selection cluster

### Söderblom

Cheyenne material appears in a section on superior survival/future condition determined by status, prowess or other non-moral qualification.

### Lovejoy 005

Lovejoy treats Cheyenne powers/access to happy hunting ground while refusing to translate scalping, warrior status or other bodily/ritual conditions automatically into moral merit.

### Evidentiary value

The case class is shared; the local use differs. This is consistent with a shared source apparatus followed by student-specific redivision.

## 6. Manner-of-death dossier

Both Söderblom and Lovejoy repeatedly use:

- drowning;
- childbirth;
- battle/violent death;
- suicide;
- burial/funeral qualification.

Söderblom serializes these in his published typology. Lovejoy uses the same family of cases more densely as active tests of competing selectors.

This is no longer evidence that Lovejoy invented the determinant categories. Its value here is source/dossier circulation: the same unusual comparative case family appears across products connected to Marillier's survivance work.

## 7. Söderblom's explicit testimony about redivision

Around printed p.33 Söderblom says he attaches his inquiry to Marillier's remarkable study but proceeds:

> `avec d’autres divisions et à un point de vue un peu différent`.

This is the best actor-near model for how shared source apparatus can coexist with divergent student arguments.

The historical category is therefore neither simple copying nor isolated originality:

> **inherited documents/problems + redivision + distinct application.**

That model should discipline interpretation of Lovejoy 005.

## 8. What this dossier proves and does not prove

### Secure

- Lovejoy was an active participant in Marillier's 1898–99 conference environment;
- Lovejoy 005 contains the internal `Marillier — Survivance — 12 June` marker;
- Söderblom explicitly acknowledges Marillier's conferences/study and later redivides the material;
- multiple exact or near-exact source/case overlaps exist: Murray/Pawnee, Greenland/Egede problem, Madagascar/Abinal–Vaissière/Hova/second death, Cheyenne/status, manner-of-death cluster.

### Strong inference

- Lovejoy and Söderblom worked from a substantially shared Marillier source/problem apparatus, through some mixture of published texts, seminar discussion, working notes and common sources.

### Unproved

- Lovejoy physically consulted the same Marillier sheets used by Söderblom;
- any specific Lovejoy passage was copied from a Marillier working note;
- Söderblom was a direct source for Lovejoy;
- exact chronology of every 005 leaf beyond internal anchors.
