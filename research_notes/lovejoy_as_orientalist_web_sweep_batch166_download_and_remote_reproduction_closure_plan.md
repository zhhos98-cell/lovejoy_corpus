## 2. Johns Hopkins MS-0873 — highest-probability remote archival win

Repo control identifies:

**MS-0873 — Daniel J. Wilson transcriptions of Arthur O. Lovejoy letters**

One box; transcriptions mostly typewritten, some handwritten; 1872–1962.

Highest-value targets are the letters Wilson cites for Lovejoy's Paris year:

- Lovejoy to Wallace W. Lovejoy, 17 Oct. 1898;
- 1 Dec. 1898;
- 5 Dec. 1898.

Ask for:

1. confirmation that these dated transcriptions occur in MS-0873;
2. digital research copies of those three transcriptions;
3. if staff can search the one-box file efficiently, identification of any further 1898–99 Paris letters containing `Sylvain Lévi`, `Pali/Pāli`, `Buddhism`, `Marillier`, `EPHE`, `Sorbonne`, `Vernes`, `Réville`, `Bibliothèque Nationale`, `religion`.

Why this is first archival priority:

- the request is tiny rather than open-ended;
- Wilson's published claims already point to exact dates;
- the payoff is direct Lovejoy wording for the Paris/Orientalist chronology;
- it could identify the language/texts read privately with Sylvain Lévi and distinguish private Indic work from Marillier's comparative-religion seminar.

Current Johns Hopkins policy explicitly allows researchers to request digital copies of collection materials not available online; digitization requests are reviewed case by case and fees may apply. Therefore this is a genuine remote-reproduction route, not merely an on-site-access query.

## 3. RAI MS 99 — highest intellectual archival payoff, but ask about existing digitization before paying for scans

Official object:

**Royal Anthropological Institute, GB 1446 MS 99, Léon Marillier, `Notes and extracts`.**

Public contents:

- `/2` folklore / survivance / psychology of religion;
- `/3` totemism;
- `/4` ritual anthropology / human sacrifices (Africa, India);
- `/5–6` marriage;
- `/7` myths;
- `/8` flood;
- `/9` taboo;
- `/10` cult of dead / sorcery;
- `/11` bibliographical and miscellaneous notes.

The current paper-level priority remains `/1–4`, `/9–11`, especially `/4` and `/11`.

### First request: existing digital object check

The RAI states that visitors have full in-house access to Wiley Digital Archives, but the public catalogue does not expose a WDA identifier for MS 99. Ask the Archivist:

- Is GB 1446 MS 99 `/1–11` digitized in Wiley Digital Archives?
- If yes, what is its WDA collection/item/folder identifier, and is there any way to provide remote research access or digital research copies?
- If not, can the RAI quote for targeted digitization/reproduction?

### If new scanning is needed

Do **not** initially order all folders. Request in stages:

Stage 1 — folder covers / outer inscriptions for `/1–4`, `/9–11`.

Reason: the `E. Torday` annotations create a provenance problem; cover images may distinguish Marillier-era headings from later archival additions and establish handwriting/arrangement before expensive inner-leaf scanning.

Stage 2 — `/4` and `/11` inner leaves.

Reason: `/4` is the closest structural match to 005 pp.46–119; `/11` is the best source-order/bibliography transmission test.

Stage 3 — `/1–3`, `/9–10` only if Stage 2 shows discriminating concordance.

Use `/5–8` mainly as negative/control dossiers unless cost is low.

RAI policy states that permission to copy certain manuscript/archive materials may be granted after a signed personal-research declaration, at the Archivist's discretion, with charges possible. The website does not explicitly promise a remote digitization service, so frame this as a **copy/scan enquiry**, not as an assumed entitlement.

## 5. Archives nationales / EPHE — remote research-aid + reproduction is institutionally supported

EPHE's official archive inventory places the Vth Section institutional archive (1886–2005) at the Archives nationales as **20190568/001–399**, with **scolarité / student records at 20190568/185–368**.

Current Archives nationales remote services are unusually promising:

- the Salle de lecture virtuelle supports remote research-aid requests;
- the personal account can request reproduction of a document;
- exact cotes are required for reproduction orders, but research-aid can be requested when the exact cote is not yet known;
- secure remote access also exists for some non-public online images, though there is no evidence yet that these EPHE cartons are among them.

Best first query:

> identify the exact article(s) within `20190568/185–368` containing Vth Section registration, attendance, or student-administration records for 1898–1899, especially the conferences of Léon Marillier and the participants Arthur O. Lovejoy, Nathan Söderblom, Travers, Schaefer, W. S. Andrews, and Lorette.

What this could realistically solve:

- full identities of unresolved seminar participants;
- registration category, nationality/address/cross-enrolment if preserved;
- possibly attendance/administrative traces.

What not to assume:

- a Lovejoy seminar essay/presentation will survive there;
- `active part in the work` can necessarily be unpacked beyond the published EPHE report.

Once the exact cote/article is identified, request the smallest relevant reproduction.
