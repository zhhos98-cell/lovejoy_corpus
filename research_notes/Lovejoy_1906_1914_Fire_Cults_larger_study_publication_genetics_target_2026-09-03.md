## II. 1914 direct primary raises the folder's genetic value

Primary:

Arthur O. Lovejoy, review of James H. Leuba, *A Psychological Study of Religion*, *International Journal of Ethics* 24.2 (Jan. 1914): 216–220, especially p.219.

Lovejoy corrects Leuba's account of his 1906 paper and says that Leuba underuses the hypothesis.

His forward-looking claim is explicit:

`MANA / MANITOU IDEA`
-> `INTERPENETRATES OTHER ELEMENTS OF EARLY BELIEF`
-> `AFFECTS DEVELOPMENT OF RELIGION IN MANY SIGNIFICANT WAYS`.

He then criticizes Leuba for beginning a psychological interpretation of religious history but stopping at the supposed primitive stage; what Lovejoy expects is continuation through the `higher phases of religious evolution`.

Direct facsimile/page control:
- *International Journal of Ethics* 24.2, printed p.219.

This is a crucial retrospective witness because it shows that Lovejoy in 1914 still conceptualized `primitive energetics` as a **developmentally productive relation**, not merely a static taxonomy of primitive belief.

## IV. Why `fire cults` is analytically high-value

The phrase `fire cults` may force several live distinctions.

A fire practice can potentially be coded as:

- direct manipulation of efficacy/power;
- personal deity/spirit cult;
- purification;
- sacrificial mechanism;
- survivance/reinterpretation;
- later religious institution built over an earlier causal mechanism;
- analogy admitted to the source pool but excluded from print.

Thus the folder may show whether Lovejoy's larger project was meant to proceed by:

`ONE POWER CATEGORY`
-> `MULTIPLE RITUAL APPLICATIONS`
-> `LATER INSTITUTIONAL / RELIGIOUS DIFFERENTIATION`.

That would provide a direct manuscript test of the 1914 statement about mana/manitou `interpenetrating` other beliefs and affecting religious development.

But this is a test, not a current claim.

## V. Relation to the Preuss priority correction

The new Preuss 1904–05 control changes how this folder should be read.

Do not approach Box 23/11 looking for proof that Lovejoy invented dynamism / impersonal power / priority over personality.

Those broad operators were already field-available.

Instead code the folder for:

1. **source selection** — which cases enter;
2. **source rejection** — which cases are marked unusable;
3. **witness resolution** — field report vs grammar vs lexical usage vs theory;
4. **causal relation** — efficacy, transfer, coercion, purification, personal agency, etc.;
5. **jurisdiction** — magic / religion / ritual / philosophy;
6. **temporal status** — primitive layer, survival, later reinterpretation, developmental consequence;
7. **population/source status** — ethnographic living peoples vs historical textual traditions;
8. **print relation** — copied into 1906, omitted from 1906, added after 1906;
9. **larger-study markers** — headings, chapter language, cross-references, continuation cues;
10. **version dates** — Washington University envelopes or other material evidence.

The likely Lovejoy-specific historical object is configuration / selection / evidence architecture, not ingredient priority.
