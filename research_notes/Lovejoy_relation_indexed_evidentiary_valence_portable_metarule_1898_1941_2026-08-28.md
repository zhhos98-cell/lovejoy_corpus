# Lovejoy — relation-indexed evidentiary valence as a portable metarule, 1898–1941

Date: 2026-08-28  
Status: **NEW GOVERNING SYNTHESIS / STRUCTURAL HOMOLOGY, NOT SOURCE-GENEALOGY CLAIM**

## Core result

Across Lovejoy's early philological/comparative work, philosophical history, formal logic, source genealogy, and mature history of ideas, one recurrent discipline can now be stated more precisely than `typed relations` or `careful warrant`:

> **The evidentiary force of a datum is relation-indexed. A piece of evidence is not simply positive, negative, strong, or weak in the abstract; its force depends on the specific relation-predicate being tested.**

The same datum can:

- support one relation;
- undermine another;
- leave a third undecided;
- become a different kind of historical fact when the relation changes.

This is a particularly strong candidate for what can be called `logician-like` in Lovejoy without claiming that every historical practice is literally formal logic.

It also matches his 1910 formal-logical procedure unusually closely: a proposition retains some possible relations, excludes others, and warrants suspension among the remaining cases.

The recurrence across time does **not** prove formal logic caused the earlier or later historical practices.

---

# I. 1898 — similarity/adjacency can support comparison without identity or genealogy

Notebook 004 repeatedly separates:

- semantic proximity;
- classificatory identity;
- logical inclusion/subdivision;
- temporal priority;
- borrowing/genealogy;
- systemic function.

A resemblance can be relevant evidence for:

`AFFINITY / COMPARABILITY`

while failing to establish:

`IDENTITY`

or

`BORROWING / DIRECTION`.

Thus the datum `A resembles B` has no relation-independent historical implication.

---

# II. 1902 — cross-cultural affinity vs Western persistence vs Eastern borrowing

`Religion and the Time-Process` provides a clean mixed case.

The comparative recurrence of otherworldly structures in Indian and Western thought can support:

`COMPARATIVE TYPE / RECURRENCE`.

Within Western history, Platonic/Neo-Platonic/Christian development supports:

`PERSISTENCE / HISTORICAL CAREER`.

But the same structural affinity does not automatically support:

`INDIA → GREECE BORROWING`.

Lovejoy can reject the transmission proposition as gratuitous when internal Platonic dialectic is sufficient.

Evidence changes valence when the relation predicate changes.

---

# III. 1904 — documented contact establishes an edge, not its causal weight

In `The Dialectic of Bruno and Spinoza`, Lovejoy accepts that Spinoza read Bruno.

That datum strongly supports:

`EDGE EXISTS / CONTACT`.

It does not by itself establish:

`SOURCE EXCLUSIVITY`

or

`HIGH CAUSAL DEPENDENCE ON BRUNO`,

because a broader Neo-Platonic reservoir could supply similar concepts independently of that intermediary.

Thus one of the strongest possible pieces of classic influence evidence — documented reading — has limited jurisdiction.

---

# IV. 1906 — independence reverses evidentiary sign

`The Fundamental Concept of the Primitive Philosophy` provides the cleanest single example.

Lovejoy emphasizes convergent descriptions from historically independent cultural/observational fields.

For the predicate:

`COMMON HISTORICAL TRANSMISSION / BORROWING`,

independence is **negative evidence / a brake**.

For the predicate:

`INDEPENDENT RECURRENCE / ROBUST COMPARATIVE TYPE`,

independence is **positive evidence**.

Symbolically:

`E = historical independence`

then:

`Valence(E | transmission) < 0`

while

`Valence(E | recurrence) > 0`.

This analyst notation is not Lovejoy's.

It captures the historically important fact that the same datum can reverse inferential direction when the claim changes.

---

# V. 1910 formal logic — direct technical analogue

In `The Treatment of 'Opposition' in Formal Logic`, Lovejoy constructs five mutually exclusive and jointly exhaustive possible relations between the denotations of two terms.

A categorical proposition normally does not identify one unique real relation.

Instead it:

- retains a subset of compatible cases;
- excludes incompatible cases;
- leaves judgment suspended where multiple cases survive.

Thus the proposition has **case-relative informational force**.

The formal operation is:

`PROPOSITION E`
→ `eliminate some relation-cases`
→ `retain others`
→ `infer only what the surviving case-space licenses`.

This is the closest direct technical analogue of relation-indexed evidentiary valence in the corpus.

But chronology and source discipline matter:

- comparable historical practices exist before 1910;
- therefore 1910 is evidence of explicit formal articulation, not proof of origin.

---

# VI. 1911 — formal implication vs existence/evidence

`Existence and Formal Logic` makes jurisdiction explicit at a broader philosophical level.

A proposition can be:

- formally meaningful/intelligible;
- related by implication to other propositions;

without thereby being:

- empirically verified;
- historically evidenced;
- existentially true.

Thus formal relations have evidentiary force for one question and none for another.

This blocks a universal `logic decides history` reading at actor level.

---

# VII. 1924 — shared label can be evidence for semantic history but not macro-identity

The recurrence of `Romanticism` as a word is evidence for:

`SEMASIOLOGICAL HISTORY OF THE LABEL`.

It is not evidence sufficient for:

`ONE HISTORICAL ROMANTICISM / ESSENTIAL IDENTITY`.

Pairwise overlapping components can support local relations among episodes without producing a transitive macro-essence.

Again:

`same evidence object (label)`
→ different inferential value under different predicates.

---

# VIII. 1933 — similarity, priority, access, acknowledgment still have typed limits

Monboddo/Rousseau is especially instructive because several strong evidentiary facts coexist:

- Rousseau's priority;
- Monboddo's reading/access;
- strong clustered similarity;
- acknowledgment of agreement.

These support:

- content similarity;
- possible edge/contact;
- historical availability.

But alternative common sources and independent development prevent Lovejoy from assigning certainty to:

`DIRECT CAUSAL INFLUENCE / DIRECTION`.

The evidence is dense; the stronger relation remains underdetermined.

---

# IX. 1941 — analyst implication vs actor doctrine vs negative historical fact

Lovejoy's mature three-relation scheme provides a particularly sophisticated version.

The historian may analytically derive a logical implication from an idea.

That supports:

`ANALYST-LEVEL LOGICAL RELATION`.

It does not automatically support:

`ACTOR RECOGNIZED / HELD THE IMPLICATION`.

If the actor fails to draw the implication, that absence can itself become:

`NEGATIVE HISTORICAL FACT`

requiring psychological/historical explanation.

Thus the same logical structure has three different statuses depending on the historical predicate.

---

# X. Relation-indexed uncertainty

Confidence must be typed for the same reason.

A case should not receive one global number such as:

`confidence = medium`.

Instead:

- `C_target`;
- `C_identity`;
- `C_edge`;
- `C_exclusivity`;
- `C_causal_weight`;
- `C_propagation`;
- `C_actor_recognition`;
- etc.

This explains recurrent Lovejoyian judgments such as:

`contact certain`
+
`direct causal weight uncertain`.

Uncertainty itself has jurisdiction.

---

# XI. Why this is more precise than `typed relations`

`Typed relations` says:

> implication, affinity, transmission, psychological association, historical continuity, etc. are different relations.

`Relation-indexed evidentiary valence` adds:

> **the very same datum changes what it proves when the target relation changes.**

This is a stronger operational statement.

It explains why a disciplined historian must specify the predicate before evaluating evidence.

---

# XII. Connection to Gate A and Gate B

The rule applies on both sides of the new architecture.

## Gate A — aggregation/identity

Evidence may support:

- same lexical item;
- same functional type;
- same conceptual core;
- same proposition;
- recurrence;

without supporting another identity scale.

## Gate B — relations among identified objects

Evidence may support:

- contact;
- logical implication;
- psychological affinity;
- historical transmission;
- causal weight;

without supporting neighboring relations.

Therefore the governing demand is:

> **name the relation before grading the evidence.**

---

# XIII. `Logician-like` without reducing history to logic

The project can now define `logician-like` narrowly and historically:

> **Lovejoy often treats inquiry as a problem of delimiting possible relation-states and restricting what a datum licenses among them.**

This resembles his documented formal-logical practice because both involve:

- explicit case/relation partition;
- exclusion rather than premature unique determination;
- suspension among surviving alternatives;
- anti-transfer of warrant.

But local historical techniques remain domain-specific:

- philological usage;
- source criticism;
- ethnographic comparison;
- historical chronology;
- psychology;
- textual genealogy;
- institutional/practice evidence.

Thus:

`structural homology`
≠ `one formal-logical method`
≠ `demonstrated causal origin in logic`.

---

# XIV. Strongest current portable-metaprocedure hypothesis

The current best candidate is:

> **relation-indexed warrant control: identify the exact relation under test, determine what the available evidence eliminates or supports for that relation, preserve alternatives that remain compatible, and require a new proof step before upgrading to a different or stronger relation.**

This subsumes but sharpens earlier formulations:

- typed relations;
- inferential jurisdiction;
- mechanism-sensitive warrant;
- relation-specific confidence;
- controlled promotion.

It still requires historical genealogy: recurrence of the procedure does not prove one continuous technical source.

---

## Current shorthand

> **For Lovejoy, a datum does not have one evidentiary meaning. Historical independence can defeat a borrowing claim and strengthen a recurrence claim; documented reading can establish contact without establishing causal dependence; logical implication can be analyst-valid without being actor-held. This relation-indexed evidentiary valence is the most precise current candidate for the `logician-like` feature that recurs across his different contexts.**
