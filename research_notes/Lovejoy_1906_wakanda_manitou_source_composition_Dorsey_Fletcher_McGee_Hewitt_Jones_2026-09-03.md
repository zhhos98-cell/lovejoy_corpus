# Lovejoy 1906 — Wakanda / Manitou source composition: Dorsey → Fletcher → McGee → Hewitt → Jones

Date: 2026-09-03  
Status: **PRE-1906 NORTH-AMERICAN SOURCE CHAIN DIRECTLY CONTROLLED AT MULTIPLE NODES / LOVEJOY RESIDUAL RECUT TO CROSS-AUTHOR CONFIGURATION + RECLASSIFICATION**

## Core result

The North-American evidence behind Lovejoy's 1906 `Primitive Energetics` is not a single native concept moving transparently into philosophical abstraction.

Before Lovejoy, the ethnographic/language field already contained **competing and partially incompatible second-order readings**:

`PERSONAL / DEITY-LIKE AGENCY`
+
`LIFE-CONTINUITY / TRANSFER`
+
`MYSTERY / POTENCY`
+
`QUALITY RATHER THAN ENTITY`
+
`EFFICIENT CAUSAL POWER`
+
`IMPERSONAL COSMIC PROPERTY`.

The pre-1906 sequence can be schematized:

`DORSEY 1884`
→ many Wakanda usages remain prayerful, personalizing and Deity/Superior-Being-like

`FLETCHER 1897`
→ continuity of life, part-whole transfer, unequal potency

`McGEE 1897`
→ `wakanda` redefined as quality rather than definite entity; vague semantic bundle includes power/sacred/ancient/animate/immortal; earliest belief stage contains mysterious potencies everywhere

`HEWITT 1902`
→ `orenda` as mystic potency / property of all things / efficient cause

`JONES 1905`
→ `manitou` as impersonal cosmic mysterious property diffused through nature and differing by function/degree

`LOVEJOY 1906`
→ cross-author and cross-region recomposition into a diffused, all-pervasive, invisible, manipulable and transferable life-energy / universal force; diagnostic center shifted to causation/efficacy; category ranked above personality and reaggregated as `primitive philosophy`.

The strongest Lovejoy-specific residual is therefore:

> **cross-author configurational synthesis + selective de-personalization + causal reclassification + architectonic ranking**.

Not discovery of the individual semantic components.

## I. Dorsey 1884 — an important personalizing control

Primary:

James Owen Dorsey, `Omaha Sociology` (Bureau of Ethnology, 1884), public-domain Project Gutenberg text.

Direct text repeatedly treats Wakanda in strongly agentive/personal terms.

Examples include:

- pipe smoke is offered upward to Wakanda because Wakanda gave the pipes and `rules over them`;
- prayers ask Wakanda to pity, help, protect or grant success;
- some fasters think Wakanda speaks to them;
- moral conduct can gain Wakanda's goodwill or incur punishment;
- religious law/custom is said to come from Wakanda;
- Dorsey's summary glossary can call Wakanda the `Deity or Superior Being`.

This does **not** mean Dorsey has one consistent theological interpretation or that every native use is personal.

It does establish a critical source-control:

> **the North-American field did not begin as an unambiguous impersonal-energy dossier. Strong personalizing/agentive readings were present in the primary ethnographic record used by later scholars.**

This matters because Lovejoy's 1906 abstraction selectively privileges one side of a contested semantic field.

Public direct text:

Project Gutenberg, `Omaha Sociology`.

## II. Fletcher 1897 — continuity, transfer and unequal potency

Primary/reprint identity:

Alice C. Fletcher, `The Import of the Totem: A Study from the Omaha Tribe`, paper read at AAAS Detroit meeting, Aug. 1897; 12-page Salem Press reprint survives in Fletcher Papers/NAA.

A contemporaneous *Popular Science Monthly* report preserves the substance of the argument.

Fletcher explains individual-totem efficacy through an Omaha belief in the **continuity of life**:

- visible is linked to invisible;
- living to dead;
- a thread of life runs through all things;
- part and whole cannot simply be dissociated;
- possession of a lock of hair can give one person power over another;
- totems communicate between person and environment;
- totems differ in potency.

Thus Fletcher already supplies a relation packet highly relevant to Lovejoy:

`LIFE CONTINUITY`
+
`PART-WHOLE CONNECTION`
+
`TRANSFER / CONTROL THROUGH CONTACT`
+
`UNEQUAL POTENCY`.

These propositions should not be counted as Lovejoy inventions merely because they are later absorbed into an energy/transfer model.

Evidence ceiling:

The 1897 *Popular Science Monthly* report is contemporaneous but is still a report of Fletcher's paper; direct diplomatic collation against the 12-page reprint remains desirable.

## III. McGee 1897 — the biggest direct upstream recut

Primary:

W. J. McGee, `The Siouan Indians: A Preliminary Sketch`, Fifteenth Annual Report of the Bureau of Ethnology, published 1897, pp.153–204.

Direct Smithsonian PDF is public domain and directly read.

### A. McGee already has a staged `mythologic philosophy`

McGee says philosophies/beliefs can be seriated into four stages:

1. hecastotheism;
2. zootheism;
3. physitheism;
4. psychotheism.

He explicitly characterizes the primitive thinker's ideation, states that early stages contain mysterious potencies everywhere, and treats stage differentiation as essential.

This means even the object address

`PRIMITIVE PHILOSOPHY / STAGED PHILOSOPHIES OF BELIEF`

has a major American ethnological precedent before Lovejoy.

### B. Early-stage potency is already everywhere

In hecastotheism McGee says every object is endowed with occult/mysterious power and some are more potent/mysterious than others.

Thus:

`DISTRIBUTED POTENCY`
+
`UNEQUAL POTENCY`

are direct upstream propositions.

But McGee's package is **not purely impersonal**: he also attributes vague volition, capricious humor and shadowy motive to the early field.

That internal mixture is historically important.

### C. McGee directly makes Wakanda a quality rather than entity

McGee says the popular `Great Spirit` interpretation was an error.

For Siouan `wakanda`, he writes that inquiry shows it is **rather a quality than a definite entity**.

The term can apply to:

- sun;
- moon;
- thunder/lightning/stars/winds;
- cedar;
- persons/shamans;
- mythic monsters;
- earth/upper/lower worlds/darkness;
- fetishes and ceremonial objects;
- animals and striking natural places.

He says `mystery` may be the least bad single English rendering, but even that is too narrow; the term also vaguely connotes:

- power;
- sacred;
- ancient;
- grandeur;
- animate;
- immortal;
- and other meanings.

This directly supplies a major part of Lovejoy's anti-`spirit` semantic correction.

### D. McGee already has internal survival/overlap geometry

McGee says the four stages are fundamentally distinct but overlap/coexist/blend.

Beliefs are largely vestigial; later stages can retain outcroppings of earlier stages.

Therefore:

`LATER PHILOSOPHICAL / BELIEF FORM`
contains
`EARLIER-STAGE VESTIGES`.

This is another direct upstream internal-stratigraphy control.

### E. McGee's field is internally unstable in exactly the useful way

Crucially, McGee's earliest power field combines:

`MYSTERIOUS POTENCY`
with
`VAGUE VOLITION / SHADOWY MOTIVE`.

And his later Wakanda discussion moves toward:

`QUALITY, NOT ENTITY`.

Thus the source itself does not hand Lovejoy one clean energy theory.

It hands him a mixed semantic/causal packet that can be reorganized.

## IV. Hewitt 1902 — potency becomes efficient causality

Primary identity:

J. N. B. Hewitt, `Orenda and a Definition of Religion`, *American Anthropologist* n.s. 4.1 (1902): 33–46.

Hewitt directly supplies:

- mystic potence;
- property of all things;
- variable intensity/quantity;
- orenda as efficient cause of phenomena.

This advances the North-American field from

`MYSTERY / QUALITY / POTENCY`

toward

`CAUSAL POTENCY`.

Lovejoy consequently cannot own the basic causation move ex nihilo.

---

## V. Jones 1905 — Algonkin manitou becomes impersonal cosmic property

Primary identity:

William Jones, `The Algonkin Manitou`, *Journal of American Folk-Lore* 18.70 (1905): 183–190.

Jones's p.190 formulation describes a cosmic/mysterious property throughout nature that can be conceived impersonally.

The same mystic virtue appears in different agencies, varying by function and degree of possession.

Thus Jones supplies:

`IMPERSONAL PROPERTY`
+
`COSMIC DISTRIBUTION`
+
`COMMON VIRTUE ACROSS OBJECTS`
+
`FUNCTION / DEGREE DIFFERENTIATION`.

This is only months before Lovejoy's July 1906 article.

## VI. Lovejoy 1906 — what remains after source ownership

Lovejoy's direct-primary article is already controlled in the repo.

Published architecture:

- North America: `manitou / oki / wakonda`;
- Oceania: `mana`;
- Bantu/African power terms;
- impersonal/quasi-mechanical efficacy;
- diffused/communicable/transferable power;
- manipulation/control/insulation;
- magic/religion boundary rejected as a causal boundary;
- category ranked `more fundamental than personality`;
- `Primitive Energetics` / `fundamental concept of primitive philosophy`.

Later Jung quotation preserves Lovejoy's p.365 synthesis as a `diffused, all-pervasive, invisible, manipulable and transferable life-energy and universal force`.

Once Dorsey/Fletcher/McGee/Hewitt/Jones are removed as component owners, the historically interesting operation becomes:

### 1. Selection

Lovejoy chooses from a field containing both personalizing and impersonalizing readings.

### 2. De-personalization / demotion

McGee's `vague volition / shadowy motive` and Dorsey's Deity-like uses do not become the architectonic center.

### 3. Cross-source recomposition

Lovejoy can combine:

- Fletcher continuity/contact transfer;
- McGee distributed quality/potency + anti-Great-Spirit translation;
- Hewitt efficient causality;
- Jones impersonal cosmic property + variable degree;
- Oceanic mana transfer;
- African lexical analogues.

No single one of these sources owns the complete Lovejoy configuration.

### 4. Diagnostic reassignment

The target is no longer principally:

- deity;
- mystery;
- awe;
- totemic connection;
- native lexical ambiguity.

It becomes:

`EFFICACY / CAUSATION`.

### 5. Ranking

The recomposed causal type is ranked above personality as more fundamental.

### 6. Architectonic reattribution

The analyst's synthetic relation becomes the `fundamental concept of primitive philosophy` and a broad claim about `savage thought`.

## VIII. Important source-critical asymmetry

The evidence chain itself shows that Lovejoy's `primitive philosophy` is a second-order construction layered over earlier second-order constructions.

The sequence is not:

`NATIVE CONCEPT`
→ `LOVEJOY DISCOVERS ITS TRUE MEANING`.

It is closer to:

`LOCAL PRACTICES / SPEECH`
→ `DORSEY / FLETCHER / McGEE / HEWITT / JONES DIFFERENT ABSTRACTIONS`
→ `LOVEJOY SELECTS / RECOMBINES / RERANKS THOSE ABSTRACTIONS`
→ `PRIMITIVE PHILOSOPHY`.

That is crucial for epistemic address.

The actor-level category is built through multiple layers of scholarly mediation.

## XI. Strongest current formulation

> **By 1906 the North-American power vocabulary had already been theorized in several incompatible directions: Dorsey preserved strongly agentive Wakanda uses; Fletcher emphasized continuity and transferable potency; McGee converted Wakanda from a definite entity into a protean quality within an evolutionary philosophy of mysterious potencies; Hewitt made orenda an efficient causal power; Jones made manitou an impersonal cosmic property varying in degree. Lovejoy's historical delta is therefore not discovery of an impersonal force. It is the selective recomposition of these mediated abstractions — together with Oceanic and African analogues — into a more internally purified causal architecture, followed by its ranking as more fundamental than personality and its reattribution as the fundamental concept of `primitive philosophy`.**
