## 1. 004 p.42 closes the viññāṇa material-form question

PDF p.42 (manuscript p.123) is stronger materially than the clean transcription alone suggested.

At the top of the right-hand page Lovejoy draws a hierarchical diagram:

`nāmarūpa → rūpam / nāma → vedanā / saññā / saṅkhārā / viññāṇam`

The diagram therefore **spatially nests viññāṇa inside nāma and nāmarūpa**. Immediately below it, in continuous prose, Lovejoy writes that viññāṇa is **temporally (?) antecedent to nāmarūpa and logically a subdivision of it**.

The important control is material rather than merely lexical:

> **the temporal and logical relations are not accidentally conflated in one sentence; Lovejoy places a nested classificatory diagram immediately above a prose statement that assigns a different temporal relation.**

No visible erasure or overwritten replacement creates the key sentence. The surviving page presents both relations together as a problem to be held simultaneously.

Adjacent PDF p.41 strengthens this reading. Its left page is itself a large diagram mapping dependent-origination / `nāmarūpa` relations against the khandhas, while the facing page defines the khandhas. Thus pp.41–42 form a short diagrammatic sequence in which Lovejoy actively maps one classificatory system onto another before stating the temporal/logical mismatch.

Safe JHI formulation:

> The notebook page makes the mismatch visible. `Viññāṇa` is drawn inside `nāmarūpa` as a classificatory part while being described below as temporally prior to it. Lovejoy is not choosing one relation and suppressing the other; he is keeping relation types separate on the page.

Do **not** inflate this into a claim that the diagram itself proves a later general method. It proves a local working practice in this notebook.
