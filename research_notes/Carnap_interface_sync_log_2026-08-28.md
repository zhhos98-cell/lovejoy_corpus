### Interpretive consequence

Do not model the 1930 Lovejoy dispute as a simple `Lovejoy versus Carnapian verificationism` opposition. Lovejoy's 1930 target is a Bridgman-style/experimental theory of meaning in which verifying operations threaten to become identical with semantic content. Carnap's 1936 formulation is more differentiated. The useful comparison is therefore operator-level and historically local, not school-label-level.
