## 2. p.50 — Robertson-Smith classificatory spine + Marillier course problem + local fourth class/rebracketing

The inserted slip is W3 for the four-class French scheme:

1. `Alimentaires et honorifiques.`
2. `Sacrifices mystiques ou sacramentaires`, with `a. nourriture du dieu` and `b. sacrifice d'union`.
3. `Sacrifices expiatoires.`
4. `Sacrifices magiques.`

### A. Robertson Smith supplies the three-class spine

W. Robertson Smith's `Sacrifice` article in the ninth edition of the *Encyclopaedia Britannica*, vol. XXI (1886), already distinguishes the principal theoretical classes later summarized as:

- honorific;
- piacular / expiatory;
- mystical or sacramental.

The article itself contrasts honorific gifts with sacrifices of special atoning or mystic significance, and Robertson Smith's later *Lectures on the Religion of the Semites* (1889; 2nd ed. 1894) develops communion/common-meal/blood-bond interpretations of sacramental sacrifice.

Therefore notebook items 1–3 are **not** an original Lovejoy fourfold invention.

### B. Marillier supplies the determinant-specific classificatory task, but not this exact fourfold list

Marillier's 1898 EPHE report for the 1898–99 course explicitly says the Tuesday conference sought to determine the reasons human victims were used in different sacrifice types, there listed as:

`expiatory / funerary / anthropophagic / magical`.

It also records parallel work on animal, agrarian, and fecundating sacrifices and names Lovejoy among auditors who took an active part.

This establishes direct institutional proximity to a **functional classification task**, but the annual-report fourfold classification is not identical with the p.50 inserted slip.

### C. Local delta

The p.50 sheet therefore currently reads best as:

`ROBERTSON-SMITH THREE-CLASS SPINE`
+
`MARILLIER FUNCTIONAL-CLASSIFICATION PROBLEM`
->
`LOCAL ADDITION / REBRACKETING: MAGICAL FOURTH CLASS + INTERNAL DIVISION OF MYSTICAL/SACRAMENTAL`.

No pre- or co-1899 exact source has yet been recovered for the complete four-line French scheme or its brace `nourriture du dieu / sacrifice d'union`.

Calibration:

- first three class families: F / A0;
- fourth class `magiques`: provisional S/A2 as a **local classificatory addition**, not a priority claim that Lovejoy invented magical sacrifice;
- internal brace under class 2: provisional S/A2 until a closer lecture/print carrier appears.

The key historical point is **redivision**, not taxonomic invention ex nihilo.

## 4. p.52 — expiation/scapegoat/purification belongs to the inherited sacrifice problem field

The p.52 heading `3. Sac. Expiation.` and current summary join collective guilt, purification, scapegoat transfer and victim removal.

Robertson Smith already separates piacular/expiatory sacrifice from honorific sacrifice, while the contemporary controversy repeatedly debates the relation among purification, propitiation and expiation. Hubert and Mauss's 1899 critique later makes precisely the complaint that Robertson Smith's `piaculum` category confounds purification, propitiation and expiation.

Marillier's EPHE sacrifice course likewise treats expiatory sacrifice as a separate functional type.

### Ownership

The existence of an expiatory/scapegoat mechanism class is field-inherited, A0. A Lovejoy-specific operation would require image evidence of a new internal distinction, causal allocation or reclassification beyond the source field. The present low-medium editorial summary does not license that upgrade.

## 5. p.53 — two inherited grammars are recombined: Frazer's impersonal magic + Marillier's magical sacrifice

The W3 notebook wording includes:

- `merely a means of magical compulsion`;
- `as it were purely mechanical`;
- charm directly controls natural phenomena;
- sometimes the god is not concerned at all;
- divine will is not the operative relation.

Earlier project language risked treating this page as an unusually early independent statement of Lovejoy's later `quasi-mechanical` primitive philosophy. Clause-level source control requires a substantial downgrade of that originality reading.

### A. Frazer 1890 owns the general magic grammar

In the first edition of *The Golden Bough*, sympathetic magic is explicitly described as operating:

- necessarily and invariably;
- without the intervention of spiritual or personal agency;
- by directly influencing the course of nature;
- through a logic analogous, though mistaken, to physical causation.

Frazer therefore supplies almost the whole **mechanical/direct-natural-causation grammar** visible on p.53.

### B. Marillier owns sacrifice-as-magical-force / coercion

Marillier's 1897–98 sacrifice theory, as summarized in the contemporary controversy and later *Encyclopaedia Britannica* account, treats sacrifice at origin as essentially magical: the shedding of the victim's blood releases a magical force used to bend gods to human will. His EPHE course explicitly separates magical sacrifice as a functional type.

### C. What remains local

The p.53 page therefore appears to perform a recombination:

`FRAZER: IMPERSONAL / MECHANICAL MAGIC`
+
`MARILLIER: SACRIFICE CAN BE MAGICAL COERCION`
->
`SACRIFICE ITSELF DESCRIBED AS CHARM-LIKE DIRECT NATURAL CONTROL, WITH GOD POSSIBLY IRRELEVANT`.

The component propositions are source-owned. The **application of Frazer's general magic grammar to Marillier's sacrificial class** may remain a local Lovejoy synthesis if no closer source is recovered.

Calibration:

- `purely mechanical` as a general analytic idea: F/A0;
- sacrifice-as-magical-compulsion: Marillier field A0;
- exact combined clause architecture on p.53: provisional S/A2, but strictly as **recomposition**, not priority.

Do not use p.53 by itself to claim that Lovejoy invented `primitive energetics` or first opposed impersonal power to divine personality.

## 7. pp.56–60 — anti-Robertson-Smith conclusion is field-inherited; Lovejoy's local delta is evidentiary redeployment across multiple mechanism classes

The batch returns explicitly to Robertson Smith and eventually says that complete religious `union` does not appear in every sacrificial form and that Robertson Smith's theory is too narrow / insufficiently elastic.

### A. The criticism was already a live field position

Marillier's 1897–98 `La place du totémisme...` directly attacks Robertson Smith's attempt to derive sacrifice from totemic communion/common meal and questions whether common sacrificial eating establishes the historical/genealogical conclusion Smith draws.

Hubert and Mauss's *Essai sur la nature et la fonction du sacrifice* (1899) then makes the objection explicit at methodological level: Robertson Smith had not established either the historical or logical connection between the common meal and other sacrifice types; his classificatory genealogy is too reductive for the complexity of sacrificial procedure.

Because Marillier's criticism clearly predates/is contemporaneous with Lovejoy's Paris work, p.59 cannot carry a priority claim for rejecting a single communion/union theory. Hubert/Mauss is a strong field control but direct uptake by Lovejoy has not been established and exact page-layer chronology must remain open.

### B. Lovejoy's possible local operation

The notebook's distinctive value lies not in first saying `Robertson Smith is too narrow`, but in the way pp.50–58 assemble a dossier of divergent mechanisms before reaching that conclusion:

- honorific/alimentary;
- mystical/sacramental;
- expiatory;
- magical;
- vitality/god-man transfer;
- funerary;
- fecundative;
- cannibalistic/alimentary;
- communion/union.

Thus the p.59–60 move is best described as:

`FIELD-SHARED ANTI-SINGLE-ORIGIN OBJECTION`
+
`LOVEJOY NOTEBOOK'S CASE-BY-CASE FUNCTIONAL REPARTITION`
->
`ROBERTSON-SMITH UNION MODEL RESTRICTED IN SCOPE`.

Calibration:

- anti-Robertson-Smith proposition: F/A0–A1;
- use of the locally assembled mechanism inventory to restrict that proposition across cases: provisional A2, contingent on the page sequence/hand rather than a claim of theoretical priority.

A more accurate map is:

`ROBERTSON SMITH`
- honorific / piacular / mystical-sacramental classification;
- common meal / blood bond / communion;

`FRAZER`
- god-man vitality transfer to vigorous successor;
- sympathetic magic as impersonal/direct/mechanical natural causation;

`MARILLIER`
- functional determination of multiple sacrifice types;
- magical sacrifice / magical force;
- explicit criticism of a universal Robertson-Smith communion genealogy;

`CAMERON / ETHNOGRAPHIC SOURCES`
- concrete Manyéma and other case materials;

`LOVEJOY NOTEBOOK`
- ranks sources by evidential discipline (p.49);
- modifies/rebrackets an inherited sacrifice taxonomy, notably adding `magiques` to the Robertson-Smith three-class spine (p.50);
- recombines Frazer's mechanical magic grammar with Marillier's sacrificial mechanism (p.53);
- locally reclassifies a source-owned cannibalism case as `not ritual but alimentary` (p.55);
- uses a multi-mechanism dossier to restrict the scope of Robertson Smith's union theory (pp.59–60).

The strongest archival formulation is therefore:

> **Notebook 005 does not document Lovejoy inventing mechanism-specific analysis of sacrifice. It documents him working inside a field already divided among competing functional explanations and repeatedly reclassifying inherited cases or redistributing those explanations across a comparative dossier.**

This is fully compatible with the existing EPHE/Marillier formation correction and with notebook 004's field-method / actor-operation distinction.

## 9. Relation to 1906 — revised ceiling

The 1906 `The Fundamental Concept of the Primitive Philosophy` later makes impersonal, transferable, quasi-mechanical efficacy a broad comparative category.

Round 23 requires a tighter manuscript-to-print statement:

- p.53 alone does not prove the invention of that category because Frazer and Marillier already supply its main component grammars;
- the meaningful continuity lies in **reaggregation**: notebook 005 repeatedly separates sacrificial efficacy from personal divine will, alimentary use, expiation, communion, and other mechanisms, while 1906 gathers impersonal efficacy into a broader comparative abstraction;
- any claim of direct causal derivation from Marillier or Frazer remains unlicensed without version-level evidence.

Safe formulation:

> **Paris supplied a source field in which magical, sacramental, alimentary, expiatory and other mechanisms were already under active dispute. The notebook shows Lovejoy locally reassigning those mechanisms; the 1906 article later generalizes one of the available relation-types into a much wider category.**
