## New control

Mauss cites Steinmetz, Endokannibalismus, p.52 §26 directly in the discussion of endocannibalism. The citation appears at the point where Mauss discusses the belief that relatives consuming the dead absorb the virtues of the deceased and where he questions the interpretation of such practices.

## Significance

The p.52 §26 reference fixes one specific location where Steinmetz's argument entered the French comparative-religion debate. The issue was not merely the existence of endocannibalism as a named category, but the explanatory mechanism attached to it.

## Revised matrix

Steinmetz: endocannibalism as broad comparative category, with a strong historical reconstruction.

Mauss: accepts the empirical problem but separates clan solidarity, funerary practice, magical representation and communion.

Lovejoy 005: retains transfer mechanisms as comparative objects but separates sympathetic efficacy from sacramental union.
