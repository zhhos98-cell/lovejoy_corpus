## New direct lock

R. N. Berki and Bhiku Parekh, `On Quentin Skinner's “Some Problems in the Analysis of Political Thought and Action”`, *Political Theory* 4.2 (May 1976), p.235, is now directly machine-readable in full through vLex.

They explicitly distinguish:

- a historico-biographical question about Skinner's intellectual development/current position, which would require a broader oeuvre;
- a philosophical question about the adequacy of a specific methodological approach, for which they deliberately selected Skinner's 1969 article as a representative articulation.

Therefore:

`UNIT ADEQUACY = QUESTION + CLAIM + PROOF BURDEN`.

And:

`ARTICLE REPRESENTATIVE OF METHOD POSITION`
!=
`ARTICLE EXHAUSTIVE OF ACTOR DEVELOPMENT / CURRENT POSITION`.

This is a direct contemporary statement of the project's rule:

`UNIT TYPE BELONGS TO CLAIM, NOT ONCE-FOR-ALL TO PHYSICAL OBJECT`.
