Date: 2026-09-03

## Core result

Konrad Theodor Preuss's serialized `Der Ursprung der Religion und Kunst`, published in *Globus* 86 (1904) and 87 (1905), materially narrows what may be treated as Lovejoy's 1906 theoretical priority.

Before Lovejoy's July 1906 `The Fundamental Concept of the Primitive Philosophy`, Preuss had already developed a dynamistic/preanimistic account in which impersonal magical power precedes or underlies soul/spirit/personality constructions and in which soul/spirit can be treated as transformations of an earlier power-field.

Therefore the Lovejoy-specific residual should no longer include, without qualification:

- priority for impersonal power over personality;
- priority for a preanimistic/dynamistic stage;
- priority for treating `orenda`-type potency as distinct from soul/spirit;
- priority for broadly mechanistic/automatic efficacy in early religion/magic.

The strongest surviving Lovejoy residual is now narrower:

> **evidence architecture + claim-resolution hierarchy + semantic/grammatical control + cross-boundary reassignment + configurational ranking / reaggregation.**

In particular, Lovejoy's 1906 paper regrades witnesses, restricts what older missionary/observer evidence can establish, recruits newer professional linguistic/grammatical controls, carries quasi-mechanical efficacy across the magic/religion boundary, and reaggregates the surviving relation as `Primitive Energetics` / the `fundamental concept of primitive philosophy`.

This is a configuration/evidentiary-resolution claim, not a broad theory-priority claim.

## II. Contemporary reception: Preuss was already read as a major new origin theory

A 1906 review in *Folk-Lore* calls Preuss's communication of the `highest interest and importance` and characterizes it as an `almost wholly novel view` of cultural and especially religious origins.

The review summarizes Preuss as making magic the root of religion, art, games, speech, clothing, agriculture, marriage and other institutions.

It says one of the two general features of magic in Preuss is the exertion of a kind of **power / orenda**.

This is useful as a period control because it shows that immediately before/around Lovejoy's paper, Preuss was publicly visible as a theorist of a power-centered cultural/religious origin, not merely a later retrospective precursor.

Public period control:
- *Folk-Lore* 17 (1906), review of Preuss, p.126ff.

## III. Preuss explicitly separates orenda from soul/spirit

Later scholarship directly quotes Preuss, *Globus* 86, p.321:

> `Das der Irokesen 'orenda' hat mit Seele, Geist, Leben, Verstand, Gehirn oder mit physischer Kraft, Macht u. dgl. nichts zu tun.`

The exact quotation is preserved in later critical discussion of Preuss's theory.

Evidence grade:

`EXACT PREUSS WORDING THROUGH IDENTIFIED SECONDARY RELAY / PRIMARY LOCATOR p.321`.

Do not silently upgrade this to a fresh facsimile transcription until the *Globus* page itself is retrieved.

The proposition nevertheless has a highly specific primary locator and is consistent with the contemporary reception field.

## IV. Preuss also advances the chronological/transformational priority of impersonal force

Durkheim's early retrospective account of the preanimist/dynamist literature explicitly summarizes Preuss's 1904–05 series as arguing that:

- ideas of soul and spirit developed **after** ideas of power / impersonal force;
- soul/spirit are transformations of the earlier force;
- they retain marks of original impersonality far into later religious forms;
- these forces appear as automatic effluvia / emanations escaping from bodies and things;
- their plasticity allows them to perform many functions.

Durkheim notes that Preuss calls them `Zauber / Zauberkräfte`, but interprets the mechanism as operating inside religious rites as well.

This is not yet a substitute for direct Preuss page collation, but it is a precise early source map tied by Durkheim to exact pages in the 1904 and 1905 series.

The historical implication is unavoidable at the level of priority control:

> **the broad claim `impersonal power is earlier/more fundamental than soul/personality` was already in the field before Lovejoy 1906.**

## V. Relation to Hewitt / Hubert–Mauss / Marett

Preuss does not stand alone.

By 1906 the field already contains:

- Marett 1900: preanimistic / non-animistic problem field;
- Hewitt 1902: `orenda` as mystic potency / efficient cause and a major category in Iroquoian thought;
- Hubert–Mauss 1902–03/1904 publication: mana/orenda/brahman and related power notions correlated as a general magical-power category;
- Preuss 1904–05: power/Zauberkraft made prior to soul/spirit and central to a broad genesis of religion/culture.

Thus even Lovejoy's 1906 ranking of impersonal efficacy as `more fundamental than personality` must be treated as participation/reconfiguration inside a rapidly consolidating dynamist/preanimist problem field.

`LOVEJOY MORE FUNDAMENTAL THAN PERSONALITY`
!=
`LOVEJOY FIRST TO MAKE POWER PRIOR TO PERSONALITY`.

## VI. Lovejoy 1914 actor priority claim now has an even lower ceiling

Lovejoy's 1914 review of Leuba says:

- Marett 1900 gave the first important expression of the general non-animistic tendency;
- ethnographic evidence was independently forcing the same general view on many scholars;
- nevertheless Lovejoy's own 1906 paper was the `original formulation` of the more specific hypothesis about the origin/content of impersonal power and of its principal evidence.

The Preuss control makes the second ownership claim difficult to accept at broad theoretical resolution.

A source-critical decomposition now gives:

### Not safely Lovejoy-priority
- nonanimist/dynamist turn;
- impersonal power itself;
- power vocabulary;
- cross-cultural power comparison;
- causal potency;
- power as prior to / more fundamental than soul or personality;
- broad automatic/mechanistic efficacy.

### Still potentially Lovejoy-distinctive
- precise semantic recoding toward `efficacy / productivity / quasi-mechanical causation`;
- rejection of magic/religion jurisdiction as the boundary for that mechanism;
- source-by-source limitation of personifying reporter glosses;
- use of newer professional grammar/native-use evidence to raise semantic resolution;
- asymmetric regional evidence architecture;
- final reaggregation as `Primitive Energetics` / architectonic `primitive philosophy`.

Thus:

`ACTOR CLAIM: ORIGINAL FORMULATION`

must be recoded as a historical fact about Lovejoy's self-positioning, not accepted as an unqualified source-critical priority verdict.

## VII. The strongest surviving `principal evidence` residual

Brinton 1897 already had `wakan / huaca-wakan / mana` as linguistic evidence.

Lovejoy's evidence architecture nevertheless differs materially.

Current direct-primary matrix shows:

1. older missionary / traveler witnesses are retained only for concrete lexical/custom applications;
2. their theological/personifying glosses are frequently rejected;
3. older evidence is explicitly prevented from carrying the universal abstract-property claim;
4. **William Jones 1905** enters exactly where the older North-American packet reaches its semantic ceiling;
5. Jones supplies Algonkin grammatical/gender evidence distinguishing `manitou` as property/abstraction from object-personification;
6. **Alexander Hetherwick 1902** supplies a parallel Yao noun-class control for `mulungu`, showing that the form need not denote personality and can designate an inhering state/property;
7. Lovejoy preserves adverse evidence rather than forcing every regional witness to identical resolution;
8. the p.376–77 synthesis explicitly builds a minimal cross-regional core while admitting that richer mechanism claims are best evidenced in some regions and extrapolated probabilistically to others.

Authority:
`research_notes/Lovejoy1906_cross_regional_evidence_resolution_matrix_batch78.csv`.

Therefore a defensible reconstruction of Lovejoy's 1914 phrase `principal evidence` is:

> **not first use of native power terms, but a more differentiated hierarchy of evidence in which recent specialist linguistic/grammatical controls are used to adjudicate older observer translations and to license stronger semantic/causal claims.**

This is a narrower, technically meaningful originality residual.
