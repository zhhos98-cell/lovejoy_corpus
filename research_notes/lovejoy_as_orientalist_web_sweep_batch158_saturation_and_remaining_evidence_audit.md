Date: 2026-08-19

## Core result

The project is now **substantively saturated at the level of its central documentary arguments**, but it is not literally exhausted.

The distinction is important:

- the main 004 argument can already be drafted from primary-secure manuscript/article/source comparisons;
- the main 005 argument can already be drafted from the corrected notebook, Marillier/EPHE controls, the seminar-production ecology, and the 1906 primary text;
- the 004→005→1906 methodological bridge now has a stable analytical form through Batches 153–157;
- the three manual items currently being retrieved are best classified as **high-value falsification / delta-compression controls**, not prerequisites for having an argument at all.

Accordingly, broad undirected web harvesting now has sharply diminishing returns. The remaining work falls into four classes:

1. **Class A — decisive falsification controls**: could materially narrow or revise an existing claim;
2. **Class B — documentary transmission upgrades**: could strengthen causal/provenance claims without changing the core analytical result;
3. **Class C — extension tests**: could show whether the same proof discipline persists in later domains;
4. **Class D — citation/facsimile hygiene and contextual refinement**: useful for publication but unlikely to alter the argument.

The project should continue, but in a **targeted closing mode**, not an open-ended discovery mode.

## 4. Class C — extension tests still worth running

These are the main reasons the project is not literally `finished` after the three manual items.

### C1. Lovejoy 1908 vs W. B. Smith 1909

This remains the cleanest unclosed methodological extension.

Known route/state:

- Lovejoy, `The Theory of a Pre-Christian Cult of Jesus`, _Monist_ 18 (1908), 597–609;
- Smith reply, _Monist_ 19 (1909), 409–420;
- both volumes are public-domain/digitally preserved;
- present repo statement about Lovejoy rejecting Smith's grand syncretic genealogy still rests on Wilson's secondary synopsis rather than a proposition-level primary collation.

Why it is worth doing:

- it tests whether the two-gate / anti-shortcut genealogy discipline persists in a new historical-religion domain;
- unlike 004 and 005, it supplies a direct adversarial reply, allowing a claim/evidence/objection/rejoinder matrix;
- it could show whether Lovejoy's standards change when dealing with Christianity rather than Indian philosophy or ethnographic ritual.

Status: **extension test, not core blocker**.

Only two pages and potentially useful for showing continued specialist Indic engagement after the 1901 Buddhism syllabus.

Could clarify whether Lovejoy was still thinking as a teacher/reviewer inside Hindu-philosophy scholarship while the comparative-religion line was developing.

Status: useful continuity control, low cost once text is accessible.

### C3. 1907 Jewish/religion articles against the 1905 `Hist. Relig.` layer

Especially `The Origins of Ethical Inwardness in Jewish Thought`.

Could test whether the Hebrew eschatology / `nephesh` / `ruach` material in 005 feeds into later publication in the same way the Buddhist and primitive-religion blocks do.

This would provide a second manuscript→publication microgenesis inside 005, but the main paper does not require it.

### C4. 1905 Washington University context

Identify the exact institutional meaning of `Hist. Relig. — Dec. 20, 1905` through catalogues, Association programmes, bulletins, or local notices.

This could turn a secure internal manuscript date into a secure teaching/public-lecture event.

## 7. What `saturation` means here

The corpus has reached a point where new material is increasingly likely to do one of four things:

1. narrow attribution of an operator already identified;
2. improve chronology;
3. strengthen documentary transmission;
4. extend the operator into another domain.

It is now much less likely that an ordinary additional source will create an entirely new central thesis.

This is visible in the recent batch sequence:

- Batch153 narrowed novelty from generic family resemblance to diagnostic specificity;
- Batch154 separated diagnostic insufficiency from genealogical redundancy;
- Batch155 showed evidentiary compression from manuscript to publication;
- Batch156 shifted the classification target from system to mechanism to causal category;
- Batch157 distinguished mechanism universalism from genealogical monism and located the remaining tension at evidentiary scope.

These are increasingly **second-order refinements of a stable architecture**, which is a classic saturation signal.
