# Lovejoy 1896–1907 — Hebrew/Wisdom corpus reactivation and question shift

Date: 2026-09-03  
Status: **NEW LONGITUDINAL ACTOR-LEVEL CORPUS REACTIVATION CONTROL**

## Core result

The newly received MS-0873 France 1898–99 transcription packet extends the existing 1905→1907 Hebrew-corpus line backward to Lovejoy's Paris year and, through the Harvard record card, to formal training in 1896–97.

The defensible pattern is not nine years of continuous work on one project. It is:

`FORMAL HEBREW-RELIGION TRAINING`
→ `1898 WISDOM / PROBLEM-OF-EVIL PROJECT`
→ `[INTERVENING CONTINUITY NOT DEMONSTRATED]`
→ `1905 HEBREW ESCHATOLOGY / SOUL-SEMANTICS REACTIVATION`
→ `1907 ETHICAL-INWARDNESS REPARTITION`.

The same or overlapping textual corpus is repeatedly rebuilt around different questions.

Strong rule:

> **CORPUS REACTIVATION != PROJECT CONTINUITY != UNCHANGED HISTORICAL OBJECT.**

## I. 1896–97 — Toy: direct formal enrollment

Lovejoy's Harvard graduate record card records:

- `Semitic 13`, grade A.

The same-year course announcement identifies Semitic 13 as Crawford H. Toy:

`History of the Hebrew Religion, with comparison of other Semitic religions`.

Thus the Harvard/Toy relation is direct actor-level enrollment evidence, not merely Wilson-mediated context.

Authority:

`research_notes/Harvard_Lovejoy_student_record_card_direct_transcription_and_Pali5_closure_2026-08-31.md`.

What this licenses:

- formal historical/comparative Hebrew-religion training before Paris;
- direct institutional continuity into the later Toy authority relation.

What it does not license:

- Toy assigned a later Paris Wisdom project;
- all later Hebrew/Wisdom work is derivative of Toy;
- one stable method was transmitted intact.

## II. Oct–Dec 1898 — Paris: Wisdom becomes a named live project

MS-0873 Wilson transcription witness supplies the first direct actor-level statement of the Paris Wisdom line.

### 20 Oct 1898

Lovejoy is discussing biblical Wisdom literature and then pairs it with his proposed Buddhist project.

The crucial bridge is the **problem of evil**:

- Buddhism ultimately confronts the problem of evil;
- the biblical Wisdom literature does likewise;
- Ecclesiastes can appear strikingly Buddhist/pessimistic in this respect.

Lovejoy simultaneously marks an institutional boundary: his fellowship requires his principal work to remain within what is ordinarily understood as philosophy, so he cannot spend too much time on work that `savors of Comp Religion`.

This is unusually strong actor-level evidence because it shows three things at once:

1. textual objects can be compared through a shared philosophical problem;
2. comparative-religion work and philosophy are analytically adjacent but institutionally non-identical;
3. Lovejoy is actively allocating effort across the boundary rather than simply occupying a fixed disciplinary box.

### 1/5 Dec 1898

The Paris letter makes `Wisdom work` concrete.

It records:

- Israel Lévi / a Hebrew Ecclesiastes textual fragment in the BnF context;
- Maurice Vernes on Psalms, chosen explicitly to put Lovejoy `en rapport` for his `Wisdom work`;
- residual Hebrew competence becoming useful;
- authorization to use the Faculty of Theology library because its Wisdom-book commentaries are better suited to his purpose than what he had at the BnF;
- Jean Réville's Fourth Gospel as an adjacent `parergon`;
- Sabatier on Acts tried and then dropped because it presupposed too much patristic/extracanonical knowledge and lay outside the year's line of work.

This is not merely a course list. It is evidence of **research selection and infrastructure use**:

`PROJECT QUESTION`
→ `COURSE CHOICE`
→ `LANGUAGE RESOURCE`
→ `LIBRARY RESOURCE`
→ `ADJACENT LINE ACCEPTED / REJECTED`.

## III. 1898 object: Wisdom through evil / pessimism / comparative philosophical problem

The 1898 Wisdom object is best described as:

`BIBLICAL WISDOM / ECCLESIASTES`
+
`PROBLEM OF EVIL / PESSIMISM`
+
`COMPARABILITY WITH BUDDHISM`
+
`PHILOSOPHICAL QUESTION UNDER A FELLOWSHIP BOUNDARY`.

This is not yet the 1907 `ethical inwardness` object.

It is also not identical with the 1905 Hebrew-escha­tology object.

## IV. 1905 — notebook 005: same broad corpus, different relation

The directly dated `Hist. Relig. — Dec. 20, 1905` block in notebook 005 organizes Hebrew materials around:

- cognition/condition of the dead;
- Sheol;
- whether the dead remain within Yahweh's jurisdiction;
- `nephesh / ruach`;
- blood / breath / life;
- comparative soul conceptions;
- Old Testament inside a broader comparative eschatology teaching sequence.

Overlapping textual field includes Job and Ecclesiastes.

The operative question has changed from:

`HOW DO BUDDHISM AND WISDOM ADDRESS EVIL?`

to:

`WHAT IS THE CONDITION / SOUL-CONCEPTION / ESCHATOLOGICAL STATUS OF THE DEAD?`

## V. 1907 — Ethical Inwardness: corpus repartitioned again

Lovejoy's `The Origins of Ethical Inwardness in Jewish Thought` (AJT 11.2, April 1907, 228–249) works through:

- Job;
- Psalms;
- Proverbs;
- Ben Sira;
- Ecclesiastes;
- Wisdom of Solomon.

The question is now:

`HOW / IN WHAT LOGICAL STAGES DOES ETHICAL VALUE MOVE TOWARD INWARD VIRTUE / INTEGRITY OF HEART?`

Lovejoy explicitly defers to Toy on the relative chronology of the Wisdom texts while treating the logical-developmental sequence as a distinct analytical question.

Thus the 1907 evidence architecture is itself split:

`TOY -> RELATIVE TEXTUAL CHRONOLOGY`

while

`LOVEJOY -> LOGICAL DEVELOPMENT / STAGING OF ETHICAL CONCEPTION`.

This makes the 1896–97 Toy enrollment historically more concrete without reducing the 1907 argument to teacher transmission.

## VII. Strongest historical inference

The new letters allow a more powerful statement than the earlier 1905→1907 comparison:

> **Lovejoy repeatedly returned to overlapping Hebrew/Wisdom materials while changing the relation he wanted those texts to establish. The stable carrier was partly the corpus; the historical object was rebuilt around different questions.**

This is a concrete early case of:

`SAME / OVERLAPPING CORPUS`
+
`QUESTION SHIFT`
+
`AUTHORITY REALLOCATION`
+
`NEW HISTORICAL OBJECT`.

It is therefore highly relevant to the repo's unit/relation architecture.
