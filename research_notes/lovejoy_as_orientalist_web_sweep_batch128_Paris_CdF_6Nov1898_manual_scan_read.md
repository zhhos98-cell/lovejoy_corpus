## Page structure

### p.113

The upper part contains a ministerial decree dated 23 May 1898 concerning Gaston Maspero and is not part of the 6 November meeting.

At the bottom begins:

`Procès-verbal de l'Assemblée tenue par MM. les Professeurs du Collège de France le Dimanche 6 Novembre 1898...`

followed by the attendance list.

No Lévi name is visible in the attendance list as captured. This should be treated only as a meeting-attendance negative, not as evidence of absence from Paris or from teaching.

### p.114

The minutes record opening of the session, approval of the previous minutes, election/re-election of a secretary for the academic year, then the agenda item concerning substitutes/replacements.

The page explicitly introduces a list under wording equivalent to:

`L'ordre du jour appelle la présentation de suppléants et de remplaçants.`

and then:

`Sont proposés comme suppléants:`

A series of pairings follows (e.g. Marcel Dupuy for Joseph Bertrand and other professorial substitutions). The page then records that the presentations receive the Assembly's approval and are to be submitted for ministerial approval.

**Critical negative:** no Lévi/Foucher pair occurs in the visible list.

The lower part moves to an unrelated prize/commission matter.

### p.115

The Administrator reports the death of Mlle Pocot and discusses the Collège de France's rights under her earlier donation and testament. The minutes note her wish that part of the endowment support a young French or foreign scholar giving temporary courses at the Collège on new subjects and presenting personal research. A commission is elected to handle the new foundation.

The meeting then discusses the 1900 Exposition and the possibility for professors to present scientific work or laboratory instruments.

The minutes end with the session being closed at approximately 2:45.

No Lévi/Foucher/mission/Sanskrit material appears on this page.

### p.116

This page begins:

`Paris le 6 Novembre 1898 — Compte annuel de MM. les Professeurs au 1er Novembre 1898`

and is a financial account. The lower part starts the next assembly, dated 8 January 1899.

Therefore p.116 should not be cited as substantive continuation of the 6 November minutes.
