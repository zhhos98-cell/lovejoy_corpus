## 3. What this changes in the `RHR as switchboard` model

Batch 58 called RHR 37 a transatlantic `switchboard` because the same volume contained:

- Marillier's totemism work;
- a review of Rhys Davids, a direct 004 source;
- a review of Trumbull, adjacent to the 005 blood/covenant source field;
- Buddhist/Indian material such as the Mahâbhârata-in-Buddhist-literature item.

The issue topology now makes the claim more precise.

The relevant materials were not simply scattered through one annual bound volume. They appear in **issue-specific review ecologies adjacent to Marillier's continuing intervention**. This is stronger evidence for the editorial/intellectual co-presence of:

- Buddhist/Indian textual history;
- comparative religion and totemism;
- Anglophone ritual/covenant literature;
- Paris `sciences religieuses` review culture.

Still, this does **not** establish that Lovejoy read either review or obtained Rhys Davids/Trumbull through RHR.

Use as:

> `Paris reception ecology / disciplinary information architecture`.

Do not use as:

> `source-transmission route to Lovejoy`.

## 5. Why the Steinmetz review remains highest-value despite access closure

The substantive discriminating question is unchanged.

Lovejoy 005 explicitly notes `Endokannibalismus — Steinmetz`. Mauss 1896 later narrows the relevant practice to ritual anthropophagy of relatives and classifies transfer of the dead person's virtues to survivors as a kind of communion. Lovejoy's later 005 treatment separates analogous transfer-of-quality cases from automatic `communion`, allowing sympathetic or transferable efficacy as different causal mechanisms.

The Marillier review now needs only to answer:

> **How did Marillier himself classify Steinmetz's kin/endocannibalistic mechanism in 1896 — communion, magic, funerary solidarity, social mechanism, or plural/unresolved causes?**

If Marillier already refuses communion as a universal class, the Marillier→005 methodological bridge strengthens sharply.

If Marillier accepts a communion classification, Lovejoy's later divergence becomes more interesting.

If the review is primarily descriptive and does not decide the mechanism, that negative result is still useful: the stronger reclassification may belong to seminar work or Lovejoy's own downstream analysis rather than the printed notice.
