# Paper architecture — MS38_004_001_061_005 as a documentary microhistory of comparative-religion method

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: working publication architecture

## Core decision

The paper can be built around **one Lovejoy notebook, 005**, rather than requiring a survey of the remaining Lovejoy notebooks. Other Lovejoy manuscripts may later serve as controls, but they are not necessary to establish the main historical mechanism.

The evidentiary chain is already unusually complete:

1. **institutional programme** — Marillier’s EPHE annual reports specify the problems, controversy, regions, and participant status;
2. **teacher’s published argument** — Marillier, *Survivance* (1893/94);
3. **teacher’s surviving/attributed research dossiers** — RAI MS 99;
4. **independent eyewitness to pedagogy** — N. W. Thomas on student essays, teacher comments and peer discussion;
5. **independent evidence for household paper-work** — Tarbell / Jeanne Marillier;
6. **same-seminar student product** — Söderblom’s diploma / *Les Fravashis* / *La vie future*;
7. **direct proof of teacher-note circulation** — Söderblom’s statement that Marillier gave/loaned his notes, with `M.`-marked extracts;
8. **live controversy** — Steinmetz → Mauss → Marillier’s explicit 1898–99 refutation programme;
9. **Lovejoy’s material trace** — notebook 005, with dates, inserted slips, source clusters, classificatory work and later reuse;
10. **endpoint transformation** — Lovejoy’s 1906 `Primitive Philosophy`, which abstracts a causal category from the comparative field.

This is enough to write a documentary history of **how an analytical practice was produced**, without first reconstructing Lovejoy’s whole intellectual development.

## Possible titles

### Most precise
**From Dossier to Determinant: Arthur Lovejoy, Léon Marillier, and the Making of Comparative Method at the École Pratique des Hautes Études**

### More material
**A Notebook in Motion: Arthur Lovejoy and Léon Marillier’s Comparative-Religion Workshop**

### More history-of-ideas facing
**Before the Unit-Idea: Arthur Lovejoy’s Paris Notebook and the Practice of Decomposition**

The third title is rhetorically strong but needs caution because the paper should not imply that the mature unit-idea method was already present in 1898–99.

## One-sentence argument

**Lovejoy’s Paris notebook 005 records not the passive reception of a comparative-religion doctrine but participation in a paper-based research workshop in which ethnographic reports were criticized, circulated, reclassified and contested; by comparing Lovejoy with Marillier’s programme, Söderblom’s independently documented use of Marillier’s dossiers, and the Steinmetz controversy, we can see Lovejoy learning to decompose apparently similar beliefs and rites by the mechanism that produced them — an analytical operation later abstracted in his 1906 account of “primitive” causal efficacy.**

## Stronger historiographical version

The paper is not fundamentally about whether Lovejoy `was an Orientalist` or whether Marillier `influenced` him. Those formulations are useful entry points but too static.

The historical object is **research production**:

> documents → dossiers → seminar problems → competing classifications → student redivision → later abstraction.

The key finding is that the transmission was not primarily transmission of conclusions. It was transmission of **a field of documents and a practice for criticizing and sorting them**.

## Section architecture

### 1. The object: one notebook that refuses to be one thing

Open with 005 as material object:

- Paris stationer’s leaf, 162 rue Saint-Jacques;
- faint `Symbolism [?]` title;
- bibliography + dated notes + inserted slips;
- `Dec. 14`, `May 29`, `Marillier — Survivance — 12 June`;
- later `Hist. Relig. — Dec. 20, 1905` reuse;
- loose/inset paper structure in later pages.

Argument: page order is not composition chronology; the notebook is a **longitudinal working object**. This immediately blocks a simplistic `lecture notebook` genre assignment.

### 2. What the seminar was doing, not merely what it was “about”

Use the official 1898–99 report:

- Monday: survivance, amoral eschatology, explicit refutation of Steinmetz, new American facts;
- Tuesday: sacrifice / ritual anthropophagy classified by reasons/mechanisms;
- Lovejoy = active auditor, not merely registered listener.

Then N. W. Thomas:

- work by pupils directed by lecturer;
- essay / oral presentation / Marillier comment / peer discussion.

This establishes a production setting before interpreting any notebook sentence.

### 3. The teacher’s documents moved

This is the material center of the paper.

Combine:

- RAI MS 99 thematic dossiers;
- Tarbell on Marillier’s papers and Jeanne’s documentary labour;
- Söderblom preface + p.191: Marillier gave/loaned his own notes; `M.` marks extracts from them.

Argument: **dossier circulation is directly documented**, not inferred from intellectual resemblance.

Important limit: we can prove the practice for Söderblom; identity of sheets used by Lovejoy awaits MS 99 collation.

### 4. Two students, one dossier field, different divisions

Compare Söderblom and Lovejoy.

Shared clusters:

- Murray / Pawnee;
- Greenland/Eskimo two abodes;
- Cheyenne/status;
- Madagascar / Abinal & Vaissière / Hova / second death.

Söderblom explicitly says he takes Marillier’s problem but uses `different divisions` and a `somewhat different point of view`.

Analytical contrast:

- Söderblom: morphology of survival / abodes / classes of dead / transition to retribution;
- Lovejoy: determinant/mechanism — status, rite, social membership, manner of death, moral desert.

This section demonstrates **reclassification rather than copying**.

### 5. A controversy teaches source criticism

Reconstruct Steinmetz → Mauss → Marillier 1898–99.

Mauss’s key methodological move:

- raw ethnographic statements are insufficient;
- Christian / Hindu / Buddhist contact must be separated;
- ritual performance and manner of death can produce differential destinies without moral justice.

005 pp.16–30:

- missionary contamination;
- diffusion / Hindu influence;
- suicide and clan solidarity;
- Murray chronology warning;
- two destinations ≠ retribution.

Argument: Lovejoy is learning to ask **what produced the reported belief and what produced the observed distinction**. Source criticism and causal classification are continuous operations here.

### 6. From future-life determinants to sacrificial mechanisms

Move into 005 pp.46–119 without narrating every example.

Select only the passages that show the same operation generalizing:

- sacrifice directed to personal god vs direct action on nature;
- funerary provision vs communion;
- blood as life/transferable power;
- fetish / indwelling spirit vs charm / intrinsic efficacy;
- agrarian/fecundative life transfer;
- cannibalism classified by motive/mechanism;
- circumcision rejected as partial sacrifice;
- p.105 `quasi-mechanical and magical rather than sacramental`;
- p.117 sacramental union exceptional.

Argument: the notebook changes topic but preserves the analytical operation: **outwardly similar rites must be decomposed by causal mechanism and historical layering**.

### 7. 1906 as abstraction, not origin

Use `The Fundamental Concept of the Primitive Philosophy` as endpoint, not as the paper’s organizing text.

Show continuity:

- impersonal efficacy;
- transferability;
- contact;
- concentration / diffusion;
- quasi-mechanical action;
- resistance to animist/personality master theory.

But distinguish levels:

- 005 = empirical/classificatory laboratory;
- 1906 = abstraction/systematization of a cross-cutting category.

This prevents a teleological story in which every 1898 note secretly anticipates the 1906 article.

### 8. Conclusion: from intellectual influence to documentary transmission

Return to the historiographical payoff.

We can observe:

- a teacher’s problem;
- a teacher’s dossiers;
- documented movement of notes to one student;
- the same source field in another student notebook;
- divergent reclassification;
- later abstraction by Lovejoy.

Therefore the unit of intellectual history here is neither a canonical book nor an isolated `idea`. It is a **research operation carried by documents across seminar, household, notebook and print**.

## What the paper should avoid

1. **Do not make the paper a biography of Lovejoy.** Only enough Harvard/Paris chronology to locate the object.
2. **Do not make “Orientalism” the thesis.** It is context; the stronger object is comparative-religion research practice.
3. **Do not claim 005 is a lecture transcript.** Evidence supports an active, heterogeneous research notebook.
4. **Do not claim Lovejoy used the identical MS 99 sheets until bibliography/order or handwriting proves it.**
5. **Do not call Moon/Conard a Marillier student yet.** Use her as an American-input/control node.
6. **Do not project `unit-ideas` backward.** Use vocabulary such as decomposition, determinant, causal mechanism, historical layering, source criticism.
7. **Do not over-narrate ethnographic content.** Cases matter when they show documentary transmission or classificatory difference.
8. **Do not require the other Lovejoy notebooks for the main claim.** They can later test generalization, but the paper’s evidentiary architecture already closes around 005.

## Claims ranked by evidentiary strength

### A — directly demonstrable now

- Lovejoy actively participated in Marillier’s 1898–99 conferences.
- 005 contains explicit Marillier/survivance/date material and matches both conference problem blocks.
- Marillier’s seminar was interactive/student-work based.
- Marillier possessed thematic research dossiers.
- Marillier directly circulated his notes to Söderblom.
- Söderblom and Lovejoy share multiple source/case clusters yet classify them differently.
- Marillier’s 1898–99 survivance seminar explicitly resumed the Steinmetz controversy.
- 005 contains the same source-critical distinctions central to that controversy.
- 005 was reused in 1905.
- the 1906 paper systematizes causal-efficacy problems directly present in 005.

### B — strong inference suitable with careful wording

- 005 was produced partly through the seminar’s research workflow rather than solely from private reading.
- Lovejoy likely had access to a shared Marillier source apparatus, even if the physical sheets are not yet identified.
- the seminar controversy helped train Lovejoy’s mechanism-sensitive source criticism.
- 005 is an empirical/classificatory laboratory for the 1906 abstraction.

### C — hold until more evidence

- Lovejoy physically borrowed the same Marillier dossier sheets as Söderblom.
- Moon/Conard directly supplied the 1898–99 seminar’s new American facts.
- a particular 005 passage is a transcription of Marillier’s spoken words.
- the 1905 `Hist. Relig.` heading is a specific Washington University Association lecture.

## Highest-value remaining evidence

Only a small number of discoveries could substantially upgrade the paper now:

1. **RAI MS 99 images** showing distinctive bibliography/order/page references matching 005.
2. **EPHE or personal-paper evidence of Lovejoy receiving/presenting a seminar assignment.**
3. **A second student notebook** from Söderblom, Travers, Schaefer, Andrews or Lorette.
4. **Moon/Conard correspondence/enrollment** proving direct Marillier seminar/research exchange before 1898–99.
5. **Washington University evidence** identifying the 20 Dec. 1905 notebook reuse context.

Everything else is refinement rather than structural necessity.
