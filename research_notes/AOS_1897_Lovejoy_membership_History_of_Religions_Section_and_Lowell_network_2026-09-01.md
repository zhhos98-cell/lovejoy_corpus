# AOS 1897 Lovejoy membership, History of Religions Section, and Boston comparative-religion network

Date: 2026-09-01

## 1. New evidentiary correction: membership is separate from paper announcement

Current chronology must distinguish:

1. 10 April 1897 American Oriental Society Final Circular announced:

`Mr. ARTHUR O. LOVEJOY, Harvard University; Critical summary of the argument of the Milinda-pañha.`

2. AOS membership lists later identify Arthur Oncken Lovejoy as a member elected in 1897.

Therefore 1897 contains at least two independent institutional events:

- scholarly communication announcement;
- election into AOS membership.

Do not collapse these into a single event or infer the mechanism of election without nomination/minutes evidence.

## 2. Negative evidence: AOS member != History of Religions Section member

The 1898 AOS membership material distinguishes ordinary membership from the special Section for the Historical Study of Religions.

Lovejoy appears among ordinary members but not among the separately listed Section members.

Research implication:

`institutional proximity` ≠ `organizational membership`.

This parallels the broader project method: thematic overlap does not itself establish genealogical or organizational transmission.

## 3. History of Religions Section

The Section for the Historical Study of Religions was being institutionalized around the 1897 AOS meeting cycle.

The 1898 proceedings discuss the Section and its organization; the section membership structure later allowed persons beyond ordinary AOS members to participate.

Relevant unresolved question:

- Did the Baltimore 1897 meeting formally create the Section?
- Was Lovejoy's Milinda communication connected to Section activity, or only to the general program?

## 4. Lowell Institute / Boston comparative religion infrastructure

A separate but important contextual layer:

Daniel G. Brinton delivered the second series of American Lectures on the History of Religions in Boston at the Lowell Institute during 1896–97.

The Boston/Cambridge organizing network included figures overlapping with Lovejoy's Harvard environment:

- Crawford Howell Toy — instructor of Lovejoy's Semitic 13 and later connected with the History of Religions Section;
- Charles Rockwell Lanman — instructor of Lovejoy's advanced Pāli 5 and AOS figure;
- other Harvard/Boston comparative religion actors.

One lecture is dated 9 November 1896 in the published record.

Status:

CLOSED:
- Brinton lectures occurred in the same local intellectual environment as Lovejoy's 1896–97 Harvard training.

HOLD:
- Lovejoy attended Brinton's Lowell lectures.
- Brinton directly influenced Lovejoy's Harvard coursework.
