## Governing change

The article should no longer be organized as:

`Buddhist notebook -> recurring relation-specific method -> political consequences -> mature history of ideas`.

That architecture makes the article second-order too early and turns 005/1906 into competing sub-arguments.

The revised first-order architecture is:

`WHAT HISTORICAL OBJECT CALLED “BUDDHISM” IS LOVEJOY TRYING TO RECONSTRUCT?`

->

`HOW DOES 004/1898 REPRESENT THAT OBJECT AS INTERNALLY STRATIFIED YET SYSTEMATICALLY INTELLIGIBLE?`

->

`WHAT HAPPENS WHEN THAT RECONSTRUCTED BUDDHISM IS RE-QUESTIONED AT A DIFFERENT SCALE IN 1902?`

->

`WHAT HISTORICAL ROLE DOES BUDDHISM OCCUPY BY THE TIME OF THE GREAT CHAIN?`

The history-of-ideas intervention follows from changes in the historical role of the object, not from claiming a continuous method.

### 4. 1898 print: old materials, new arrangement, 330–390 words

This should become the argumentative center of the article, not merely the publication endpoint of notebook technique.

Use three publication moves:

1. borrowed terms / `perhaps even the ideas` vs `arrangement and application` as `original and characteristic`;
2. karma as an inherited/pre-Buddhist moral-rebirth vocabulary, retained but given a new relation to upādāna and cessation of rebirth;

The upādisesa usage-over-etymology passage can remain as a short evidentiary coda, not a co-equal method example.

This section should answer:

Lovejoy’s answer is not purity of origin but a historically specific arrangement, application, and functional relation.

This is the strongest first-order historiographical intervention in the piece.

This is the long-durée payoff.

## Historiographical intervention

The intervention should be stated at the level of historical objects and disciplinary memory:

> **The early Lovejoy did not encounter Buddhism as a ready-made “Eastern philosophy” to compare with a pre-existing Western canon. He worked inside a late-nineteenth-century scholarly problem in which ancient Indian traditions had to be reconstructed from philological witnesses while their conceptual coherence, historical layering, and disciplinary jurisdiction remained contested. The later history of ideas did not simply preserve or discard that Buddhism; it reassigned its historical role.**

This is stronger than a claim of `Buddhism influenced Lovejoy’s method` and more specific than `Lovejoy had non-Western interests`.

## Compression test

Every body section must answer one of four questions:

1. **What was the object?**
2. **How was it historically reconstructed?**
3. **What new question was it later made to answer?**
4. **Where was it finally placed in the mature genealogy?**

If a paragraph primarily answers “what was Lovejoy’s method?”, it should be cut, subordinated, or rewritten at first order.
