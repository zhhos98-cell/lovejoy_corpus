# Averroës / Butterworth across APSR and *Political Theory*, 1972–1976: same object, changing carrier role

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: **DIRECT APSR ARTICLE ABSTRACT + DIRECT PT REVIEW/COMMUNICATION METADATA / SAME-ACTOR SAME-OBJECT CROSS-VENUE ROLE MIGRATION LOCKED / NO CAUSAL SUBMISSION-ROUTE CLAIM**

## Core result

Averroës provides a clean test case for the project's rule that intellectual-object presence must be separated from carrier role.

Within four years, closely connected Averroës material appears in two political-science/political-theory venues under three distinct roles:

1. **APSR 1972** — Charles E. Butterworth, original research article: `Averroës: Politics and Opinion`;
2. ***Political Theory* 1975** — Paul Sigmund, book review of Ralph Lerner's *Averroes on Plato's Republic*;
3. ***Political Theory* 1976** — Charles E. Butterworth, communication responding to Sigmund's review.

Compact sequence:

`AVERROËS AS ORIGINAL RESEARCH OBJECT (APSR)`
-> later
`AVERROËS AS REVIEW / TRANSLATION-RECEPTION OBJECT (PT)`
->
`BUTTERWORTH AS COMMUNICATION RESPONDENT (PT)`.

This is not a simple migration from an old venue to a new one. The **same broad intellectual object changes carrier role**, and the same scholar changes from research-article author to review-controversy respondent.

Core rules:

`SAME OBJECT != SAME CARRIER ROLE`;

`SAME ACTOR != SAME ROLE`;

`NEW SPECIALIST VENUE != AUTOMATIC UPGRADE OF OBJECT TO ORIGINAL-RESEARCH ROLE`.

---

## I. APSR September 1972 — original research article

Charles E. Butterworth, `Averroës: Politics and Opinion`, *American Political Science Review* 66.3 (September 1972), 894–901.

Direct Cambridge/RePEc abstract control establishes that the article treats Averroës's political thought as the main intellectual object.

The article asks how Averroës understood the relation between theory and practice and reconstructs the problem through:

- his commentary on Plato's *Republic*;
- logical works concerning public speech/common opinion;
- public writings on philosophy and religiously grounded opposition to philosophy;
- the place of philosophy/philosophers within his own religious community.

Under the current regional-object role taxonomy this is a strong:

`INTERNAL_TRADITION / INTERNAL THINKER OBJECT`

or, more cautiously,

`ISLAMIC / ANDALUSIAN POLITICAL-PHILOSOPHY OBJECT AS ORIGINAL RESEARCH ARTICLE`.

The named historical/intellectual object is Averroës himself, not a European thinker looking at Islam.

Thus APSR directly demonstrates article-level capacity for internally addressed medieval Islamic political thought in 1972.

This should not be generalized into an APSR-wide globality claim.

`ONE INTERNAL-TRADITION ARTICLE != BROAD CIVILIZATIONAL REPRESENTATIVENESS`.

---

## II. The article sits inside a heterogeneous APSR issue

APSR 66.3 directly places Butterworth immediately after:

- the Miller positivism/historicism method controversy cluster;
- Ruggie on collective goods/international collaboration;

and before:

- African political-development research;
- public-opinion/electoral articles;
- communications;
- editorial comment;
- review essay and book reviews.

This is relevant because later retrospective descriptions can treat APSR's early-1970s center of gravity as behavioral/empirical.

That output skew remains plausible/controlled by the 1971 editorial census.

But the issue architecture demonstrates:

`EMPIRICAL / FORMAL OUTPUT SKEW`
!= `EXCLUSION OF HISTORICAL POLITICAL PHILOSOPHY AS ARTICLE FORM`.

---

## III. *Political Theory* May 1975 — Averroës becomes a book-review object

Paul Sigmund reviews Ralph Lerner's translation *Averroes on Plato's Republic* in *Political Theory* 3.2 (May 1975), 235–236.

SAGE directly identifies:

- book-review carrier;
- reviewer Paul Sigmund, Princeton;
- reviewed Lerner volume;
- issue/date/pages/DOI.

The object is still Averroës / his commentary on Plato.

But the journal role has changed:

`ORIGINAL RESEARCH ARTICLE`
-> `BOOK REVIEW / RECEPTION / TRANSLATION ASSESSMENT`.

Do not infer that PT lacked interest in Averroës because the item is a review.

The precise point is that **interest and carrier role are distinct variables**.

---

## IV. *Political Theory* November 1976 — Butterworth enters as communication respondent

SAGE directly lists:

Charles E. Butterworth, `On Paul Sigmund's "Review of Ralph Lerner's Averroes on Plato's Republic" (Volume 3, No. 2, May 1975)`, *Political Theory* 4.4 (Nov. 1976), 505–506, DOI `10.1177/009059177600400408`.

SAGE labels it `Other`; JSTOR places it in the issue's communication/follow-up layer.

Thus Butterworth re-enters the object history not as original-article author but as:

`REVIEW-CONTROVERSY / COMMUNICATION RESPONDENT`.

This gives a traceable object-carrier sequence inside PT:

`REVIEW`
-> `COMMUNICATION / CORRECTION / RESPONSE`.

The currently available secondary full-text rendering indicates Butterworth challenges Sigmund's review on the handling of Lerner versus Rosenthal and translation/manuscript interpretation. Do not use that rendering as primary full-text authority; the direct SAGE/JSTOR metadata are enough for the carrier-role proposition.

## V. What this does to the APSR -> PT modernization story

A tempting narrative would be:

`APSR / POLITICAL SCIENCE`
= empirical behavioral flagship;

then

`POLITICAL THEORY`
= new home for historical political philosophy.

The Averroës sequence blocks that binary.

Before PT launches, APSR has already published Averroës as an original political-philosophy research object.

After PT launches, the same broad object initially enters its visible 1973–77 architecture through:

- book review;
- communication/follow-up;

rather than a title-level original research article.

Therefore:

`SPECIALIST VENUE FORMATION`
!= `MONOPOLIZATION OF SPECIALIST OBJECTS`;

`OLDER FLAGSHIP`
!= `OBJECT ABSENCE`;

`NEW SPECIALIST CARRIER`
!= `SAME OBJECT RECEIVES HIGHER ARTICLE ROLE`.

This directly complements the matched JHI/PT regional-object result.

## VI. Same actor, changing role

Butterworth's role sequence is especially clean:

`1972 APSR RESEARCH-ARTICLE AUTHOR`
->
`1976 PT COMMUNICATION RESPONDENT`.

The object family remains Averroës/political philosophy, but the institutional role changes.

This reinforces the existing actor-traffic rule:

`ACTOR CONTINUITY != ROLE CONTINUITY`.

It also shows why generic network maps lose information. A line `Butterworth -- APSR -- PT` does not encode whether he is:

- research author;
- reviewer;
- reviewed author/translator;
- respondent;
- correspondent.

The relation-typed edge is the historical unit that matters.

---

## VII. Relation to global/regional carrier comparison

The current matched JHI/PT 1973–77 census concerns only those two venues and one fixed window.

Do **not** simply add APSR 1972 Butterworth into that denominator.

Instead use it as an existence/role comparator:

> **A broad disciplinary flagship could already make an internally addressed Islamic political thinker into an original research-article object immediately before PT's founding, while PT's early treatment of the same thinker is directly visible through review and communication roles.**

This complicates any claim that methodological specialization and specialist-journal formation automatically broadened or upgraded civilizational-object roles.

Possible causes remain open:

- submission ecology;
- editor networks;
- disciplinary jurisdiction;
- timing of books/translations;
- individual author choices;
- review/research allocation;
- chance in a small number of cases.

`ROLE DIFFERENCE != CAUSAL EDITORIAL POLICY`.
