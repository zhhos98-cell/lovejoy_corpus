## Core result

The archive of the Political Studies Association at the British Library of Political and Economic Science / LSE provides a concrete independent institutional route to the April 1968 performance-state of Quentin Skinner's paper `Political Theory: the Unimportance of the Great Texts`.

Direct archive control:

- collection: `Political Studies Association`;
- reference: **GB 0097 PSA**;
- dates: 1950–1983;
- extent: 25 boxes and ring binders;
- conference papers: **1952–1981, including material on organisation**;
- Chairman's correspondence: **1965–1968**;
- Executive Committee correspondence: **1965–1968**;
- other contemporaneous correspondence includes Treasurer and Oxford University Press, 1965–68.

Independent source controls establish:

- Skinner gave the paper at the 1968 Political Studies Association conference at the University of Sussex;
- the conference paper was presented on **4 April 1968**;
- title: `Political Theory: the Unimportance of the Great Texts`;
- Margaret Leslie 1970 explicitly says her essay arose from reflections on Skinner's 1968 PSA paper, which was later published in revised form as `Meaning and Understanding in the History of Ideas`.

Therefore the archive query is no longer generic:

> **Within GB 0097 PSA, locate the conference papers / organisational material for the 1968 annual conference at the University of Sussex, especially programme, paper lists, correspondence, and any deposited or circulated copy of Quentin Skinner's `Political Theory: the Unimportance of the Great Texts`, presented 4 April 1968. Also check Chairman's and Executive Committee correspondence for conference planning or paper routing.**
