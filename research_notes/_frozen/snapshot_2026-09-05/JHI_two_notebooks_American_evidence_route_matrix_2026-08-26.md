# JHI support matrix — routes of American evidence into notebook 005

Date: 2026-08-26
Status: **WORKING SOURCE-ROUTE MATRIX / CLAIM-DISCIPLINE FIRST**

## Purpose

Test how far the JHI-facing phrase `American evidence inside a Paris controversy` can be specified without inventing a blanket transmission chain.

The target is not geographical counting. The target is to distinguish four routes:

1. **Lovejoy-direct source use** — a source is visibly cited/abstracted in 005.
2. **Marillier-shared source field** — a source/problem is securely present in Marillier's published/seminar apparatus and overlaps 005.
3. **same-workshop parallel use** — Söderblom or another participant uses the same/near-same source cluster.
4. **contextual American input node** — relevant material entered the Marillier/Paris field, but no direct bridge to Lovejoy's specific page is yet proved.

No route may be silently upgraded to another.

## Core result

The American emphasis is historically defensible, but it must be phrased as an **evidence ecology**, not a single provenance chain.

The 1898–99 EPHE report independently states that Marillier's reopened `survivance` controversy added new facts especially from `les deux Amériques`. Lovejoy was an active auditor. Notebook 005 then contains dense North-American material and repeatedly uses contact/missionary chronology, manner-of-death selectors, status, ritual qualification and observer-category correction.

This establishes:

> **American evidence was structurally central to the controversy field in which 005 was made.**

It does not establish that every North-American datum passed through Marillier, or that every datum in the seminar was copied by Lovejoy.

Her source/problem field overlaps 005: missionary relations, Schoolcraft, Cheyenne and other North-American materials, corporeal soul, ordinary continuation after death, Christian/contact elements, and cases where bravery/status/worldly success rather than moral virtue structure paradise.

Route grade: **PARALLEL AMERICAN-INPUT NODE / NO DIRECT LOVEJOY EDGE**.

## American evidence as argumentative work

The relevant common operations are not simply `collect examples from America`.

American materials repeatedly force distinctions among:

- report versus indigenous category;
- present form versus historical layer;
- missionary/contact contamination versus older local structure;
- differential destination versus operative selector;
- moral evaluation versus rank/status/ritual/death-type;
- comparison versus mechanism;
- mechanism versus genealogy.

Hence the historically useful proposition is:

> **North-American evidence was not merely one regional sample inside 005. It was one of the principal stress-tests through which a live comparative-religion controversy calibrated the inferential distance between observed difference, moral interpretation, causal mechanism and historical genealogy.**

This remains an ecology-level formulation; exact source provenance must stay source-by-source.
