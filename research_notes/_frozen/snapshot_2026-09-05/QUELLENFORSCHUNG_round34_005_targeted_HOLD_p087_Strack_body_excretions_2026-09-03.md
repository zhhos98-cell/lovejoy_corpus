## 3. Strack's preserved corpus contains the four p.87 substances in one list

In Strack's later expanded/translated presentation of the same blood-superstition corpus, he reproduces an early-modern household-medicine list of bodily materials including:

- `Harn` — urine;
- `Kot` — dung/excrement;
- `Schweiss` — sweat/perspiration;
- `Speichel` — spittle/saliva.

These are a one-to-one lexical match for the four-item p.87 cluster:

`urine / excrement / perspiration / saliva`.

The preserved list belongs to Strack's discussion of wonderful powers attributed to the human body, corpse, body parts, and bodily evacuations.

This is substantially closer than a generic Frazer-style statement that detached bodily substances may remain magically connected to a person.
