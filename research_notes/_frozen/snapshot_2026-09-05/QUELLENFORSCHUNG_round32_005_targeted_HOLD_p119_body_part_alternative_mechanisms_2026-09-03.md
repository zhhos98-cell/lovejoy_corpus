## 2. Specific-organ / specific-quality efficacy is upstream

The first mechanism belongs securely to the contemporary cannibalism/body-efficacy field.

Carl Lumholtz's *Among Cannibals* (1889), for example, reports Australian preference for particular human body parts and says that eating kidneys is believed to confer part of the slain person's strength; the kidneys are treated as the `centre of life`.

The broader Frazer/Trumbull/cannibalism dossier already supplies analogous propositions:

- heart/blood/body matter as carriers of courage, strength, life or nature;
- selected bodily material as seat of a valued quality;
- ingestion or contact as a means of acquiring that quality.

Notebook 005 itself has earlier Trumbull-derived pages on blood/body-part transmission.

Therefore:

`SELECTED ORGAN -> SPECIFIC LOCALIZED VIRTUE / FORCE`

is F/A0, not Lovejoy's invention.

## 3. Part-for-whole / pars-pro-toto reasoning is also upstream

The second mechanism is likewise available before Lovejoy.

### Tylor

*Primitive Culture* explicitly includes substitution in which a **part is given for the whole**, alongside inferior life for superior life and effigy/representative substitution. This establishes part-for-whole reasoning as an ordinary comparative-religion explanatory resource.

### Frazer

Frazer's sympathetic/contagious magic generalizes the principle that something once physically connected to a person — including material that formed part of the body — continues to stand in an operative relation to the person as a whole.

Thus a `part -> whole` relation is not a Lovejoy-origin concept.

The exact phraseology `part represents the whole` becomes standard in later magic theory, but Lovejoy does not need to have invented the relation for p.119 to be analytically significant.
