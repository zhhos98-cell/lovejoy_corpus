# MS38_004_001_061_005 — Marillier / 1900 chronology bridge

## Why this note matters

Direct collation of Lovejoy's 1906 *Monist* article with notebook 005 produced a stronger chronological link than the earlier comparison had recognized.

On published p. 381 n. 21 Lovejoy states that the conclusions developed in “The Fundamental Concept of the Primitive Philosophy” were **reached in 1900** and that they were strongly influenced by his studies under **Léon Marillier**.

Notebook 005 contains at least two independent Marillier traces.

## 1. PDF p. 29: substantive inserted slip

Corrected batch `MS38_004_001_061_005_p016-030_clean.json`, PDF p. 29, records an inserted bibliographic/research slip headed:

`Marillier — Survivance — 12 June`

The slip belongs inside the notebook's substantive future-life / survival / Madagascar research sequence, not merely to back-matter scheduling. It is therefore direct evidence that Marillier material entered the notebook's working apparatus.

The associated notes concern Madagascar populations, survival, Hova/Sakalava material and comparative eschatology. This does not prove that the slip dates to 1900, but it materially strengthens the connection between notebook 005 and the Marillier research context that Lovejoy later names in print.

## 2. PDF p. 120: back-leaf appointment/reminder

The final back leaf contains a second, physically distinct memorandum in which `Marillier` is visually legible. High-resolution inspection confirms the surname. The initial and surrounding French scheduling/address words remain uncertain and should not yet be normalized.

This second occurrence is weaker as evidence for the content of the research, because it is back-matter scheduling rather than analytical notes. Taken together with p. 29, however, it makes accidental OCR coincidence very unlikely.

## 3. Published p. 381 n. 21

Lovejoy's own 1906 retrospective statement supplies the external chronological anchor:

- conclusions reached in **1900**;
- reached independently of several subsequently published formulations;
- significantly influenced by study under **Léon Marillier**.

The notebook therefore contains internal material from the very teacher/research context Lovejoy explicitly credits for the formation of the published theory.

## Evidential judgment

### Strong inference

- At least part of notebook 005 belongs to, or directly preserves material from, Lovejoy's Marillier-related comparative-religion research environment.
