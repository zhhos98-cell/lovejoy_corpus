## Exact-text / OCR cautions

The Internet Archive OCR is strong enough to recover the whole argument but distorts diacritics and some proper names. Normalize only where identity is independently certain:

- `Veddnta / Vedtntic` → Vedānta / Vedāntic;
- `Shanhara` → Shankara;
- `Hamanuja / Bamanuja / Kamanuja` → Rāmānuja;
- `Upaniahads` → Upaniṣads.

Do **not** yet silently normalize OCR `Boyce` to `Royce`; context makes Josiah Royce highly probable, but exact page-image control is still required.

## Primary relation codes added / strengthened

- `RESEARCH-PRIORITY`: judgment about where specialist effort should be allocated.
- `REPRESENTATIVE-STATUS`: whether a school is allowed to stand for a larger tradition.
- `MEDIATION-CAUSE`: claimed mechanism by which one school acquires disproportionate visibility.
- `METHOD-PRESCRIPTION`: criteria specified for an adequate history/survey.
- `STRUCTURAL-COMPARANDUM`: cross-tradition philosophical analogy without transmission claim.

## Cautions

- Lovejoy's hostile wording about Śaṅkara/Māyā is evidence about Lovejoy's evaluative position, not repository terminology.
- His account of Deussen/Ramakrishna mediation is a historical claim requiring independent testing.
- Later historiography can corroborate the existence of Advaita privileging without making Lovejoy's explanation complete.
- The review proves bibliographic knowledge of Thibaut SBE 48, not exhaustive reading.
