# Batch 44 — Lovejoy 005 only: Marillier’s notes circulate into Mauss, while `communion` remains a contested classificatory box

Date: 2026-08-18

Scope lock: **MS38_004_001_061_005 only**.

## Research move

Two evidence strands that had previously been kept separate can now be joined tightly:

1. Mauss explicitly acknowledges that Léon Marillier communicated to him **his bibliography and notes from several courses** for the third part of Mauss’s critique of Steinmetz.
2. In the same critical essay, Mauss reclassifies Steinmetz’s `endocannibalisme` as `l’anthropophagie rituelle des parents`, explains it by transfer of the dead person’s virtues, and finally calls the practice **`un simple acte de communion`**.

Against this, Lovejoy 005 later treats acquisition of bodily qualities as compatible with **sympathetic / transferable efficacy rather than communion**, and regards sacramental union as comparatively exceptional.

The resulting historical object is not only an intellectual disagreement. It is a disagreement occurring inside a **documentary transmission environment** in which Marillier’s bibliographies and course notes demonstrably circulated into comparative-religion research.

## 1. Direct Mauss testimony: Marillier supplied bibliography and course notes

In the critical continuation of `La religion et les origines du droit pénal d’après un livre récent`, Mauss places a footnote at the transition into the third part of his argument. The searchable electronic reproduction preserves the key wording:

> `Pour toute cette troisième partie de mon travail je dois beaucoup à mon maître, M. Marillier, qui a bien voulu me communiquer sa bibliographie et les notes de ses différents cours...`

The continuation after `sur le` is not fully exposed by current search snippets and should not be silently reconstructed. The evidentiary point is already secure without it:

- Marillier possessed working bibliographies;
- he possessed notes from multiple courses;
- he communicated these materials directly to Mauss;
- Mauss used them in published research on Steinmetz, vengeance, mortuary religion, taboo, and ritual sanction.

This is stronger than an inference from common citations. It is explicit evidence of **teacher-held documentary apparatus moving into another scholar’s research text**.

## 2. This independently confirms the paper-based workshop model

This testimony now sits alongside the already recovered Söderblom evidence that Marillier lent/gave him personal notes, with extracts marked `M.` in Söderblom’s work.

The two witnesses are independent and concern different research outputs. Together they establish that Marillier’s teaching/research practice included circulation of:

- bibliographies;
- course notes;
- personal research notes / extracts;
- source packets assembled around particular comparative problems.

This means the paper need no longer infer a paper-based research workshop primarily from Lovejoy 005’s form. The circulation practice is directly documented around Marillier before and during the period in which Lovejoy participated in his EPHE conferences.

Important limit: this does **not** prove that Lovejoy received the same physical sheets. It makes access to a circulating documentary apparatus historically plausible and institutionally grounded.

## 3. The same Mauss essay shows what could happen to a category inside this apparatus

Mauss’s critical discussion of Steinmetz is especially useful because he does not merely inherit a category. He renames and redivides it.

Steinmetz’s broad `endocannibalisme` becomes more exactly:

> `l’anthropophagie rituelle des parents`

Mauss then reports that eating dead relatives transfers the `vertus du mort` to survivors. Hair, a bodily fragment, or a drop of blood can magically represent the person, establishing a community between dead and living. He concludes:

> `Cette pratique est donc un simple acte de communion.`

The operation is therefore:

> broad comparative class → specify kin relation → specify funerary/ritual setting → specify transfer mechanism → assign mechanism to a higher-order category (`communion`).

## 4. Lovejoy 005 keeps the mechanism but changes the higher-order classification

The contrast to 005 is now local and precise.

### PDF p.108

Lovejoy treats the acquisition of strength, courage, intelligence, or another quality from human flesh/body parts as potentially **sympathetic magic rather than sacrifice or communion**.

### PDF p.117

In his synthetic distribution of sacrificial types, alimentary and expiatory/propitiatory sacrifice are common while **sacramental union is comparatively exceptional**.

### PDF p.119

Transfer is localized anatomically:

- head / brain → intelligence;
- heart → courage / breath / life;
- sexual organs → sexual power.

The same formal causal relation — property resides in dead person/body part → ingestion/contact → property passes to survivor — is therefore assigned differently:

> **Mauss: transfer-through-ingestion belongs to communion.**
>
> **Lovejoy: transfer-through-ingestion may instead belong to sympathetic / transferable efficacy and need not establish communion.**

This is an unusually clean classificatory redivision.

## 5. Why note circulation matters for interpreting the disagreement

Without the documentary evidence, the comparison could remain a parallel-text exercise. The Mauss footnote changes the scale of the claim.

The surrounding Paris field now has demonstrable material transmission:

- Marillier supplies bibliography and course notes to Mauss;
- Marillier’s 1896 Melanesian taboo study functions as an assembled source packet inside Mauss’s Steinmetz critique;
- Marillier separately reviews Steinmetz’s exact *Endokannibalismus* in RHR 34 (1896), pp.113–115;
- the 1898–99 EPHE programme explicitly returns to Steinmetz-related problems;
- Lovejoy is documented as an active auditor;
- Lovejoy 005 records the exact title `Endokannibalismus — Steinmetz.`

Thus the paper can move from **document circulation** to **category dispute** without assuming textual copying.

The strongest interpretation is that Lovejoy participated in a research environment where the objects being transmitted were not finished doctrines but bibliographies, source packets, controversy dossiers, and classificatory problems.

## 6. Bibliographic control on the missing Marillier review

Google Books’ contents for RHR 34 fixes the local sequence:

- `R Steinmetz Endokannibalismus M L Marillier` — p.113;
- an unrelated notice begins p.116;
- `J B Bérenger-Féraud Superstitions et survivances M L Marillier` — p.119.

Therefore Marillier’s review can safely be treated as **pp.113–115**. The prose remains unrecovered at publication-grade quality.

This matters because those three pages are now a sharply defined discriminating test. We need to know whether Marillier places kin/body-part transfer under:

- communion;
- magical efficacy;
- funerary solidarity;
- or a plural mechanism framework.

Any of these outcomes is informative for the Lovejoy comparison.

## 7. Strongest safe formulation

> **Mauss explicitly states that Marillier supplied him with bibliography and notes from several courses for the critical part of his Steinmetz study. In that same research sequence Mauss narrows `endocannibalisme` to ritual anthropophagy of relatives and classifies the transfer of the dead person’s virtues as communion. Lovejoy’s 005 notebook, which records Steinmetz’s exact title and was produced in the later Marillier seminar environment, retains the transfer problem but reclassifies it: bodily qualities can pass by sympathetic or transferable efficacy without constituting communion. The evidence therefore points to circulation of documentary problems and sources, followed by active redivision rather than doctrinal copying.**
