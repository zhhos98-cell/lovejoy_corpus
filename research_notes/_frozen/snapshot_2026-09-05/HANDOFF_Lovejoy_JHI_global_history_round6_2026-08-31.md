# III. APSR editors themselves identify a possible feedback mechanism

`Present and Absent` is stronger than a bare count because the editors explicitly worry about the journal's visible profile.

Their mechanism is:

`VISIBLE PUBLISHED MIX`
-> `AUTHORS INFER EDITORIAL PREFERENCES`
-> `UNLIKE MANUSCRIPTS MAY NOT BE SUBMITTED`
-> `JOURNAL LOSES ACCESS TO THEM`.

This is contemporaneous actor-level causal reasoning.

It is not a demonstrated empirical effect.

It nevertheless offers a historically plausible bridge between:

- weak visible political-philosophy representation;
- theorists' perception that established journals offered little space;
- creation of specialist carrier infrastructure.

Thus the current institutional model is **carrier-capacity engineering**, not necessarily formal exclusion.

# V. Odajnyk is the key personal-access counterexample

APSR's June 1972 accepted-future list includes Walter Odajnyk's `The Political Ideas of C. G. Jung`.

Thus:

`FOUNDER OF SPECIALIST THEORY JOURNAL`
!= `PERSONALLY EXCLUDED FROM APSR`.

The stronger proposition is:

`PERSONAL ACCESS`
!= `ADEQUATE FIELD INFRASTRUCTURE / REGULAR CAPACITY`.
