# *Political Theory* 1973–1977: programme persistence across editorial succession

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: **DIRECT 1977 CONTEMPORARY SAGE AD / PDF PAGE-IMAGE CONTROL / PUBLIC PROGRAMME PERSISTENCE LOCKED / REPRESENTATIVENESS CLAIM NOT TREATED AS CENSUS**

## Core result

A direct 1977 SAGE advertisement for *Political Theory* shows that the journal's inaugural pluralist carrier architecture remained part of its public institutional identity several years after launch and under a different named editor.

The February 1973 inaugural `Editorial Statement` had declared broad/international scope, rejected exclusive identification with one school or orientation, and proposed multiple publication roles including history of political thought, modern theory, conceptual analysis, polemic argumentation, special issues, discussion and bibliographic awareness.

The 1977 SAGE advertisement, naming **Benjamin R. Barber** as editor, again describes the journal as:

- an international journal of political philosophy;
- committed to no particular school or orientation;
- covering both historical and contemporary perspectives;
- covering history of political thought, modern theory, American political thought, dialectical analysis, epistemology and conceptual analysis;
- publishing major articles plus a forum for debate/dialogue;
- carrying book reviews/review essays, bibliographies, readers' communications, symposia and translations.

Therefore the safest institutional proposition is:

`1973 FOUNDING PROGRAMME`
-> `1977 PUBLIC JOURNAL BRAND / ROLE ARCHITECTURE`.

This is evidence of **programme persistence across editorial-personnel change**.

It is not proof that every published issue actually represented every major perspective equally.

## II. Persistence is stronger than a one-off founding declaration

The February 1973 statement could, by itself, be read as launch rhetoric whose relationship to later editorial practice remained uncertain.

The 1975 direct editorial census already showed execution-level category overlap and active metatheory.

The 1977 advertisement adds a third temporal state:

`FEB 1973 CORPORATE PROGRAMME`
-> `MAY 1975 EDITORIAL EXECUTION / SELF-CENSUS`
-> `1977 PUBLIC BRANDING / ROLE ARCHITECTURE`.

The relevant persistence is not exact wording identity alone. It is the continued institutional packaging of multiple intellectual roles inside one carrier.

Thus:

`FOUNDING RHETORIC`
!= automatically `TEMPORARY RHETORIC`.

At least through 1977, the no-single-school claim and multi-role publication architecture remained publicly advertised features of the journal.

---

## III. Editorial succession matters

The 1973 journal was launched by the founding editorial group centered on Robert Lamb and Walter Odajnyk.

The 1977 advertisement names Benjamin R. Barber as editor.

This makes the continuity analytically stronger than a founder simply repeating his own original programme.

Safe formulation:

`PROGRAMME PERSISTENCE`
coexists with
`NAMED EDITOR CHANGE`.

Therefore:

`INSTITUTIONALIZED CARRIER DESIGN`
can outlast
`FOUNDING PERSONNEL CONFIGURATION`.

Do not infer from this alone:

- the exact date or mechanism of editorial succession;
- that Barber interpreted every 1973 category identically;
- that the founding editors exercised continuing control;
- that no internal editorial priorities changed.

The claim is only persistence of the public carrier architecture.

---

## IV. The 1977 ad is unusually explicit about publication roles

The ad does not merely list topical fields. It enumerates distinct publication mechanisms:

- major research articles;
- forum for debate and dialogue on controversial topics;
- book reviews and review essays;
- bibliographies of new books/articles;
- readers' communication section;
- symposia on contemporary theorists;
- translations of previously untranslated European texts.

This supports the repo's emerging distinction:

`SUBJECT COVERAGE`
!= `CARRIER ROLE ARCHITECTURE`.

A specialist journal can increase field capacity not merely by publishing more items on a topic, but by supplying multiple recurring slots through which disagreement, reception, review, translation and response can become visible.

This sharpens `ACCESS != CAPACITY`:

> **Capacity includes the diversity and recurrence of publication roles, not only whether an article can enter a venue at all.**

## V. Advertised May 1977 contents give a contemporaneous execution sample

The same advertisement lists contents for May 1977, including:

- George Kateb on Hannah Arendt;
- an `Understanding Weber: An Exchange` between J. Donald Moon and W. G. Runciman;
- Conal Condren on Marsilius of Padua;
- a three-article cluster on Robert Nozick.

This one advertised issue visibly combines:

- history / interpretation of a modern theorist;
- explicit exchange/reply architecture;
- history of political thought;
- contemporary normative/conceptual controversy.

That is congruent with the advertised role architecture.

But one issue remains only an execution example.

`ONE ISSUE CONGRUENT WITH PROGRAMME`
!= `REPRESENTATIVE CENSUS OF 1973–77 OUTPUT`.

Do not turn it into a quantitative pluralism claim.

---

## VI. Marketing language has its own evidence ceiling

The ad says the journal offers representative articles from every major ideological, methodological and political perspective.

Treat this as:

`PUBLISHER / JOURNAL PUBLIC SELF-DESCRIPTION`.

It does not establish:

- exhaustive ideological representation;
- equal acceptance rates;
- equal article shares;
- neutrality among perspectives;
- absence of editorial preference.

Thus:

`PUBLIC CLAIM OF REPRESENTATIVENESS`
!= `MEASURED REPRESENTATIVENESS`.

The historically secure use is narrower: it demonstrates how SAGE/*Political Theory* continued to **market and institutionalize school-nonexclusive breadth as part of the journal's identity**.

## VII. Relation to APSR and carrier-capacity differentiation

The APSR 1972 self-audit establishes that political philosophy remained accessible but weakly represented in flagship research-article output.

The 1977 *Political Theory* ad shows what a dedicated carrier offered beyond mere access:

- guaranteed recurring major-article space devoted to political philosophy;
- explicit debate/dialogue infrastructure;
- communications/rejoinder space;
- symposia;
- reviews/bibliographic awareness;
- translation infrastructure;
- publicly declared coexistence of historical and contemporary approaches.

The field differentiation can therefore be expressed more exactly as:

`FLAGSHIP ACCESS`
!= `SPECIALIST MULTI-ROLE CAPACITY`.

This is stronger than saying simply that political theorists wanted `their own journal`.

The new journal was a different **communications architecture**.

---

## VIII. Relation to later canonization

Later method canons often extract individual articles or named schools from this period.

The 1977 public programme reminds us that the venue was institutionally designed around:

- co-presence;
- controversy;
- multiple genres;
- historical/contemporary interchange;
- explicit school non-identification.

Therefore later canonical concentration on one method should be compared with the carrier's broader contemporary design.

`LATER METHOD CANON`
!= `CONTEMPORARY VENUE SELF-ARCHITECTURE`.

This does not imply the later canon is false; it means canonization selects from a wider institutional repertoire.
