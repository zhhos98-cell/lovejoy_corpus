Direct primary recovered from University of Chicago Press, five-page PDF, pp.216–220; key pages image-checked.

Major results:

### 1914 does NOT demote 1906 Primitive Energetics

Lovejoy says Leuba's accepted `dynamism` / theory of primitive impersonal power is essentially / precisely Lovejoy's 1906 `Primitive Energetics` / `manitouism`.

He says Leuba uses the hypothesis **less than the facts justify** and argues mana/manitou interpenetrates early belief more strongly than Leuba allows.

This falsifies the working possibility that the 1914 review documents a retreat from 1906.

### Actor-level aggregation-mode distinction on p.220

Lovejoy distinguishes three things hidden under `definition of religion`:

(a) what an analyst stipulates the name to designate;
(b) common elements in the enormously diverse historical applications of the term;
(c) analytical description of certain complex observable phenomena across phases of development.

He says (c) is what is wanted and criticizes inclusion/exclusion that merely follows a definition or prevailing usage as circular.

This is strong direct evidence that aggregation-mode discrimination is not only retrospective project formalization.

Best current logic coding:

`M/C,E,A` rather than formal-logic object `O/F`.
