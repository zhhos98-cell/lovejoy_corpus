## 2. P.33 / ms p.97 — substantive transcription correction: `Up.` not `MP`

The right page heading is clear:

> `Upādāna, Relation to Karma.`

The first-pass clean transcription had:

> `May MP be called ...`

A high-resolution crop of the original hand shows that the abbreviation is **`Up.`**, i.e. upādāna. The corrected question is therefore:

> `May Up. be called t. cause of t. fact of continued existence, & karma t. cause of t. manner of continued existence?`

followed by `Hardy, p. 394`.

This correction has been propagated to the authoritative clean JSON.

### Consequence

The page is testing a remarkably explicit **division of causal labor**:

`UPĀDĀNA -> FACT OF CONTINUED EXISTENCE`

`KARMA -> MANNER OF CONTINUED EXISTENCE`.

One explanatory variable accounts for persistence as such; another accounts for the differentiated form that persistence takes.

This is direct notebook evidence that Lovejoy does not assume one undifferentiated causal relation merely because two terms participate in the same rebirth problem.

## 3. P.34 / ms p.99 — khandha as aggregate and unequal composition

Direct image control clears a numerical uncertainty in the Childers note:

- rūpakkhandha: assemblage of **28** elements/properties;
- viññāṇakkhandha: **89** divisions;
- saṅkhāra: **55**.

The five khandhas jointly embrace the essential properties of sentient beings, but different beings possess them in different degrees; inhabitants of the four arūpa-brahmalokas lack rūpa.

### Consequence

`KHANDHA` is being read as an aggregate category whose members and internal multiplicities are heterogeneous. Classification does not imply equal granularity.

This bears directly on the later pages where the same constituents are nested, repeated, or placed in causal sequences.

## 4. P.35 / ms p.101 — continuity while constituents are replaced

The left page continues the previous quotation/summary:

- at death a new set of khandhas starts into existence through the force of karma;
- the new being possesses different khandhas and another form;
- nevertheless the note says the being is `in reality identical` with the one who died because `the Kamma is t. same`;
- karma is described as the `link` preserving identity through countless changes in saṁsāra;
- the khandhas perish, yet there is `no break in t. line of organized existence`;
- with Nirvāṇa the potentiality for rebirth conveyed by karma ceases.

On the facing right page Lovejoy places two classification controls:

- vedanā, saññā and saṅkhāras are collectively termed `dhamma` / `faculties`;
- a Hopkins quotation defines the Buddhist ego as only a collective of five skandhas that disperses and reforms.

### Material-form consequence

The spread juxtaposes:

`REPLACED CONSTITUENTS`

with

`CLAIMED CONTINUITY THROUGH A LINK`.

The page does not solve the philosophical problem by preserving identical material constituents. It makes the **relation** carry continuity while the classified components change.

This should be kept distinct from Lovejoy's own endorsement: part of the page is source extraction / paraphrase, not necessarily a freestanding metaphysical conclusion.
