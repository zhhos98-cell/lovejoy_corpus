# Batch 131 — Collège de France corrected course poster, first semester 1898–1899: Foucher substitution cancelled on Lévi's return

Date: 2026-08-19
Status: synced
Source type: user-supplied screenshots from Salamandre digital image viewer; primary institutional object.

## Core result

The digitized Collège de France course poster is not merely a clean printed programme. It is a working correction copy in which a printed **second-semester 1898** poster was manually altered for the **first semester 1898–1899**.

Visible corrections at the top of the sheet include:

- printed `SECOND ... 1898` corrected by hand to `premier ... 1898-1899`;
- printed opening date `lundi 18 avril 1898` struck out and replaced by handwritten `5 Décembre`;
- many course entries throughout the sheet are crossed out, overwritten, or supplied with replacement strips.

This makes the sheet especially valuable because it preserves the institutional transition between the spring 1898 teaching arrangement and the opening of the 1898–1899 year.

## Sanskrit block

Heading:

`LANGUE ET LITTÉRATURES SANSCRITES.`

The printed block visibly contains:

- `M. SYLVAIN LÉVI, professeur.`
- immediately below, `M. H. FOUCHER, remplaçant, ...`

The Foucher replacement line and the printed course description beneath it are visibly cancelled/overwritten in the correction state of the poster. The surviving printed course wording is partly obscured by corrections and a pasted strip, but one phrase is legible enough to identify the subject as the **Villes saintes ... de l'Hindouisme**; a second printed line begins `expliquera le Kausitaki...` (exact textual form should remain provisional until a cleaner frame or final published programme is recovered).

The documentary point does not depend on forcing the obscured course title. What is secure from the correction state is:

> **The spring-1898 printed arrangement named Alfred Foucher as replacement (`remplaçant`) under Sylvain Lévi's Sanskrit chair; in the working copy revised for first semester 1898–1899, that Foucher replacement arrangement is cancelled.**

This is direct primary administrative evidence for the end of Foucher's Collège de France substitution after Lévi's 1897–1898 Asian mission.

## Cross-control

A modern institutional history based on course summaries independently states that Foucher replaced Lévi at the Collège de France in **1897–1898**, while in **1898–1899** Foucher instead replaced Louis Finot in the EPHE IVe Section. This matches the correction logic visible on the poster.

The EPHE 1897–1898 report also describes Foucher's course on `Villes saintes et pèlerinages de l'Hindouisme`, based on material gathered during his Indian mission. This independently explains the legible `Villes saintes ... de l'Hindouisme` wording in the Collège poster's spring-1898 Foucher block.

## Relation to the 6 Nov 1898 faculty minutes

Batch128 read the 6 Nov 1898 Collège de France faculty minutes and found a list of 1898–1899 substitute appointments in which neither Lévi nor Foucher appeared. By itself that was only a bounded negative.

The corrected course poster upgrades the interpretation:

1. spring 1898 poster state: Foucher explicitly printed as Lévi's `remplaçant`;
2. first-semester 1898–1899 correction state: Foucher replacement block cancelled;
3. 6 Nov 1898 substitute-approval list: no Lévi/Foucher pair;
4. EPHE 1898–1899: Lévi again actively teaching after the mission year.

Together these form a strong institutional return sequence. The safest formulation is:

> **By the opening of the 1898–1899 academic year, the Collège de France was no longer treating Foucher as the continuing substitute for Lévi's Sanskrit chair.**

This is stronger than the prior inference from mission dates alone.

## Important limit

The correction sheet does **not yet reveal the final revised Collège de France course title(s)** that Lévi actually advertised for first semester 1898–1899, because the replacement text is partly handwritten/obscured. It therefore does not yet directly prove the absence of a Pāli/Buddhism course. That question still requires either:

- the final clean 1898–1899 programme/poster;
- a cleaner frame of the handwritten replacement text;
- or an official published course summary.

The present poster nevertheless closes the substitution chronology itself.
