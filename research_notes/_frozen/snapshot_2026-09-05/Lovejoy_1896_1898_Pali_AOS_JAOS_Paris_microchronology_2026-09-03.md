### 20 Oct 1898 — actor-level formulation of a jurisdiction gap

Lovejoy proposes to elucidate Buddhist theoretical presuppositions and doctrines `from a philosophical point of view`.

He says, in substance:

- philologists have expounded the Buddhist literature but care little for philosophy;
- philosophers reconstruct Buddhist conceptions `more or less out of their own heads`;
- philosophical work has neglected `the technicalities of the texts`;
- Sylvain Lévi is the scholar he hopes to study under for this purpose.

This is the strongest actor-level statement yet recovered for the project's central **textual-control / philosophical-reconstruction jurisdiction problem**.

Its chronological role is **explicit formulation / continuation**, not demonstrated origin.
