# Pocock's method programme: dispersal into essays, 1973 `Verbalising`, and 2009 retrospective reassembly

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: **WHATMORE-PROVENANCE + POCOCK 2009 ACTOR-LEVEL REASSEMBLY SECURE / DIRECT FRAGMENT-OF-MONOGRAPH CLAIM STILL HOLD**

## Core result

The early Pocock–Skinner programme network now reveals two different publication/canon trajectories.

For Pocock:

`1968–69 PROGRAMME CONSCIOUSNESS / JOINT-MANIFESTO DISCUSSION`
+
`PLANNED METHOD MONOGRAPH: The Cave of Speech / How to Do Things to People with Words`
->
`METHOD PROBLEMS DISPERSED INTO ARTICLES / SCRIPTS`
->
`1973 Verbalising a Political Act: Towards a Politics of Speech`
+
`1980 / 1981 / 1987 further method essays`
->
`2009 POLITICAL THOUGHT AND HISTORY: ACTOR-CURATED RETROSPECTIVE METHOD CORPUS`.

For Skinner:

`1966–68 MANUSCRIPT / DISCUSSION / PERFORMANCE ECOLOGY`
->
`1969 CONCENTRATED PORTABLE ARTICLE`
->
`1988 SKINNER + CRITICS ANTHOLOGY`
->
`2002 AUTHORIAL REVISION`
->
`2012 / 2026 ANNIVERSARY CANONIZATION`.

The distinction is not `Pocock lacked a method` versus `Skinner had one`.

It is a difference in **canonical materialization path**:

`DISPERSED PROGRAMME -> LATE RETROSPECTIVE REASSEMBLY`

versus

`EARLY TEXTUAL CONCENTRATION -> REPEATED REPRINT / CRITIC PACKAGING`.

This offers a concrete mechanism for why Skinner 1969 became a more portable manifesto-object in later methodological memory.

---

## I. Whatmore's note architecture directly links the planned monograph programme to `Verbalising`

Richard Whatmore's 2016 introduction says that in the years after Pocock's October 1968 `joint manifesto` suggestion, Pocock seriously contemplated a monograph on method under possible titles including:

- `The Cave of Speech`;
- `How to Do Things to People with Words`.

Crucially, Whatmore's note supporting this statement points to Pocock's 1973 `Verbalising a Political Act: Towards a Politics of Speech`, as later reprinted in *Political Thought and History* (2009), pp.33–50.

This does **not** yet prove Pocock explicitly called the 1973 essay a chapter/fragment of the abandoned monograph.

But it securely establishes that Whatmore treats `Verbalising` as the published textual witness for that planned methodological programme.

Safe relation:

`PLANNED METHOD MONOGRAPH PROGRAMME`
-> documented through / materially witnessed by
`1973 VERBALISING`.

Hold:

`1973 VERBALISING = DIRECTLY REWORKED CHAPTER OF THE MONOGRAPH`.

That stronger genetic claim requires Pocock's own statement, draft comparison, or correspondence.

---

## II. 2009 creates an actor-curated method corpus

Pocock's *Political Thought and History: Essays on Theory and Method* (2009) assembles previously published essays under a first part titled `Political Thought as History`.

The sequence is deliberately chronological/methodological:

1. `The history of political thought: a methodological inquiry` (1962);
2. `Working on ideas in time` (1971);
3. `Verbalizing a political act: towards a politics of speech` (1973);
4. `Political ideas as historical events: political philosophers as historical actors` (1980);
5. `The reconstruction of discourse: towards the historiography of political thought` (1981);
6. `The concept of a language and the métier d'historien` (1987);
7. `Texts as events` (1987);
8. an intermezzo on Quentin Skinner (2004).

In the preface Pocock explicitly explains the collection as a retrospective attempt to indicate what he had taken, and still took, the method and its implications to be.

Therefore this is not merely a publisher-collected miscellany.

It is:

### `ACTOR-CURATED RETROSPECTIVE METHOD CORPUS`.

The corpus retrospectively performs a function the contemplated early method monograph never performed in one contemporaneous volume.

---

## III. Canonical materialization can be delayed

This case sharpens the canon infrastructure model.

A method can exist as:

- discussions;
- scripts;
- planned but unwritten/unfinished books;
- articles across decades;
- research practice;

before being packaged into a portable retrospective corpus.

Therefore:

`PROGRAMME EXISTENCE`
!=
`SINGLE MANIFESTO EXISTENCE`.

And:

`CANONICAL PORTABILITY`
can be delayed relative to
`METHOD PRACTICE / PROGRAMME CONSCIOUSNESS`.

Pocock's 2009 volume is a particularly clear case of **retrospective corpus construction**.

---

## IV. Why Skinner 1969 acquired a different memory profile

Skinner's 1969 essay had several properties Pocock's dispersed programme initially lacked:

- one citable object;
- a striking taxonomy of `mythologies`;
- broad methodological jurisdiction;
- polemical compression;
- a stable journal locus;
- later anthology republication;
- an authorial reply tradition;
- anniversary symposia.

Pocock's comparable method reflections were distributed across a 1962 essay, correspondence/scripts, *Politics, Language and Time*, the 1973 speech/politics essay, later discourse essays and practice-based reflections.

Thus later historiography faced asymmetric source ergonomics:

`ONE PORTABLE SKINNER TEXT`
vs
`DISTRIBUTED POCOCK METHOD CORPUS`.

This may help explain why Skinner more readily became the canonical `methodologist` even when contemporaries saw Pocock as a co-formulator and Pocock himself imagined joint or shared programme labels.

This is a mechanism hypothesis, not a claim about intellectual priority.

---

## V. `Verbalising` also indicates a different preferred unit

The 1973 essay's argument, as recoverable from later citations/reprints, treats speech not merely as an isolated authorial utterance but as a political move that alters the conditions of subsequent response.

A speaker uses available conventions, may challenge them, and thereby constrains or elicits countermoves from interlocutors. Language supplies resources for response; communication has a two-way character, and relationships of linguistic authority/power shape who can define or answer meaning.

Thus the characteristic unit is closer to:

`MOVE <-> COUNTERMOVE WITHIN A LANGUAGE / POLITICAL RELATION`

rather than only:

`AUTHOR -> SINGLE INTENDED SPEECH ACT`.

This makes Pocock's October 1968 self-description (`more sociological` vs Skinner's `more philosophical`) historically intelligible without turning it into a rigid school distinction.

In UNIT × RELATION terms:

- Skinner canonical tendency: `U3 agent/utterance + R1 meaning/R6 action`;
- Pocock `Verbalising` tendency: `U7 interactional discourse arena / U3 utterances + reciprocal R6 political action / R10 language transformation`.

The two approaches overlap but are not unit-identical.

---

## VI. Method formation and canon formation should therefore be separated

The emerging chronology now supports at least four layers:

1. **practice / problem formation** — historical research and discussion;
2. **programme consciousness** — actors recognize common or contrasting method ambitions;
3. **textual materialization** — article/book/script publication paths;
4. **canonical packaging** — anthology, collected essays, syllabus, anniversary volume.

For Pocock these layers are unusually separated in time.

For Skinner they become more tightly concentrated around 1969 and its subsequent republication history.

This provides a concrete history-of-knowledge explanation for later `founder` asymmetry that does not require assuming that the intellectually most original actor automatically becomes the canonical one.

## Evidence firewall

Secure:

- Whatmore says Pocock contemplated the method monograph and supports that statement with a note to `Verbalising`;
- `Verbalising` first appeared in *Political Theory* 1.1 (1973), pp.27–44/45 depending carrier;
- 2009 *Political Thought and History* reprints it pp.33–50 in a chronological method section;
- Pocock's 2009 preface says the volume is intended to indicate what he had taken and still took the method and its implications to be;
- the volume collects theory/method essays from 1962–2005.

Open:

- whether `Verbalising` was literally drafted as a chapter of `The Cave of Speech`;
- whether a manuscript of the planned monograph survives;
- exact genetic relation between 1969/71 letters and the 1973 essay;
- why Pocock abandoned the monograph form;
- whether editors encouraged the essay publication instead.

Do not write `abandoned book became the 1973 article` without direct genetic evidence.
