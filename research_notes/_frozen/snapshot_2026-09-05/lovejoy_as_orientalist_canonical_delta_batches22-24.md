# Canonical delta — Lovejoy as Orientalist through batches 22–24

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: current emphasis override for the older live-sweep queue

## Central research object

The project is **Arthur O. Lovejoy as an Orientalist**, understood as a historically variable set of scholarly practices rather than a permanent professional identity.

Notebook 005 / Marillier remains a high-value adjacent comparative-religion case. It should support the main question, not replace it.

## Current strict-core spine

1. **Harvard 1895–98:** Sanskrit/Buddhist-text training with Lanman; comparative religion with Everett; Hebrew religion with Toy (Wilson/archives; primary correspondence still to retrieve).
2. **1896–98 live Buddhist controversy:** Garbe/Jacobi/Senart/La Vallée Poussin establish the specialist Sāṁkhya–Buddhism / nidāna problem-field around notebook 004 and Lovejoy's intervention.
3. **1898 JAOS:** direct specialist Buddhist philology on `upādāna/upādisesa`, dependent origination, Buddhist psychology and Nirvāṇa.
4. **Paris 1898–99:** Lévi text-reading and Marillier comparative religion; 005 is evidence for one mode of this formation.
5. **1901:** *Syllabus: The Philosophy of Buddhism* — four lectures, primary artifact still missing.
6. **1902:** *Religion and the Time-Process* — Buddhism/Vedānta reappear in comparative category-history, with explicit authorial return to the 1898 paper.
7. **1907:** Deussen/Śaṅkara review — exact pp.23–24 confirmed; primary text pending; Wilson reports a Rāmānuja intervention.
8. **Hopkins mature phase:** Orientalist knowledge shifts toward distributed expertise and institutional networks: Haupt, Albright, Dumont, Lattimore; 1935 *Primitivism* explicitly incorporates specialist essays by Albright and Dumont.

## Historiographical formulation

A clean `1898 Buddhist paper → abandonment → mature history of ideas` story is no longer adequate.

The defensible model is:

> **early personal Orientalist practice → continued teaching/comparative use/reviewing through at least 1907 → later institutional and collaborative deployment of area expertise at Johns Hopkins.**

This does not make Lovejoy a lifelong professional Buddhologist/Indologist. It makes the forms and transformations of Orientalist practice themselves the historical problem.

## Evidence discipline

Keep four levels separate:

- specialist primary practice;
- teaching/review/comparative use;
- institutional or correspondence relation;
- inferred intellectual transmission.

The project should resist both overclaiming (`Lovejoy was continuously an Orientalist`) and premature closure (`Lovejoy never returned after 1898`).
