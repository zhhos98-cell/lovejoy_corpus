## Governing question

For each notebook sentence / proposition, separate:

1. `DIRECT QUOTATION / CLOSE TRANSLATION` — wording can be tied to a specific printed source/passsage;
2. `SOURCE-FRAMED PARAPHRASE` — Lovejoy follows a source's selection/order/framing but rewrites wording;
3. `SOURCE-FAMILY / ROUTER` — a synthesis (Warren, Oldenberg, Brinton, Robertson Smith, etc.) supplies a problem/case cluster, but exact lower witness may differ;
4. `LOWER WITNESS` — Lovejoy demonstrably descends to or independently cites an underlying ethnographic/philological witness;
5. `LOVEJOY INFERENTIAL SURPLUS` — proposition is not supplied by the cited source as such; Lovejoy adds a historical, semantic, causal, authenticity, classificatory or representativeness inference;
6. `RECOMPOSITION` — Lovejoy combines propositions or case-material from multiple sources into a new relation/claim;
7. `HOLD` — exact provenance remains unresolved.

Firewalls:

`SOURCE ON SAME PAGE != SOURCE OF EVERY SENTENCE`.

`CANONICAL TEXT != IMMEDIATE MATERIAL SOURCE`.

`TRANSLATOR / SYNTHESIZER FRAMING != PRIMARY TEXT ITSELF`.

`SOURCE PRIORITY != LOVEJOY UPTAKE`.

`QUOTE PROVENANCE != INFERENCE PROVENANCE`.

`CASE SOURCE != CLASSIFICATION SOURCE != GENERALIZATION SOURCE`.
