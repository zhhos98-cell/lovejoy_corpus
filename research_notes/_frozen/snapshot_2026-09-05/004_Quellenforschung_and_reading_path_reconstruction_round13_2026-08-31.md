# I. Stage 1 — begin inside an already methodologically articulated Indology

## pp.4–5 / MS 8–9: Garbe 1894 pp.150–151

The notebook opens `The Method of the Sankhya / Garbe p.150`.

Round-12 exact collation closes the source boundary: the long passage is a close English working translation/abridgment of Garbe 1894 pp.150–151.

Directly Garbe-owned propositions include:

- Indian systems account for their sources of knowledge / `pramāṇa`;
- orthodox systems show extensive agreement over inference;
- Vaiśeṣika–Nyāya logical material was adopted elsewhere;
- such extended inference theory in Sāṃkhya is a `foreign element`;
- a European expositor need notice it only insofar as it bears on the method of the system;
- Sāṃkhya recognizes perception, inference and reliable testimony.

Therefore 004 does **not** begin with Lovejoy inventing the distinction between historical provenance and local systematic relevance. He begins by closely reading an Indologist who already makes it.

Reading-path role:

`GARBE SYSTEMATIC-HISTORICAL EXPOSITION`
→ `LOVEJOY WORKING TRANSLATION`
→ establishes a source environment in which borrowed element, system method, and scholarly selection are already separable.

This is a field-availability fact, not a causal claim that Garbe taught Lovejoy his later proof architecture.

### p.22 Warren: a crucial Round-12 correction

The notebook says Warren treats taṇhā/upādāna as `identity`. Direct Warren collation shows that this is too strong as a description of Warren himself.

Warren writes attachment as `tenacity of desire` but also preserves an alternative stage distinction: desire before obtaining its object, attachment once the object is seized.

Therefore:

`WARREN = IDENTITY`
= **LOVEJOY'S SHARPENED CHARACTERIZATION**.

The actual micro-operation is:

`WARREN CLOSE CONTINUITY / ALTERNATIVE STAGE DISTINCTION`
→ `LOVEJOY CONSTRUES A STRONGER IDENTITY PROBLEM`
→ `FOUR ATTACHMENTS vs SIX DESIRES`
→ `REJECT STRONG IDENTITY`.

This correction matters directly for the current JHI Blog draft.

`COMPOSITENESS / BORROWED TERMS GRANTED`
