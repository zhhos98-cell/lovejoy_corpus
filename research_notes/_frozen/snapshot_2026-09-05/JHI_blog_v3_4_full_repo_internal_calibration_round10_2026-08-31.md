## Core result

**No full-repo evidence currently requires a large revision of v3.4.**

The blog's governing architecture survives the expanded corpus unusually well because the public draft already obeys distinctions that the later UNIT×RELATION work made explicit:

- biographical presence != analytical centrality;
- topic label != homogeneous historical unit;
- semantic identity != temporal sequence != classificatory inclusion != causation != transmission;
- resemblance != genealogy;
- source provenance != function after combination;
- notebook claim state != publication claim state;
- decomposition != anti-generalization;
- changed narrative center != disappearance;
- later visibility/classification != demonstrated causal determination by a discipline or venue.

Round 10 therefore functions mainly as a **reverse stress test**. It increases confidence in the existing public argument while preventing later methodological vocabulary from being retrojected into Lovejoy.

Strongest full-repo judgment:

> **v3.4 is not under-theorized because it omits Round-10 jargon. It is stronger precisely because the relation/unit controls remain backstage while the public prose stays source-led.**
