### p.66 / ms p.173 — ethical versus physical residue

The original supports the argument that `anupādisesa` in the relevant Sutta-Nipāta passages does not mean extinction of the five skandhas / physical death. Lovejoy reads the contrast as ethical: residual impurity / attachment versus freedom from it. Positive and negative uses can therefore apply across different living/post-mortem states without the word itself denoting destruction of the present skandhas.

Safe relation rule:

`TERMINOLOGICAL OPPOSITION != PHYSICAL-STATE OPPOSITION`.

### p.73 / ms p.147 — inherited practice, new rationale

The page directly supports the first-pass historical mechanism: destruction/burning of funerary property can acquire a new explanation over time. A practice initially intelligible as preventing return of the dead can later be explained as releasing/transferring the spirit of an object so that it belongs to the dead.

Safe operator:

`PRACTICE CONTINUITY + EXPLANATORY REINTERPRETATION`.

This should be kept distinct from a claim that the outward rite itself changed at the same moment as its rationale.

### p.89 / ms p.181 — intrinsic versus indwelling efficacy

The page confirms the sharp analytic distinction already present in the clean batch:

- fetish: efficacy through an indwelling/present spirit;
- amulet/charm/plant/drug: efficacy may be intrinsic to the object.

Lovejoy also refuses a clean retrospective split between `natural` and `supernatural` efficacy in the conceptual world he is reconstructing. The point is classificatory: apparently similar efficacious objects can work through different assumed mechanisms.

### p.103 / ms p.193 — developmental hypothesis tested by chronology

The original image confirms the branching developmental sketch and, more importantly, the explicit objection immediately following it. Lovejoy writes that the proposed development `involves a vicious circle` if agrarian sacrifice is supposed to pass from wild to domestic animal victims while domestication itself is assumed to precede agriculture.

He then rejects the universal premise behind the objection: the supposed fact is not generally true, and in Africa agriculture can be anterior to domestic animals; domestic animals need not assist agriculture.

The exact diagram labels and the authorities in the lower/right notes remain partly compressed, so they stay conservative.

Methodologically secure sequence:

`PROPOSE DEVELOPMENTAL SEQUENCE -> TEST WITH CHRONOLOGY -> IDENTIFY VICIOUS CIRCLE -> TEST PREMISE AGAINST COMPARATIVE EVIDENCE -> QUALIFY / RETAIN ONLY WHAT SURVIVES`.
