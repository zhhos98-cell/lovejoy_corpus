## Core result

A matched-window, matched-role census of *Journal of the History of Ideas* for 1973–1977 changes the earlier comparison in an important way.

The earlier PT dossier could safely say only that JHI had already demonstrated the **possibility** of internally addressed Indian, Chinese, Islamic and Latin-American intellectual-history objects at article level, because the JHI evidence then available was a selective historical seed rather than a like-for-like five-year denominator.

That denominator now exists.

Under a strict JHI substantive-unit rule harmonized as closely as possible to the PT 1973–77 census:

- JHI 1973–77 = **233 strict substantive units**;
- unambiguous title-level non-European/non-North-American regional/civilizational hits under the narrow geographic/object rule = **5**;
- PT 1973–77 = **122 strict substantive units**;
- corresponding PT hits = **2**.

Approximate strict incidence:

- JHI: **2.15%**;
- PT: **1.64%**.

That difference is small. It should **not** be used to claim that JHI was quantitatively or generally much more `global` than PT.

The stronger result is **role composition**.

JHI's five strict hits contain:

- **3 `INTERNAL_TRADITION_OBJECT`** cases;
- **1 `COMPARATIVE_COCASE`**;
- **1 `CONTACT_TRANSFORMATION`**.

PT's two strict hits contain:

- **0 `INTERNAL_TRADITION_OBJECT`**;
- **1 `COMPARATIVE_COCASE`**;
- **1 `EXTERNAL_GAZE / CONTACT`**.

Thus the same-window result is:

> **The overall title-level incidence of explicitly non-Euro-American regional objects is sparse in both venues. The clearer difference is that JHI repeatedly makes some non-Western/regional intellectual traditions into internally addressed article-level historical objects, whereas early PT's corresponding title-level cases enter through comparison or colonial/contact framing.**

This upgrades the prior existence/role comparison without turning it into a triumphalist JHI-globalism claim.

Core controls:

`ROLE COMPOSITION > RAW SHARE DIFFERENCE` for the present historical question;

`JHI INTERNAL-TRADITION CAPACITY != JHI GENERALLY GLOBAL`;

`PT INTERNATIONALISM != GLOBAL RESEARCH-OBJECT JURISDICTION`;

`LOW FREQUENCY != ABSENCE`;

`TITLE VISIBILITY != COMPLETE ARTICLE CONTENT`.

# I. Harmonized denominator

The PT census defines a strict substantive denominator that includes:

- research articles;
- named article-level comments/rejoinders;
- symposium/exchange components;
- other substantive issue units;

and excludes:

- editorials;
- book reviews;
- communications sections where not article-level substantive units;
- bibliographies;
- announcements;
- errata/front/back matter.

For JHI, the closest historical analogue is:

### Included

- ordinary research articles;
- substantive `Notes`;
- substantive `Discussion`, reply and rejoinder items.

JHI `Notes` in this period are often 8–15 pages and operate as independent research interventions, so excluding them would create an artificial genre mismatch with PT comments/rejoinders.

### Excluded

- `Review-Article` / `Review-Articles`;
- `Books Received`;
- front/back matter;
- acknowledgments;
- errata;
- explicit bibliographic surveys where the carrier is field surveillance rather than an ordinary historical argument.

The latter rule matters in JHI 37.2 (1976): `Machiavelli Studies since 1969` is a long, substantive item but an explicit literature survey. It is excluded from the strict denominator because PT's own bibliography layer is also excluded from its 122-unit substantive denominator.

Thus:

`LONG ITEM != AUTOMATIC STRICT RESEARCH UNIT`.

Carrier role controls inclusion.

`STRICT ARTICLE-LAYER SPARSITY`

This is directly comparable to PT, where Chinese statecraft, Averroes, Gandhi-related literature and other non-Euro-American materials often appear more visibly in review, communication or bibliographic-surveillance roles than in internally addressed original research titles.

## Strict denominator

- JHI: 233 substantive units / 5 narrow regional hits ≈ 2.15%.
- PT: 122 substantive units / 2 narrow regional hits ≈ 1.64%.

The denominator difference reflects journal issue density and genre architecture. The percentages are low in both venues.

Do **not** write:

`JHI WAS MUCH MORE GLOBAL THAN PT`.

The data do not warrant that.

## Role composition

### JHI

- internal tradition: 3;
- comparative co-case: 1;
- contact transformation: 1.

### PT

- internal tradition: 0;
- comparative co-case: 1;
- external-gaze/contact: 1.

This makes a narrower historical claim possible:

> **Within the same 1973–77 window and under a matched title-level role test, JHI visibly retained an article-level capacity for internally addressed regional intellectual traditions that early PT's substantive-title layer did not yet display, even though both venues had sparse non-Euro-American title incidence overall.**

This is an observed carrier-role difference, not its cause.

Possible causal explanations remain open:

- inherited journal jurisdiction;
- author/submission ecology;
- editorial networks;
- disciplinary location;
- canon availability;
- field-specific historiographical traditions;
- language/access structures.

`OUTPUT ROLE DIFFERENCE`
!= `EDITORIAL POLICY CAUSE`.

# VII. What this does to the modernization story

The matched census strengthens a non-linear result.

The newer PT carrier is genuinely innovative in:

- method/ideological pluralism;
- school non-identification;
- comments/replies;
- symposium architecture;
- communications;
- international/transatlantic personnel and circulation.

But those innovations do not automatically produce broader civilizational historical units in the original research layer.

JHI's older and more eclectic jurisdiction, meanwhile, can still make Confucian transmission, Latin-American political traditions and Chilean intellectual history into direct article-level objects.

Therefore:

`METHOD MODERNIZATION`
!= `GLOBALIZATION OF HISTORICAL UNITS`;

`NEWER SPECIALIST CARRIER`
!= `BROADER CIVILIZATIONAL OBJECT SET`;

`OLDER ECLECTIC VENUE`
!= `NARROWER OBJECT JURISDICTION BY DEFINITION`.

This is a stronger empirical basis for rejecting a simple:

`OLD HISTORY OF IDEAS`
-> `NEW CONTEXTUAL POLITICAL THEORY`
-> `BROADER / MORE PLURAL FIELD`

sequence.

# VIII. Implication for UNIT × RELATION

The census itself demonstrates why `global` cannot be encoded as a single property of a journal.

At minimum distinguish:

- international authors/personnel;
- circulation across national venues;
- comparative co-cases;
- colonial/contact objects;
- internally addressed regional traditions;
- review/bibliographic surveillance;
- civilizational textual traditions.

Thus:

> **GLOBALITY / INTERNATIONALISM IS RELATION-AND-ROLE SPECIFIC, NOT A STICKY VENUE ATTRIBUTE.**

The same journal can be international in circulation, Euro-American in much of its canonical article center, and simultaneously open to selected internally addressed regional traditions.
