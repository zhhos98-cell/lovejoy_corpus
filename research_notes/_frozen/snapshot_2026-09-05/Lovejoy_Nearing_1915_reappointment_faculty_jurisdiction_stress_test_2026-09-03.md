## Core result

The Scott Nearing case is a more direct stress test for the first 1915 Practical Proposal — faculty participation in reappointments — than the Utah case alone.

But chronology does not permit a simple source claim.

Lovejoy's 1914 governance programme already assigned substantial personnel jurisdiction to faculty. The Nearing investigation developed during the same late-1915 period in which the General Report and Lovejoy's handwritten Practical Proposals were being prepared. The exact date of the surviving preliminary Nearing report relative to Lovejoy's undated handwritten `Suggestions` is not yet closed.

Therefore the safe relation is:

`PRE-EXISTING LOVEJOY FACULTY-JURISDICTION PROGRAMME`
+
`UTAH FACULTY-EXCLUSION STRESS TEST`
+
`NEARING TRUSTEES-OVERRIDE-FACULTY-JUDGMENT STRESS TEST`
-> `1915 PRESCRIPTIVE CLOSURE ON FACULTY ACTION IN REAPPOINTMENT`.

Do not write `Nearing caused Proposal 1` until version chronology is recovered.

## I. Why the Nearing case is unusually clean for jurisdiction

Nearing was an awkward test case for the AAUP founders.

Tiede shows that Lovejoy, Seligman and Fetter had serious reservations about:

- Nearing's tact/courtesy;
- some of his extramural utterances;
- the quality of his scholarship.

Lovejoy himself thought the trustees might be able to present a fairly effective case against Nearing on some dimensions.

This makes the committee's eventual argument more analytically useful.

The conclusion does not depend on liking Nearing or independently certifying his scholarship.

Instead Lovejoy asks who had already rendered the competent professional judgment.

---

## II. Local professional judgment had already been made

The central institutional facts are:

- Nearing held professorial rank with an expectation of reappointment;
- his departmental faculty recommended reappointment;
- his chair recommended reappointment;
- his dean recommended reappointment;
- trustees refused reappointment.

Lovejoy's move is jurisdictional.

Because the faculty had rendered the relevant professional judgment, the investigating committee did not need to conduct its own de novo inquiry into Nearing's professional qualifications/work.

Thus:

`QUESTION: IS NEARING PROFESSIONALLY QUALIFIED?`

is reassigned to

`LOCAL PROFESSIONAL JUDGMENT ALREADY RENDERED`.

The committee's historical question becomes:

`WHAT LICENSES LAY TRUSTEES TO OVERRIDE THAT JUDGMENT?`.

This is not merely a free-speech claim.

---

## III. The unit of injury is rescaled from the individual to faculty/profession

Lovejoy's report reasoning says the summary action is not only or mainly an action against the individual teacher.

Because trustees override a faculty recommendation on an essentially professional matter, the action bears upon:

- local faculty as a body;
- academic profession at large.

In Lovejoy's stronger preliminary draft, later softened for more conservative committee members, he treats hasty/informal overriding of carefully considered professional judgment as in itself an academic-freedom infringement.

Thus:

`INDIVIDUAL NONREAPPOINTMENT`
-> `OVERRIDE OF LOCAL PROFESSIONAL JURISDICTION`
-> `PROFESSION-LEVEL INJURY`.

This is an unusually clear instance of analytic-unit rescaling.

---

## IV. Relation to Lovejoy's 1914 programme

The Nearing relation does not begin in 1915.

Lovejoy's 1914 `Profession of the Professorate` already calls for:

- reapportionment of powers/responsibilities;
- professoriate as a self-governing body;
- faculty/president participation in appointments/promotions/personnel;
- restrictions on unilateral lay-corporate control.

Nearing supplies an adverse case in which the exact opposite structure appears:

`FACULTY PROFESSIONAL JUDGMENT`
-> `LAY TRUSTEE OVERRIDE`.

Thus the case is best read as a **stress test / application** of an existing Lovejoy governance relation, not its origin.

## V. Relation to Proposal 1

The final Practical Proposal 1 says official action on reappointments/refusals should occur only with advice and consent of a representative faculty body.

Tiede establishes that the Practical Proposals were based entirely on Lovejoy's handwritten notes, in which his stronger proposed `senate` had advice and consent over:

- appointments;
- promotions;
- reappointments;
- refusals to reappoint.

The Nearing case is structurally almost exact:

`LOCAL FACULTY RECOMMENDS REAPPOINTMENT`
+
`TRUSTEES OVERRIDE`
-> `PROFESSIONAL-JURISDICTION CONFLICT`.

But source chronology remains open:

- Nearing was not renewed 14 Jun 1915;
- case was publicly active through summer;
- Lovejoy stressed local faculty position before the formal committee was fully underway;
- trustees issued their fuller explanation in October;
- Lovejoy chaired the investigation;
- `Preliminary Report of the Committee of Inquiry on the Dismissal of Professor Scott Nearing...` survives in Pound Papers, Reel 9;
- Lovejoy -> Seligman, 12 Oct 1915 exists in Cornell `Nearing Case`;
- Lovejoy's handwritten General-Report suggestions are unsigned/undated but precede Seligman's 20 Nov prefinal.

Therefore:

`STRONG STRUCTURAL CONCURRENCE`
+
`SAME ACTOR / SAME COMMITTEE YEAR`

but

## VI. Nearing also reinforces tenure specification

The investigating report's two main points include Nearing's professorial rank and expectation of reappointment.

The later report also analyzes Pennsylvania's revised tenure/reappointment rules and how they would have applied to Nearing.

Thus Nearing supplies another independent 1915 input for:

- rank/tenure expectation;
- presumption of reappointment;
- notice;
- faculty participation.

This further weakens any Utah-alone origin story for tenure rules.

The correct historical object is **multi-case procedural consolidation**.

---

## VII. Methodologically important: competence is not re-litigated when proper jurisdiction has judged it

The committee's own doubts about Nearing's scholarship make this particularly important.

Lovejoy does not allow personal/committee doubts to erase the local professional decision.

Safe operation:

`COMMITTEE HAS PRIVATE SUBSTANTIVE DOUBTS`
+
`LOCAL COMPETENT PROFESSIONAL BODY HAS FORMALLY JUDGED`
-> `DO NOT REOPEN PROFESSIONAL-QUALIFICATION QUESTION WITHOUT WARRANT`.

This is a jurisdiction-sensitive evidence rule.

It resembles the Utah report's insistence that evidence/license and decision competence must be distinguished, but the relation is not identical.

---

## VIII. Preliminary draft versus final committee text

Tiede records a stronger Lovejoy preliminary formulation:

- trustees had not only rejected the faculty recommendation;
- they had failed to show reasonable respect/courtesy to the educational staff;
- hasty informal overriding of professional judgment could itself constitute an infringement of academic freedom.

This language was softened to accommodate conservative committee members.

Therefore the Nearing file is a high-value version target because it can show:

`LOVEJOY STRONG JURISDICTION CLAIM`
-> `COMMITTEE MODERATION`
-> `FINAL REPORT`.

This closely parallels the weakening of Lovejoy's 1915 faculty-senate proposal in the General Report.
