## Retained interpretive value

Friedman remains useful for four bounded controls:

1. Carnap is not historically intelligible as a free-standing, timeless `verificationist`; his formation includes neo-Kantian/scientific-philosophy problems and Fregean mathematical logic.
2. Formal reconstruction is a philosophical technology, not merely a topic; compare operations rather than school labels.
3. Anti-metaphysics depends on claims about the jurisdiction of logic, language and scientific meaning, so a surface `metaphysics versus science` opposition is historically too coarse.
4. Shared vocabulary such as `meaning`, `analysis`, or `logic` does not establish shared semantics or method.

Use Friedman only to locate the local problem-space; use primary Carnap/Lovejoy evidence for the actual claim.
