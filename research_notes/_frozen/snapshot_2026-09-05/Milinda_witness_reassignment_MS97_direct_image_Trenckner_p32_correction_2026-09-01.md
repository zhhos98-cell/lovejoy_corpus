## 1. Why this correction is necessary

Two earlier September 1 synthesis files were written before the latest direct-image pass on notebook 004 and contain a now-superseded conjunction:

- they correctly recognized that MS97 formulates the `upādāna` / karma fact-versus-manner distinction;
- but they inherited an earlier transcription in which the opening abbreviation of that question had been read as `MP`, and they also referred to the published Milinda witness as `MP p.33`.

Both points now require correction.

The direct original-image second pass and the public JAOS/JSTOR footnote register establish:

1. **MS97 reads `Up.` = upādāna, not `MP` = Milinda Pañha.**
2. The 1898 published footnote is **`MP. p. 32. 12`**, not p.33.

This does not weaken the Milinda-to-print publication-path argument. It changes its architecture from simple witness retention to **witness reassignment / evidentiary reattachment in publication**.

`witness reassignment` and `evidentiary reattachment` are project terms, not Lovejoy's vocabulary.

## 2. MS95 — explicit Milinda witness for an alternate causal sequence

Direct-image control of notebook 004, PDF p.32 / manuscript p.95, secures an explicit Milinda source:

`Paticca-samuppāda. Various versions given by author F. MP (II, 32) ...`

The recorded series runs in substance:

`eye + forms`
`-> eye-consciousness`
`-> contact`
`-> sensation`
`-> desire`
`-> attachment`

Lovejoy then performs his own relation audit. He treats form and consciousness as locally parallel / order-invertible, later links through taṇhā as fixed in sequence, 3–9 as a single group, 1–2 as separately treatable, and 10–12 as 3–9 viewed from another standpoint.

Therefore MS95 is the secure notebook locus for:

> **Milinda as a witness used to test the internal geometry, grouping and sequence-rules of dependent origination.**

## 3. MS97 — analytical causal partition, but not an MP citation

Direct original-image control of PDF p.33 / manuscript p.97 gives the heading:

`May Up. be called t. cause of t. fact of continued existence, & karma t. cause of t. manner of continued existence?`

followed by:

`Hardy, p. 394.`

The earlier first-pass `May MP be called ...` is superseded.

Thus the secure notebook architecture is:

`UPĀDĀNA -> FACT OF CONTINUED EXISTENCE`

`KARMA -> MANNER OF CONTINUED EXISTENCE`.

But the **immediate visible source attachment on MS97 is Hardy p.394**, not Milinda.

This distinction must be preserved. MS97 is direct evidence for Lovejoy's analytical causal partition; it is not direct evidence that he derived that partition from a Milinda passage on that page.

## 4. 1898 publication — Milinda is attached as proposition-specific warrant

JSTOR's extracted reference register for Lovejoy's 1898 article preserves three explicit Milinda loci in Section I (`upādāna`):

- `Milinda Pañha, p. 65. 11`;
- **`MP. p. 32. 12`**;
- `Milinda Pañha, p. 60`.

The p.32 reference is the paired warrant for the proposition that upādāna accounts for renewed existence / rebirth per se. The p.65 locus supports karmic differentiation / the particular condition of rebirth; p.60 supports vedanā / sensation inside the psychological reconstruction.

The corresponding publication-level causal partition is:

- karma = cause of the **particular condition** of rebirth;
- upādāna = cause of the **fact of rebirth itself**.

Thus the same analytic partition already visible on MS97 is stabilized in print, but **print now attaches Milinda p.65 and p.32 as local textual warrants**.

## 5. Trenckner control closes the printed pagination

The separate source-resolution dossier has already collated Lovejoy's three printed Milinda references against Vilhelm Trenckner's 1880 Roman-script Pāli edition, *The Milindapañho*.

The key results are:

### p.32

Trenckner p.32 contains the explicit conditional:

`sace ... saupādāno bhavissāmi paṭisandahissāmi, sace anupādāno ... na paṭisandahissāmi`

in substance: with upādāna, renewed conjunction/rebirth; without upādāna, no renewed conjunction.

This matches Lovejoy's published burden for **upādāna -> fact of rebirth**.

### p.65

Trenckner p.65 treats differentiation through distinct karmas, matching Lovejoy's published burden for **karma -> particular condition of rebirth**.

### p.60

Trenckner p.60 treats the arising and characteristic of `vedanā`, matching Lovejoy's published sensation / pleasure-pain locus.

Therefore the printed citation interface is now closed as Trenckner pagination for these three loci.
