## Research move

The source genealogy behind 005’s blood/charm/fetish pages can now be tightened one step further. The important result is not merely that Marillier used the Melanesian term `mana` in 1900. We can reconstruct a longer sequence around **R. H. Codrington’s 1891 *The Melanesians***:

> **Codrington 1891 → Marillier reviews Codrington in 1892 and builds Melanesian taboo/myth courses around the same source field → Lovejoy 005 visibly lists and cites Codrington → Marillier 1900 generalizes `mana` in `Religion`, with the first occurrence localized to printed p.349 → Lovejoy’s 005 later distinguishes indwelling spirit from efficacy intrinsic to objects → 1906 `Primitive Energetics`.**

This still does not prove a single linear transmission route. It does, however, replace a generic `mana` background with a source-specific and institutionally continuous genealogy.

## 3. Marillier’s EPHE programme then makes Codrington a working research source

The 1892–93 EPHE report describes Marillier’s work on Oceanian taboo and says Melanesian institutions, rites and legends were often observed after their original beliefs had begun to dissolve; rites/institutions could survive the beliefs that had generated them.

Primary control:
https://www.persee.fr/doc/ephe_0000-0002_1892_num_6_2_19363

The 1893–94 report is even more specific. In the critical study of Melanesian heroic/divine legends it explicitly names **Codrington** as the collector used for Melanesia proper and **Haddon** for Torres Strait material.

Primary control:
https://www.persee.fr/doc/ephe_0000-0002_1893_num_7_3_19386

Thus Codrington was not merely a book Marillier happened to review. It became part of his EPHE research infrastructure years before Lovejoy’s 1898–99 participation.

## 7. Why p.349 matters for 005

The high-yield 005 distinction appears later in the blood/charm/fetish sequence, especially PDF pp.87–88.

### 005 p.87 / MS p.177

Lovejoy treats blood as potentially possessing **efficacious power in itself**, rather than merely serving as a pledge/index of a spirit’s presence. Saliva, perspiration, urine and excrement can acquire analogous magical value.

### 005 p.88 / MS p.179

He separates two causal ontologies:

- **fetish**: effective because a spirit is believed to inhabit or be present in the object;
- **charm / amulet / stone / plant / ju-ju / gri-gri**: the object may be credited with efficacy **in itself** for a particular purpose.

This is a much sharper conceptual bridge to 1906 than the mere occurrence of `mana` vocabulary. The notebook has already isolated the key relation:

> **personal indwelling agency vs intrinsic efficacious property/power.**

## 9. The source genealogy is now more concrete than `French pre-animism`

The strongest source-specific sequence is now:

> **1891 Codrington, *The Melanesians***
> → **1892 Marillier reviews Codrington, RHR 25:231–232**
> → **1892–94 Marillier EPHE courses use Melanesian taboo/myth material, explicitly naming Codrington**
> → **1898–99 Lovejoy actively participates in Marillier’s EPHE work; 005 itself lists/cites Codrington**
> → **1900 Marillier `Religion`, printed p.349: `mana` enters the synthetic definition of supernatural power**
> → **005 pp.87–88: Lovejoy distinguishes indwelling spirit from intrinsic efficacy**
> → **1906: impersonal, transferable, quasi-mechanical efficacy generalized as `Primitive Energetics`.**

This is a genealogy of **source use + classification + abstraction**, not merely terminology.

## 10. Important correction to the Endokannibalismus page-range control

A fresh check of the contemporary RHR 34 table of contents clarifies the pagination of Marillier’s `Endokannibalismus` review.

The sequence is:

- `R Steinmetz Endokannibalismus M L Marillier` — **p.113**;
- `Une fausse lettre de saint Basile contre Eunomius` — **p.116**;
- `J. B. Bérenger-Féraud, Superstitions et survivances, M. L. Marillier` — **p.119**.

Therefore Marillier’s Steinmetz notice is very strongly localized to **pp.113–115**, pending direct facsimile collation. The earlier shorthand `beginning p.113 and before p.119` was too loose because an intervening item begins on p.116.

Contemporary volume control:
https://books.google.com/books?id=Gx4pAAAAYAAJ

Batch 34 already records the correct `pp.113–115` inference. Any later working note that says `before p.119` should be read as superseded by this correction.

## 11. Paper-level payoff

The most useful formulation after this pass is:

> **The route into Lovejoy’s 1906 category can be reconstructed through a concrete source ecology rather than a retrospective history of “pre-animism.” Codrington’s *The Melanesians* was reviewed and then used as a working source by Marillier, appears independently in Lovejoy’s 005 bibliography, and supplied the vocabulary of `mana` that Marillier generalized on printed p.349 of his 1900 `Religion`. Yet the decisive operation in 005 is Lovejoy’s own causal distinction between a spirit inhabiting an object and efficacy intrinsic to the object itself.**

This puts the historical weight on the **operation of abstraction**, not on vocabulary alone.
