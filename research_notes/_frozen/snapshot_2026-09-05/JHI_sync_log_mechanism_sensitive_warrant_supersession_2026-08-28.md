# JHI sync log — supersession: from logical migration / evidentiary asymmetry to mechanism-sensitive warrant

Date: 2026-08-28  
Status: **RESTART-SAFE GOVERNING MODEL / SUPERSEDES SEVERAL STRONGER EARLIER HYPOTHESES**

## Why this sync exists

The logical-analysis branch has changed substantially during the 28 August run. Several initially plausible formulations have been weakened or directly falsified by later primary evidence.

This note records the current governing model and explicitly marks superseded claims so that future work does not reopen already-corrected assumptions.

---

# I. Superseded model 1 — `formal logic migrated into history of ideas`

### Why it fails

Lovejoy's uses of `logical` differ by context:

- formal logic as object;
- actor-explicit logical analysis in philosophy/history;
- analyst-observed logic-shaped relation partition in philology/comparative work.

1924, 1936 and 1941 explicitly distinguish logical relations from psychological, affective, alogical and historical/genetic relations.

### Current replacement

> **Different local analytical repertoires may share a portable discipline of typed relations and inferential jurisdiction; formal logic is one arena, not the demonstrated universal source.**

---

# II. Superseded model 2 — `decomposition is the stable Lovejoyian method`

### Why it fails

1932 and 1936 show that Lovejoy also returns from elements to complex unity, pattern and arrangement. Historical novelty can lie in configuration rather than components.

### Current replacement

> **Scale is selected problem-dependently. Lovejoy moves between component, complex, pattern, tradition and system depending on the historical question.**

---

# III. Superseded model 3 — `unit identity is the first problem`

### Why it fails

Before asking whether an idea persists across contexts, Lovejoy's practice repeatedly requires target individuation:

- identify what relation/object exists in the recipient text;
- distinguish senses/denotations;
- separate generic resemblance from diagnostic target identity.

### Current replacement

The sequence begins:

`scale selection`
→ `target-state individuation`
→ only then `identity / genealogy / causal explanation`.

---

# IV. Superseded model 4 — `genealogy strength is one-dimensional`

### Why it fails

Bruno–Spinoza, Monboddo–Rousseau, China, Schiller–Schlegel and Milton show that different dimensions can diverge.

A direct transmission edge can exist while causal dependence is limited; a tradition can be secure while immediate source remains unidentified; recipient prior state can reduce source delta.

### Current replacement

Genealogy should be treated as a vector including at least:

- `T` target-state identification;
- `R` recipient prior state;
- `A` ambient availability / tradition;
- `E` direct edge/contact;
- `X` alternative routes / exclusivity;
- `D` causal delta / weight;
- `P` serial propagation;
- relation-specific confidence.

No single `influence=yes/no` verdict captures all dimensions.

---

# V. Superseded model 5 — `Lovejoy's causal ontology privileges ideas over social/affective causes`

### Why it fails

1938 `The Historiography of Ideas` programmatically allows bidirectional causation:

`ideas / climates of opinion`
↔ `institutions / laws / mores / social conditions`.

Ideas may be causes or consequences/rationalizations of social conditions.

1941 direct primary goes further in actual historical explanation:

- prior affective taste can predispose acceptance of a justificatory philosophy;
- leaders' ambitions and followers' passions can produce ideological rationalizations;
- ideology can then mediate ambitions/passions into political deeds and mass action;
- prior intellectual conditioning can determine ideological receptivity.

### Current replacement

> **Lovejoy's mature causal ontology is genuinely pluralist and often reciprocal.**

---

# VI. Superseded model 6 — `Lovejoy's evidence regime is basically textual`

### Why it fails

1924/1932 landscape/Gothic studies treat garden design, built form, aesthetic practice and landscape taste as historically probative evidence and chronological carriers.

### Current replacement

Distinguish:

1. **evidentiary carrier** — text, practice, built artifact, institution, behavior, etc.;
2. **claimed causal engine** — idea, affect, institution, social structure, practice, semantic change, etc.

A non-textual carrier does not automatically imply a non-ideational causal explanation.

---

# VII. Weakened model — `evidentiary asymmetry favors intellectual causes`

### Why the strong form fails

1941 demonstrates positive affective and political causal mechanisms in Lovejoy's own historical argument, not merely programmatic permission.

Landscape practice also has a genuine evidentiary route.

### What remains live

A narrower question survives:

> **Do different causal mechanisms receive different levels of verification density, mechanism specificity and counterfactual control in Lovejoy's actual case law?**

This is now a practice-quality question, not a causal-admissibility question.

---

# VIII. New governing hypothesis — mechanism-sensitive warrant

The best current abstraction is:

> **Lovejoy's strongest recurring discipline is control over inferential promotion. A candidate relation becomes historically assertible when the target is sufficiently individuated, a mediation mechanism is specified, and evidence shows that mechanism operating at the relevant scale.**

This is more precise than `logical analysis`, `decomposition`, or `textual influence`.

Working architecture:

`problem-dependent scale`
→ `target-state individuation`
→ `typed relation / candidate cause`
→ `mediation mechanism`
→ `relation-specific evidence`
→ `confidence allocation`
→ `controlled promotion to stronger claim`
→ `reaggregation / causal weight / historical configuration`.

---

# IX. Why mechanism specificity now looks important

## Weakly mediated case

1937 Trinkhaus:

`Athenian class hierarchy`
~ `Platonic cosmic hierarchy`.

Lovejoy remains agnostic because structural resemblance/coexistence does not provide a sufficiently specified historical mediation.

## More strongly mediated psychological case

1941 Shakespeare taste:

`prior repressed taste`
→ `receptivity to a philosophy that justifies it`.

Lovejoy offers a positive but bounded causal attribution.

## More strongly mediated political case

1941:

`ambitions/passions`
→ `ideological rationalization`
→ `mass action / political deeds`.

Again positive but bounded by necessity/sufficiency caveats.

## Textual genealogy

China / Bruno / Coleridge:

carrier, contact, uptake, reuse or justificatory role can be specified.

### Hypothesis

> **The main threshold may be mechanism-sensitive rather than ontology-sensitive: resemblance or coexistence is weak; specified mediation that can be observed historically permits stronger promotion.**

This must be tested across additional cases.

---

# X. 1944 adds an explicit hypothesis/fact distinction

In `Reply to Professor Spitzer`, Lovejoy treats claims about discriminable recurrent ideas and intellectual compounds as working hypotheses.

Their truth is a matter of historical fact and must be checked case by case against the relevant evidence, here explicitly texts because the proposition concerns textual/intellectual recurrence.

This supplies a mature epistemic formula:

`analytical construction`
≠ `historical fact`

and

`working hypothesis`
→ `historical verification`.

It supports the controlled-promotion model but does not solve the identity criterion.

---

# XI. Current identity problem

The unit-idea issue is now partitioned into:

1. scale selection;
2. target individuation;
3. local conceptual/propositional identity;
4. logical/inferential profile;
5. configuration/function;
6. historical recurrence;
7. genetic continuity;
8. causal weight.

Lovejoy can often distinguish these levels, but his explicit criterion for when transformed components remain `the same idea` remains incomplete.

The strongest Skinner-facing question remains:

> **What warrants trans-contextual identity after semantic, pragmatic, motivational and configurational change?**

But this question should no longer be posed as if Lovejoy believed in context-free essences or treated analytical units as a priori entities.

---

# XII. Current strongest developmental model

The repository now supports a non-teleological sequence:

`local specialist problem-solving in multiple fields`
→ `relation-specific discriminations and proof controls`
→ `selective publication / claim calibration`
→ `cross-context philosophical-historical use`
→ `increasingly explicit differentiation of relation types`
→ `mature methodological abstraction as revisable working hypotheses`.

This is compatible with Lovejoy's retrospective statement that his general conceptions grew out of rather than preceded most special inquiries.

Do not narrate this as a single method being invented in 1898 and preserved unchanged.

---

1. **Institution/social-condition → idea positive case:** find actual Lovejoy historical analyses, not programme statements, with a specified institutional/social mediation mechanism.
2. **Verification-density comparison:** compare evidentiary support in textual, psychological, affective, political/social and practice-based causal cases.
3. **1944 direct facsimile lock:** recover original JHI pp.204–219 before publication-grade quotation.
4. **Identity criterion census:** collect explicit actor judgments of same / modified / analogous / different / uncertain across mature corpus.
5. **Mechanism vocabulary:** search `predispose`, `condition`, `mediate`, `rationalization`, `instrument`, `factor`, `sufficient`, `necessary`, `influence`, `cause`, `motive`, `association`, `environment`.
6. Test whether mechanism-sensitive warrant appears outside the best-known Romanticism/genealogy cases.

---

# XIV. Guardrails

Do not claim:

- Lovejoy's historical method is simply formal logic;
- decomposition is always the preferred scale;
- social or affective causation is excluded;
- historical evidence is text-only;
- Lovejoy is a contextualist in the later Cambridge-school sense;
- unit-idea identity has been fully justified by Lovejoy's empirical framing;
- mechanism-sensitive warrant is already proven as one consciously articulated invariant method across his career.

Preferred shorthand:

> **The strongest recurrence is an epistemic architecture rather than one technical procedure: identify the target, type the relation, specify mediation, match evidence to the relation, and control the promotion from possibility to historical fact. The thresholds and successes remain uneven across cases.**
