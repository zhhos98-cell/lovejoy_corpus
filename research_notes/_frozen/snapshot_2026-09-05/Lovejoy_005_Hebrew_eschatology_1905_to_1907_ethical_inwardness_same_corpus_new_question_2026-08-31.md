## Core result

A previously underdeveloped notebook-005 comparison can now be stated more precisely.

Notebook 005 contains a directly dated `Hist. Relig. — Dec. 20, 1905` Hebrew / comparative-religion teaching-research block. Lovejoy's 1907 `The Origins of Ethical Inwardness in Jewish Thought` uses a substantially overlapping Old Testament / Wisdom textual corpus.

But the two objects are not the same argument at different stages.

The safer relation is:

`SAME / OVERLAPPING TEXTUAL CORPUS`
+
`DIFFERENT QUESTION`
-> `DIFFERENT HISTORICAL OBJECT / CLAIM ARCHITECTURE`.

This is a strong example of **corpus reuse without simple draft-to-publication identity**.

## II. 1907 published object

Arthur O. Lovejoy, `The Origins of Ethical Inwardness in Jewish Thought`, *American Journal of Theology* 11.2 (April 1907), 228–249.

JSTOR issue/article metadata are direct and public.

Existing repo primary control:
`research_notes/Toy_Lovejoy_1907_authority_scope_logical_chronological_redivision_control_2026-08-26.md`.

The published article asks a different question:

- Christian ethics is characteristically associated with inward virtue / integrity of heart;
- Lovejoy argues that an antecedent development can be reconstructed within Jewish Wisdom literature;
- he examines Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ben Sira, Ecclesiastes and Wisdom of Solomon;
- he distinguishes relative textual chronology from the logical sequence/stage of an ethical conception;

Thus the article object is:

`DEVELOPMENT OF ETHICAL INWARDNESS WITHIN WISDOM LITERATURE`,

not:

`HEBREW ESCHATOLOGY / SOUL SEMANTICS / AFTERLIFE`.

## III. Overlap without identity

The corpora overlap significantly:

- Job;
- Psalms;
- Proverbs;
- Ecclesiastes;
- Old Testament textual interpretation more generally.

But the propositions differ.

### Notebook questions

`WHAT DO THE DEAD KNOW?`

`WHAT IS SHEOL?`

`WHAT DO NEPHESH / RUACH DENOTE?`

`HOW DO BLOOD / BREATH / SOUL CONCEPTS RELATE?`

`HOW SHOULD HEBREW ESCHATOLOGY BE POSITIONED IN A COMPARATIVE RELIGION OUTLINE?`

### 1907 questions

`WHEN / HOW DOES MORAL VALUE MOVE FROM EXTERNAL CONDUCT TOWARD INTEGRITY OF HEART?`

`WHICH WISDOM TEXTS EMBODY WHICH LOGICAL STAGE OF THAT DEVELOPMENT?`

Therefore:

`SAME TEXT`
!= `SAME QUESTION`
!= `SAME HISTORICAL UNIT`.

## IV. Why this is stronger than a simple continuity claim

It would be tempting to write:

`005 HEBREW NOTES -> 1907 ARTICLE`.

That would be too strong and too crude.

The current evidence does not establish that the dated 1905 pages are a draft of the 1907 article.

Instead they show that Lovejoy had an active Old Testament / Hebrew historical corpus in hand and could later **repartition the corpus around a different relation**.

Possible operation:

`TEXTUAL CORPUS RETAINED`
-> `QUESTION CHANGED`
-> `ANALYTIC UNIT REBUILT`
-> `NEW PUBLISHED ARGUMENT`.

This resembles later repo findings where:

- case unit can change while claim stays fixed;
- claim can change while some evidence stays fixed;
- the same source can carry different roles at different moments.

But it is a Lovejoy-specific early instance, not merely a later method analogy.

## V. Toy relation becomes more precise

The 1907 article explicitly accepts Toy as an authority for the relative chronology of the Wisdom materials.

This is important because it shows that the new published object has a new evidentiary architecture:

`TOY RELATIVE CHRONOLOGY`
-> scaffold / constraint

while

`LOVEJOY LOGICAL DEVELOPMENT OF ETHICAL INWARDNESS`
-> separate reconstruction.

The notebook's Hebrew eschatology pages do not need Toy to have the same role.

Therefore:

`SAME CORPUS`
+
`NEW QUESTION`
can also generate
`NEW AUTHORITY ALLOCATION`.

This is another reason not to treat source lists as stable once-for-all properties of an actor or notebook.

## VII. Historiographic afterlife

The 1907 paper itself also had an afterlife independent of Lovejoy's later unit-idea canon.

It was immediately registered in contemporary theological bibliography and later scholarship has continued to cite its claim about the emergence of ethical inwardness in Hebrew Wisdom literature.

This supplies a third specialist-afterlife route alongside:

- 1898 Buddhist technical scholarship;
- 1906 mana / primitive-religion theory.

Do not collapse the routes into one `religion` afterlife; they occupy different specialist literatures.
