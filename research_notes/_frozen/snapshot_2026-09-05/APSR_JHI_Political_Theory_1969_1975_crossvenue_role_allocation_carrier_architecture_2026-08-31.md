# APSR / JHI / *Political Theory*, 1969–1975: cross-venue role allocation and carrier architecture

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: **QUALITATIVE CROSS-VENUE CONTROL / DIRECT ACTOR-LEVEL EXECUTION EVIDENCE / NO EXHAUSTIVE THREE-JOURNAL SHARE CLAIM**

## Core result

The 1969–1975 method ecology is poorly represented by asking which journal `belonged` to which methodological school.

A more precise comparison asks:

> **What kinds of intellectual work did each venue make available in what publication roles?**

The current direct evidence shows different **carrier architectures** rather than three mutually exclusive ideological jurisdictions.

- **APSR** remained permeable to political-theory and methodological research articles, maintained an explicit review category for `Political Theory, History of Political Thought, and Methodology`, and simultaneously gave much greater article visibility to formal/quantitative modes. Its own editors later diagnosed low political-philosophy article representation.
- **JHI** could make methodological controversy itself a full-length history-of-ideas research object: Parekh and Berki's 1973 critique of Skinner appears as a lead article, while regional/historiographic field-building also occurs through ordinary articles and reviews. The current JHI census is an expanded seed, not an exhaustive article-share census.
- ***Political Theory*** declared from its first issue that history of political thought, modern theory, conceptual analysis and polemic argumentation would coexist; its early execution then used research articles, comments, a named Skinner symposium, a history-of-ideas method article, and an editorial metatheory census to aggregate controversy explicitly.

Thus the field-level shift is better expressed as:

`METHOD / OBJECT DIFFERENTIATION`
+
`ROLE ALLOCATION ACROSS CARRIERS`
+
`CROSS-VENUE ARGUMENT MIGRATION`

rather than:

`ONE SCHOOL -> ONE JOURNAL`.

Core rule:

> **VENUE IDENTITY != ONE METHOD; CARRIER ROLE IS PART OF THE HISTORICAL RELATION.**

---

# I. Comparison must be role-specific, not presence/absence

A simple presence test produces misleading conclusions.

For example, political theory is present in APSR throughout the period. But `presence` can mean at least:

- full research article;
- presidential address;
- review essay;
- book-review category;
- methodological article;
- formal-theory article;
- editorial discussion of the field.

Similarly, `history of ideas` in JHI can mean:

- internal conceptual history;
- source/influence history;
- regional historiography;
- review infrastructure;
- explicit methodological critique.

And in *Political Theory*, Skinner can appear as:

- critic of empirical democratic theory;
- object of a comment;
- object/respondent in a method symposium;
- one interlocutor inside Haddock's history-of-ideas problem-space.

Therefore:

`TOPIC / ACTOR PRESENT IN VENUE`
!= `SAME ROLE IN VENUE`.

The role itself has to be typed before comparison.

# II. APSR: permeability plus uneven research-article allocation

Primary control:

`research_notes/APSR_1966_1973_political_theory_venue_capacity_Ranney_Polsby_present_absent_2026-08-31.md`.

The flagship political-science journal did not eliminate theory.

Direct late-1960s/early-1970s examples include:

- Kalleberg, normative/empirical concept formation;
- Wolin, political theory and the behavioral `methodist`;
- Sartori, concept formation in comparative politics;
- Miller, positivism/historicism/political inquiry;
- Odajnyk, Jung's political ideas;
- Scaff, Weber and political education.

At the same time, APSR repeatedly maintained a review/book-note jurisdiction explicitly named:

`Political Theory, History of Political Thought, and Methodology`.

This reveals two distinct channels:

`RESEARCH-ARTICLE CHANNEL`
+
`REVIEW / FIELD-SURVEILLANCE CHANNEL`.

The June 1972 `Present and Absent` editorial then audits the 1971 research-article output:

- 46 articles total;
- 3 political philosophy;
- 13 operationally formal theory;
- 35 containing at least one table/figure.

Thus the direct problem is not absence but **role and capacity asymmetry**.

Compact formula:

`THEORY RECOGNIZED AS A FIELD`
+
`THEORY SOMETIMES PUBLISHED AS RESEARCH`
+
`POLITICAL PHILOSOPHY WEAKLY REPRESENTED IN ARTICLE OUTPUT`.

This is fully compatible with specialist-carrier formation without a categorical exclusion hypothesis.

# III. APSR's editorial apparatus also crosses the supposed faction line

Companion:

`research_notes/APSR_1970_Richter_editorial_board_anonymous_review_institutional_overlap_addendum_2026-08-31.md`.

In September 1970, direct APSR front matter lists Melvin Richter and Harvey Mansfield on Austin Ranney's Editorial Board and states that manuscripts were sent out anonymously for editorial evaluation.

Richter had participated in the 1967 CSPT founding correspondence and expressed publication anxiety about the Ranney transition.

Therefore the APSR boundary itself is porous at the institutional level:

`THEORY-INFRASTRUCTURE BUILDER`
can also be
`FLAGSHIP EDITORIAL-APPARATUS PARTICIPANT`.

This prevents carrier specialization from being mapped mechanically onto social separation.

`DIFFERENTIATED CARRIER`
!= `DIFFERENTIATED POPULATION OF ACTORS`.

# IV. JHI: methodological controversy can itself be a research article

Primary companion:

`research_notes/JHI_Parekh_Berki_1973_Skinner_1974_1976_roundtrip_method_debate_2026-08-31.md`.

JHI 34.2 (1973) carries Bhikhu Parekh and R. N. Berki, `The History of Political Ideas: A Critique of Q. Skinner's Methodology`, pp.163–184, as a **full-length lead article**.

That role matters.

The journal does not need a dedicated `methodology section` for methodological argument to become central historical scholarship. The object of research can be:

`HOW THE HISTORY OF POLITICAL IDEAS SHOULD BE DONE`.

Therefore in JHI:

`METHOD DEBATE`
can occupy
`ORDINARY / LEAD RESEARCH-ARTICLE ROLE`.

The article then migrates into another venue's explicit symposium ecology: Skinner responds at length in *Political Theory* in 1974, and Berki/Parekh reply there in 1976.

This creates a direct carrier transformation:

`JHI FULL-LENGTH CRITIQUE ARTICLE`
->
`POLITICAL THEORY SYMPOSIUM / RESPONSE`
->
`POLITICAL THEORY COMMUNICATION / REJOINDER`.

The argument is continuous enough to trace, while the publication role changes with venue.

# V. JHI's ordinary execution also diversifies object/relation roles

Companion census:

`research_notes/JHI_region_relation_role_census_audit_1940_1975_2026-08-31.md`.

The current expanded seed contains 40 non-Western / cross-regional cases across 1940–75 and explicitly warns that it is **not** an exhaustive issue-by-issue census.

What it does securely show is role diversification:

- internally addressed intellectual traditions;
- source/antecedent roles;
- comparative co-cases;
- transmission/contact;
- reaction/transformation;
- regional historiography;
- representation;
- ideas-to-action / present-identity questions;
- field-method/interface questions.

These roles appear through ordinary articles and reviews rather than one dedicated `global` or `method` section.

Thus JHI's carrier architecture is comparatively **low in explicit sectioning but high in object/relation heterogeneity inside standard scholarly forms**.

Do not translate that qualitative observation into an article-share statistic.

# VI. *Political Theory*: plural role allocation is an explicit inaugural programme

Primary companion:

`research_notes/Political_Theory_1973_inaugural_Editorial_Statement_direct_full_pluralist_programme_2026-08-31.md`.

The February 1973 `Editorial Statement`, direct-full, makes publication-role plurality part of the journal's declared architecture from the outset.

It promises broad/international scope and explicitly rejects exclusive identification with a particular school or orientation.

The planned content includes parallel roles such as:

- history of political thought;
- modern theory;
- conceptual analysis;
- polemic argumentation;
- graduate-student contributions;
- special issues;
- multi-viewpoint book discussion;
- author reassessment/self-review;
- bibliographic awareness across journals.

This means the journal's corporate programme is not merely `publish political theory`.

It is closer to:

> **construct an infrastructure in which several genres and methodological positions can be made mutually visible and disputable.**

Therefore `pluralism` here is first an **editorial carrier design**, not yet a claim about equal acceptance or intellectual neutrality.

# VII. *Political Theory* execution turns method into an explicit controversy object

The 1973–75 execution sequence is unusually clear.

### August 1973

Skinner writes on empirical theorists of democracy and the dispute over value-neutral political science.

Role:

`SUBSTANTIVE / METATHEORETICAL BOUNDARY ARTICLE`.

### May 1974

Carole Pateman publishes a named comment on Skinner.

Role:

`DIRECT SAME-VENUE CRITICAL COMMENT`.

### August 1974

The journal bundles:

- Jonathan M. Wiener on Skinner's Hobbes;
- Gordon J. Schochet on Skinner's method;
- Skinner's extended response.

Role:

`NAMED METHOD SYMPOSIUM / CONTROVERSY CLUSTER`.

### November 1974

Haddock publishes `The History of Ideas and the Study of Politics`, aggregating older history-of-ideas, Cambridge and philosophical resources.

Role:

`CROSS-METHOD JURISDICTION / METHOD-AGGREGATION ARTICLE`.

### May 1975

The editors publish a census that explicitly classifies methodology / philosophy of intellectual history / philosophy of social science as metatheory and admits overlapping `historical / modern / methodological / conceptual` categories.

Role:

`EDITORIAL SELF-OBSERVATION OF METHOD MIX`.

Thus the new venue converts method heterogeneity into an explicit, recurring editorial object.

# VIII. The same controversy changes carrier form across journals

The Skinner sequence makes the comparison concrete.

A simplified route is:

`JHI 1973`

Parekh/Berki critique = full-length research article

->

`POLITICAL THEORY 1974`

Skinner criticism/response = symposium architecture

->

`POLITICAL THEORY 1976`

Berki/Parekh continuation = communication/rejoinder

This demonstrates:

`ARGUMENT CONTINUITY`
!= `CARRIER-FORM CONTINUITY`.

When later canonization extracts a small set of `method essays`, it can obscure this changing publication architecture.

The historical ecology is not only a network of texts; it is a network of **text-role transformations**.

# IX. Cross-venue comparison

The safest qualitative matrix is:

| Venue | Directly controlled carrier roles relevant here | Strongest execution feature | Main inference ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| APSR | research article; formal theory; political-philosophy article; review/book-note field category; editorial process comment | permeable flagship with uneven article-space visibility | low political-philosophy output does not establish discriminatory rejection |
| JHI | ordinary/lead research article; review; regional historiographic/field-method article | method and historiography can become ordinary research objects without a dedicated method section | current 1940–75 census is seeded, not exhaustive share data |
| *Political Theory* | research article; comment; symposium; communication/rejoinder; editorial metatheory census; declared multi-genre programme | explicit aggregation and classification of controversy | declared pluralism does not establish equal acceptance or no preference |

The point is not that one architecture is `better`.

It is that the same intellectual activity occupies different institutional slots.

# X. Revised model of field differentiation

The evidence now supports a more specific version of `embedded differentiation`.

Political theory's specialized infrastructure did not simply relocate a pre-existing school from APSR into a new journal.

It reorganized **carrier capacity**:

- more reliable room for theory articles;
- explicit co-presence of historical, conceptual, normative and polemical roles;
- dedicated mechanisms for direct controversy;
- editorial self-observation of method categories;
- continued traffic back to APSR, JHI and other venues.

Thus:

`SPECIALIZATION`
can mean
`NEW ROLE ARCHITECTURE`
without
`DISCIPLINARY EXIT`.

This is stronger than a simple jurisdiction claim because it identifies what the new infrastructure actually does.

# XI. Relation to Lovejoy/JHI historiography

This comparison also helps with the larger Lovejoy problem.

A later story can compress intellectual-history methods into a sequence of named schools:

`LOVEJOY / HISTORY OF IDEAS`
-> `SKINNER / CONTEXTUALISM`
-> later alternatives.

The venue evidence instead shows method changing through:

- article/review differentiation;
- proof-burden disputes;
- symposium construction;
- editorial classification;
- cross-journal reply routes;
- later anthology/canon selection.

Therefore the historiographic object is not only `method doctrine`.

It is also:

> **the carrier architecture through which methodological propositions were made visible, criticized, grouped, repeated and later remembered.**

This supplies an institutional bridge from Lovejoy/JHI history to the later contextualist canon without making any one venue the cause of a method.

---

# XII. What remains open

Do not yet claim:

- exhaustive comparative article shares for APSR / JHI / *Political Theory*;
- equal pluralism across the three venues;
- that *Political Theory*'s role architecture caused contextualism's later prominence;
- that JHI had no implicit editorial taxonomy merely because it lacked the same public section design;
- that APSR review-role allocation was a deliberate containment strategy;
- that a move from JHI article to PT symposium changed an author's intellectual position.

Those are separate propositions with separate proof burdens.

> **CROSS-VENUE ROLE ALLOCATION, 1969–75: stop mapping schools to journals. APSR is a permeable flagship with theory/method research articles and a standing `Political Theory, History of Political Thought, and Methodology` review jurisdiction, but its own 1972 census shows weak political-philosophy article representation; `PRESENCE != CAPACITY`. JHI can make method controversy itself a full-length lead research article (Parekh/Berki 1973) while ordinary articles/reviews carry diverse relation roles; current JHI census is seeded, not exhaustive. *Political Theory* explicitly designs multi-role, school-nonexclusive carrier architecture in Feb.1973 and executes it through article, comment, symposium, history-of-ideas method aggregation, communication/rejoinder and editorial metatheory census. Parekh/Berki 1973 JHI -> Skinner 1974 PT symposium/response -> Berki/Parekh 1976 PT rejoinder directly shows `ARGUMENT CONTINUITY != CARRIER-FORM CONTINUITY`.
