## 2. p.112 — the rejected `circumcision = substitute/partial human sacrifice` genealogy is a historically concrete rival theory family

Round 27 treated p.112 as a strong source-adjudication seam because the notebook rejects a partial-human-sacrifice explanation of circumcision and instead prefers initiation / blood-covenant / incorporation.

This round makes the rejected theory historically more concrete.

### A. Frederick Starr, 1891

Frederick Starr's `Dress and Adornment IV`, *Popular Science Monthly* 40 (December 1891), explicitly interprets Jewish circumcision as a blood covenant and says it is also very clearly **a substitute for human sacrifice**.

This is strikingly close to the exact theory Lovejoy later refuses.

### B. Kuenen and related nineteenth-century genealogy

J. M. Wheeler's *Bible Studies* (1892) states directly that **Kuenen holds circumcision to be in the nature of a substitute for human sacrifice**. The early twentieth-century Jewish Encyclopedia still lists the sacrificial theory as a recognized controversy and names Movers/Ghillany and Letourneau/Élie Reclus among exponents of related views.

Thus the rival was not an isolated or retrospective formulation. It was a live nineteenth-century explanatory family.

### C. Trumbull supplies the preferred alternative field

Notebook 005 elsewhere directly extracts Trumbull. *The Blood Covenant* supplies circumcision as blood-covenant, fraternity, incorporation, adoption, rights-and-obligations relation. The notebook therefore has a source-available alternative with which to reject the sacrifice genealogy.

### Ownership recut

p.112 can now be represented as:

`HISTORICALLY REAL RIVAL FAMILY:`
`CIRCUMCISION -> SUBSTITUTE / PARTIAL HUMAN SACRIFICE`

versus

`TRUMBULL / INITIATION FIELD:`
`CIRCUMCISION -> BLOOD COVENANT / INCORPORATION / STATUS TRANSITION`

followed by

`LOVEJOY LOCAL ADJUDICATION:`
`REJECT FIRST GENEALOGY; PREFER SECOND EXPLANATORY FIELD`.

### Transmission caution

No current notebook citation to Starr, Kuenen, Movers, or Ghillany has been recovered at p.112. Therefore:

- existence of the rival theory: **F/S3**;
- Lovejoy's direct reading of any one named exponent: **HOLD**;
- p.112 rejection/preference relation: **strong A2**, because the rival theory itself is historically real and the notebook explicitly adjudicates against it.

Safe formulation:

> **Lovejoy does not invent the incorporation interpretation of circumcision, and he does not invent the sacrificial interpretation he rejects. His local act is to adjudicate between two already available genealogies.**

## 3. p.117 — ranked terminal synthesis remains an authorship HOLD despite a quantitative Steinmetz/Marillier source environment

The original-image Round 16 secured the key p.117 sequence at W3:

- `in gt. number of cases` — alimentary / anthropophagic;
- `in [the] next most numerous cases` — expiatory & propitiatory;
- `a human sacrifice for union is altogether exceptional`;
- a following union-comparison clause remains partly illegible;
- actual examples are `relatively rare`.

Round 28 correctly separated W3 wording from proposition authorship and retained an authorship HOLD because the page sits at the end of a Marillier-centered human-sacrifice/anthropophagy problem field.

### A. Steinmetz supplies a quantitative/motive-classification environment

Rudolf Steinmetz's 1896 *Endokannibalismus* explicitly organizes cannibalism comparatively by motives and presents tabular/statistical evidence. A contemporary American review summarizes the motives as including lack of food, desire for meat, relish for human flesh, and animistic belief, with tabular treatment of tribes, motives, and source reliability.

Thus quantitative ranking by motive is not alien to the source environment of 005.

### B. Marillier is a direct contemporary mediator of the anthropophagy problem

Marillier reviewed Steinmetz in *Revue de l'histoire des religions* 34 (1896), pp.113–118. His 1897–98 and 1898–99 EPHE programmes then explicitly classify human sacrifice and ritual anthropophagy by multiple functional types. In 1898–99, while Lovejoy is documented as an active auditor, Marillier's course determines reasons for human victims in expiatory, funerary, anthropophagic and magical sacrifice.

## 4. p.119 — Frazer owns the part-specific property mechanism; part→whole sympathy is also field-owned; Lovejoy's delta is the explicit fork under uncertainty

Round 16's original-image pass recovered a more cautious p.119 than the first clean summary. Secure structure includes:

- selected human flesh/body parts indicate a `specific virtue or force`;
- some organs can be understood through conventional seat-of-quality associations;
- for other selections the `reason ... is not entirely clear`;
- Lovejoy proposes `perh. as a repr. of [the] whole body`, thereby giving the eater possession of the force of the eaten person.

### A. Frazer directly owns the organ/property mechanism

Frazer's comparative magic/cannibalism dossier explicitly states that dead men's flesh and blood may be eaten to acquire bravery, wisdom or other qualities, especially where the desired property is believed to have its `special seat in the particular part eaten`.

He gives a striking list from South-Eastern Africa:

- liver -> valour;
- ears -> intelligence;
- forehead skin -> perseverance;
- testicles -> strength;
- other members -> other virtues.

This is close enough to make the notebook's first explanatory branch source-owned at mechanism level.

### B. Part-as-representative-of-whole is also part of the sympathetic/contagious-magic field

The principle that a detached part can stand for, remain related to, or act upon the whole is basic to the late-nineteenth-century sympathetic/contagious-magic grammar. Hair, nails, blood and other bodily parts function precisely through a part/whole relation.

Therefore Lovejoy does not originate the second mechanism family either.

### C. Local delta

What the image does show locally is a more precise operation:

`EVIDENCE: SELECTED BODY PART`

-> possible explanation 1:
`SPECIFIC ORGAN IS SEAT/CARRIER OF SPECIFIC PROPERTY`

versus possible explanation 2:
`PART REPRESENTS WHOLE PERSON AND CONVEYS GENERAL FORCE`

with the explicit admission:

`reason ... not entirely clear`.

The A2 object is therefore not either mechanism. It is the **explicit rival-explanation fork applied to one evidence class while withholding certainty**.

### Calibration

- specific organ/property: Frazer S3 / A0;
- part-whole sympathetic efficacy: field F/A0;
- p.119 explicit alternative-explanation structure: W2–W3 / **A2 relation**;
- exact illegible body-part nouns remain a diplomatic HOLD and should not be repaired from Frazer.

Safe formulation:

> **Lovejoy's p.119 contribution is not a new theory of bodily efficacy. It is an actor-level refusal to collapse two source-available explanations for selective consumption into one.**
