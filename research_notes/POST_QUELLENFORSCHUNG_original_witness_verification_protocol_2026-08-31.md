## Layout / material form as an analytical object

Record, where present:

- diagrammatic hierarchy and arrows;
- spatial nesting and juxtaposition;
- numbering / Roman-numeral schemas;
- indentation and list architecture;
- headings, underlining, brackets, boxes and emphatic spacing;
- marginal additions and interlinear additions;
- strike-throughs, overwriting and replacement placement;
- recto/verso and facing-page relations;
- slips, inserts, foldouts, rotated notes and overlays;
- discontinuities in paper/form that indicate accretion;
- whether source quotation, Lovejoy connective prose and classification occupy visibly different zones on the page.

Material observations must be described before interpretation. A diagram proves a spatial/classificatory act; a strike-through proves revision activity; an insert proves accretion. None of these alone proves conceptual direction, chronology, or causal origin.
