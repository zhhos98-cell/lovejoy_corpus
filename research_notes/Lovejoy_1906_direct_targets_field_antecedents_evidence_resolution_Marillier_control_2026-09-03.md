# Lovejoy 1906 — direct targets, field antecedents, evidence resolution, and the Marillier control

Date: 2026-09-03  
Status: **DIRECT-PRIMARY LOVEJOY ARCHITECTURE / DIRECT MARILLIER INSTITUTIONAL PROCEDURE / FIELD-ANTECEDENT READING FIREWALL / LOVEJOY RESIDUAL NARROWED TO DEPLOYMENT + CONFIGURATION**

## Core result

The source-ownership audit of Arthur O. Lovejoy's `The Fundamental Concept of the Primitive Philosophy` (1906) now needs three evidentiary relations kept separate:

1. **direct textual target/source** — an author or witness Lovejoy demonstrably names/uses in the 1906 paper;
2. **field antecedent** — an earlier text shows that a proposition/operator was already available in the field, without proving Lovejoy read it;
3. **training/procedural control** — Lovejoy's documented Marillier environment already practiced critical differentiation of testimony and licensed induction.

After these controls, Lovejoy cannot safely be credited with inventing:

- impersonal power;
- native power-term comparison;
- causal potency;
- power prior to soul/personality;
- natural-law / quasi-mechanical magic;
- internal cultural stratigraphy;
- cross-cultural mechanism abstraction;
- source criticism or grading testimony by type.

The strongest surviving Lovejoy-level object is therefore not a new ingredient. It is a **resolution-sensitive deployment and configuration**:

`FIELD-AVAILABLE POWER / DYNAMIST DOSSIER`
→ `CLAIM-TYPE SOURCE GRADING`
→ `SOURCE SUBSTITUTION AT A PROOF CEILING`
→ `GRAMMAR / NATIVE EXPLANATION FOR HIGH-RESOLUTION SEMANTICS`
→ `LOW-RESOLUTION CROSS-REGIONAL CONVERGENCE + HIGH-RESOLUTION LOCAL KEYS`
→ `PROBABILISTIC EXTENSION TO LOWER-RESOLUTION REGIONS`
→ `CROSS MAGIC / RELIGION JURISDICTION`
→ `PRIMITIVE ENERGETICS / FUNDAMENTAL CONCEPT OF PRIMITIVE PHILOSOPHY`.

This is still not an originality claim for the component procedures. The historical residual is **how Lovejoy deploys and recombines them around a particular claim architecture**.

## I. Direct targets and direct sources in Lovejoy 1906

The existing direct-primary concordances establish that Lovejoy's actual 1906 argumentative field includes, among others:

### Direct theoretical targets

- Brinton — anthropomorphic / personality-centered causal theory is challenged;
- Frazer — sharp allocation of mechanical causation to magic versus personal relation to religion is challenged; p.378 also criticizes overworking sympathetic magic;
- Marett — pp.380–81 directly reject `supernormal` / awe as the diagnostic essence in favor of efficacy / power / productivity;
- Tylor, Guyau, Spencer, Grant Allen — opening theoretical targets in the personality/anthropomorphism field.

### Direct empirical / lexical witnesses

- McGee;
- Alice C. Fletcher;
- Roger Williams and early missionary material;
- Jesuit / Bressani `oki` material;
- William Jones 1905 on Algonkin `manitou`;
- Dorsey;
- Codrington on `mana`;
- Alexander Hetherwick on Yao `mulungu`;
- further North-American, Oceanic and African witnesses mapped in the canonical concordance.

The relevant rule is:

> **direct citation/use demonstrates a textual relation; field priority alone does not.**

## II. Field antecedents whose direct Lovejoy uptake is not presently established

The field-ownership audit has recovered several strong pre-1906 controls:

- J. N. B. Hewitt 1902: `orenda` as mystic potency / property / efficient cause, variable in degree;
- Hubert–Mauss 1902–03: mana/orenda/brahman and related notions correlated as a general magical/causal power category;
- Konrad Theodor Preuss 1904–05: impersonal magical power treated as prior to or underlying soul/spirit/personality, with automatic/mechanistic efficacy;
- broader Tylor/Frazer/Marett controls for survival, mechanism abstraction and preanimist power.

At present the repository has **not securely established a direct 1906 Lovejoy citation to Hewitt, Hubert–Mauss, or Preuss**.

Therefore:

`PRE-1906 FIELD AVAILABILITY`
!=
`LOVEJOY DIRECT READING`
!=
`TEXTUAL TRANSMISSION`.

These sources lower Lovejoy's priority ceiling even where no direct uptake is proved, because they show that the relevant propositions/operators were already in circulation. They cannot by themselves supply an influence chain.

## III. The crucial direct 1906 operation: source substitution at a proof ceiling

The canonical direct-primary proof-warrant dossier establishes that Lovejoy does not assign a single global reliability value to a witness.

He works by:

`SOURCE × STATEMENT TYPE`
→ `EVIDENTIARY COMPETENCE`
→ `PROPOSITION LICENSED`.

The strongest sequence occurs in the North-American lexical packet.

### Older observers / missionaries

Roger Williams, Jesuit/Bressani and related early evidence can establish:

- concrete lexical occurrence;
- application of `manitou / oki` to unusually efficacious persons or things;
- existence of a recurrent semantic field.

But Lovejoy explicitly refuses to let these witnesses establish:

- one universal abstract property;
- personality / deity ontology merely because the observer glosses the term as `god` or `spirit`;
- full semantic system from isolated usage.

This is a literal proof ceiling.

### William Jones 1905 enters when the older packet reaches that ceiling

Jones supplies a different evidentiary resolution:

- grammatical/gender evidence;
- professional field study;
- native causal explanations;
- impersonal / abstract use;
- omnipresence / distribution;
- transferability.

The enemy-heart case is especially important because the transfer of `manitou` is supplied as an indigenous causal explanation rather than merely inferred by Lovejoy from external morphology.

Thus the sequence is not:

`MORE CITATIONS -> STRONGER CLAIM`.

It is:

`OLD WITNESS REACHES CLAIM CEILING`
→ `CHANGE WITNESS / EVIDENCE TYPE`
→ `PAY FOR STRONGER SEMANTIC PROPOSITION`.

This is one of the strongest currently defensible Lovejoy-specific deployments.

## IV. Parallel high-resolution control: Hetherwick / Yao

Lovejoy's Yao/Bantu packet supplies an independent grammatical control.

Hetherwick's noun-class evidence is used against a personal-being translation of `mulungu` and toward a property/state reading.

This matters because Lovejoy is not relying only on the richer North-American packet.

The intended proof architecture contains at least two high-resolution semantic controls:

- Algonkin grammar/native explanation;
- Yao noun-class grammar.

But their density remains unequal across regions.

---

## V. Cross-regional synthesis is explicitly asymmetric

Lovejoy's 1906 proof does not give North America, Oceania and Africa equal evidentiary resolution.

The direct-primary dossier distinguishes:

1. **low-resolution convergence:** many independent observers report broadly parallel uses of `wakonda`, `manitou`, `oki`, `mana`, `mulungu`, `ngai`, etc.;
2. **high-resolution controls:** grammar, native-language distinctions and indigenous causal explanation discriminate property/person/efficacy/transfer more sharply.

Printed p.376 acknowledges that many observers capture the minimal common pattern while missing finer ramifications.

Printed p.377 then uses the richer North-American analysis as an analogical key: because superficial term-use is strongly parallel across regions, Lovejoy argues that it is not improbable that deeper meanings disclosed by the better-observed packet also belong to lower-resolution analogues.

The strongest description is therefore:

> **triangulation under asymmetric evidence**.

Or more explicitly:

`MINIMAL CROSS-REGIONAL CONVERGENCE`
+
`DEEP LOCAL SEMANTIC CONTROLS`
→ `PROBABILISTIC EXTENSION OF RICHER SEMANTICS`.

This is methodologically calibrated but also the point at which the macro generalization can outrun the internal resolution of each regional dossier.

## VI. Marillier: evidence grading is training-field owned, not Lovejoy-owned

Lovejoy's p.381 n.21 says his conclusions were reached in 1900 and were strongly influenced by his studies under Léon Marillier.

The Marillier relation must now be recut in light of direct institutional evidence.

### 1888 EPHE — direct institutional primary

The EPHE annual report for Marillier's free course on religious phenomena says that his first-semester work included:

> critical study of the **comparative value of different orders of testimony** concerning the religions of `non-civilized` peoples,

and separately names:

- beliefs known by direct testimony;
- myths;
- rites;
- customs.

Thus differentiated testimony / evidence classes are directly documented in Marillier's teaching well before Lovejoy.

### 1901–02 Mauss succession — direct institutional continuity

After Marillier's death, Mauss's EPHE course on Melanesian magic explicitly says he sought to maintain the traditions implanted by his teacher Marillier.

The same report describes:

- detailed analysis of ethnographic documents;
- the inductions one is authorized to draw from them;
- training in ethnographic bibliography.

This is a direct institutional continuity control for exactly the sort of relation-specific evidentiary licensing reconstructed in Lovejoy's 005/1906 work.

Therefore:

`SOURCE / DOCUMENT GRADING`
!=
`LOVEJOY INVENTION`.

It belongs to the immediate comparative-religion training ecology.

## VII. Marillier 1900 `Religion`: substantive proximity without theoretical identity

A searchable transcription/mirror of Marillier's 1900 `Religion` entry in *La Grande Encyclopédie* confirms several relevant substantive elements.

Marillier directly discusses:

- concentrated sacred/magical force;
- fertility and destructive efficacy;
- contagion/contact;
- taboo as management of dangerous/sacred force;
- `mana` as varying in power;
- sympathetic rites and direct action on nature;
- ritual qualification/personality of the operator;
- force released or manipulated in ritual.

Modern source history additionally identifies six uses of `mana` in the article and locates the first at printed p.349.

But Marillier's overall 1900 synthesis is not simply Lovejoy's `Primitive Energetics` before the name.

The article embeds these materials in a broader psychological/evolutionary account of religious emotion, differentiation of gods, worship, moralization and spiritualization. Mauss's contemporary review criticized precisely the sentimentalist / quasi-inner-divinity structure of Marillier's theory.

Therefore the strongest relation is not:

`MARILLIER THEORY -> LOVEJOY COPIES THEORY`.

It is:

`MARILLIER MULTI-MECHANISM / DOCUMENT-CRITICAL PROBLEM FIELD`
→ Lovejoy selects `efficacy / causation`
→ promotes it to architectonic explanatory status.

This is **reallocation inside a documented influence relation**, not simple inheritance.

## VIII. Söderblom 1901: independent student witness to Marillier-derived mana language

Nathan Söderblom was another documented Marillier student in the same broad EPHE environment.

His 1901 *La vie future d'après le mazdéisme* p.37 uses a generalized expression explicitly attributed to Marillier: `le mana de l'homme`.

This is an independent student witness showing that generalized Marillier-linked `mana` language circulated immediately after the seminar period.

It does **not** prove Lovejoy heard the same oral formula.

Use:

`MARILLIER-ORBIT VOCABULARY CIRCULATION = DIRECTLY CONTROLLED BY A SECOND STUDENT WITNESS`.

Do not use:

`SÖDERBLOM PROVES LOVEJOY LEARNED MANA FROM MARILLIER`.

## X. Direct-target versus field-antecedent firewall

Do not collapse these relations:

`AUTHOR PREDATES LOVEJOY`
!=
`LOVEJOY READ AUTHOR`.

`LOVEJOY DIRECTLY CITES AUTHOR`
!=
`LOVEJOY ACCEPTS AUTHOR'S THEORY`.

`LOVEJOY USES A SOURCE FOR FACT P`
!=
`SOURCE CONTROLS LOVEJOY'S ABSTRACTION Q`.

`MARILLIER INFLUENCE ACKNOWLEDGED`
!=
`EVERY 1906 OPERATOR DERIVES FROM MARILLIER`.

The clearest current direct/field distinction is:

- Jones, Codrington, Marett, Frazer, Brinton and multiple ethnographic witnesses: **direct 1906 textual relation controlled**;
- Hewitt, Hubert–Mauss, Preuss: **strong upstream field controls; direct 1906 reading/citation not yet closed**;
- Marillier: **direct actor acknowledgment of strong influence + direct institutional evidence for document/testimony grading**, but exact clause-level transmission remains open.

## XI. India / brahman selection boundary remains HOLD

Hubert–Mauss include ancient Indian neuter `brahman` in their cross-cultural power series.

Lovejoy's positive 1906 proof architecture, as currently directly collated, is organized around North-American, Oceanic and African/Bantu materials.

This creates a potentially important selection-boundary hypothesis because Lovejoy separately possessed Sanskrit/Pāli competence and treated Indian textual philosophy as technical philosophy.

However the present pass has **not closed a diplomatic/full-text literal negative** for every occurrence of:

`India / Indian / Vedic / Sanskrit / brahman`.

Therefore:

> **candidate selection boundary, not closed exclusion claim.**

Do not infer motive (`prestige`, `classicality`, `Orientalist jurisdiction`) from the current absence.

## XII. Strongest current formulation

> **Lovejoy's 1906 contribution is least defensible as a priority claim about impersonal power and most defensible as a particular evidentiary and classificatory deployment. Working inside a field that already possessed survivals, dynamism, mana/orenda comparison, mechanism abstraction and differentiated testimony criticism, he limits older witnesses to the propositions they can bear, substitutes higher-resolution grammatical/native-use evidence when stronger semantic claims are required, combines low-resolution regional convergence with a few deeper local controls, probabilistically extends the richer interpretation, crosses the inherited magic/religion boundary, and reaggregates the surviving causal relation as `Primitive Energetics` / the fundamental concept of `primitive philosophy`.**

`FIELD-OWNED OPERATORS`
+
`LOVEJOY-SPECIFIC DEPLOYMENT / SELECTION / RANKING / REAGGREGATION`.

Not:

`LOVEJOY INVENTED THE OPERATORS`.
