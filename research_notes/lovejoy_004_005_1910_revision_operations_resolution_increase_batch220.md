# Lovejoy 004 / 005 / 1910 — three revision operations and the shrinking unit of historical judgment (Batch 220)

Date: 2026-08-26  
Status: **SYNTHESIS WITH DIRECT PRIMARY / NOTEBOOK CONTROLS; NON-CAUSAL LONGITUDINAL MODEL**  
Scope: notebooks 004 and 005, their immediate source environments, and the direct 1909→1910 Kant self-correction. Ordinary forward endpoint = 1910.

## Core result

The latest attribution corrections make it increasingly unhelpful to describe early Lovejoy’s distinctive work as the invention of analytical categories. In both notebooks, many of the relevant categories and discriminations are demonstrably available in his source/workshop environment.

What remains directly observable is a family of **revision operations** in which the unit carrying a historical or interpretive judgment becomes smaller and more relation-specific.

Three cases now need to be distinguished rather than collapsed:

1. **004 — source-fork reweighting.**  
   Warren already supplies two explanations for the Buddha’s silence on metaphysical questions: the questions presuppose what Buddhism denies, and Buddhism has a practical/applied aim. Lovejoy’s notebook initially registers the familiar `philosophical agnosticism of a purely practical teacher` reading, then gives analytical priority to the objection to the **form of the questions**, because Buddhist salvation still requires theoretical insight into suffering and causation.

2. **005 — ambiguous-case reallocation.**  
   The relevant selector categories are largely workshop-shared, but one ethnographic case — suicide / special manner of death — moves serially among ritual, custom/taboo, clan-solidarity, social exclusion, contact/later moralization, and finally strict desert. The case is not accepted as morally classified until the operative relation is specified.

3. **1909→1910 — actor-predicate fission.**  
   In 1909 Lovejoy directly labels Kant an early `evolutionist`. In 1910, after coming `to close quarters with the subject`, he explicitly calls that classification his own error and splits `evolutionism` into cosmic, anthropological, biological-transformist and organic-mechanistic predicates.

These are not identical events, and no direct causal chain among them is proved. But together they establish a robust longitudinal property:

> **Lovejoy repeatedly resists allowing a broad visible label to carry more historical work than the evidence licenses. Under closer analysis, judgment migrates from whole source/whole case/whole actor toward specific propositions, selectors and relations.**

Project shorthand:

> **resolution increase in historical classification.**

`source-fork reweighting`, `case reallocation`, `predicate fission`, and `resolution increase` are project analytical terms.

## 2. 004: the categories are upstream, the revision lies in weighting and problem-form

### 2.1 Warren already supplies both explanatory branches

Henry Clarke Warren’s *Buddhism in Translations* (1896), pp.111–116, asks why the Buddha refuses the standard questions about:

- eternity/finitude of the world;
- soul/body identity;
- post-mortem existence.

Warren gives two answers.

First, the questions are effectively **out of court** because they take for granted what Buddhism denies. A yes/no reply would concede a false presupposition.

Second, the Buddha has a **purely practical aim** and objects to speculative metaphysics irrelevant to suffering and its cessation.

Rhys Davids supplies a related upstream distinction: Buddhism’s negative response to soul-theory/metaphysics is not the whole positive Dharma; the causal/theoretical side still needs reconstruction.

Therefore Lovejoy cannot receive priority for either branch as such.

### 2.2 Lovejoy changes the relation between the branches

Notebook 004 p.18 treats the `philosophical agnosticism of a purely practical teacher` reading as an initial appearance, then says closer scrutiny makes this interpretation insufficient. The objection to the **form of the questions** becomes analytically prior.

Why? Because Buddhism remains a salvation doctrine based on **insight**. It must know the origin of suffering/existence before prescribing its removal.

The operation is not:

> source wrong → Lovejoy invents correct answer.

It is:

> source supplies A + B  
> → notebook reweights A/B  
> → B (`practical aim`) cannot exhaust the system  
> → A (`question presupposition`) opens the way to reconstructing the positive causal theory.

The next pages then turn to the Three Characteristics and dependent origination.

### 2.3 Unit of revision

The historical object under revision is an **interpretive fork**.

Lovejoy is choosing which source-supplied explanation should organize the subsequent reconstruction.

This is a form of resolution increase because a global label — `agnosticism` — is replaced by a more exact question:

> what precisely is being refused: theory, speculation, or the presuppositional form of a question?

## 3. 005: the categories are workshop-shared, the case migrates among them

Batches203–207 show that the early survivance discriminants have substantial Marillier/Mauss/Söderblom controls:

- terrestrial status;
- ritual qualification;
- manner of death;
- burial/provision;
- clan/social consequence;
- genuinely moral differentiation;
- contact/imported moralization;
- moral/quasi-moral transition.

This makes category ownership a weak place to locate Lovejoy’s delta.

### 3.1 The notebook instead moves one case

Across 005 pp.6, 11, 16–20, differentiated future destiny is repeatedly withheld from moral classification.

Suicide / special death moves through:

`manner of death`

→ `ritual/manual qualification`

→ `custom/taboo`

→ `social obligation / clan solidarity`

→ `social/religious exclusion / blood-contact consequence`

→ `later moralized layer through contact or higher-stage ideas`

→ `strictly moral only if allocation depends on desert`.

### 3.2 The stable object is the outcome, not its interpretation

A person may receive a different post-mortem destination throughout the analysis. What changes is the inferred **selector**.

This is crucial.

The notebook does not need a new ethnographic fact each time. It can redistribute the same visible outcome by asking which relation actually produces it.

Thus:

`difference in outcome`

is not the classification.

It is the **problem requiring classification**.

### 3.3 Unit of revision

The unit is an **ambiguous case-relation pair**.

Lovejoy is not revising `suicide` as a lexical concept in the abstract. He is revising the relation that connects suicide to post-mortem exclusion/punishment.

This is why `desert` functions more securely as a **stopping criterion** than as a Lovejoyan conceptual invention.

## 4. 1909→1910: a whole-actor label breaks into domain predicates

The Kant episode provides the strongest direct self-correction in the present chronological window.

### 4.1 1909

In `The Argument for Organic Evolution Before the Origin of Species`, p.513, Lovejoy says comparative-anatomical facts had made `evolutionists of Diderot, of Kant` and nearly Buffon.

The historical predicate is actor-level:

> `Kant = evolutionist`.

### 4.2 1910

In `Kant and Evolution` I, Lovejoy explicitly points back to the 1909 sentence as his own earlier error, made before he had come `to close quarters with the subject`.

Closer source audit requires:

- approximate chronological order of Kant’s writings;
- contextual control;
- historical term-meaning control;
- separation of logically non-equivalent doctrines.

The resulting Kant is:

- cosmically evolutionary;
- anthropologically evolutionary;
- not thereby a general biological transformist;
- not thereby a mechanistic theorist of organic generation.

### 4.3 Unit of revision

The unit shifts from **whole actor** to **actor × domain × proposition**.

The correction does not merely change Kant’s value from YES to NO. It destroys the sufficiency of the binary predicate itself.

That is why `category fission` is the appropriate analytical term.

## 5. Three revision types should not be collapsed

| Site | Initial broad object | What closer analysis does | Revised unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 004 p.18 | `Buddhist silence = practical agnosticism` | separates/reweights two source-supplied explanations | question-frame / theoretical function |
| 005 suicide block | `differential afterlife = punishment` | moves one case through competing selectors | case × operative relation |
| 1909→1910 Kant | `Kant = evolutionist` | splits umbrella historical predicate by domain | actor × proposition/domain |

The commonality lies only at a higher formal level:

> **classification loses scope as evidentiary resolution rises.**

But the mechanisms are distinct:

- reweighting;
- reallocation;
- fission.

This distinction should be preserved in publication rather than converted into a single master algorithm.

## 6. Relation to Batch77: the evidentiary atom and the classificatory atom both shrink

Batch77 showed a source-side granularity increase in 1906:

`source`

→ `statement type`

→ `proposition licensed`.

A missionary can be admissible for a concrete usage and inadmissible for theological interpretation.

Batch220 identifies the corresponding classification-side phenomenon:

`whole interpretive label / case / actor`

→ `specific relation or proposition`

→ `narrower predicate licensed`.

These two movements are complementary:

> **finer evidence allocation makes finer historical classification possible and necessary.**

The important caution is chronological. Batch77’s 1906 published statement-level source criticism cannot be projected backward as the explicit rule of the Paris notebooks. The notebooks provide earlier case-specific analogues, not a fully articulated source theory.

## 8. Relation to the larger `primitive = stratification` thesis

The revision operations help explain how `primitive` can become a stratifying rather than merely whole-population category.

A broad synchronized chronology depends on large classificatory units:

> whole people / whole religion / whole thinker → one stage.

Resolution increase weakens that requirement.

Once predicates attach to narrower relations:

- a Greek layer can be primitive while later Greek/Christian materials are not;
- an older non-moral mechanism can survive under later moralized interpretation;
- a rite can be historically old but currently perform a new function;
- Kant can be evolutionary on one axis and non-evolutionary on another.

The resulting historical topology is **stratified by predicate**.

This does not abolish whole-population hierarchy. Batch208 and Batch210 show that Lovejoy still uses higher/lower-stage language and can reaggregate local mechanisms into `primitive philosophy` across broad populations.

The important point is technical:

> **relation-specific classification makes it possible both to internalize chronology within objects and later to compare equivalent strata transversely across objects.**

## 10. Strongest current formulation

> **The early Lovejoy archive increasingly points away from category invention and toward revision at changing scales. In notebook 004, he reweights two explanations already supplied by Warren, treating the form of a Buddhist question rather than practical agnosticism as the key to reconstructing the system. In notebook 005, determinant categories largely shared with the Marillier workshop become a working decision-space through which an ambiguous suicide case is repeatedly reallocated. By 1910, the same pressure for narrower classification is explicit in print: Lovejoy retracts his own 1909 designation of Kant as an `evolutionist` and replaces the actor-wide label with separate cosmic, anthropological, biological and mechanistic predicates. These episodes do not prove one continuously self-conscious method, but they do show a recurrent increase in classificatory resolution: the scope of a historical judgment contracts toward the specific relation the evidence can sustain.**
