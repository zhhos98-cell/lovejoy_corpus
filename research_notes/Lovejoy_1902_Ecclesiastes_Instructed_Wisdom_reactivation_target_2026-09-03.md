# Lovejoy 1902 `Ecclesiastes Instructed` — Wisdom reactivation target

Date: 2026-09-03  
Status: **BIBLIOGRAPHIC PRIMARY TARGET / 1898→1902 OBJECT REAPPEARANCE / SAME-YEAR SEMANTIC CONTROL / ORIGINAL PAGE NOT YET READ**

## Discovery

Daniel J. Wilson's *Arthur O. Lovejoy: An Annotated Bibliography* includes a Lovejoy item:

`"Ecclesiastes Instructed." University of California Magazine 8 (1902): 285.`

Wilson describes it as:

> a sonnet on the theme of finding meaning in life by helping others.

The web OCR of Wilson's bibliography misreads the title as `Roclesiastes Instructed`; Wilson's index elsewhere gives `Ecclesiastes Instructed`, confirming the intended title.

This item had not previously been integrated into the repo's Hebrew/Wisdom chronology at the evidentiary level now made possible by the recovered 1898 letters.

## Why it matters

The newly recovered MS-0873 France witness gives direct 1898 actor-level evidence that:

- Lovejoy had an explicit `Wisdom work` project in Paris;
- Ecclesiastes was a live textual object;
- Buddhism and Wisdom literature were paired through the problem of evil;
- the Wisdom project shaped his choice of Vernes's Psalms course and library resources.

The 1902 item changes the gap structure:

`1898 explicit Wisdom / Ecclesiastes project`
→ `[1899–1901 open]`
→ **`1902 published Ecclesiastes literary-philosophical item`**
→ `[1903–04 open]`
→ `1905 Hebrew eschatology reactivation`
→ `1907 Ethical Inwardness`.

This does **not** prove continuous research or a publication outcome of the 1898 project.

## Original-page problem and serial-title refinement

The primary page has not yet been directly recovered.

Independent bibliographic controls place:

- John Galen Howard, `The Hearst Memorial Mining Building for the College of Mining`, vol.8 (Oct.1902), pp.285–291;
- Harry A. Overstreet, `Oxford Student Customs`, vol.8 no.6 (Oct.1902), pp.292–300.

Wilson assigns Lovejoy's sonnet to p.285. These locators are not necessarily contradictory: a short poem and the opening of another contribution may share a page, or bibliographic conventions may differ. Direct page inspection controls the issue.

A further serial-history control matters for retrieval. Berkeley Alumni Association's institutional history says its alumni publication began as *University of California Magazine* and became known as **`The Graduate` in 1902**. Sacramento Public Library catalogs the physical 1895–1904 run under the older serial title. Thus the retrieval target should use both title states:

> **`University of California Magazine / The Graduate`, vol.8 no.6, October 1902, around p.285; Arthur O. Lovejoy, `Ecclesiastes Instructed`.**

Biodiversity Heritage Library exposes v.7 no.5 (Sep.1901) but current public search has not surfaced the 1902 volume/issue. This is a digital-availability limitation, not evidence that the issue is absent from Berkeley/Sacramento holdings.

Until the issue/page image is inspected:

- do not treat p.285 layout as closed;
- do not quote the sonnet from Wilson's summary;
- do not infer which verse/claim of Ecclesiastes is being answered;
- do not call it a direct continuation of the 1898 project.

## New same-year semantic control: `Religion and the Time-Process`

Lovejoy's signed July 1902 article is directly primary-controlled in the repo and supplies a useful **same-year semantic control** independent of the unrecovered poem.

In *Religion and the Time-Process*, Lovejoy's positive valuation of temporal life explicitly includes:

- `process`;
- `social fellowship`;
- aims with `social and collective significance`;
- `devotion to the service of society`.

Wilson's summary of `Ecclesiastes Instructed` says the sonnet finds meaning in life by helping others.

This supports a carefully bounded statement:

> **Wilson's description of the 1902 sonnet places it in the same positive semantic field as Lovejoy's directly readable 1902 philosophy of temporal/processual, social and collective value.**

It does **not** establish:

- that the sonnet is a poetic digest of *Religion and the Time-Process*;
- that one text caused the other;
- that the poem uses the same vocabulary;
- that the 1898 Wisdom project developed continuously into the 1902 value theory.

The relation is currently:

`SAME YEAR + COMPATIBLE VALUE DIRECTION`

not

`TEXTUAL / GENETIC IDENTITY`.
