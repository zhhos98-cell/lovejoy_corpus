# Lovejoy 1902 `Ecclesiastes Instructed` — Wisdom reactivation target

Date: 2026-09-03  
Status: **BIBLIOGRAPHIC PRIMARY TARGET / POTENTIAL 1898→1902 BRIDGE / ORIGINAL PAGE NOT YET READ**

## Discovery

Daniel J. Wilson's *Arthur O. Lovejoy: An Annotated Bibliography* includes a Lovejoy item:

`"Ecclesiastes Instructed." University of California Magazine 8 (1902): 285.`

Wilson describes it as:

> a sonnet on the theme of finding meaning in life by helping others.

The web OCR of Wilson's bibliography misreads the title as `Roclesiastes Instructed`; the index elsewhere in the same book gives `Ecclesiastes Instructed`, confirming the intended title.

This item has not previously been integrated into the repo's Hebrew/Wisdom chronology.

## Why it matters

The newly recovered MS-0873 France witness gives direct 1898 actor-level evidence that:

- Lovejoy had an explicit `Wisdom work` project in Paris;
- Ecclesiastes was a live textual object;
- Buddhism and Wisdom literature were paired through the problem of evil;
- the Wisdom project shaped his choice of Vernes's Psalms course and library resources.

Before this discovery the working chronology was:

`1898 explicit Wisdom project`
→ `[1899–1904 continuity open]`
→ `1905 Hebrew eschatology reactivation`
→ `1907 Ethical Inwardness`.

The 1902 item changes the gap structure:

`1898 explicit Wisdom / Ecclesiastes project`
→ `[1899–1901 open]`
→ **`1902 published Ecclesiastes literary-philosophical item`**
→ `[1903–04 open]`
→ `1905 Hebrew eschatology reactivation`
→ `1907 Ethical Inwardness`.

This does **not** yet prove continuous research or a publication outcome of the 1898 project.

## Original-page problem

The primary page has not yet been directly recovered in this run.

Independent bibliographic controls place John Galen Howard's `The Hearst Memorial Mining Building for the College of Mining` in *University of California Magazine* 8 (Oct. 1902), pp.285–291, while Wilson assigns Lovejoy's short sonnet to p.285.

These locators are not necessarily contradictory: a short poem could appear on the same page before another article begins. But until the issue/page image is inspected:

- do not treat p.285 layout as closed;
- do not quote the sonnet from Wilson's summary;
- do not infer which verse/claim of Ecclesiastes is being answered;
- do not call it a direct continuation of the 1898 project.

The issue can be narrowed independently: another bibliography identifies *University of California Magazine* **8.6 (October 1902)** with pp.292–300 assigned to Harry A. Overstreet's `Oxford Student Customs`; the Howard bibliography gives pp.285–291. Thus October 1902 / vol.8 no.6 is the probable issue target.

## Consequence for longitudinal model

The current best model becomes **reactivation with an intervening public Ecclesiastes object**, not continuous project identity:

`1898 project object: Wisdom + Buddhism / problem of evil`

`1902 public literary object: Ecclesiastes — exact operation pending primary read`

`1905 research/teaching object: Hebrew eschatology / Sheol / nephesh-ruach`

`1907 published historical object: ethical inwardness / logical development`.

The same named text can therefore recur across **different genres** as well as different questions. This adds a carrier-form dimension to the existing rule:

`SAME / OVERLAPPING CORPUS != SAME QUESTION != SAME HISTORICAL OBJECT != SAME CARRIER FORM`.
