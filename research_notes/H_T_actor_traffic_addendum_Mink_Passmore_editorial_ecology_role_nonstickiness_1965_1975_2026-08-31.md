# H&T actor-traffic addendum: Mink as editorial-ecology actor, Passmore as role-nonstickiness control, 1965–1975

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: **MINK CORE-TRAFFIC UPGRADE / PASSMORE COMPARATOR CONTROL / NO LIST INFLATION**

## Core result

The first actor-traffic model centered on Pocock, Shklar and Mandelbaum because each supplies multiple distinct role transitions across H&T / JHI / CSPT / *Political Theory* / private circulation.

A follow-up check of Louis O. Mink and John Passmore produces two different outcomes:

- **Mink should be added to the core traffic model**, because he contributes a genuinely new role combination: public history-of-ideas method disputant + H&T associate editor inside a contemporaneously documented operational submission ecology.
- **Passmore should remain a comparator rather than a core formation actor**, because his strongest contribution here is a clean demonstration that an actor's `methodological` role in one carrier does not permanently define the role of later publications.

Thus this addendum adds analytical discrimination rather than names.

---

## I. Mink adds a new role type: method disputant inside an editorial ecology

### 1. H&T 1968 — ordinary method author

Louis O. Mink's `Collingwood's Dialectic of History`, H&T 7.1 (1968), is part of the pre-Skinner method ecology reconstructed in the companion Volume-7 dossier.

Role:

`METAHISTORICAL / HISTORICAL-UNDERSTANDING AUTHOR`.

### 2. *Eighteenth-Century Studies* 1968–69 — public history-of-ideas method disputant

Mink publishes:

`Change and Causality in the History of Ideas`, *Eighteenth-Century Studies* 2.1 (1968), 7–25.

Philip P. Wiener responds in Spring 1969:

`Some Remarks on Professor Mink's Views of Methodology in the History of Ideas`, 311–317.

Mink replies, 318–320.

Role:

`CROSS-VENUE PUBLIC METHOD DISPUTANT`.

The dispute concerns:

- thing-like unit ideas;
- historical change;
- causality;
- interpretation;
- what causal language licenses.

The forum makes the JHI/history-of-ideas ↔ H&T ecology publicly argumentative through a third venue rather than merely adjacent through citations.

### 3. H&T 1969 — Associate Editor

Contemporary Volume-8 masthead directly lists:

- George H. Nadel — Editor;
- Richard T. Vann — Executive Editor;
- Louis O. Mink — Associate Editor;
- Maurice Mandelbaum — Editorial Committee.

Role:

`FORMAL H&T ASSOCIATE-EDITOR ROLE`.

This matters because Mink's public dispute with Wiener over Lovejoy/history-of-ideas method occurs immediately around Skinner's 1969 publication while Mink sits inside the receiving journal's editorial ecology.

But preserve:

`ASSOCIATE EDITOR + METHOD DISPUTE`
!= `SKINNER MANUSCRIPT HANDLER / REFEREE`.

### 4. Operational routing is differentiated

The same 1969 front matter directly routes:

`Correspondence and Contributions`

to

`Executive Editor, Richard T. Vann`.

A separately documented 1966 H&T office memorandum by Mink on another submitted paper proves that internal editorial memorandum handling existed in the office during the relevant period.

Thus generic workflow evidence permits:

`INCOMING SUBMISSION`
-> `VANN EXECUTIVE-EDITOR CHANNEL`
-> possible `INTERNAL EDITORIAL MEMO / CONSULTATION`.

It does **not** permit:

`SKINNER SUBMISSION -> MINK MEMO`.

The Skinner-specific middle edge remains archival.

### Mink role sequence

`H&T METHOD AUTHOR`
+
`ECS CROSS-VENUE METHOD DISPUTANT`
+
`H&T ASSOCIATE EDITOR`.

This combination adds something the Pocock/Shklar/Mandelbaum model lacked: **a public method critic situated simultaneously inside the operational editorial ecology of the venue carrying Skinner's canonical essay**.

The historical conclusion remains ecological, not causal.

## II. Passmore does not add a new formation edge — and that is analytically useful

### 1. H&T Beiheft 5, 1965 — methodological author / assembler

John Passmore's `The Idea of a History of Philosophy`, pp.1–32, opens H&T Beiheft 5, `The Historiography of the History of Philosophy`.

JSTOR directly controls the issue/article. Bibliographic records for the separately issued supplement identify Passmore as the assembler/editor of the symposium volume.

Role:

`METHOD AUTHOR + THEMATIC-CARRIER ASSEMBLER`.

His methodological intervention belongs to the same thematic infrastructure as Mandelbaum's history-of-ideas/intellectual-history article.

### 2. JHI 1975 — ordinary substantive research article

JSTOR direct issue control for JHI 36.2 (Apr.–Jun. 1975) places Passmore's:

`The Treatment of Animals`, pp.195–218

as the opening ordinary research article of the issue.

Role:

`SUBSTANTIVE INTELLECTUAL-HISTORY AUTHOR`.

This article's carrier role is not `methodology of intellectual history` merely because Passmore had been a method author/assembler in H&T ten years earlier.

### Passmore control

`SAME ACTOR`
+
`EARLIER METHOD ROLE`
!=
`ALL LATER OUTPUT = METHOD ROLE`.

This is a useful negative control against actor-essentialism.

It parallels the 1975 *Political Theory* editors' own problem: a named theorist/object does not mechanically determine an article's method role.

### Comparator

Passmore is analytically useful precisely because he does **not** establish a formation/network edge comparable to the four above.

Do not inflate the central actor graph merely because an actor appears in multiple venues.

## IV. New firewall: role non-stickiness

The actor-traffic project now needs an explicit rule:

> **A ROLE IS AN ACTOR × CARRIER × INTERVENTION RELATION, NOT A PERMANENT PROPERTY OF THE PERSON.**

Hence:

`METHOD AUTHOR IN 1965`
!= `METHOD AUTHOR IN EVERY LATER PUBLICATION`;

`EDITOR IN ONE VENUE`
!= `EDITORIAL CAUSE OF EVERY ARTICLE IN THAT VENUE`;

`SOCIETY FOUNDER`
!= `JOURNAL BOARD MEMBER`;

`DRAFT READER`
!= `REFEREE`.

This prevents personnel history from silently replacing proposition-level relation typing.

## V. Implication for the Skinner rupture story

Mink adds density around Skinner 1969 but should not be used to make Skinner derivative.

The direct institutional architecture now includes:

- H&T's prior methodological carrier forms;
- Mandelbaum's Beiheft 5 article and draft comments;
- Mink's public 1968–69 history-of-ideas dispute and H&T associate editorship;
- Vann's operational incoming-submission channel;
- Pocock/Dunn/Skinner private programme traffic;
- Wiener/JHI method disputes.

Therefore:

`SKINNER 1969 = MAJOR PORTABLE ARGUMENT`
inside
`DENSE PRE-EXISTING ARGUMENT/CARRIER/PERSONNEL ECOLOGY`.

Not:

`SKINNER 1969 = DERIVATIVE`.

And not:

`H&T EDITORIAL ECOLOGY = CAUSE OF SKINNER TEXT`.
