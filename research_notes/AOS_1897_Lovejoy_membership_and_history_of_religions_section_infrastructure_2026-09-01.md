# AOS 1897: Lovejoy membership and history of religions infrastructure

Date: 2026-09-01

## New state-bearing points

Arthur O. Lovejoy's relation to the American Oriental Society is now separated into three levels:

1. 10 April 1897 Final Circular: AOS announced a paper by Lovejoy, Harvard University, titled "Critical summary of the argument of the Milinda-pañha."
2. 1897 membership chronology: Lovejoy was elected a member of the American Oriental Society in 1897.
3. 1898 membership structure: Lovejoy appears among ordinary members, but not among the separately listed members of the Section for the Historical Study of Religions.

## Evidentiary rule

Do not merge:

- announced communication;
- AOS membership;
- membership in the Historical Study of Religions Section.

They indicate increasing institutional proximity, not identical status.

## Research significance

The 1897 Baltimore meeting becomes a critical node because it may connect:

Harvard advanced Pali / comparative religion training
-> AOS Milinda-pañha communication
-> formation of organized historical study of religions networks.

However, the following remain unresolved:

- whether Lovejoy's Milinda communication was actually read at the meeting;
- nomination/election mechanism for Lovejoy's 1897 membership;
- whether he attended the newly forming religion-history section activities.
