Date: 2026-09-04  
Status: **CLOSED / NO FURTHER BROAD ACTOR EXPANSION**

## Decision

The comparative `primitive` extension line stops with the present Brinton–Boas–Lovejoy control set. Do not continue broad actor-by-actor expansion into Tylor, Lang, Frazer, Jevons, Durkheim, or other period figures merely to enlarge the taxonomy of `primitive`.

The existing synthesis is sufficient for the current Lovejoy project:

- **Brinton = stage-first synchronization**;
- **Boas = process-first comparability**;
- **Lovejoy = relation-first reaggregation**.

This triad remains a methodological control, not an influence chain.

## Why the line is closed

The project has already established the point needed for the Lovejoy argument: `primitive` does not carry one fixed chronological or methodological license. Its historical force depends on what is classified, what proof burdens are imposed, and what inferences are permitted after classification.

Further actor accumulation now has declining marginal value and risks turning a Lovejoy-centered archival project into a general history of `primitive`.

## What remains usable

Keep the existing analytical distinctions:

- predicate address;
- date versus historical/developmental position;
- relational/internal stratification;
- comparability gate;
- population synchronization;
- transverse reaggregation;
- predicate/inference split.

Use them only when they sharpen a Lovejoy-local proposition or a directly necessary period control.

## Reopening rule

Reopen this line only if new evidence materially bears on a core Lovejoy proposition, for example:

1. direct Lovejoy reading, citation, correspondence, course attendance, or source use involving another actor;
2. a source directly required to explain a specific notebook or publication passage;
3. a contradiction that cannot be resolved inside the current Brinton–Boas–Lovejoy control set;
4. a publication-facing claim that requires one additional actor-level comparison.

Do **not** reopen for generic completeness, vocabulary harvesting, or because another period author also uses `primitive`.

## Routing consequence

Future `continue` requests after discussion of `primitive` should return to Lovejoy-local evidence, chronology, publication trajectory, politics, notebooks 004/005, or the 1906 reaggregation problem. They should not automatically trigger new comparative actor audits.
