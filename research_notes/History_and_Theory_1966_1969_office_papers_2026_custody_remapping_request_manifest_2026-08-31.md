## II. Why two routes are necessary

The historical citation uses:

`History and Theory Papers, History and Theory Offices`

rather than a conventional archives collection number.

That wording leaves at least three plausible current states:

1. transferred to Wesleyan SC&A under a later/different collection label;
2. retained or partly retained by the journal office;
3. split between office and archives.

No one state should be assumed.

Therefore:

`HISTORICAL CITATION LABEL`
!= `CURRENT CATALOG LABEL`
!= `CURRENT PHYSICAL CUSTODY`.

The first request should ask **where the corpus is now**, before asking for extensive reproduction.
