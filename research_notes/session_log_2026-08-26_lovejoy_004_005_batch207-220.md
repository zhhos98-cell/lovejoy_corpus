## 10. Batches217–218 — 1910 endpoint and direct self-correction

### 1910 primary

In `Kant and Evolution` I, Lovejoy explicitly cites his own 1909 statement as an error made before he had come `to close quarters with the subject`.

- approximate chronological ordering of Kant's writings;

- control of the historical meanings of terms;

- Kant can be cosmically evolutionary;
- anthropologically evolutionary;
- yet not a general biological transformist;
- and not therefore a mechanistic theorist of organic generation.
