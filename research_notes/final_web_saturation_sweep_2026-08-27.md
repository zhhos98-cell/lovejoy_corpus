## 1. MF-002 RESOLVED — Lovejoy 1907 direct primary

Internet Archive exact item:

- https://archive.org/details/hibbertjournal05londuoft
- full OCR: https://archive.org/stream/hibbertjournal05londuoft/hibbertjournal05londuoft_djvu.txt
- identifier `hibbertjournal05londuoft`.

The item exposes full text, OCR search, page-number JSON and PDF and is marked public domain.

Direct collation establishes three high-value results.

### A. Actor-explicit historical warrant

Lovejoy directly distinguishes universal/cosmic religious truth from contingent historical propositions, limited testimonial verification, ordinary historical probability and the stronger burden borne by events contrary to ordinary experience.

Thus the project's claim-type/warrant architecture now has a direct Lovejoy primary analogue by 1907 rather than relying on Warfield 1912 or structural reconstruction alone.

### B. Political relativity

Lovejoy directly characterizes politics as practical, relative and temporal and rejects the self-evidence of one identical government/social order for such different societies as France/England and China/Peru.

This confirms a direct 1907 political-relativity continuity but does **not** resolve the 1906 meaning of `race`, culture or stage. MD-001 therefore remains active.

### C. India / Occident differentiation

Lovejoy directly uses more than one `great system of India` as an example of an unhistorical religious type and contrasts this type with the historical temper of Christianity / the Occident.

This is a direct primary control for an India/Occident typological differentiation in 1907, but the broad Indian category must not be silently identified with Buddhism alone.

Canonical incorporation:

`research_notes/Lovejoy_1907_Entangling_historical_probability_contemporary_relay_control_2026-08-26.md`.
