## p.49 / ms p.137 — Senart objection and `discoverable logical system`

### Diplomatic controls

The left leaf contains the explicit transition:

`On Senart's account of the matter there is simply this to be said: It is true that the terms of the formula are not original to Buddhism, but are brought together fr. pre-existing sources; ...`

The sentence continues across the gutter. At the top of the right leaf, `homogeneous` is written above the baseline as a visible insertion after `the sequence is not`. The following word after `strictly` is struck/overwritten and is **not** publication-grade legible in the present image. The older clean wording `not homogeneous in strict temporal relation` is therefore too smooth at this exact point and should not be quoted as diplomatic text.

The decisive following clauses are image-secure:

`But it is entirely false to say that F. formula is illogical & unintelligible & th. F. various terms have been jumbled together in an order more or less accidental.`

`There is a discoverable logical system wh. governs the whole arrangement, and the repetitions are intentional; as I hope I have succeeded in showing.`

Immediately afterward Lovejoy writes that `M. Senart's misconception is due to two errors`.

### Calibration against clean JSON

The existing summary is substantively right about Lovejoy rejecting unintelligibility/accident, but one lexical correction matters:

- old summary: `discoverable technical system`;
- original image: **`discoverable logical system`**.

This is a genuine transcription correction, not stylistic modernization.

The page therefore preserves revision in the very clause used to characterize the sequence. The material fact supports a cautious reading: Lovejoy is stabilizing the relation-language on the working page, not simply copying a finished formulation.
