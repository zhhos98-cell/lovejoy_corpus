# MS38_005 paper architecture — Batch 48 addendum: upgrade Thomas from pedagogy witness to methodological control

Date: 2026-08-18
Status: architecture addendum; merge into canonical paper architecture at drafting stage
Scope: **005 only**

## Revision to the evidentiary architecture

The canonical architecture currently uses N. W. Thomas primarily as an eyewitness to Marillier’s EPHE pedagogy. Batch 48 adds a second, distinct evidentiary role:

> **Thomas is also an independent former-student methodological control.**

His 1905 `The Religious Ideas of the Arunta` shows the method in operation: conflicting ethnographic sources are tested through chronology, missionary contamination, translation, diffusion, geographical boundary, and internal evolution before a developmental sequence is accepted. His 1911 `Animal Worship` then states the core operator abstractly by distinguishing **outward form** from **inward meaning** and by insisting that present meaning need not be identical with the historical cause that first produced a cult.

The later `Cannibalism`, `Fetishism`, `Taboo`, `Animism`, and `Sacrifice` entries instantiate the same operation across precisely the domains that occupy 005.

## Recommended revised chain

The student/workshop portion of the paper should now use three different evidentiary functions rather than treating all student parallels alike:

### Söderblom — direct paper transmission

- Marillier notes actually move to a student;
- extracts can be identified as coming from those notes;
- the student explicitly says he uses different divisions / a somewhat different point of view.

Use Söderblom to prove **document circulation plus redivision**.

### Thomas — reproducibility of the analytical operation

- direct eyewitness to `work by pupils, directed by the lecturer`;
- 1905 source-critical performance: contamination / translation / diffusion / internal evolution as rival hypotheses;
- 1911 formal distinction: outward form / inward meaning / historical cause;
- domain-specific decompositions in cannibalism, fetishism, taboo, animism, sacrifice.

Use Thomas to prove **independent reproducibility / persistence of a research grammar**.

### Lovejoy — archive trace of redivision in practice

- 005 repeatedly removes diagnostic force from outward features;
- cases are reassigned by determinant, causal bearer, social relation, or historical layer;
- negative cases constrain inherited genera;
- 1906 abstracts one cross-cutting efficacy family.

Use Lovejoy to show **the operation becoming a portable causal abstraction**.

## Revised core historiographical language

Avoid:

> `Marillier taught a doctrine that Lovejoy adopted.`

Prefer:

> **Marillier’s seminar appears to have been generative precisely because its taxonomies were revisable. What recurs across Söderblom, Thomas, and Lovejoy is not a stable theory but a research grammar: distinguish outward form from operative meaning and historical origin; test contamination and borrowing before genealogical inference; use negative cases to withdraw diagnostic force from inherited labels; and reclassify formally similar rites by causal mechanism or determinant.**

The phrase **`practice of redivision`** is useful because it is demonstrable without claiming a coherent `Marillier school` doctrine.

## Strongest new section-level transition

At the end of the existing section on the teacher’s moving documents, the paper can now distinguish **material transmission** from **methodological reproducibility**:

> The movement of Marillier’s notes into Söderblom’s work proves that paper circulated. Thomas supplies a different control. A former pupil who had described the seminar as research by students under Marillier’s direction later approached conflicting ethnographic evidence by testing contamination, translation, borrowing and internal transformation as rival hypotheses; he eventually stated explicitly that a cult’s outward form, its inward meaning and the cause that first produced it need not coincide. Lovejoy’s notebook should therefore be read not as an isolated instance of clever reclassification but as one product of a research environment in which classification itself was repeatedly made answerable to provenance, causal mechanism and historical change.

## Highest-value test of this revision

Recover N. W. Thomas, `La survivance du culte totémique des animaux et les rites agraires dans le Pays de Galles`, *RHR* 38 (1898), 295–347.

If the 1898 student product already contains the later form/meaning/origin operator or explicit controls on survivance inference, the Thomas evidence moves chronologically from `later independent control` toward `near-contemporaneous seminar product`. If it does not, the 1905/1911 material remains useful but should stay explicitly later.
