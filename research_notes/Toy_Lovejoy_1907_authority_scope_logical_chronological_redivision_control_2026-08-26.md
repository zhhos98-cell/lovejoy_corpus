# Toy → Lovejoy 1907 — authority scope, chronology, and logical redivision

Date: 2026-08-26
Status: **DIRECT LOVEJOY PRIMARY / AMERICAN TEACHER-CONTINUITY CONTROL / 005 UPTAKE FIREWALL ACTIVE**

## Core result

Lovejoy's 1907 `The Origins of Ethical Inwardness in Jewish Thought` provides a cleaner American teacher-to-student methodological control than thematic similarity between Crawford H. Toy's comparative-religion publications and notebook 005.

Lovejoy explicitly says that he accepts the relative sequence of the relevant Wisdom materials **`in deference to the authority of Professor Toy`**. He then immediately separates the evidentiary work of chronology from the object of his own argument: texts of approximately the same date can embody different stages of reflection, older phases can survive beside later ones, and for the inquiry at hand the **logical sequence** of the ethical development matters more than absolute chronological ordering.

The strongest reconstruction is therefore not `Toy taught Lovejoy a method`. It is narrower and better documented:

> **Toy is explicitly admitted as an authority for one proposition — relative textual chronology — while Lovejoy separately defines the proposition he wants to reconstruct — a logical sequence of ethical development. Authority is question-relative rather than globally transferable.**

This is a direct American published analogue of the larger proof-warrant architecture recovered across 004/005: a source or authority competent for one claim does not automatically settle a stronger or differently typed claim.

## I. Direct Lovejoy primary state

The full primary text of Arthur O. Lovejoy, `The Origins of Ethical Inwardness in Jewish Thought`, *The American Journal of Theology* 11.2 (April 1907), 228–249, has already been recovered and summarized in `lovejoy_as_orientalist_web_sweep_batch11_1902_1907_primary_texts.md`.

The controlled primary points are:

1. Lovejoy explicitly acknowledges Toy's authority for the relative sequence of Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ben Sira, Ecclesiastes and Wisdom of Solomon.
2. Lovejoy explicitly distinguishes:
   - absolute/calendar dating;
   - relative textual chronology;
   - logical sequence of a developing conception;
   - coexistence/survival of earlier and later intellectual phases.
3. The article reconstructs ethical `inwardness` as an indirect and gradual conceptual development across heterogeneous texts rather than as an indivisible property of `Judaism` or one book.

The relation is especially valuable because Toy was one of Lovejoy's documented Harvard teachers in Hebrew religion. The 1907 acknowledgement is therefore a direct publication-level continuation of a real pedagogical relation, not a reconstructed institutional proximity.

## II. The inferential allocation

The relation can be stated as:

`Toy / textual scholarship`
→ warrants a relative chronological scaffold

but

`chronological position`
≠ automatically `logical-intellectual stage`.

Lovejoy therefore performs a second operation:

`relative chronology`
→ hold as constraint
→ distinguish logical stage from date
→ allow asynchronous survival/coexistence
→ reconstruct a conceptual sequence.

The important point is not that chronology is discarded. Lovejoy accepts it from an acknowledged specialist authority and then limits its inferential jurisdiction.

JHI-facing formulation:

> **Lovejoy could defer to a teacher's authority without allowing that authority to answer every historical question. In 1907 Toy helps establish when the texts stand relative to one another; Lovejoy separately asks what stage of an ethical argument each text embodies.**

This is one of the cleanest American examples currently in the corpus of **authority-scope partition**.

## IV. Toy 1899 problem-field control: relevant but not a direct uptake edge

Crawford H. Toy published two unusually relevant pieces in *Journal of the American Oriental Society* 20 (1899):

- `Taboo and Morality`, pp.151–156;
- `Relation between Magic and Religion`, pp.327–331.

Their existence, titles, authorship, pagination and public-domain JSTOR/Google Books records are direct bibliographic primary evidence. `Relation between Magic and Religion` also has a directly exposed JSTOR reference apparatus including Frazer, Spencer, J. H. King's *The Supernatural*, Spencer and Gillen, Jastrow and Tiele.

These articles sit almost exactly in the problem-space traversed by 005: taboo versus moral interpretation; magic versus religion; comparative classification of early religious practices.

However, current repository-wide code search has **not recovered an explicit Toy citation in notebook 005**. This is a bounded search result, not proof that Lovejoy never read the papers.

Therefore:

- Toy 1899 = **direct American problem-field / teacher publication control**;
- Toy 1899 → Lovejoy 005 direct reading = **UNPROVED**;
- thematic proximity must not be converted into pedagogy or textual dependence.

## V. Toy's later authorial continuity (1913) — useful only with a chronology firewall

Toy's own *Introduction to the History of Religions* (1913) directly treats the distinctions in much fuller form. Among the directly recoverable propositions:

- ethical/social obligations and taboo have different bases;
- taboo may oppose or coalesce with morality;
- taboo and magic share a conception of occult force while treating different sides/functions of it;
- composite social constitutions can contain ordinary law, taboo, custom and magic while their lines can often be analytically distinguished;
- some historical origins are unrecoverable or obscured by later decay/transformation.

This later book demonstrates **authorial continuity of Toy's problem field**, not the exact wording or full argumentative content of the two 1899 articles. Until the 1899 article bodies are directly collated, do not retroject 1913 formulations into 1899.

The value for the Lovejoy project is contextual:

> **a documented Harvard teacher remained committed to separating co-present normative/religious mechanisms, while his former student in 1907 explicitly assigned that teacher authority over one evidentiary level and retained another level of historical reconstruction for his own argument.**

## VI. Historiographical payoff for the American-centered JHI version

This edge makes the American emphasis more precise without forcing 005 back into Harvard.

The article can now distinguish three relations:

1. **documented pedagogy:** Lovejoy studied Hebrew religion under Toy at Harvard;
2. **documented later authority:** Lovejoy explicitly defers to Toy for relative textual chronology in 1907;
3. **adjacent American problem field:** Toy's 1899 JAOS publications directly treat taboo/morality and magic/religion, but direct Lovejoy uptake into 005 is not established.

This is better than a national influence story. It shows that the same American scholarly ecology contained multiple specialized jurisdictions — philological, chronological, comparative, psychological, historical — and that Lovejoy's practice could accept one jurisdiction while redividing the next inferential task.

Compact project rule:

> **Teacher authority is not total authority; chronology is not logical sequence; thematic proximity is not textual uptake.**
