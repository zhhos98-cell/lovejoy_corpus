## 3. One standing wording caution

The main risk is not literal false quotation. It is **syntactic actorization**: a project term can be correctly identified as ours in one note but later appear in prose with Lovejoy as grammatical subject, making it sound like a self-conscious method.

Therefore preferred prose patterns remain:

- `I use X to describe...`
- `the notebook can be read as...`
- `the project term X names...`
- `this sequence exhibits what I call...`

Avoid unmarked forms such as:

- `Lovejoy's method of chronological non-isomorphism...`
- `Lovejoy developed provenance compression...`
- `Lovejoy's selector procedure...`

unless the wording is explicitly framed as retrospective analysis.
