## Core decision

The JHI Blog remains primarily a guided tour of notebook 004, but the public body no longer tours the notebook by accumulating mini-cases. It follows the editor-linked JHI Blog model more closely: intellectual/institutional context is compressed; Warren, Senart, *upādisesa*, and the AOS witness path establish terrain in one or two sentences each; manuscript p. 123 carries the sustained demonstration; the 1898 article supplies the principal print comparison.

The central public claim is deliberately narrow:

> **one relation is not allowed automatically to answer for another.**

MS p. 123 makes this visible in the cleanest possible form. *Viññāṇa* can be temporally antecedent to *nāmarūpa* while being logically a subdivision of it. What appears contradictory on a single axis becomes intelligible when temporal sequence and logical/classificatory inclusion are kept distinct. The question mark beside `temporally` remains important because one relation is still being tested while the other is stated.

Notebook 005 now mirrors the same architecture with one principal case rather than an inventory. Its public example is the pp. 103–104 chronology test: a proposed developmental ordering of sacrifice is checked against independent agriculture/domestication chronology, producing a possible `vicious circle` and forcing the premise itself to be tested separately. The public gain is that classificatory/developmental order does not establish historical order by itself.

Later Lovejoy remains a bounded exit. The body now keeps only relation-specific recurrences that can be stated compactly: 1902 comparison ≠ transmission; 1906 developmental stage ≠ racial/national difference, including the political consequences of that distinction; *Great Chain* as a change in the function of Asian materials inside an Occidental narrative center. The former 1906 decomposition/generalization scalar counter-case returns to the evidence apparatus rather than remaining a co-equal public paragraph.

## 1. What the reader must learn before interpreting notebook 004

The notebook is not a continuous authored exposition. It mixes:

- copied / paraphrased source propositions;
- translations and lexical apparatus;
- bibliographic cues;
- questions and objections;
- local authorial supplements;
- classificatory diagrams and recompositions.

Public rule:

> **Lovejoy handwriting is a carrier, not automatic proposition authorship.**

The article therefore illustrates rather than announces a source-critical reading sequence:

`identify source packet`
→ `return source-owned proposition to source`
→ `locate explicit interruption / connective / reassignment`
→ `state only the Lovejoy-local residual`.

## 3. Witness criticism remains in the evidence layer

## 5. 005 public showcase: one order does not establish another

The principal body example is pp. 103–104. A proposed developmental path in sacrifice is tested against an independently relevant chronology of agriculture and animal domestication. Under one assumed stage sequence, the genealogy risks a `vicious circle`; Lovejoy then tests the premise required to generate that objection rather than simply accepting the contradiction.

Compact public result:

> **classificatory or developmental sequence is not yet historical sequence.**

This repeats the structural lesson of p. 123 without pretending that the objects are the same. In 004, temporal sequence and logical inclusion can coexist; in 005, morphological/developmental order and historical chronology require separate warrants.

Other 005 Lovejoy-local residuals stay in the note/terminal synthesis unless a later revision specifically needs them:

## 6. Later publications are relation-specific exits

The current public body retains three later exits, each with one job:

- **1902:** comparison != transmission. Aristotle/Vedānta resemblance can be admitted while eastern influence is rejected where an internal Greek route suffices. This is the cleanest later verbal analogue to the notebook’s refusal to let one relation establish another.
- **1906 politics:** developmental stage and racial/national difference are explicitly separated. The distinction then enters colonial and franchise argument, showing that relation-sensitive differentiation can carry political consequences without being presented as an inevitable implication of the Buddhist notebooks.
- ***Great Chain*:** Asian materials remain present but change historical function inside an explicitly Occidental narrative center. The comparison concerns narrative address and object-function, not disappearance, betrayal, or hidden Buddhist origin.

The 1906 Kant / *Primitive Philosophy* decomposition-generalization counter-case remains a useful evidence-layer control against tidy maturation stories, but it is no longer needed in the public body. Brinton, Boas, and the 1905 `primitive` material remain backstage controls unless an exact later bridge becomes necessary.

## 7. Closing historiographic gain

The ending should return to the bibliography hook through the same relation-specific language established by the showcase cases. The notebook does not reveal a forgotten Buddhist origin of the history of ideas. It changes the evidentiary status of a formation already known biographically by showing what Lovejoy was actually doing inside it.

The final recurrence can be stated concretely:

- p. 123: temporal antecedence can coexist with logical inclusion;
- 1898 print: borrowing can coexist with an original arrangement/application;
- 1902 print: resemblance can coexist with rejection of transmission.

Do not convert this recurrence into a causal genealogy of mature method. The defensible claim is repetition of a problem of warranted relations, not the birth of the history of ideas in Buddhist studies.

The source-critical floor remains:

> a notebook page is not yet a proposition; handwriting is not yet authorship; source match is not yet assent; publication is not simply a cleaned notebook.
