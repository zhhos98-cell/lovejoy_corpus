# JHI Blog — field method versus Lovejoy operation: provenance matrix for notebook 004

Date: 2026-08-20
Status: active focused synthesis
Scope: distinguish relation-discrimination already present in Lovejoy's source field from relation-types that notebook 004 visibly reassigns, sharpens, recombines, or formulates in Lovejoy's own working voice. This is a provenance-control pass, not a priority claim.

## Core result

The notebook's methodological significance becomes stronger, not weaker, once the upstream field is separated from Lovejoy's own operations.

The field already supplied several powerful inferential disciplines:

- Garbe: generic resemblance versus diagnostic resemblance; formal detail can support historical attribution;
- Rhys Davids: chronology/textual survival limits whole-system inference;
- Warren 1893: adjacent links in dependent origination stand in heterogeneous relation-types; dependence is not uniformly causal or temporal;
- Senart: textual repetition/heterogeneity can support compositional and genealogical reconstruction;
- Oldenberg: canonical usage and functional position can block over-rapid genealogy or semantic identity;
- Hardy: rebirth may involve more than one causal factor.

Lovejoy's documentary delta is therefore not invention of `comparison`, `context`, `function`, or `relation-types`. It is visible in four operations:

1. **relation reassignment** — accepting the question but changing the relation assigned by an authority;
2. **scope restriction / scale control** — limiting what a true relation is entitled to prove about a larger historical object;
3. **cross-axis separation** — keeping temporal, logical, causal, lexical and historical relations distinct even when they concern the same concepts;
4. **recomposition with calibrated confidence** — combining inherited propositions into a new configuration while refusing to let semantic, functional or historical certainty automatically travel across levels.

The strongest compact formulation is:

> **Lovejoy did not invent the field's relational method. Notebook 004 shows him reassigning relations, tightening their scale, and preventing one established relation from automatically settling another.**

## 1. Warren 1893 now gives direct primary control for the field-level relation grammar

A direct searchable PDF of Warren's 1893 AOS paper confirms the point at unusually high resolution.

Warren explicitly says:

- the Western title `Chain of Causation` imposes overly exact Occidental categories;
- Time plays only a secondary role;
- `paccaya` expresses dependence, which need not be causal;
- the relation of each member to the next must be examined separately.

He then assigns different relation-types across the chain:

- birth → old age/death: antecedent condition;
- existence → birth: general category / particular instance;
- attachment → desire: identity;
- sensation → desire: necessary antecedent/condition;
- contact → sensation: cause/effect;
- nāmarūpa → sense organs: material cause;
- consciousness ↔ nāmarūpa: interdependence;
- some consciousnesses ← saṅkhāras: effect/cause;
- ignorance → saṅkhāras: antecedent condition.

He concludes that only a minority of the dependencies are efficient causes and explicitly refuses to make the chain uniformly temporal.

This directly establishes that **relation-type discrimination is an upstream field method before 004**.

Important consequence: the article should never imply that Lovejoy discovered that different conceptual relations require different descriptions. Warren was already doing this in the Harvard/AOS Buddhist world.

## 2. Provenance classes

Use four classes internally.

### F — field-inherited

The relation rule or inferential problem is directly present in a source Lovejoy demonstrably uses.

### R — Lovejoy reassignment / restriction

The upstream source supplies the question or relation, but Lovejoy changes the assigned relation or restricts the scale of the inference.

### S — Lovejoy synthesis / recomposition

The source materials are inherited, but no exact upstream formulation has yet been found for the relation Lovejoy constructs among them.

### C — confidence / proof recalibration

Lovejoy largely accepts an inherited semantic or historical direction but changes how strongly different evidentiary layers are allowed to support it.

These classes can overlap.

- detach that result from a settled etymology;

- describe evidence as relevant but not necessarily conclusive;

This is the cleanest case of **confidence/proof recalibration**.

## 4. What is genuinely most Lovejoy-specific in the surviving notebook?

Not a single method term.

The most documentable deltas are operations on inherited relations.

### 1. Relation reassignment

Best case: Warren's `identity` → Lovejoy's `But not so...` → differentiated desire/attachment functions.

### 2. Unit/scale restriction

Best case: a possible Sāṃkhyan feature does not prove the later system `existed as a whole`.

### 3. Cross-axis relation separation

Best case: viññāṇa is `temporally` antecedent yet `logically` included/subdivided.

### 4. Causal division of labour

Best case: Hardy's two factors become fact-of-rebirth versus particular-condition-of-rebirth.

### 5. Genealogy/configuration partition

Best case: borrowed/composite components do not by themselves determine arrangement/application or system-level intelligibility.

### 6. Epistemic calibration

Best case: upādisesa can have a strong contextual/ethical reading while etymology and total textual genealogy remain unsettled.

These are all **transformations of a field method**, not inventions ex nihilo.

## 6. Blog deployment consequence

The Blog should make the field/operation distinction visible in prose.

A compact narrative sequence:

1. **Warren gives the rule**: dependent origination contains different kinds of relation; not every `paccaya` is the same sort of cause.
2. **Lovejoy turns the rule back on Warren**: Warren calls desire/attachment identical; notebook says `But not so if we follow the text`.
3. **Lovejoy scales the problem upward**: a doctrinal resemblance does not prove a whole Sāṃkhya system; composite/borrowed parts do not determine a configuration's identity.
4. **Publication calibrates proof**: usage can support a semantic judgment while etymology/text history remain unsettled.

This gives a much sharper relation between `field` and `formation`:

> **The field supplied the problem of heterogeneous relations. The notebook shows Lovejoy learning how to police the traffic among them.**

That sentence may be close to the Blog's methodological center.
