# Batch 43 addendum — WZKM 1897 Dahlmann notice: Leopold von Schroeder attribution

Date: 2026-08-18

A later primary self-reference by **Leopold von Schroeder** materially strengthens attribution of the 1897 _Wiener Zeitschrift für die Kunde des Morgenlandes_ notice of Joseph Dahlmann’s _Nirvāṇa_, pp.190–197.

In the preface/notes to von Schroeder’s _Bhagavadgita: Des Erhabenen Sang_, he refers to Dahlmann’s 1896 _Nirvāṇa_ and says:

> `Vgl. meine Bemerkungen über dies bedeutende Werk in der Wiener Zeitschrift für die Kunde des Morgenlandes ... Jahrg. 1897. p. 190–197.`

This is direct authorial self-attribution: von Schroeder calls the WZKM pp.190–197 remarks **“meine Bemerkungen.”** It is therefore stronger than an inference from later bibliography.

## Bibliographic anomaly

There is a small discrepancy that should be preserved rather than silently corrected:

- the digitized WZKM object is **Volume 11 (1897)** and its table of contents places `JOSEPH DAHLMANN S. J. Nirvana. Eine Studie zur Vorgeschichte des Buddhismus` at **p.190**;
- von Schroeder’s later note gives **“Bd. X. Jahrg. 1897. p.190–197.”**

The page range and year agree; the volume numeral does not. The safest explanation is a later bibliographic slip in von Schroeder’s note, but this should remain an inference until the original review heading/signature is directly inspected.

## Current evidence status

Secure:
- a WZKM 1897 item on Dahlmann’s _Nirvāṇa_ begins at p.190 in the digitized Volume 11 contents;
- von Schroeder later explicitly calls pp.190–197 his own remarks on Dahlmann’s work.

Still worth direct control:
- printed p.190 title/heading;
- printed p.197 signature/end matter;
- exact substantive position taken by von Schroeder in 1897.

## Significance for 004

This strengthens the evidence that Dahlmann’s 1896 book immediately entered a live German-language debate. It does **not** establish Lovejoy’s access. The useful historical point is the density of the controversy: Dahlmann was reviewed by Jacobi in the GGA and by von Schroeder in WZKM in 1897, before the Jacobi/Oldenberg/Lovejoy interventions of 1898.

This supports a history of **problem circulation and standards of inference**, not a direct influence chain to Lovejoy.
