Official volume control remains BSB/MDZ `bsb11809713`, URN `urn:nbn:de:bvb:12-bsb11809713-2`; redundant IA route `revuedelhistoir32alphgoog`.

Primary result:

- p.113: Marillier accurately reports Steinmetz's distinction between superadded religious motives and a proposed nonreligious original cause, while stressing that the persistence of many observed endocannibal practices is inseparable from animistic belief;
- p.114: the decisive statement is that the reasons given for cannibal practice are varied and disparate; the cases are grouped only by **external resemblance** and are **not amenable to a single interpretation**;
- p.115: physiological/economic causes may be decisive in some cases, but Steinmetz has not established a **unique origin**; animistic or magical conceptions can independently account for other cases, so no necessary chronological priority of endocannibalism over exocannibalism follows;
- Marillier closes by criticizing Steinmetz's `théories exclusives et parfois hasardées` while valuing the study's source criticism.

Attribution consequence:

> The anti-morphological / mechanism-plurality operator isolated in 005 is **already explicitly articulated by Marillier in 1896**. Lovejoy 005 should therefore be described as uptake/redeployment/elaboration within a Marillierian methodological environment, not as independent invention of the rule.

This also sharpens the Steinmetz comparison:

> **Steinmetz = local motive plurality + secondary refunctionalization under a strong universal nutritional/phylogenetic origin hierarchy.**  
> **Marillier = external similarity does not warrant one explanatory class, and even origin may be plural or independently animistic/magical.**
