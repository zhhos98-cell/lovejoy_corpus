# Project 2 — contemporaneous student notebooks / Marillier controls

**Sweep date:** 2026-08-19

## Research question

This subproject asks whether Arthur O. Lovejoy's Paris notebook `MS38_004_001_061_005` can be controlled against **independent notes or working papers by other students in Léon Marillier's EPHE seminars**, ideally from the same 1898–1899 cohort. One or two genuine comparanda would materially strengthen the paper by separating Marillier's seminar material from Lovejoy's own later organization and abstraction.

The search therefore prioritizes, in order:

1. same teacher + same year + surviving notebook/working papers;
2. same teacher + adjacent years + surviving notebook/working papers;
3. institutional records that resolve student identities and seminar practice;
4. broader period controls only when they clarify documentary genre or method.

Marcel Mauss is **ancillary context, not the center of Project 2**.

### Separate Paris-years material

A user-supplied 25-page Uppsala finding-aid PDF inspected on 2026-08-19 is **not the notebook catalogue itself**, but it contains a distinct archival block on p. 3:

`Minnen 1894-1901. Parisåren`

with subcategories including:

- `Föreningar och institut`
- `Religion och kyrka`
- invitations/programmes around visits and events
- `Diverse`

This is a secondary Paris-era target: it may preserve programmes, institutional ephemera, invitations, or loose scholarly material, but it must not be confused with `B. Dagböcker / anteckningsböcker`.

### Immediate archival question

> Which physical volume(s) inside `Dag- och anteckningsböcker 1894-1899`, `1896-1901`, or `1898-1915` actually cover Paris / EPHE / Marillier, and can Uppsala supply targeted scans?

Söderblom remains the highest-value possible control because his 1898 diploma project, `Traces dans le Mazdéisme d'une ancienne conception sur la survivance des morts`, was prepared under Marillier and is very close to the first half of Lovejoy 005.

## 3. Travers — upgraded to second-priority identity/archive target

Travers is no longer merely a surname in the 1898–99 Marillier list. Persée confirms a sustained presence in the Vth Section:

- 1897–98: sole titular student in Émile Amélineau's `Religions de l'Égypte` conference.
  - https://www.persee.fr/doc/ephe_0000-0002_1897_num_11_7_19482
- 1898–99: Amélineau explicitly says the Coptic `Vie de saint Macaire de Scété` was read **avec la collaboration de M. Travers**; Travers is again titular student.
  - https://www.persee.fr/doc/ephe_0000-0002_1898_num_12_8_19505
- 1901–02: Travers remains titular student under Amélineau.
  - https://www.persee.fr/doc/ephe_0000-0002_1901_num_15_11_19574
- 1902–03: Travers is still listed as titular student.
  - https://www.persee.fr/doc/ephe_0000-0002_1902_num_16_12_19597

This makes Travers a much better archival target than a one-year auditor: he was embedded in the Vth Section over several years and participated in textual work. His **full identity remains unresolved**. The next task is to identify him from EPHE administrative records and then search personal papers / university archives / Egyptological-Coptic networks for surviving notebooks.

## 6. SIV image-delivery layer: browser front end can be bypassed

The Archives nationales SIV gallery exposes stable per-image JPEG URLs of the form:

`https://www.siv.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/mm/media/download/FRAN_0464_XXXXX_L-medium.jpg`

A gallery page therefore does not have to be manually paged once one has established the sequential image-ID range.

### Range A — 383 views

Browser-derived range:

- first image: `FRAN_0464_06736_L-medium.jpg`
- last image: `FRAN_0464_07118_L-medium.jpg`
- total: **383**

The first inspected image is an item dated **26 July 1897** (Lycée Louis-le-Grand letterhead). Navigation context indicates this is the late 1886–1899 correspondence gallery being targeted for 1898–99, but the exact parent `udId` should still be recorded in the manifest before treating that parent mapping as final.

### Range B — 435 views

Gallery URL captured:

`https://www.siv.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/siv/rechercheconsultation/consultation/multimedia/Galerie.action?irId=FRAN_IR_061975&udId=c-4djk5r1n8--72f9e5zjfghl`

Sequential range:

- `FRAN_0464_07119_L-medium.jpg`
- through `FRAN_0464_07553_L-medium.jpg`
- total: **435**

Internal evidence (1901 Mauss candidature material near views 426–434) indicates that this gallery belongs to the **1900–1912** correspondence sequence, almost certainly `20190568/70`, not the 1886–1899 target.

The two recovered image ranges are directly consecutive (`07118` → `07119`), which is useful for reconstructing SIV's digitization sequence even though gallery-level parentage still needs explicit recording.

### Harvest script

Generic PowerShell downloader + PDF merger added at:

`tools/harvest_siv_gallery.ps1`

Intended commands:

```powershell
.\tools\harvest_siv_gallery.ps1 -StartImage 6736 -EndImage 7118 -Name "EPHE_FRAN0464_06736_07118"
.\tools\harvest_siv_gallery.ps1 -StartImage 7119 -EndImage 7553 -Name "EPHE_FRAN0464_07119_07553"
```

The script downloads sequential JPEGs with retries and a polite delay, validates file count/size, and merges the images in numeric order to a PDF using `img2pdf`.

## 7. Incidental Mauss dossier — retain as context, do not let it drive Project 2

The 435-view gallery accidentally surfaced a 1901 candidature dossier concerning the chair left vacant by Marillier's death.

Relevant views / image IDs recovered:

- gallery view 428 → `FRAN_0464_07546_L-medium.jpg`
- 429 → `...07547...`
- 430 → `...07548...`
- 431 → `...07549...`
- 432 → `...07550...`
- 433 → `...07551...`
- 434 → `...07552...`

The manuscript evidence includes:

- Mauss's candidature / career statement, signed `Marcel Mauss`;
- a recommendation from C. P. Tiele dated Leiden, 1 November 1901;
- Mauss describing his critical contribution to *L'Année sociologique* and a continually refined `classement` of religious facts;
- an explicit statement that, if chosen, he would initially direct his work in the direction in which Marillier had directed his, especially the **gathering and criticism of ethnographic materials**;
- attached bibliographic/classification sheets for the *Revue de l'histoire des religions*, *Revue philosophique*, *Internationales Archiv für Ethnographie*, and *L'Année sociologique*;
- one classification sheet places `Söderblom: Les Fravashis` under beliefs/rites concerning the dead.

This is potentially useful for contextualizing Marillier's paper-based research method and the later succession of the programme. It is **not a substitute for the same-year student-notebook control**, and Project 2 should not drift into a Mauss paper.

## 10. Stopping rule for this subproject

Project 2 does **not** require an exhaustive prosopography of the entire Vth Section. A decisive result would be:

- one same-year second notebook (ideal: Söderblom, Travers, Schaefer), or
- two adjacent but clearly Marillier-linked student working-note corpora,

combined with the institutional evidence already available for the 1898–99 seminar.

Until then, the highest-return path is:

**Söderblom volume identification → EPHE identity resolution → Travers/Schaefer archive search.**
