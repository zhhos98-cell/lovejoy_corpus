## 1. Transcription-layer rule

The `*_clean.json` corpus is heterogeneous in transcription mode. Some entries are close diplomatic/manual readings; others retain source-supported argumentative structure where handwriting was too difficult. Therefore:

> **`corrected_text` is not uniformly equivalent to Lovejoy's exact manuscript wording.**

The anti-circularity rule remains:

`MANUSCRIPT VISIBLE TEXT` ≠ `EDITORIAL ARGUMENT SUMMARY` ≠ `EXTERNAL SOURCE COLLATION` ≠ `MATERIAL LAYOUT OBSERVATION`.

Page layout is evidence in its own right: diagrams, spatial nesting, numbering, brackets, marginal/interlinear additions, strike-throughs, facing-page relations, slips, foldouts and overlays may document an operation that linear transcription hides. Material observation comes before conceptual interpretation.

## 2. Four-axis protocol

Every consequential source claim is coded separately:

- **W — manuscript witness**: `W3` direct image/diplomatic secure; `W2` key wording visible but syntax/source label partly uncertain; `W1` editorial/source-supported argumentative reconstruction; `W0` no manuscript witness.
- **S — source-text match**: `S3` explicit bibliographic anchor + distinctive text match; `S2` distinctive phrase/sequence; `S1` proposition/case-cluster; `S0` controversy-field only.
- **T — transmission/uptake**: `T3` direct uptake demonstrated; `T2` strong carrier/source-family hypothesis; `T1` documented field route only; `T0` comparison only.
- **A — Lovejoy authorial operation**: `A3` image-secure manuscript operation + publication transformation; `A2` direct local supplementation/reweighting/recomposition; `A1` probable operation inferred from editorial reconstruction; `A0` source-owned or presently unassignable.

No single `closed` label substitutes for the four axes. An explicit author/page manuscript anchor can establish direct textual uptake without proving possession of one particular physical edition or copy.
