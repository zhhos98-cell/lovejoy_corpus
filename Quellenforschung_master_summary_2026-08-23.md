## 0. Corpus and method / 语料与方法

**Object / 对象.** Johns Hopkins Lovejoy papers MS-0038, two notebooks: `004` (`Sankhya + Buddhism`, 71 pp.) and `005` (Paris notebook, 120 pp.), **191 pages fully corrected and transcribed**, English–Chinese parallel, PaddleOCR-VL used only as scaffold, page-by-page human correction, preserving abbreviations, uncertain readings, confidence levels, and original manuscript/PDF pagination and SHA. / 约翰斯·霍普金斯 MS-0038 中的两册手稿，共 191 页全文校正转录，中英对照，OCR 仅作脚手架，逐页人工复核，保留缩写、疑难读法、置信度及原始页码与校验值。

**Method / 方法.** A six-code source-critical apparatus (`Q·D` collated direct source / `Q·N` named but edition/page not locked / `Q·A` Lovejoy's own intervention / `Q·C` contextual-comparative control / `Q·H` unresolved source hypothesis / `Q·V` transcription/pagination correction), **184 sentence-level judgments**, backed by 500+ source-register rows and 187 concordance matrices. The governing rule is **proof-level separation**: an L4 (historical/textual) claim must be argued as an L4 claim, never smuggled in from a weaker resemblance or lexical association. / 自建六级来源批判标记，184 条逐句判定，配 500+ 行来源登记与 187 张校勘矩阵。核心纪律是证据分级不可混用：L4 历史/文本结论必须作为 L4 论证，不得从更弱的相似或词形关联偷渡。

**Governing thesis / 总命题.** The strongest first-order claim is Lovejoy's own: terms, "perhaps even the ideas," may be borrowed while their "arrangement and application" remain "original and characteristic" — **a philosophical formation can be historically composite without being merely derivative**. The corpus repeatedly refuses the origin claim that Buddhism "caused" the unit-idea method; it demonstrates instead that a decomposition-and-recomposition habit was already visible in Lovejoy's Harvard and Paris work. / 最稳的一阶命题直接来自 Lovejoy 本人：术语、"甚至也许观念"可借来，而其"排列与应用"仍可独创——哲学体系可历史复合而不仅是模仿。全库反复拒绝"佛教导致 unit-idea 方法"的起源论，转而证明这套分解—重组的分析习惯在其哈佛与巴黎时期已可实证可见。

## Wing I / 第一翼: 004 → 1898
### A dispute over what kind of relation the evidence can establish / 一场关于"证据能证明什么关系"的争论

`004` is a coherent working dossier, not a reading notebook: it runs from Sāṃkhya method to Nirvāṇa and closes with Lovejoy's own historical stratification (original elements = flux / Three Characteristics + Dependent Origination; derived elements = sense-perception theory, psychology, khandhas). Its published counterpart is "The Buddhistic Technical Terms upādāna and upādisesa," *JAOS* 19 (1898), 126–136. Five locked scenes: / `004` 是连贯的工作卷宗而非读书笔记，从数论方法一路推进到涅槃，末尾以 Lovejoy 自己的历史分层收束。其刊本对应即 1898 年 JAOS 那篇论文。五个已锁场景：

**① Entry via a proof problem (Garbe) / 起点即导入证明问题.** PDF pp.4–5 / MS pp.8–9, headed `The Method of the Sankhya / Garbe p.150`, digests Garbe 1894 on the rule that unusually dense terminological/definitional agreement can mark a logical apparatus as an adopted "alien element." **Control:** the proof problem is not Lovejoy's invention; his substantive notebook opens inside a methodology specialists were already arguing. / 004 开篇标注 "Garbe p.150"，抄入 Garbe 1894 的判据规则。控制：证明问题非 Lovejoy 发明，他一进门就站在专家已争的方法论里。

**② The cleanest authorial hinge (Rhys Davids) / 最干净的作者转折.** PDF pp.16–17 / MS pp.61–63. After digesting Rhys Davids's Sāṃkhya/Buddhism chronology, Lovejoy refuses to let one early passage prove the Sāṃkhya system "existed as a whole," marks `To R.D.'s remark it should be added…`, restricts the diagnostic to the separateness of Self and World / thorough-going dualism, and turns to Majjhima Nikāya 63 for a more genuinely Sāṃkhyan parallel. The five-step operation — **source uptake → identify insufficient diagnostic → restrict scale → propose sharper test → seek a better witness** — is visible without projecting mature "unit-idea" language backward. / 抄完 Rhys Davids 年表后，Lovejoy 拒绝让一处早期段落证明数论体系"整体已存在"，写下 "To R.D.'s remark it should be added…"，把诊断收紧为"自我与世界的彻底二元"，转向 MN 63。这条五步操作是全库最能说明问题的一处。

**③ Relation reassignment (Warren) / 关系重指派.** PDF p.22 / MS p.75. Warren himself held that *paccaya* marks different relations in different links, yet classified the taṇhā–upādāna relation as one of "identity." Lovejoy copies the judgment, then breaks: `But not so if we are to follow the present text`, comparing four kinds of attachment with six forms of desire. Warren's own rule (examine each link separately) is turned against one of Warren's own conclusions. **Upstream control:** Warren 1896 already suspects the formula is a "piece of patchwork," so decomposition is inherited; Lovejoy's increment lies in **recomposition**, not discovery. / Warren 本人主张缘起各支关系不同，却把欲—取判为"同一"。Lovejoy 抄下后断开："But not so…"，以四取六欲比对。用 Warren 自己的规则反驳其自身结论。上游控制：Warren 1896 已疑公式为拼合，故分解是继承的；Lovejoy 增量在重组。

**④ The main battlefield — composite ≠ meaningless (Senart) / 主战场：复合 ≠ 无意义.** PDF p.49 / MS p.137, against Senart 1896 (pp.284–295). Senart calls the twelve nidānas an `ordre plus ou moins accidentel` and reduces upādāna to upādānaskandhas (skandhas appearing twice = secondary construction). Lovejoy nearly translates the conclusion, then shifts: `On Senart's account of the matter there is simply this to be said`. He **concedes almost the whole premise** (terms not original to Buddhism, heterogeneous categories, non-homogeneous temporal sequence, real repetitions) yet rejects the inference: calling the formula "illogical & unintelligible" or accidentally assembled is `entirely false`. This becomes the 1898 principle (p.132): borrowed terms, "perhaps even ideas," with `original and characteristic` arrangement/application. **Historiographic control:** Senart 1896 p.295 himself advocates the "analysis and filiation" of terms and warns against imputing reflective speculation to inherited nomenclatures — so genealogy/stratification/term-analysis are **not** Lovejoy-specific; his increment is strictly to restrict what such evidence may infer about present semantic/systematic function. / 主战场对 Senart 1896。Senart 判十二支为偶然拼合、化约 upādāna。Lovejoy 让步几乎全部前提，却拒绝"不可理解/纯属偶然"的推论（"entirely false"），长成 1898 那句总原则。历史学控制：Senart p.295 本人即提倡术语的分析与谱系，故谱系/分层/术语分析非 Lovejoy 独有；其增量严格限定在"这类证据能推出什么关于当下语义/系统功能的结论"。

**⑤ What print adds — a scale of confidence (upādisesa) / 刊本增加的一层：置信度刻度.** PDF p.66 / MS p.173 passes near-verbatim into print (treating death itself as the *summum bonum* is profoundly un-Buddhistic). Print adds a scale the notebook leaves implicit: meaning `by an examination of its use than by etymological arguments`; evidence `relevant but not necessarily conclusive`; `cannot be regarded as finally settled` (1898, pp.134–136). **Upstream:** Oldenberg 1882 (Eng. tr. p.433, cited by Lovejoy) already supplies the ethical-residue thesis, Itivuttaka counter-evidence, and the upādi=upadhi orthographic hypothesis (with a stronger orthographic resolution than Lovejoy accepts). Lovejoy's move is **controlled recomposition**: keep the ethical direction, decouple it from settled etymology, add usage evidence, preserve contradictory witnesses and provisionality. / 004 p.173 近乎逐字进入刊本；印本额外加了置信度尺子。上游 Oldenberg 1882 已给出伦理残余论与正字假设（结论比 Lovejoy 更强），故 Lovejoy 是受控重组：保留伦理方向、与已定词源解耦、补用法证据、保留矛盾证人与临时性。

**Manuscript → publication net transformation / 手稿→刊本净变换.** Broad Sāṃkhya/Buddhism comparison → narrow two-term technical article; collect competing definitions → polemic against Senart; nidāna↔khandha mapping → three-stage causal interpretation; "does heterogeneity make it incoherent?" → "borrowed materials can have original arrangement"; contradictory etymologies kept → explicitly provisional conclusion. **Publication selects the propositions that can bear an explicit burden of proof; the notebook preserves the branching proof problems.** / 净变换：宽域比较→窄技术文章；竞争定义→对 Senart 的论战；映射→三段因果；开放探究→"借来材料可有独创排列"；保留矛盾→临时结论。刊本选取能承担明确举证责任者，手稿保存分叉的证明问题本身。

## Wing II / 第二翼: 005 → Marillier/EPHE → 1906
### The corpus's most original — and most honest — chain / 全库最原创、也最诚实的一条

`005` is a **longitudinal composite notebook**, not a single-date object: strong Paris 1898–99 core (Paris stationery; internal `Dec. 14`, `May 29`, `Marillier — Survivance — 12 June`; the official EPHE 1898–99 report names Lovejoy among auditors who "took an active part" in Marillier's conferences on survival of the soul, human sacrifice and ritual anthropophagy), with documented reuse to `Hist. Relig. — Dec. 20, 1905` (p.44, second-pass correction superseding a first-pass "1805?"). Published counterpart: "The Fundamental Concept of the Primitive Philosophy," *The Monist* 16.3 (1906), 357–382 (full 26-pp. scan in the File Library; the comparison no longer depends on later histories of `mana`). / `005` 是纵向复合笔记：强巴黎 1898–99 内核（巴黎信纸、内部日期、`Marillier — Survivance — 12 June`；官方 EPHE 报告点名 Lovejoy 积极参与），确证续用至 1905 年 12 月 20 日（p.44 二次校对推翻先前 "1805?"）。刊本对应即 1906 年《The Monist》那篇。

**The self-correction that raises the whole corpus's integrity / 提升全库诚信的自我纠正.** The first-round judgment described 005 as Lovejoy independently inventing mechanism-first decomposition of ritual. A broad reading of Marillier's 1890s EPHE reports forced a revision: / 第一轮把 005 判为 Lovejoy 独立发明机制优先的仪式分解；通读 Marillier 报告后被迫改判：

- Marillier 1892–93: ritual form can survive its original belief (institutions/rites/legends survive the beliefs that generated them) — 005 p.73 (funerary destruction of property) performs exactly this. / 仪式形式可脱离原初信仰存活——005 p.73 焚随葬品同一操作。
- Marillier 1894–97: mechanisms inside one ceremony must be analytically separated (religious union vs magical purification vs fecundation) — the rule that dominates 005: **co-presence in one rite does not prove common function or origin**. / 同一仪典内机制须分开——支配 005 的规则。
- Marillier 1897–99: the human-sacrifice course divides West-African material into funerary / expiatory / anthropophagic / magical and makes the task "determine the reasons for the use of human victims in the different types" — almost the architecture of 005 pp.46–119. / 人祭课分四类并以"确定各类型献祭中使用人牲的理由"为任务——几乎就是 005 pp.46–119 的建筑。

**Revised judgment / 修正后判断.** The analytic habit in 005 should in significant part be treated as a **learned EPHE/Marillier practice**; Lovejoy's distinctive move is the later **abstraction and generalization** into a theory of impersonal, transferable, quasi-mechanical efficacy — the 1906 "category more fundamental than personality" (p.359). The 1906 paper is itself a continuation of Quellenforschung: lexical/philological (manitou/oki/wakonda; Bantu terms), warning that ethnographers mistranslate power/energy as "god"/"spirit" — structurally the same as the 1898 Buddhist technical-term work, in a different language and discipline. / 005 的分析习惯很大程度是习得的 EPHE/Marillier 实践；Lovejoy 独创在于 1906 年把它抽象、普遍化为非人格、可转移、准机械的效力理论。1906 那篇本身是 Quellenforschung 的延续，与 1898 佛教术语工作同构。

**Honest evidential ceiling / 诚实的证据上限.** Concept/mechanism/vocabulary/source-ecology continuity is "strong"; a **literal draft relationship remains unproved**; `mana / manitou / wakonda / orenda` do **not** appear as secure readings in the first-pass 005 transcription. A second student witness (Nathan Söderblom, same Marillier seminar, 1898 EPHE diploma directed by Marillier) upgrades this from single-witness reconstruction to a controlled two-student comparison, but still does not prove Lovejoy copied any Marillier lecture formula. / 概念/机制/词汇/文献生态连续性"强"；逐字底稿关系未证；`mana` 等在首轮转录中未作确读出现。第二学生证人 Söderblom 把单证人重构升级为受控双学生比较，但仍不证明抄录。

## Ledger / 总账

### Established (supportable on primary evidence) / 已确立（可用主要证据支撑）
1. Three page-locked manuscript→print scenes (Warren p.75 / Senart p.137 / upādisesa p.173), plus the Garbe entry and the Rhys Davids hinge. / 三个 page-lock 场景加 Garbe 起点与 Rhys Davids 转折。
2. An analytic habit already visible in 1898: **decomposition → borrowing without identity → recomposition into intelligibility**. / 1898 已可见的分析习惯：分解→无同一性借用→重组为可理解性。
3. Paris side: archival confirmation of Lovejoy as active participant in Marillier's EPHE environment, and 005↔1906 architectural continuity. / 巴黎侧：档案确证的积极参与与 005↔1906 架构连续性。

The 2026-08-23 audit maps *Great Chain* (p.30 / endnote 43 / p.218) and the *Essays* bibliography page by page — negative soteriology, the ten unanswered points, and the "elements vs arrangement" parallel all connect to 004, **without idealizing Lovejoy** (GCB p.142 racialized judgment recorded, not suppressed). / 8/23 审计逐页核对 GCB 与 Essays 书目，且不美化（GCB p.142 种族化判断照录）。

## Cross-field consolidation, 2026-08-23/24 / 跨学科综合增补

The later sweep does not displace the manuscript-level argument. It locates the same operations within historically documented assignments of persons, archives, and explanatory authority. / 本轮补充不取代逐页手稿论证，而是将同样的工作操作放入人物、文献和解释权的具体制度分配之中。

**1. The 1899 contemporary classification is explicit / 1899 年的分类在一手文本中明确存在.** Morris Jastrow, [“The Historical Study of Religions in Universities and Colleges”](https://www.jstor.org/stable/592338), *JAOS* 20 (1899), 317–325, classifies Buddhism with other “advanced religions” on pp. 318–319, then assigns Indian religions to Indologists and “primitive” religious phenomena to anthropologists on p. 321. He explicitly takes the Paris EPHE structure as an institutional model. The same journal volume puts Washburn Hopkins, Everett, Jastrow, Toy, and Lanman into a single pp. 303–337 publication sequence. Jastrow is a period-native witness to disciplinary allocation; no claim is made that Lovejoy read or followed his article. / Jastrow 第 318—319 页将佛教列入「高级宗教」，第 321 页分别将印度宗教和「原始」宗教现象指派给印度学家和人类学家，并以巴黎 EPHE 为制度模型。同卷第 303—337 页集中呈现原始宗教、印度哲学、宗教史、巫术和佛学网络。该文章是同时代制度见证，不能直接证明 Lovejoy 阅读或奉行其纲领。

**2. Paris participation is already proven / 巴黎参与已经由官方报告证实.** Marillier's [official EPHE report](https://www.persee.fr/doc/ephe_0000-0002_1898_num_12_8_19501), printed p. 33, names Lovejoy among active auditors. The status is stronger than thematic proximity and weaker than formal *élève titulaire* or diploma status. Mauss's earlier attendance under Marillier and Lévi shows shared pedagogical infrastructure; it does not establish a meeting with Lovejoy. / Marillier 官方报告第 33 页将 Lovejoy 列为积极参与研讨的旁听者，证据强于主题相近，但不足以证明正式学籍或文凭身份。Mauss 更早随 Marillier 和 Lévi 听课，只证明共享教学基础设施，不证明与 Lovejoy 会面。

**3. The 1901 syllabus has an earlier bibliographic witness / 1901 年课程有更早的书目证据.** The *Essays in the History of Ideas* bibliography, original collection 1948, inspected in the 1960 Capricorn reprint at printed p. 340 / PDF p. 362, lists `Syllabus: The philosophy of Buddhism, four lectures. The Washington University Association, 1901.` Wilson 1982 supplies the four lecture titles, but is not the earliest surviving bibliographic witness. The syllabus itself and actual lecture delivery remain uninspected. / 《观念史论文集》1948 年原始编纂、1960 年重印本第 340 页已著录 1901 年四讲佛教课程；Wilson 1982 提供四讲标题，但并非最早书目证据。课程提纲原件和实际讲授情况仍未核实。

**4. Dumont's omission is primary-text verified, and the standard page range needs correction / Dumont 的省略已有一手页码锁定，常见页码范围应修正.** In the repository's Octagon second printing of 1973, reproducing the 1935 volume, Dumont's `Primitivism in Indian Literature` starts at printed p. 433 / PDF p. 451 and ends at printed p. 446 / PDF p. 464. Its last paragraph expressly excludes the entire Buddhist and Jain literatures. Printed p. 447 / PDF p. 465 begins `APPENDIX SOME MEANINGS OF 'NATURE'`; therefore the repeatedly cited range `433–447` is inaccurate for the inspected book. The club register independently places Dumont's `Primitivism in Ancient India` on March 8, 1934. Seminar inclusion, supplementary placement, and omitted archives are distinct operations; the titles differ and editorial responsibility remains unknown. / 仓库中 1973 年重印的 1935 年著作表明，Dumont 补篇实际为印刷第 433—446 页，末段明确省略全部佛教和耆那教文献；第 447 页已经进入另一附录。1934 年 3 月 8 日的俱乐部报告另证印度材料曾进入研讨。口头参与、补篇位置和文献遗漏必须分开，不能据此推定编辑责任。

**5. Primitive changes semantic function / primitive 的语义功能随材料而变化.** The 1898 Buddhist article has no occurrence of *primitive*. Jastrow 1899 treats Buddhism as “advanced” in a civilization-rank distinction. Lovejoy 1906 attributes an abstract causal category to ethnographically classified living populations. *The Great Chain of Being*, printed pp. 30, 97, and 218, explicitly says “primitive Buddhism,” where the immediate contrast concerns early Buddhist doctrine. Lovejoy and Boas 1935 use chronological and cultural *primitivism* as historian-owned analytical categories. These are temporal, anthropological, axiological, and disciplinary uses, not one interchangeable claim. / 1898 年佛教论文没有 *primitive*；Jastrow 1899 在文明分类中将佛教称作「高级」；1906 年则以民族志材料讨论当代人群的因果概念；《存在巨链》第 30、97、218 页使用「primitive Buddhism」，主要关联早期佛教教义；1935 年的年代性与文化性原始主义又属于史家自设分析范畴。时间、民族志、价值与学科分工应逐项区分。

**6. Existing scholarship constrains the novelty claim / 既有研究限定原创性表述.** [Schüttpelz, Albers and Schmid, *Gradhiva* 38 (2024)](https://journals.openedition.org/gradhiva/8458), partly translating a 2013 German contribution, already connects Lovejoy and Boas's primitivism project with its Asian supplements and *The Great Chain*. Ramkrishna Bhattacharya had already noticed Dumont's Buddhist/Jain omission in 2000, though the present repository now verifies it against the book itself and corrects the page range. [Amrita Nanda's 2024 chapter](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-55873-3_3), note 28, still cites Lovejoy 1898. The contribution is the cross-field reconstruction: notebook practice, specialist Buddhist controversy, active EPHE participation, explicit 1899 institutional sorting, asynchronous affiliation and continued teaching, the 1934 club paper, the page-446 omission, and the mature work's own use of “primitive Buddhism.” / 原始主义与观念史的关系、亚洲补篇及佛教遗漏均已有各自的研究前史，佛教学界至 2024 年仍引用 Lovejoy。原创位置在于把逐页手稿、佛学技术争论、巴黎研讨、1899 年制度分类、会员身份与教学的不同时间线、1934 年报告、第 446 页省略以及成熟著作中的「primitive Buddhism」组合为同一条证据分级清楚的历史链条。

## One-sentence close / 一句话收束

The Quellenforschung does not hand over a hidden methodology waiting to be named. It hands over something more modest and more historically useful: the documentary record of a scholar working inside a transregional comparative field in which similarity, identity, provenance, configuration, and genealogy were never interchangeable claims. Warren supplied a relation and Lovejoy reassigned it; Senart established compositeness and Lovejoy denied that compositeness alone explained the system; a lexical problem survived into print, and publication made the limits of the evidence explicit. The recurring question was never simply whether two terms, doctrines, or traditions were related — but **what kind of relation the evidence was entitled to establish**. / 这套源流批评没有交出一个待命名的隐藏方法论，而是交出更朴素、也更有史学用处的东西：一个人在跨区域比较学术场里工作的实证记录，其中 similarity / identity / provenance / configuration / genealogy 从来不可互换。反复出现的问题从来不是"两者是否相关"，而是"证据究竟有资格确立哪一种关系"。
