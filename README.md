## Project boundary

The project does **not** argue that Buddhism, Paris comparative religion, Max Müller, Brinton, Boas, or any single teacher caused Lovejoy's mature unit-idea method. It reconstructs documented practices of separating relations, proof burdens, historical positions, and scales of comparison, then tests those practices against period controls.

Core source-ownership rules:

> **Notebook handwriting is a carrier, not proof of authorship of the proposition carried.**

> **Source ownership of constituent propositions does not automatically establish source ownership of the relation drawn between them.**

> **A stronger proposition does not inherit evidence that established a weaker proposition.**

### AOS 1897 / 1898 publication path

The documented public title path is:

`Critical summary of the argument of the Milinda-pañha`

→ `On the meaning of the Buddhist technical terms ...`

→ 1898 `The Buddhistic technical terms upādāna and upādisesa`.

This is strong evidence of publication-path scale contraction. It is not proof that one unchanged manuscript simply changed title.

## Track B — `primitive`, chronology, and comparability

Current working thesis:

> **`primitive` is an operational stratifying category whose historical force depends not only on what it classifies, but on the inferential permissions attached to that classification.**

The project distinguishes at least five operations:

1. relational primitiveness;
2. internal stratification;
3. transverse reaggregation;
4. population synchronization;
5. process-first comparability.

The current three-way methodological control is:

> **Brinton = stage-first synchronization.**  
> **Boas = process-first comparability.**  
> **Lovejoy = relation-first reaggregation.**

This is a difference of inferential route, proof burden, and reaggregation scale, not a claim of direct influence. The key analytical distinction is between calendar **date** and assigned historical **position**.

Use `PROJECT_ARGUMENT_MAP.md` for the conceptual route and `CANONICAL_INDEX.md` for the current dossiers.

## Recurring firewalls

- `PAGE COVERAGE != DIPLOMATIC TRANSCRIPTION COMPLETION`.
- `NOTEBOOK HANDWRITING != PROPOSITION AUTHORSHIP`.
- `SOURCE-OWNED CONSTITUENTS != SOURCE-OWNED RELATION`.
- `PRINTED SOURCE COLLATION != RECOVERY OF ILLEGIBLE MANUSCRIPT WORDING`.
- `CITATION / ADJACENCY != UPTAKE / ASSENT`.
- `TITLE CONTINUITY != MANUSCRIPT IDENTITY`.
- `CALENDAR CONTEMPORANEITY != EQUAL DEVELOPMENTAL POSITION`.
- `PRIMITIVE PREDICATE != UNIFORM DEVELOPMENTAL INFERENCE`.
- `FORMAL RESEMBLANCE != PROVED COMPARABILITY`.
- comparative controls do not enter the Lovejoy evidence chain unless a direct historical relation is independently established.
