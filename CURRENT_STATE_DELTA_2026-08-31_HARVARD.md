### 1897 AOS

American Oriental Society Final Circular, 10 Apr 1897, item 24:

`Mr. ARTHUR O. LOVEJOY, Harvard University; Critical summary of the argument of the Milinda-pañha.`

The AOS communication therefore falls inside the **same academic year as Lovejoy's directly confirmed advanced Pāli enrollment**.

Secure chronology:

`1895–96 formal Sanskrit sequence`

`-> 1896–97 advanced Pāli 5 (A) + Toy Semitic 13 (A)`

`-> by 10 Apr 1897 AOS-announced Milinda-pañha paper`

`-> 1898 JAOS upādāna/upādisesa article`.

This is actor-level primary chronology. It does not prove Lanman assigned/caused the Milinda paper or that the 1897 paper is a version of the 1898 article.
