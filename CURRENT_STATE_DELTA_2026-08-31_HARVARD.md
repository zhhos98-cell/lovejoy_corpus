## Major state 3 — Anthropology 1 is now the key pre-Paris bridge

Direct actor fact:

> **Lovejoy completed Anthropology 1 in 1897–98 and received A.**

A Harvard/Peabody Museum primary statement dated **26 Aug 1898**, immediately after Lovejoy's third year, identifies:

`1. General Anthropology — Somatology (Physical Anthropology); Archaeology; Ethnology; Ethnography — lectures and theses — Dr. Frank Russell.`

Its Ethnology component explicitly includes:

- origin and development of **primitive arts and culture**;
- culture/environment relations;
- social life of `natural` peoples;
- primitive government, marriage, law / ethnic jurisprudence;
- **Religion: psychological origin and lines of development of primitive religions; fetishism; animism; shamanism; ritual**;
- mythology and its ethnographic value;
- folklore;
- linguistics, race/language classification;
- archaeology, ethnography, origins, prehistoric ages, races, classification and migrations.

Course 1 used Peabody Museum archaeological/ethnological collections and anthropological literature in instruction.

## Major state 5 — third-year Philosophy crosswalk

### Philosophy 12 — Dropped; Santayana course

Lovejoy card: **Philosophy 12 — Dropped**.

George Santayana retrospectively states that on returning to Harvard in **September 1897** he began a **new Philosophy 12 on Plato and Aristotle in English**, using the *Republic*, *Phaedrus*, *Symposium*, *Phaedo* and *Nicomachean Ethics*. Harvard's Feb 1898 examination schedule confirms Philosophy 12 was active that year.

Current statement:

> **Lovejoy registered for Santayana's new Plato/Aristotle Philosophy 12 in 1897–98 and then dropped it.**

Do not turn registration into completed Santayana training or causal influence.

### Philosophy 20d — exact same-year identity CLOSED

Lovejoy card: **Philosophy 20d — A**.

Harvard Schlesinger Library, Maud Perry Pope course notebooks, gives exact same-year metadata:

`Philosophy 20d (Ethical Seminary), George H. Palmer, 1897-1898.`

JHU preserves Lovejoy's `The Ethics of Authority and the Ethics of Value`, 10 Jun 1898, `Phil 20d`.

Therefore:

> **Lovejoy completed George H. Palmer's Ethical Seminary (Philosophy 20d) in 1897–98 and received A; a surviving Lovejoy course essay is identifiable at JHU.**

### Philosophy 20c — registration secure; completion/exact-year course line HOLD

Lovejoy card: `Philosophy 20c`, no visible grade mark. Royce's archive and seminar history strongly associate 20c with his seminary/logical work, and the Royce seminar's announced 1898 topic was problems of logic; however the exact 1897–98 catalogue line has not yet been directly recovered.

Do not write that Lovejoy **completed** Royce's logic seminar in 1897–98.

### Philosophy 2 hf — A; exact identity HOLD
