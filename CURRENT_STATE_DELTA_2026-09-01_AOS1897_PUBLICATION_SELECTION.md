## New state-bearing result — Milinda changes evidentiary role

The direct documentary sequence is:

`10 Apr 1897 AOS Final Circular`

`Critical summary of the argument of the Milinda-pañha`

`->`

`22–24 Apr 1897 Baltimore Proceedings, No.30`

`On the meaning of the Buddhist technical terms ...`

`->`

`1898 JAOS`

`The Buddhistic technical terms upādāna and upādisesa`.

A complete citation audit of the 1898 article finds **three explicit Milinda Pañha loci**, all in Section I (`upādāna`):

1. `Milinda Pañha, p.65.11` supports the proposition that **karma** determines the particular condition of rebirth;
2. **`MP. p.32.12`** supports the paired proposition that **upādāna** accounts for renewed existence / rebirth per se;
3. `Milinda Pañha, p.60` supports a local proposition in Lovejoy's psychological reconstruction around vedanā / sensation.

Earlier state text saying `MP p.33` is superseded. The correct published reference is **p.32**.

Direct collation identifies these as page/line-style references to Vilhelm Trenckner's 1880 Roman-script Pāli edition and closes their proposition-level fit.

Section II (`upādi / upādisesa`) contains no explicit Milinda citation. Its proof apparatus instead turns to Sutta Nipāta, Oldenberg, Childers, Rhys Davids, etymological/scribal controls and other canonical/Indological evidence.

Therefore:

> **In the eventual publication, the Milinda Pañha is not the article-wide object. It is reallocated as local witness evidence inside the upādāna proof structure.**
