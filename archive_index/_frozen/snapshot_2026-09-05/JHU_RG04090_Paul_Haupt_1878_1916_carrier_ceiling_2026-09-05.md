# JHU RG-04-090 Series 8 — Paul Haupt 1878–1916 carrier ceiling

Date: 2026-09-05  
Status: **SERIES-LEVEL CARRIER REAL / 1897 CONTAINER NOT RECOVERED / PUBLIC-DISCOVERY CEILING**

## Purpose

The 1897 Baltimore AOS search briefly promoted Paul Haupt because he was a central Johns Hopkins Oriental/Semitic institutional node and because the current Johns Hopkins departmental record group explicitly preserves a Paul Haupt series spanning the relevant period. This note fixes the evidence level so that the existence of the series is not mistaken for recovery of an AOS or Lovejoy file.

## 1. Direct institutional carrier

Johns Hopkins University Special Collections currently describes `Department of Near Eastern Studies records`, **RG-04-090**. The record group includes a distinct:

> **Series 8: Paul Haupt, 1878–1916.**

The collection-level description identifies record types including correspondence, letters/recommendations, applications, lecture agendas, annual reports and related departmental records. The department was renamed the Oriental Seminary in 1896 under Haupt, so Series 8 is chronologically and institutionally capable of preserving material around the April 1897 Baltimore AOS meeting.

This establishes a real institutional series. It does **not** establish that any surviving item concerns the AOS, Lovejoy, the Baltimore meeting, or Lovejoy's title change.

## 2. Container reduction attempted

Public web/finding-aid discovery was attacked for Series 8 child/container records and for 1896–1898 units using combinations of `Paul Haupt`, `1897`, `American Oriental Society`, `AOS`, `Oriental Seminary`, `correspondence`, `lecture agenda`, `Lovejoy`, `Oertel`, `Lanman`, `Moore`, `Bloomfield`, and `Gilman`.

The current public discovery layer confirms the Series 8 date span but has **not exposed a box/folder/container list for a 1896–1898 unit** through the available indexed interface.

Therefore the calibrated result is:

`SERIES 8 EXISTS AND COVERS 1897 != 1897 RELEVANT CONTAINER IDENTIFIED`.

Do not continue repeatedly querying the same public Series 8 landing page unless a new finding-aid export, container endpoint, or repository inventory becomes available.

## 3. Separate Paul Haupt papers are not a substitute

JHU also holds `Paul Haupt papers`, **MS-0090**. The current public description is dominated by expedition correspondence/research from the 1880s, Leipzig notebooks, early scholarly material and `Notes from Oriental Seminary`. Its publicly described profile does not supply a coherent spring-1897 AOS administrative or editorial run.

MS-0090 therefore should not be silently substituted for RG-04-090 Series 8 merely because Haupt is the creator and the overall collection span reaches the 1890s.

Project firewall:

> **HAUPT PERSONAL PAPERS != ORIENTAL SEMINARY DEPARTMENTAL SERIES; DATE RANGE != RELEVANT FILE.**

## 4. Distributed associations

JHU authority/discovery metadata associates Haupt with several larger collections, including Daniel Coit Gilman, Basil L. Gildersleeve, and Arthur O. Lovejoy materials. These associations justify name-targeted checking when a concrete correspondent/date unit appears.

They do not by themselves establish a Haupt↔Lovejoy or Haupt↔AOS item in spring 1897.

## 5. Search consequence

RG-04-090 Series 8 remains a legitimate **secondary Hopkins institutional carrier**, but current public discovery cannot reduce it below the series level. It should therefore sit below the directly requestable/directly documented carriers:

1. AOS 1897 Corresponding Secretary letter-copy book / Recording Secretary proceedings book;
2. JHU MS-0873 March–April 1897 Lovejoy correspondence tranche;
3. Oertel MS 590 Box 3 Folder 56, especially undated `I-R`;
4. JHU RG-15-010 Volume 1, March–May 1897 local-host minutes;
5. RG-04-090 Series 8 only if repository/container metadata can isolate a 1896–1898 unit.
