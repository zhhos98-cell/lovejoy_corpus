## 1. Contemporary Chicago witness

The University of Chicago `University Record`, vol. II, no. 5, dated **30 April 1897**, announces:

> `The Semitic Club will meet Tuesday, May 4, at 7:30 P.M., in Haskell Oriental Museum. A report of the recent meeting of the American Oriental Society, held at Baltimore last week, will be presented by Associate Professor Price.`

This is a directly contemporary University notice issued six days after the close of the Baltimore AOS meeting of 22–24 April 1897.

Primary public carrier: University of Chicago Campus Publications, `University Record`, vol. II, no. 5 (30 Apr 1897), p. 45, digitized PDF/text, document `mvol-0007-0002-0005`.

The notice establishes **a scheduled Chicago report by Ira M. Price on the immediately preceding Baltimore meeting**. It does not by itself establish that the 4 May meeting occurred as announced, the report's contents, Price's personal attendance at Baltimore, or any mention of Lovejoy.
