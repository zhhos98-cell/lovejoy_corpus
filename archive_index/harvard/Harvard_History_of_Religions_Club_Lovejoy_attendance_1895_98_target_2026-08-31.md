## Target proposition

The current Harvard chronology now proves Arthur O. Lovejoy took:

- Sanskrit sequence, 1895–96;
- Lanman's advanced Pāli Course 5, A, 1896–97;
- Toy's comparative History of Hebrew Religion, A, 1896–97;

Separate direct public evidence establishes a Cambridge **History of Religions Club** from winter 1891–92 and a Boston/Cambridge history-of-religions lecture network in which Lanman, David Gordon Lyon and Toy were organizers. ## Direct period control on Club function

A January 1893 *Biblical World* report states that during winter **1891–92** a History of Religions Club was formed at Cambridge; members consisted largely of Harvard professors and members of learned institutions in Boston. Meetings were held monthly, with papers followed by general discussion.

Thus, if Lovejoy appears in 1895–98 records, the result would document participation in a live comparative-religion discussion forum rather than merely nominal membership in an administrative organization.
