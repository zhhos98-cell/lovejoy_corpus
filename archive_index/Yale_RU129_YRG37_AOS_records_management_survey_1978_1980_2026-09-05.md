## Access calibration

The current RU 129 finding aid states that the collection is **restricted to Office of Origin only**. Therefore this survey entry is not presently an open research shortcut to the AOS records.

What it proves:

- Yale Manuscripts and Archives performed a records-management survey of the American Oriental Society in 1978-1980;
- AOS was represented in Yale's records-management classification as `YRG 37` at that time.

What it does **not** prove:

- that `YRG 37` is itself the call number of a surviving/open AOS corporate archive;
- what records the Society possessed in 1978-1980;
- that nineteenth-century letter books, Directors' books, minutes, or submitted manuscripts survived to that date;
- that the restricted survey can currently be inspected by an outside researcher.
