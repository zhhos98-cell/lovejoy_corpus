## III. Religion Lectures, 1900 / 1905–1906 — a second genetics carrier

The same Box 38 Folder 10 contains `Religion Lectures, 1900, 1905-1906`; the finding aid says manuscript fragments survive and notes that in 1905–06 Lovejoy offered a course on the phenomenology of religion.

This matters because Lovejoy's printed p.381 n.21 says that the conclusions of the 1906 article had been reached in 1900 and were strongly influenced by study with Léon Marillier.

The lectures can therefore test a currently open chronology:

`PARIS / MARILLIER 1898–99`
-> `LOVEJOY SAYS CONCLUSION REACHED 1900`
-> `RELIGION TEACHING 1900 / 1905–06`
-> `1906 MONIST ARTICLE`.

Key questions:

- does `mana / manitou / wakonda / efficacy / energetics` appear in the 1900 layer or only in 1905–06 material?
- are 1900 and 1905–06 layers physically distinguishable?
- which references are already present in 1900?
- do later sources such as Jones 1905 enter as updates to an older architecture?
- does the course preserve a broader phenomenology of religion from which the 1906 article selects one causal relation?
- how does teaching reclassify Marillier-owned material before publication?

This could distinguish:

`EARLY SUBSTANTIVE THESIS`
from
`LATER EVIDENTIARY RETOOLING / SOURCE UPDATE`.
