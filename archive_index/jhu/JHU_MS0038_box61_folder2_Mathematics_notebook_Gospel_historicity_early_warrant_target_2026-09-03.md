## I. Why this could matter

The current Lovejoy warrant chronology is deliberately cautious:

- 1898 Buddhist paper = local explicit warrant limits;
- 1902 = affinity does not license influence;
- 1904 = portable proposition-sensitive source criticism with explicit `weight of proof` language;
- 1906–09 = increasingly explicit historical/propositional audits;
- 1915 = institutionalized claim/jurisdiction procedure.

If this notebook can be securely dated **before or around the Harvard/Pāli period**, it could provide an independent early baseline in a non-Buddhist object.

The potentially relevant operation, as described by the finding aid, is:

`MULTIPLE HISTORICAL TESTIMONIES`
→ compare agreements/disagreements
→ preserve a weaker common-content judgment
→ suspend stronger historicity.

That would be structurally adjacent to later Lovejoy practices without proving a stable method or causal continuity.

But no such claim is licensed until the manuscript itself is read and dated.
