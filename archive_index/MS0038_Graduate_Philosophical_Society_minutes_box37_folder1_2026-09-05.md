## Why this matters for the AOS 1897 lost-paper search

Daniel J. Wilson used the `Minutes of the Graduate Philosophical Society of Harvard` in reconstructing Lovejoy's Harvard formation and reports that Lovejoy served as secretary in 1896-1897 and led a February 1897 discussion on the problem of evil. The current MS-0038 finding aid now gives an exact physical locator for those minutes.

This makes the minutes a direct, requestable Lovejoy-local institutional carrier for the period immediately preceding the documented AOS sequence:

`18 Mar 1897 AOS preliminary circular`

→ `7 Apr 1897 Lanman Cambridge Conferences Buddhist talk` [environment only; Lovejoy attendance unproved]

→ `10 Apr 1897 AOS final circular: Critical summary of the argument of the Milinda-pañha`

→ `22-24 Apr 1897 Proceedings: upādāna / upādāna-kkhandhā title state`.

## Inspection protocol

Concentrate first on **January-April 1897**, especially February through 24 April. Record every meeting date, speaker, paper/discussion title, and any wording involving:

- Lovejoy / A. O. L.
- Buddhism / Buddhist
- Milinda / Milinda-pañha
- Pāli / Pali
- India / Indian religion
- upādāna / khandha / upādānakkhandha
- Nirvāṇa
- dependent origination / nidāna
- Sāṃkhya / Sankhya
- problem of evil / comparative religion

Do not infer that a Society discussion is the AOS paper unless the minutes explicitly connect the objects. A title or problem overlap establishes an additional documentary state, not manuscript identity.
