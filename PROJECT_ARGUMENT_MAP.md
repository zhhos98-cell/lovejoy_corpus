## 1. Core problem

The project asks how Lovejoy's late-1890s work moved among textual criticism, comparative religion, philosophical reconstruction, public communication, and later abstraction.

Core control:

> **different relations require different warrants, and the research object can change between notebook work, announced communication, meeting record, publication carrier, article, and later synthesis.**

Compact Lovejoy-local rule:

> **one relation is not allowed automatically to answer for another.**

This is a local historical claim supported by direct Lovejoy evidence, not a priority claim for the mature history-of-ideas method.

## 8. Evidence firewall

Analyst terms such as `relational decomposition`, `proof-burden separation`, `scale contraction`, and `reaggregation` remain analyst vocabulary unless actor usage is independently demonstrated.

Do not infer:

- transmission from proximity;
- assent from citation;
- individual drafting from appearance in a collective signatory block;
- attendance at a specific lecture from Cambridge Conferences membership;
- manuscript identity from title continuity;
- exact title-date from meeting date when the title is recovered only in the June Proceedings;
- originality from failed source recovery;
- historical order from morphological/classificatory order;
- genealogy from analytical resemblance;
- a recovered revision mechanism from a bounded before/after title change;
- archival absence from public-discovery failure.
